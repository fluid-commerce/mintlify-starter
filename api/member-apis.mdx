A **member** is a person in a Fluid company — a customer, a rep, or a type the company defines. Fluid has two sets of member endpoints, and which one you use depends on **who is calling**:

| You are building | Use | Credential |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An app or portal where a member works with their own account, contacts, orders, and team | The **member API**, `/api/member/v2026-10/...` | The member's own credential |
| A back-office integration that manages a company's members | **Member management**, in the People section of the API reference | A company token |
| A shopper account page in a checkout | The Checkout API's shopper endpoints | A shopper's token — see [Checkout](/api/choosing-a-cart-surface) |

## The member API

The member API acts as the signed-in member. The credential alone decides which member and which company a request is about — no path, query, or body parameter ever names a member. A request for a record the member doesn't own returns `404`.

### Credentials

Send the member's credential as a Bearer token to `https://api.fluid.app`:

```bash
curl "https://api.fluid.app/api/member/v2026-10/me" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <member credential>"
```

A member credential is one of:

- A member token.
- A member session JWT.
- A portal JWT whose login is linked to a membership in that company.

The member API refuses, with `401`:

- Company, partner, public (`pub-`), and droplet installation tokens.
- Admin and impersonation credentials, and any credential for a member who is also an admin of that company. The one exception is on the team reads; see [Team](#team).
- A credential for a member who is removed, inactive, or locked, or whose company is inactive.

Member tokens can read and write. Member session JWTs and portal JWTs are **read-only**: a write with one returns `403`.

Two more limits apply:

- **Some areas depend on the membership.** A valid credential for a membership without access gets `403`:
  - Contacts, groups, notes, tasks, messaging, and the team list are for the rep member type. Any other membership gets `403`, including a type the company defines.
  - Media and sharing, asset statistics, the AI assistant, and My Site are for members whose member type has **Can act as rep** turned on. Other memberships get `403`.
  - Catalog search, the statistics dashboard, and the activity feed are open to every membership.
- **A member session JWT reads only part of the API.** That JWT also travels in order and subscription email links. Contacts, groups, notes, tasks, messaging, AI conversations, statistics, push devices, and the team list refuse it with `403`. Use a member token or a portal JWT. The member's own orders, subscriptions, and activity feed still accept a member session JWT.

### Team

A member can read their own team — the part of their company's genealogy below them — and request moves within it:

| Operation | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [List the team](/api-reference/team/list-the-members-team) and [summarize it](/api-reference/team/summarize-the-members-team) | The members below the member, and how many sit at each level |
| [Read a team seat](/api-reference/genealogy-history/read-recorded-relationship-for-member-access) and [its placements](/api-reference/genealogy-history/list-recorded-placements-for-member-access) | One seat in the member's team, with its recorded relationship and placement history |
| [Page children](/api-reference/genealogy-reads/page-current-team-children), [descendants](/api-reference/genealogy-reads/page-current-team-descendants), [subtree](/api-reference/genealogy-reads/page-current-team-subtree), and [one level](/api-reference/genealogy-reads/page-one-current-team-relative-level) | Walk the team, one cursor page at a time |
| [Count seats by level](/api-reference/genealogy-reads/count-current-team-structural-seats-by-relative-level) | Team size at each level below a seat |
| [Preview](/api-reference/genealogy-moves/preview-a-member-genealogy-move) and [request](/api-reference/genealogy-moves/request-a-member-genealogy-move) a move | Check a move, then submit it |
| [List](/api-reference/genealogy-moves/list-own-move-requests), [read](/api-reference/genealogy-moves/read-own-move-request), and [withdraw](/api-reference/genealogy-moves/withdraw-an-own-move-request) move requests, and [read a move job](/api-reference/genealogy-moves/read-an-own-durable-move-job) | Track the member's own requests |

- **Team reads are limited to the member's current team.** A member never sees seats outside it.
- **The team list and the seats use different ids.** The team list identifies each person by member id. The seat, tree, and move operations take seat ids.
- **Moves take effect on the server's clock.** An immediate move returns `200`. A move that needs approval, or runs later as a job, returns `202` with a receipt you can follow.
- **Moves stay inside the member's downline.** The member's own seat can't be moved. Only a member whose type has **Can act as rep** turned on can request a move.
- **A company has to enable moves.** Until it does, move requests are refused.

The team endpoints follow the same credential rules as the rest of the member API, including the read-only JWTs: a move request with a member session JWT returns `403`. The team list and summary are for reps, and refuse a member session JWT.

One exception: a member whose user is also an admin of the same company, such as an owner who is also a rep, can read their own team with their own member token. They can't request moves with it.

The member API also covers the rest of what a member does in their own app or portal. Each area has its own section in the API reference:

| Settings | Agreements, notification preferences, and push devices | [List applicable agreements](/api-reference/agreements/list-applicable-agreements) |
| Payments | Saved cards with 3D Secure, and the member's own plan and add-ons | [List saved payment methods](/api-reference/payment-methods/list-the-members-saved-payment-methods) |
| Contacts | Contacts, groups, notes, tasks, contact activity, bulk changes, and CSV imports. Reps only | [Contacts](#contacts), below |
| Activities | The member's activity feed and unread count | [List the activity feed](/api-reference/activities/list-the-members-activity-feed) |
| Calendar | Personal events, RSVPs to company events, and catch-ups | [List calendar events](/api-reference/calendar-events/list-calendar-events-in-a-window) |
| Orders and carts | Orders, open carts, and claiming a guest cart | [List carts](/api-reference/carts/list-the-members-carts) |
| Subscriptions | Edit, pause, skip, cancel, and bundle the member's subscriptions | [Read the next subscription](/api-reference/subscriptions/read-the-next-scheduled-subscription-summary) |
| Media and sharing | Media with direct uploads, playlists, tracked shares, and catalog search | [List media](/api-reference/media/list-the-members-media) |
| Stats | Dashboard totals and top assets | [Show the dashboard](/api-reference/stats/show-the-members-dashboard) |
| My Site | MySite settings, theme, custom links, featured products, and statistics | [Read MySite settings](/api-reference/mysite-settings/read-mysite-settings) |
| Messaging | Conversations, messages, drafts, scheduled messages, reactions, pins, and announcements | [List conversations](/api-reference/messaging/list-conversations) |
| AI | Conversations with the assistant | [List AI conversations](/api-reference/ai/list-ai-conversations) |

For live messaging, call [Admit a realtime socket](/api-reference/messaging/admit-a-realtime-socket) first. It returns a ticket that opens one socket within one minute, so the member's credential never goes in a socket URL. Get a new ticket each time you connect.

## Member management

These endpoints are in the **People** section of the API reference, under **Members**.

A company manages its members with a company token that has the `members` permission:

| Operation | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [List members](/api-reference/members/list-members) and [find a member](/api-reference/members/find-a-member-by-a-unique-identifier) | Search the company's members |
| [Create](/api-reference/members/create-a-member), [get](/api-reference/members/get-a-member), and [update](/api-reference/members/update-a-member) a member | Manage one member |
| [Change a member's type](/api-reference/members/change-a-members-type) | Move a member between customer, rep, and your own types |
| [Get a member's sponsor genealogy](/api-reference/members/get-a-members-sponsor-genealogy) | A member's sponsor line. A rep can also call this for their own downline. |

Use these endpoints rather than the older customer and member endpoints listed with them when the member's type matters: only member management returns `member_type`.

To manage the company's genealogy trees themselves — defining trees, placing members, and approving moves — use the **Genealogy** endpoints in the **People** section of the API reference, with a company token.
