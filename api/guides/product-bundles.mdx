A product bundle sells several variants as one product. The bundle is made of groups, and each group holds items. Each item is one variant of one of your products.

This guide explains the model and the rules the server applies. It doesn't restate request and response schemas; the linked API Reference pages own those. To render a bundle in a theme, see [Product bundles in themes](/themes/product-bundles).

## Groups and items

A group is either static or dynamic, and its `group_type` says which:

| `group_type` | What ships |
| --- | --- |
| `included` | Every item in the group. The shopper doesn't choose. |
| `customizable` | The items the shopper picks, within the group's selection rule. |

A bundle whose groups are all `included` is a static bundle. A bundle with at least one `customizable` group is a dynamic bundle. There is no separate bundle type to set: it follows from the groups.

Each item names a `variant_id` and a `quantity`, which is how many of the variant one pick ships (1 when omitted). An item's `config` holds its in-bundle settings, such as its price, an image, a per-bundle cap in `max_quantity`, and `is_default`, which tells a storefront to pre-select the item.

A group's `purpose` is `default` unless you set `membership` or `subscription`. A bundle's `mode` is `catalog` for a product bundle. `enrollment` is an enrollment pack, which uses the same groups but starts an enrollment at checkout.

## Selection rules

A `customizable` group must have a `selection_type`:

| `selection_type` | The shopper must pick |
| --- | --- |
| `exact` | Exactly `min_selections` |
| `min_only` | At least `min_selections` |
| `max_only` | At most `max_selections` |
| `min_and_max` | Between `min_selections` and `max_selections` |

Picks are counted by quantity, not by distinct items: two of one variant count as two picks. A bound that doesn't apply to the type is cleared when the group is saved. `exact` sets `max_selections` to `min_selections`, `min_only` clears `max_selections`, and `max_only` clears `min_selections`. `max_selections` can't be below `min_selections`.

## How a bundle is priced

A bundle's price comes from one of three places. All amounts are decimal currency amounts such as `"24.50"`, not cents.

- **Bundle-level pricing.** When the bundle's `bundle_pricing_enabled` setting is on, the bundle is charged its own per-country price and its groups' prices are not used. It can't be turned on while any group uses `dynamic_price`.
- **A fixed-price group.** A group's `pricing_type` defaults to `fixed_price`. The group adds its own price, whatever the shopper picks.
- **A dynamic-price group.** With `dynamic_price`, the group adds the sum of each picked item's price times its quantity.

When bundle-level pricing is off, the bundle's price is the sum of its groups.

A group's `min_price` is the "from" price a storefront shows before anything is picked. It is not enforced on the charged total.

For a retail buyer, an item's price inside a dynamic group is its positive country override, else its positive `config` price, else the variant's own price in the buyer's country. A zero price at either `config` level counts as unset, not free.

### Country prices

Bundle-level pricing and each group carry per-country prices in a `country_pricing` list. An entry names a `country_code`, is read only while `enabled` is `true`, and can carry `price`, `wholesale`, `subscription_price`, `wholesale_subscription_price`, `cv`, `qv`, `wholesale_cv`, `wholesale_qv` and `points`. When a group's country has no enabled entry, its `fixed_price` is the retail price.

A wholesale buyer pays the `wholesale` amount. It never falls back to the retail price: an unset wholesale amount charges 0.

A group with any `country_pricing` entries limits the bundle to the countries those entries enable. A group with none doesn't restrict the bundle. The server stamps each entry's `currency_code` with the currency the company sells that country in; a value you send is replaced.

### Hidden groups

An `included` group can set `hidden` in its `pricing_config`. Its items still ship and stay on the order, so fulfillment and inventory see them, but customer-facing cart and order views leave them out. Their entries carry `display_to_customer` set to `false`.

### Companies on the bundle price grid

Some companies store bundle prices per price type, market and currency on the bundle price grid instead. For those companies the grid owns every price field: a bundle write keeps the stored prices and ignores the ones you send. Set prices with [Show a bundle's per-price-type price grid](/api-reference/products/show-a-bundles-per-price-type-price-grid) and [Write a batch of bundle price cells](/api-reference/products/write-a-batch-of-bundle-price-cells). [Member pricing](/api/guides/member-pricing) explains when a company is on the grid.

## Create and change a bundle

A bundle belongs to a product. Create the product first with [Create a Product](/api-reference/company/create-a-product), then make it a bundle with [Create a bundle](/api-reference/products/create-a-bundle-including-a-customizable-build-your-own-bundle), sending its groups and their items in one request. Every variant must belong to your company. A product holds one bundle at a time.

The order of the groups and items arrays sets their `sort_order`. Read bundles back with [List bundles](/api-reference/products/list-bundles) and [Show a bundle](/api-reference/products/show-a-bundle).

[Update a bundle](/api-reference/products/update-a-bundle) changes only what you send. When you send the groups, send all of them:

- A group or item with an `id` is updated, and one without an `id` is added.
- A stored group whose `id` is missing from the list is deleted.
- Inside a group that sends items, a stored item whose `id` is missing is deleted. Leave out a group's items to keep them as they are.
- For a company on the bundle price grid, a group or item with stored grid prices is deleted only when you send it with `_destroy` set to `true`. Leaving it out is refused, so prices are never dropped by accident.

[Delete a bundle](/api-reference/products/delete-a-bundle) removes the bundle and clears the product's bundle flag. The product itself is kept.

A bundle's settings are replaced as a whole when you send them, so send every setting you want to keep. Pairs of mutually exclusive groups are set in the Fluid admin bundle editor, not through these endpoints.

## Read a bundle on the storefront

The public product read reports whether a product is a bundle in `is_bundle`. [Public Product by slug](/api-reference/storefront/public-product-by-slug) adds the groups and items in `bundle_groups`. To list only bundles, or only other products, pass `filter[bundle]` to [Public Products catalog](/api-reference/storefront/public-products-catalog).

## Add a bundle to a cart

Add the bundle product's variant with [Add items to cart](/api-reference/cart-items/add-items-to-cart), and list the shopper's picks in `bundled_items`. Each pick names a `variant_id`, a `quantity` and the `product_bundle_group_id` of the group it was picked from.

```json
{
  "items": [
    {
      "variant_id": 88100,
      "quantity": 1,
      "bundled_items": [
        { "variant_id": 88120, "quantity": 1, "product_bundle_group_id": 7741 },
        { "variant_id": 88121, "quantity": 1, "product_bundle_group_id": 7741 }
      ]
    }
  ]
}
```

Send only the picks from `customizable` groups. The server adds every `included` group's items itself, and drops any you send so they are never doubled. The exception is an `included` group in a mutually exclusive pair: send the items of the side the shopper chose. It doesn't add items marked `is_default`: a storefront that pre-selects them must send them.

The server checks the picks before anything is added:

- Each pick's variant must be in the group it names.
- Each group's pick count must meet its selection rule.
- A pick's quantity can't exceed the item's `max_quantity`.
- A pick must be available in the cart's country.
- An item that must be bought on a subscription needs the bundle line to be on one.

When a check fails, the response is `422` with the first problem's message, and nothing is added to the cart.

## Known limits

- An older kind of static bundle links variants directly to the product through `product_bundles`, without groups. New bundles should use groups.
- These endpoints don't set pairs of mutually exclusive groups. Use the Fluid admin bundle editor for those.
