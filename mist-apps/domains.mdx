Every Mist app has its own address on a Fluid-hosted domain, with HTTPS. You can also serve it at a domain you own, such as `events.dundermifflin.com`.

<Note>
  This page is about a Mist app's domain. To connect a domain to your storefront and checkout, see [Set up a custom domain](/setup/custom-domain). The `fluid domains` CLI commands manage storefront domains only.
</Note>

## Before you start

- You need access to the DNS settings for the domain, at your registrar or DNS provider.
- The app must be set up. While it's still provisioning, adding a domain fails.
- Through the API, your token needs the `mist.env_vars` permission.

## Connect a domain

### In the Mist desktop app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Domain">
    Right-click the Mist app and click **Domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the domain">
    Under **Add a domain**, enter the full domain, such as `events.dundermifflin.com`, and click **Add domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the DNS records">
    Mist shows each record to create, with its **Type**, **Name** and **Value**. Click **Copy value** and create each record at your DNS provider exactly as shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-check">
    Click **Re-check verification**. Repeat until the domain shows **Live**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Through the API

1. [Attach a custom domain](/api-reference/mist_domains/attach-a-custom-domain). The response lists the DNS records to create.
2. Create the records at your DNS provider.
3. Call [Inspect a custom domain](/api-reference/mist_domains/inspect-a-custom-domain) until the domain is verified and no longer misconfigured.

The CLI has no command for Mist app domains yet.

## The DNS records

You may see two kinds of record:

- **Verification record**: a `TXT` record that proves you own the domain. Mist shows it when the domain needs one.
- **Routing record**: points the domain at the app. A subdomain, such as `events.dundermifflin.com`, usually uses a `CNAME`. A root domain, such as `dundermifflin.com`, usually uses an `A` record.

Always use the exact type, name and value Mist shows for your domain. If a record already exists for the same name, replace it rather than adding a second one.

## What the status means

| Status | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pending verification** | Fluid can't confirm you own the domain yet. | Add the verification record, then re-check. |
| **Needs DNS routing** | You own the domain, but it doesn't point at the app yet. | Add the routing record, then re-check. |
| **Live** | The domain is verified and points at the app. | Nothing. |
| **Status unknown** | Mist couldn't check the domain's routing. | Re-check in a moment. If the domain doesn't load, add the records shown. |

DNS changes can take from a few minutes to a few hours to reach everyone.

## Remove a domain

In the **Domain** dialog, click **Remove domain** next to the domain, then confirm. The app stops responding at that domain, and keeps working at its Fluid address. You can add the domain again later.

Through the API, use [Detach a custom domain](/api-reference/mist_domains/detach-a-custom-domain).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The domain stays on Pending verification" id="pending-verification">
    Check that the `TXT` record's name and value match what Mist shows, character for character. Some DNS providers add your domain to the end of every name for you. If yours does, enter only the part of the name before your domain.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The domain stays on Needs DNS routing" id="needs-routing">
    Look for an older record on the same name, such as an `A` record left from a previous site, and remove it. A name can't have a `CNAME` and other records at the same time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Adding the domain fails" id="add-fails">
    A domain can belong to only one app. If it's already attached to another Mist app, remove it there first. If the app is still being set up, wait until it's live.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
