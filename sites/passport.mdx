A Site opens inside your storefront's page. The storefront keeps the parts that need its own sign-in, cart and credit, and draws them around your Site. This page covers those parts, what each one gives your Site, and what you can change.

## What the storefront adds around your Site

| Part | What it is | Who sees it |
| - | - | - |
| **The Passport** | The storefront's account menu: a round button in a corner that opens the member's account, orders, subscriptions, their sites menu and sign-out | A signed-in member |
| **Lead capture** | FairShare's button that asks a guest for their details for the member who shared the link | A guest |
| **The cart button** | FairShare's floating cart, when your company shows one | Everyone |
| **Cart drawer and checkout** | The storefront's own cart and checkout, which `fluid.cart` opens | Everyone |

A signed-in member sees the Passport and never the lead-capture button. A guest sees the lead-capture button and never the Passport. When the visitor signs in or out, the two swap in place.

## The Passport

The Passport is the storefront's account menu. On a Site it does three jobs for you:

- **It's the visitor's account.** The member's profile, addresses, payment methods, orders and subscriptions open from it, so your Site doesn't build any of them. See [What members can do in the Passport](#what-members-can-do-in-the-passport).
- **It lists the member's Sites.** Its sites menu lists the Sites the member can open, including any your company pinned for them. See [Show a Site to members](/sites/overview#show-a-site-to-members).
- **It holds the sign-out.** A Site can't sign anyone out. The visitor signs out in the Passport, and confirms it there.

### Where the Passport sits on a Site

The storefront draws the Passport outside your Site's frame, in the spot where FairShare's lead-capture button sits. Your company's FairShare widget settings decide that spot. If FairShare moves its button, the Passport follows it.

- **Your Site can't move or restyle it.** The Passport isn't in your Site's page, so your CSS and scripts don't reach it. Keep your own controls clear of that corner. Hiding FairShare's buttons, as shown below, doesn't hide the Passport.
- **The member can't move it here either.** The storefront sets its spot on a Site, so the Passport shows no **Passport settings** row.
- **It opens over your Site and stays in view.** On a phone it opens as a bottom sheet. See [How it stays in view](/themes/member-storefront/passport#how-it-stays-in-view).

To place the Passport yourself, on your storefront's theme pages, see [Place and open the Passport](/themes/member-storefront/passport).

### What members can do in the Passport

Each row under **Your account** opens inside the Passport, without leaving your Site. **Open in full page** at the foot of each one goes to the member's account on Fluid's checkout.

| Row | What the member can do there |
| - | - |
| **Profile** | See their name, email, phone and language. **Edit profile** changes their first name, last name and language |
| **Addresses** | Add an address, edit any saved one, make one the default, and delete the others |
| **Payment methods** | Make a card the default and delete a card. Adding a card stays on the checkout |
| **Orders** | See their latest orders, with **Load more** for older ones. Opening an order shows its status, items, totals, shipping address and method, payment, and tracking links. **View full receipt** opens the receipt on the checkout |
| **Subscriptions** | See their subscriptions, with **Load more** for older ones. **Manage** on a subscription shows its actions and edits: skip the next order, pause it (indefinitely, until a date, or for a number of orders), resume it on its schedule or on a date, cancel it, reactivate it to a saved address, and change its next bill date, quantity, card or shipping address |

An early order, such as a bill date inside a cycle the member already paid for, isn't placed from the Passport. The Passport links to the subscription on the checkout instead.

## Sign-in carries over from the storefront

A member who is signed in on your storefront is already signed in on every Site there. There's no second login and no sign-in screen of your own to build.

```ts
import { fluid } from "@fluid-app/sites";

const me = await fluid.auth.me();
if (me.viewer) {
  greeting.textContent = `Welcome back, ${me.viewer.name}`;
}
```

`fluid.auth.me()` gives your Site the visitor's name, avatar, user and member ids, their relation to the Site (`owner`, `admin` or `member`), and only the member fields your Site declares in `config/access.json`. See [Read who is here](/sites/sdk#read-who-is-here) and [Fluid data the site uses](/sites/declarations#fluid-data-the-site-uses).

### How it stays secure

Your Site's code runs on its own host under `sites.fluid.app`, in a sandboxed frame on the storefront's page. Because that host is a different origin from your storefront:

- **Your Site never receives the visitor's Fluid credentials.** It can't read the storefront's sign-in cookie or any Fluid token, and neither can a script you add to your Site.
- **The storefront hands your Site a short-lived session.** It's scoped to that one Site and that visitor, and the SDK renews it while the page is open. The SDK sends it only to your Site's own host.
- **Every call acts as the visitor.** `fluid.api` and `ctx.fluid.api` reach only that visitor's own data, within the scopes your Site declares. A guest's call goes as a guest.
- **A private Site's files stay private.** Its pages, scripts and functions aren't served to someone the Site doesn't admit, even when they open its host directly.
- **Sign-in takes a click.** `fluid.auth.signIn()` sends the visitor to the storefront's own sign-in only from a click, so a Site can't redirect visitors to it on load. A call outside a click is ignored, and nothing tells your code so. Newer versions of the SDK log a warning in the browser console.

## Hide the floating cart and lead-capture buttons

To hide FairShare's floating cart and lead-capture buttons (the "bubbles"), such as when your Site draws its own controls in that corner, add `config/shell.json` to your Site and publish it:

```json config/shell.json
{ "fairshare": "hidden" }
```

Only the floating buttons go. `fluid.cart`, the cart drawer, checkout and the visit's credit keep working, so give visitors your own cart button with `fluid.cart.open()` and guests your own sign-in with `fluid.auth.signIn()`. Publish the file with Sites plugin 0.1.379 or later. See [The storefront around the Site](/sites/declarations#the-storefront-around-the-site).

<Note>
`"fairshare": "hidden"` doesn't hide the Passport. A signed-in member always sees the Passport's button, because it holds their sign-out. A guest never sees it.
</Note>

## Open the Passport from your own button

To open the Passport from a button of your own, such as an avatar in your Site's header, call `fluid.auth.openPassport()` from the click:

```tsx
import { fluid } from "@fluid-app/sites";

function AccountButton({ signedIn }: { signedIn: boolean }) {
  return signedIn ? (
    <button type="button" onClick={() => fluid.auth.openPassport()}>
      My account
    </button>
  ) : (
    <button type="button" onClick={() => fluid.auth.signIn()}>
      Sign in
    </button>
  );
}
```

A plain HTML Site loads the same SDK from its own host:

```html
<button type="button" id="account">My account</button>
<script type="module">
  import { fluid } from "/_fluid/sdk/v1/sites.js";
  document.getElementById("account").addEventListener("click", () => fluid.auth.openPassport());
</script>
```

- **Call it from a click.** The storefront opens the Passport only while the visitor's click is active, so a call on load, or after a long `await`, does nothing. Newer versions of the SDK log a warning when that happens.
- **It signs nobody out.** The Passport opens, and the visitor can sign out there and confirm it.
- **Call it only for a signed-in visitor.** For a guest, it sends the visitor to the storefront's sign-in, which doesn't bring them back to your Site. Send a guest to `fluid.auth.signIn()` instead, as above, which does.
- **It needs the storefront.** Outside a storefront, such as on the Site's own host, it does nothing and logs a warning.
- **It needs `@fluid-app/sites` 0.1.153 or later.** On an older SDK, `fluid.auth.signOut()` sends the same request and opens the Passport the same way.
- **It opens on the Passport's list.** A Site can't open it on a room such as orders.
- **Mist's previews have no Passport.** Check the button on the published Site.

## What the storefront loads, and what it costs your Site

The storefront page around your Site loads the Passport, FairShare (the cart, lead capture and the visit's credit) and a small script that connects your Site to them. Each one loads as a deferred module script, so none of them blocks the page. Scripts your company adds to the storefront for Sites load as the company wrote them.

- **Your Site paints on its own.** It loads in its own frame, in parallel with the storefront's scripts. If the storefront's scripts are slow or fail, your Site still opens and works. Only the parts they draw, such as the Passport and the cart, are missing.
- **The SDK is yours to load.** The storefront doesn't add anything to your Site's own pages. Import `@fluid-app/sites` in your build, or load `/_fluid/sdk/v1/sites.js` from your Site's own host as the [plain HTML guide](/sites/frameworks/html) shows. Load it as a module, so it never blocks your first paint.
- **A call waits for the sign-in, briefly.** A call made before the SDK has connected to the storefront waits for it, for up to three seconds, then goes as a guest.

What a visitor downloads, measured on 19 November 2026 for a one-line Site on a storefront, signed out. The sizes are what crossed the network, compressed, and they change as these scripts change:

| What | Requests | About |
| - | - | - |
| FairShare: the cart, lead capture and the visit's credit | 25 | 365 KB |
| The Passport | 4 | 137 KB |
| The script that connects your Site to the storefront | 1 | 23 KB |
| The SDK, `/_fluid/sdk/v1/sites.js`, when your Site loads it | 1 | 26 KB |
| The storefront page, its API calls and your Site's own files | 10 | 20 KB |
| **In all** | **41** | **572 KB** |

None of it blocks your Site: no script loads in the way of the page, and the Site's frame painted in about half a second. FairShare and the Passport load even when `"fairshare": "hidden"` hides FairShare's buttons, and for a guest, who doesn't see the Passport, so hiding the buttons doesn't make the page lighter.

## For agents

To give a Site an account button, use the examples in [Open the Passport from your own button](#open-the-passport-from-your-own-button): `fluid.auth.openPassport()` for a signed-in visitor and `fluid.auth.signIn()` for a guest, each from the visitor's click. Then check, on the published Site on your storefront:

1. A signed-in member's click opens the Passport, and a guest's click goes to the storefront's sign-in and comes back to the Site.
2. Nothing calls `fluid.auth.openPassport()` on load or after a long `await`, since those calls do nothing.
3. Your Site draws nothing of its own in the corner where the Passport sits.
4. Your Site doesn't add `<fluid-passport>`, `fluid.passport` or `data-fluid-passport-open`. Those work only on the storefront's theme pages.
