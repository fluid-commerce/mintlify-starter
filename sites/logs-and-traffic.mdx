## Logs

```bash
fluid sites logs                                  # recent lines for the site in this folder
fluid sites logs --tail                           # follow new lines as they arrive
fluid sites logs --since 1h                       # lines from the last hour
fluid sites logs --since 2026-11-10T09:00:00Z     # lines since a time, in ISO 8601
fluid sites logs --limit 200                      # at most 200 lines
fluid sites logs ./my-event                       # the site in another folder
```

Each line says when it happened, its level, and where it came from: a **publish**, one of the site's **functions**, or the site's **host**, such as a refused request.

## Traffic

```bash
fluid sites traffic                  # the last 24 hours
fluid sites traffic --period 7d      # 24h, 7d or 30d
```

It shows visits, unique visitors, the top pages, the top referring sites, and how many requests ended in an error (`4xx` and `5xx`).

## Through the API

`GET .../logs` and `GET .../traffic` under `/api/member/v2026-10/sites/{site_id}` for a site you own, or `/api/company/v2026-10/member-sites/{id}` as an admin.
