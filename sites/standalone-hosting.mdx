A site normally opens inside your storefront at `/sites/<handle>`, the address the API and `fluid deploy` give you, with the storefront's header, cart and sign-in around it. With standalone hosting, a public company site also serves on its own, as a plain web page, which suits projects that have nothing to do with the store.

Standalone hosting is turned on for each company. To turn it on, [contact support](/help/getting-help).

## Which sites serve standalone

A site serves standalone when all of these are true:

- your company owns it. A member's own site never does;
- it's public;
- it's published.

Such a site answers at its own address on `sites.fluid.app`, such as `https://yoli-my-event.sites.fluid.app`, with no storefront around it. A private site, a member's site and a branch still open only inside the storefront.

## What changes without the storefront

- `fluid.auth.signIn()` and `fluid.auth.signOut()` do nothing and log a warning, because sign-in belongs to the storefront. Build a standalone page for signed-out visitors.
- `fluid.db` works, with your tables' rules applied to the visitor.
- `fluid.cart` has no cart to talk to, so cart calls reject. A page that sells should open inside the storefront.
- `fluid.history` isn't needed: the page owns the address bar.

## Search engines

A site can be indexed only when it's a live, public company site that isn't taken down, and your company allows indexing. Every other site is served with `noindex`.

On the standalone address, an indexable site's pages name their canonical address with a `Link` header: the site's `/home/sites/<handle>` address on your storefront, which opens the same site as `/sites/<handle>`, or the standalone address for a company with no storefront. The site serves its own `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt` when its build has them. Otherwise Fluid generates them from the routes in `config/meta.json`. A site that can't be indexed serves a `robots.txt` that disallows everything, and no sitemap or `llms.txt`.

To write the page tags, sitemap and `llms.txt` into a Vite build, see [Search engines and link previews](/sites/frameworks/vite#search-engines-and-link-previews).

## Companies without a storefront

With standalone hosting on, a company with no storefront theme can still host public company sites at their `sites.fluid.app` addresses. Any other site needs the storefront to open in.
