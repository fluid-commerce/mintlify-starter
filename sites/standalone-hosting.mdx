A site normally opens inside your storefront at `/sites/<handle>`, the address the API and `fluid deploy` give you, with the storefront's header, cart and sign-in around it. With standalone hosting, a public company site also serves on its own, as a plain web page, which suits projects that have nothing to do with the store.

Standalone hosting is turned on for each company. To turn it on, [contact support](/help/getting-help).

## Which sites serve standalone

A site serves standalone when all of these are true:

- your company owns it. A member's own site never does;
- it's public;
- it's published.

Such a site answers at its own host, `https://<label>.sites.fluid.app`, with no storefront around it. The label is one Fluid gives the Site when it's first published. A private site, a member's site and a branch still open only inside the storefront.

## What changes without the storefront

- `fluid.auth.signIn()` and `fluid.auth.signOut()` do nothing and log a warning, because sign-in belongs to the storefront. Build a standalone page for signed-out visitors.
- `fluid.db` works, with your tables' rules applied to the visitor.
- `fluid.cart` has no cart to talk to, so cart calls reject. A page that sells should open inside the storefront.
- `fluid.history` isn't needed: the page owns the address bar.

## Search engines

A standalone Site follows the same indexing rules as any Site: it's indexed only when it's a live, public company Site and someone turned indexing on in its SEO settings, which are off for a new Site. See [SEO and indexing](/sites/seo#indexing).

On the standalone host:

- each page of an indexable Site names its canonical address with a `Link` header: the page's `/home/sites/<handle>` address on your storefront, or the standalone address for a company with no storefront;
- an indexable Site serves its own `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt` when its build has them. Otherwise Fluid generates them from the routes in `config/meta.json`;
- a Site that isn't indexable is sent with `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`, a `robots.txt` that disallows everything, and no sitemap or `llms.txt`.

To write the page tags, sitemap and `llms.txt` into a Vite build, see [Add SEO at build time with fluidSite()](/sites/seo#add-seo-at-build-time-with-fluidsite).

## Companies without a storefront

With standalone hosting on, a company with no storefront theme can still host public company sites at their `sites.fluid.app` addresses. Any other site needs the storefront to open in.
