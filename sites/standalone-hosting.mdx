A site normally opens inside your storefront, with the storefront's header, cart and sign-in around it. A public company site can also serve on its own, as a plain web page, which suits projects that have nothing to do with the store.

## Where it serves

A standalone site answers at its own address on `sites.fluid.app` and at `/home/sites/<slug>` on your storefront, with no storefront around it.

## What changes without the storefront

- `fluid.auth` and `fluid.db` work as they do in the storefront.
- `fluid.cart` has no cart to talk to. A page that sells should open inside the storefront.
- `fluid.history` isn't needed: the page owns the address bar.

## Companies without a storefront

A company with no storefront theme can still host sites, as long as sites are turned on. To turn them on, [contact support](/help/getting-help).
