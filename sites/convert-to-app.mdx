When a site needs something only an app has, such as its own domain or server rendering, convert it. See [Site or App?](/sites/site-or-app) for the rule.

```bash
fluid sites convert
```

Converting needs an admin login. It asks before it starts; pass `--yes` to skip the question.

## What carries over

- The site's code, or its live build when the site has no source.
- Its database rows.
- Its environment variables and secrets.
- Its visibility.
- Its address: the old site address sends visitors to the new app.

The site is archived once the app is live.

## What can't be converted yet

A private or members-only site converts only when the app can enforce the same rule on every one of its addresses. Until then, converting a site that isn't public is refused and the site stays as it is.

## Through the API

`POST /api/company/v2026-10/member-sites/{id}/conversion` starts a conversion and `GET` on the same path shows how it's going.
