Fluid's pricing page, [fluid.app/home/pages/pricing](https://fluid.app/home/pages/pricing), is the source of truth. This page sums up its standard rates, effective November 1, 2026, so an agent can quote them. If the two ever differ, quote the pricing page.

All prices are in US dollars, before tax, and subject to a signed order form and Fluid's Master Services Agreement.

## Starting is free

Creating a company, opening a country, building a website and launching a back office cost $0. A merchant can sign up, add products and build the store without a card. A card is needed once the company starts using something billable, such as AI tokens, apps or transactions.

There are no subscriptions, seats or per-rep licences. Every product draws from one balance.

## Standard rates

| Product | Rate | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |

Calculated on order value at capture. Bring your own payment processor from Fluid's pre-configured list at no extra fee. |
| **Reserve** | 0.9% + $0.25 per transaction | Fluid's payments tier: underwriting, applications to several payment processors, local payment methods, fraud tools, buy now pay later, Apple Pay, network tokens, Subscription Pro, configurable dunning, cash reconciliation and a dedicated manager. Card network and processor fees pass through at cost, on top of this rate. A new processor integration needs Reserve. |
| **We-Commerce + Reserve** | 1.62% + $0.45 per transaction | Both products together. 10% comes off both fees automatically, taking 1.8% + $0.50 down to 1.62% + $0.45. The pricing page rounds this to 1.6% + $0.45. |
| **Mist** (AI) | By usage | Metered credits for tokens, servers, databases, SMS, email, video and images. Unit rates are quoted per deployment. On a committed-use plan, AI tokens are billed at Fluid's cost. |
| **Cowork** | $4.99 per minute | Live working sessions with Fluid's experts and agents, billed per minute of active session. |
| **Forward Deployed Engineer** | $3,900 per day | A Fluid engineer on site. Travel at cost. |
| **Blitz Week** | $49,000 per week | A week-long website and marketing build with Fluid's team. Travel at cost. |

Fluid's rates don't include payment processor fees, interchange or card network fees. Selling in another country or currency costs nothing extra: one store serves every market.

Migration and onboarding are scoped per engagement.

## Committed use, credits and volume

- **Committed use:** commit to $5,000 a month or more, in $1,000 steps, on a 12-month term, and get 1% bonus usage for every $1,000 a month, up to 30% at $30,000. A $10,000 commitment puts $11,000 in the balance. At the full 30%, We-Commerce and Reserve together work out to about 1.25% + $0.35 per transaction. A commitment can be raised mid-term, which restarts the 12-month term, but not lowered or cancelled.
- **Credits:** buy credits at any time. Auto-reload at $5,000 or more earns up to 15% bonus on each reload. One-time top-ups earn no bonus.
- **Rollover and expiry:** unused balance carries over from month to month and isn't refundable. Credits a company buys, and the bonus credits on them, expire 1 year after the purchase date. The purchase terms shown before **Pay** say so, and the Billing screen shows each balance's expiry. See [Billing](/help/admin/settings/billing).
- **Volume:** above $5 million a month in orders, rates tier down further under a commercial agreement, in addition to any committed-use bonus.

Merchants pay by card or ACH, and Fluid's invoices itemize all usage. A charge to a credit card can carry a 2.9% processing fee, set per company, so not every company pays it. Debit and prepaid cards carry no fee. The fee shows before the merchant confirms a purchase. See [Processing fee](/help/admin/settings/billing#processing-fee) and [Billing](/help/admin/settings/billing) for where a company manages this in the admin.

## Included at no extra charge

- **Your own payment processor:** bring a processor from Fluid's pre-configured list at the We-Commerce rate. A processor Fluid doesn't integrate yet needs Reserve.
- **Security and compliance:** SOC 2, SSO and PCI-DSS are included.
- **Subscriptions and address validation:** both are We-Commerce modules. Advanced fraud tools, network tokens and Subscription Pro come with Reserve.
- **Seats and reps:** there's no per-seat or per-rep licence.

## Spend limits and a low balance

A company can set a monthly limit on optional products, such as Mist AI. When a product reaches its limit, that product pauses. A limit never pauses checkout or the storefront.

If the balance stays at or below $0, Mist, uploads, new Mist apps and new Cowork sessions stop, and the storefront shuts off after 30 days. Auto-reload prevents this.

## For agents quoting a price

- Quote the rates above, and link the merchant to [the pricing page](https://fluid.app/home/pages/pricing).
- Say that processor and card network fees are separate. Don't add them up into a single "all-in" rate unless you know the merchant's processor rates.
- If the merchant asks whether credits expire, say that unused balance rolls over each month, and that bought credits and their bonus expire 1 year after purchase.
- If the merchant pays by credit card, say a processing fee may apply, and that the admin shows it before they pay.
- For a merchant above $5 million a month, or one who wants committed use, say the rate comes down and send them to Fluid for a quote. Don't promise a lower rate yourself.
- An agent can build the whole store and show a preview before the merchant pays anything. See the [agent launch guide](/api/agent-launch).
