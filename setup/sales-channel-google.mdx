Connecting Google Merchant Center sends your Fluid products to your Merchant Center account, where Google Shopping listings and Shopping ads use them. Fluid sends the product data and reads back Google's review of each product. Buyers check out on your Fluid storefront, so there are no Google orders to import. Setup takes a person in a browser for one step: approving Fluid's access in Google.

## Before you start

- A Google Merchant Center account with your storefront's website verified and claimed, and a person who can sign in to it as an admin.
- The Merchant Center account isn't connected to another Fluid company. One account connects to one company.
- A Fluid API token for the company, or your own sign-in with `fluid login`. It needs company admin access: **Company settings** permission to connect and disconnect, and **Products** permission to choose and fix products.
- Install and sign in to the CLI:

```bash
npm install -g @fluid-app/fluid-cli @fluid-app/fluid-cli-channels
fluid login
```

Every `fluid channels` command prints JSON. Failures print a JSON `error` on stderr and exit with a non-zero code.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    ```bash
    fluid channels connect google --open
    ```

    This prints the Google Merchant Center sign-in `url` and, with `--open`, opens it in your browser. Nothing changes in Fluid until someone approves it in the next step. Without `--open`, send the `url` to the person who will approve it. The link works once and expires after 10 minutes; run `connect` again for a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve Fluid in Google">
    Sign in with the Google account that administers Merchant Center and approve the access Fluid asks for. Fluid connects the first Merchant Center account that Google account can reach.

    Google Merchant Center then sends the browser back to Fluid admin. The address ends in `connect=ok` when the connection saved, or `connect=error&reason=...` when it didn't. Whatever the page shows, check the result from the CLI:

    ```bash
    fluid channels status google
    ```

    The step is done when `connection.status` is `connected`. `connection.account` names the Merchant Center account Fluid connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the products">
    This changes live configuration: it sends products to Google Merchant Center. Decide first between the whole catalog and selected products; see [Whole catalog or selected products](#whole-catalog-or-selected-products).

    ```bash
    fluid channels catalog google --all --yes
    ```

    To send only some products, name them instead:

    ```bash
    fluid channels catalog google --add 4127,4128 --yes
    ```

    The output's `added` counts the products sent. Each one starts as a draft and publishes in the background.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the sync">
    ```bash
    fluid channels status google
    ```

    `listings.byStatus` counts the listings in each state and `listings.issues` counts the ones that need you. Publishing and Google Merchant Center's review take from minutes to a few days. Check again later, backing off between checks, rather than in a loop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix rejected products">
    ```bash
    fluid channels issues google
    ```

    Each issue has an `action` and a `hint` that says what to fix. Fix the product in Fluid, then:

    - For `action: retry`, retry every failed publish at once. This changes live configuration: `fluid channels issues google --retry --yes`.
    - For `action: fix_and_relist`, remove the product and add it again so the corrected version is sent: `fluid channels catalog google --remove 4127 --yes`, then `--add 4127 --yes`.
    - For `action: resolve_on_channel`, Google Merchant Center took the listing down. Check the reason in Google Merchant Center.

    The step is done when `count` is 0.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Whole catalog or selected products

Fluid lists the products you send, one listing per product. There's no setting that keeps the whole catalog in step with Google Merchant Center on its own, so the choice is about which products you send now and how you add new ones later.

| | Whole catalog (`--all`) | Selected products (`--add`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What's sent | Every active product that isn't listed yet. `--collection 12` narrows it to products in that collection. | Only the product IDs you name. |
| Draft and archived products | Never sent. | Sent if you name them, so name active products only. |
| Products you create later | Not added automatically. Run `--all` again; it skips products already listed. | Add each one with `--add`. |
| Products you removed | Skipped, so running `--all` again never undoes a removal. Pass `--include-removed` to bring them back. | Re-added if you name them. |
| Best for | A catalog that's ready for Google Merchant Center as it is. | Testing with a few products first, or a catalog where only some products suit Google Merchant Center. |

Start with a few products if you haven't sold on Google Merchant Center before. You'll see what Google Merchant Center rejects before you send everything.

Each product needs, before it's sent:

- A SKU on every active variant, unique across the products you list on Google.
- At least one active variant.
- A title, description, price, and image. A product with no image of its own is sent with your company's fallback image, which Google's review may disapprove.

A product missing one of these fails to publish and shows up in `fluid channels issues google`.

Google reviews each product. `review` shows Google's status, and `auditStatus` is `approved` once Google approves it or `audit_failed` if Google disapproves it.

Edits you make in Fluid after a product is sent, including price changes, aren't sent to Google. Remove the product and add it again to send the current version. Fluid sends every product as in stock, whatever its inventory.

To take products off Google Merchant Center, use `--remove` with product IDs, or `--remove-all` for every listed product. Removing a product deletes it from Merchant Center.

## What the status means

| Field and value | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `connection.status`: `not_connected` | The company has no Google Merchant Center connection. | Run step 1. |
| `connection.status`: `connected` | Fluid can reach Google Merchant Center. | Continue with the products. |
| `connection.status`: `expired` or `deauthorized` | Google Merchant Center no longer accepts Fluid's access. | Run `connect` again and approve it. |
| `connection.status`: `disconnected` | Someone disconnected the channel. | Run `connect` again to reconnect. |
| listing `status`: `draft` or `pending` | The product is on its way to Google Merchant Center. | Check again later. |
| listing `status`: `active` | The product is on Google Merchant Center. | Nothing. |
| listing `status`: `publish_failed` | Fluid couldn't send the product. | Fix what `lastError` names, then retry. |
| listing `status`: `platform_deactivated` or `frozen` | Google Merchant Center took the listing down. | Check the reason in Google Merchant Center. |
| listing `status`: `deleted` or `seller_deactivated` | You removed the product. | Add it again to bring it back. |
| listing `auditStatus`: `audit_failed` | Google Merchant Center's review rejected the product. | Fix it, then remove it and add it again. |

## Troubleshooting

- **`connect` fails with `422`.** Fluid couldn't start the connection, usually because the channel isn't set up for your company yet. Contact Fluid support.
- **The address after approving ends in `connect=error`.** The `reason` says why. `invalid_state` means the link was used twice or expired: run `connect` again for a new one.
- **The reason is `no_accounts`.** The Google account you approved with can't reach a Merchant Center account. Sign in with one that administers Merchant Center and run `connect` again.
- **The reason is `account_already_connected`.** Another Fluid company already holds this Merchant Center account. Disconnect it there first.
- **A command fails with `404`.** The company has no Google Merchant Center connection. Run step 1.
- **A command fails with `403`.** Your token lacks the permission that command needs: **Company settings** for the connection, **Products** for products.
- **Adding or removing products fails with `422`.** The connection isn't active. Check `connection.status` and reconnect if it isn't `connected`.

## Disconnect

```bash
fluid channels catalog google --remove-all --yes
fluid channels disconnect google --yes
```

Both commands change live configuration. Disconnecting doesn't take your products off Google Merchant Center: they stay listed and stop syncing, and you can't remove them through Fluid until you reconnect. Remove them first, as above, unless you want them to stay. Fluid forgets its tokens and stops syncing. Google keeps the access you granted until you remove Fluid in your Google account's security settings. The output's `stillListed` counts the products left on Google Merchant Center.

## Use the API directly

The CLI calls these company operations, all authenticated with a Bearer token:

1. [Start the Google Merchant Center connect flow](/api-reference/google/start-the-google-merchant-center-connect-flow), which returns the sign-in `url`.
2. [Get the company's Google Merchant Center connection](/api-reference/google/get-the-companys-google-merchant-center-connection).
3. [Publish Fluid products to the connected Merchant Center](/api-reference/google/publish-fluid-products-to-the-connected-merchant-center).
4. [List the company's Google Shopping listings](/api-reference/google/list-the-companys-google-shopping-listings).
5. [Retry a failed Google Shopping listing publish](/api-reference/google/retry-a-failed-google-shopping-listing-publish).
6. [Unpublish a Google Shopping listing](/api-reference/google/unpublish-a-google-shopping-listing).
7. [Disconnect the company's Google Merchant Center connection](/api-reference/google/disconnect-the-companys-google-merchant-center-connection).

For the whole catalog, the CLI reads your active products from Fluid's product list and sends their IDs to the publish operation in batches of 50. Retrying only works on a listing whose publish failed.
