Member storefront brings signed-in members into the theme you already have. A member sees their own price and their own name in the same sections every visitor sees. There is no second site to design and no separate portal to assign.

Member-aware sections render on your storefront pages, which are cached once per path, country, and language. Liquid emits placeholder shells with the guest value. The member storefront SDK fills them in the browser.

<Note>
  Member storefront features require enablement for your company. Until then the member helpers render their guest values.
</Note>

## Why the member part loads in the browser

Storefront pages are cached and served to everyone. If the server rendered one member's price into a product grid, the cache would hand that price to the next visitor. Member-aware output keeps the server render identical for every viewer and moves the personal part to the browser. See [Member pricing](/themes/member-storefront/member-pricing).

## What ships with the theme

On the root themes, ordinary price displays become member prices on their own, with no extra tags.

Member-only pages are not part of a theme. Build them as a [Site](/sites/overview), which Fluid hosts and publishes on its own.

## What member storefront is not

- It does not replace or change the portal. Portal Definitions and Widget Packages are a separate product with separate contracts.
- It adds no pricing rules. Every member price the SDK shows is a value the platform already resolves for that member.
- It never puts member data into a cached page. The storefront render stays viewer-independent.
