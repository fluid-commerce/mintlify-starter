As the owner of a member site, you can inspect its published database from an external app or integration. These read-only management APIs let you see every row in your own site, including rows its browser read rules would hide. They do not let you inspect another member's site or a company-owned site.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use the owner's login">
    Authenticate with the site owner's member Bearer credential. Make these calls outside the site's browser code. The site's cookie session and a member session credential from an email link cannot use these APIs. Never put the credential in your site's build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your site">
    Use [List the member's sites](/api-reference/sites/list-the-members-sites) or [Show a site](/api-reference/sites/show-a-site) to choose the site you own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="List its tables">
    Use [List the tables of a site's database](/api-reference/site-data/list-the-tables-of-a-sites-database). The list comes from the schema in the site's current published bundle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read a table's rows">
    Use [Read the rows of a table of a site's database](/api-reference/site-data/read-the-rows-of-a-table-of-a-sites-database) with the same owner's credential. Follow the reference for paging and error handling. Inspecting rows does not add, change or delete them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The site needs an available V1 database and a published schema, and member sites must be enabled for your company. If you cannot inspect a site, check its owner and published database before checking the errors in the endpoint reference.

These management reads are separate from `fluid.db` inside your app, where the visiting member and your table's access rules decide what can be read or written. See [Query a table](/sites/database#query-a-table). For a file containing every table and row, see [Download the data](/sites/database#download-the-data).

Company admins use the separate company APIs and permissions described in [Review and delete rows](/sites/database#review-and-delete-rows). The owner inspection APIs do not provide the admin's row-deletion operation.
