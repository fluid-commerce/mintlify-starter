FairShare is Fluid's JavaScript SDK for direct sales. It works out which rep should get credit for a visit, carries that credit into the cart and the order, and adds cart, lead capture, and media widgets to the page. It runs on Fluid storefronts and on any other website you add it to.

## How FairShare works

FairShare runs in the browser. On every page load, and on every client-side navigation, it sends the page URL to Fluid. When the URL names a member, that visit establishes the member's credit. FairShare then carries the credit with the visitor, into the cart, lead capture, and media. See [How credit is established and kept](#how-credit-is-established-and-kept).

### Installation

On a Fluid storefront, FairShare is already installed: Fluid loads it through a global embed when the company is created. Don't add it again. See [FairShare SDK and the storefront](/storefront/fairshare-sdk).

On any other website, add this tag to the `<head>`:

```html
<script
  defer
  type="module"
  id="fluid-cdn-script"
  src="https://assets.fluid.app/scripts/fluid-sdk/latest/web-widgets/index.js"
  data-fluid-shop="acme"
></script>
```

- `data-fluid-shop` is your company's Fluid subdomain — `acme` for `acme.fluid.app`.
- `type="module"` is required. The script is an ES module and fails to load without it.

See [Installation](/sdk/installation) for every option.

## How a rep is resolved

Fluid resolves a rep from the page URL, checking in this order:

1. A `username` or `referral` query parameter.
2. A rep's own subdomain.
3. The first segment of the path. On a Fluid storefront this is the credit segment. `/jordan-lee/products/beet-blend` credits `jordan-lee`, and `/home/...` names no rep. See [Supported paths](/themes/supported-paths).

If the URL names no rep, the page establishes no new credit. It doesn't take away credit the visitor already has. `window.FairShareSDK.getAttribution()` returns the result for the current page's URL, so it returns `null` on such a page even for a visitor a member already credited.

### Overrides

Attributes on the script tag override the URL. Use one when a page should always credit the same rep, or when your own site already knows who the rep is:

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `data-username` | The rep's Fluid username |
| `data-fluid-rep-id` | The rep's numeric Fluid ID |
| `data-email` | The rep's email address |
| `data-external-id` | The rep's ID in your own system |

FairShare uses the first attribute present, in this order: `data-fluid-rep-id`, `data-email`, `data-username`, `data-external-id`. It doesn't check that the rep exists, so an unknown value doesn't fall through to the next attribute.

The browser remembers an override. Removing the attribute doesn't clear it for someone who has already visited — clear the site's data to test URL-based attribution again.

<Note>
  `data-share-guid` is the older name for `data-username`: Fluid matches its value against rep usernames. It is still accepted, and it takes precedence over every other override. Use `data-username` in new code.
</Note>

## How credit is established and kept

A member, or rep, earns credit for a visitor in two steps: a credit path establishes the credit, and FairShare keeps it.

- **A credit path establishes credit.** A member's credit path names them in the URL, for example `/jordan-lee/products/beet-blend`. When a visitor lands on one and FairShare logs the visit, that member gets credit for the visitor.
- **The member keeps the credit.** FairShare's tracking uses cookies, a browser fingerprint, and similar signals. It carries the credit across later pages and later visits.
- **Pages without a credit path don't change it.** `/home/...` pages, custom routes, and marketing URLs that redirect, such as `/tv-offer`, establish no new credit. They don't take away credit a visitor already has.
- **A visitor who never lands on a credit path has no rep.** For example, a visitor who arrives on `/tv-offer` with no FairShare credit, and buys from `/home/...` pages, places an order with no rep.
- **Another member's link is a second touch.** When a credited visitor later lands on a different member's credit path, the **Shared links (multi-rep)** setting decides which member earns commission: first touch, last touch, or most activity. See [Fair Share settings](/help/admin/settings/fair-share).

Your other Fair Share settings decide commission for sponsored customers' re-orders, orphaned orders with no rep, and enrollment orders.

### What members and marketers share

- **Members share credit paths.** A member shares links that carry their username, from the Fluid mobile app or their own links.
- **Marketing and legacy URLs are for customers without a rep.** A URL such as `/tv-offer` serves orphaned customers, who arrive without a member. Don't add `?username=` to it.

### From visit to order

- **The SDK creates the cart on the first add to cart.** The order inherits the cart's rep.
- **A cart started on a credit path takes that page's member.** Adding more items doesn't change the rep.
- **A cart started on a page without a credit path keeps the visitor's credit.** A visitor a member already credited keeps that member's credit. A visitor with no FairShare credit gets a cart with no rep.
- **Lead capture** credits the rep attributed on the page where the form is submitted.
- **Video views** from the media widget are credited to the page's rep.

### Across domains

Browser storage doesn't cross domains. A custom domain, a `fluid.app` subdomain, and `checkout.fluid.app` each keep their own. The cart reaches checkout through its token in the checkout URL. Keep shoppers on one domain from their first page to checkout, and make sure the page they arrive on carries the rep in its URL.

## What FairShare adds to the page

- **Cart widget** — a slide-out cart that lets shoppers add products and go to checkout. See [Cart API](/sdk/cart-api).
- **Lead capture widget** — a form that saves a visitor's contact details as a lead for the attributed rep.
- **Media widget** — plays Fluid-hosted videos, images, and playlists, with a call to action. See [Media](/sdk/media).
- **Real-time cart updates** — optional live cart syncing, so a cart changed in another tab or by the server updates without a page refresh. Turn it on with `data-realtime-enabled="true"`.

See [Components](/sdk/components) for every widget.
