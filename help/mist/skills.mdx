A skill is a set of written instructions that Mist follows to do one job, such as summarizing promo code orders or auditing a theme for accessibility. Run a skill, and Mist does the job the same way every time.

## Where skills come from

Each skill shows where it comes from:

- **From Fluid**: skills Fluid publishes in the public Mist Skills repository. Mist downloads the latest versions in the background each time it starts. See [Mist Skills](/guides/mist-skills) for the full list.
- **Built into Mist**: skills that come with the app.
- **Yours**: skills you create or copy. They're saved as files in the `Fluid/skills` folder in your home folder.

You can't change a skill **From Fluid** or **Built into Mist** directly. To change one, Mist makes a copy for you to edit.

## Browse skills

In the left nav, click **Skills**. The page lists:

- **Featured**: a few skills to try, starting with the ones you pinned.
- **Pages Mist can build**: skills that build a page, each shown as the page it makes.
- **Pinned**: skills you pinned. They're also listed under **Skills** in the left nav.
- **Yours**: the skills you wrote. Click **Write a skill** to add one.
- A section for each category, such as **Finance**, **Themes** or **Sales**.

Type in **Search skills**, or click a category at the top, to filter the page. Click **Open** on a skill to read what it does before you run it.

Each skill card has a **⋯** menu with **Pin** or **Unpin**. A skill **From Fluid** or **Built into Mist** also has **Make a copy**, which makes a copy of your own to edit. One of yours has **Delete**.

## Run a skill

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the skill">
    On the **Skills** page, click **Open** on the skill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read it">
    Read the instructions, so you know what Mist will do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Run">
    Click **Run**. Mist sends the skill to the chat and starts working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To run the skill against a particular project, click the arrow next to **Run** and pick a project under **Run on…**. A project that isn't on your computer yet is marked **Download**, and Mist downloads it first.

You can also run a skill from the chat. Type **/**, then pick a row marked **Skill**. See [Slash commands](/help/mist/slash-commands).

<Warning>
  Skills can make real changes to your company, such as updating products or publishing a theme. Read a skill before you run it. To explore without any risk, turn on [Safe Mode](/help/mist/safe-mode) first.
</Warning>

## Create a skill

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New skill dialog">
    On the **Skills** page, click **New skill** or **Write a skill**. You can also press Cmd+Shift+S, or Ctrl+Shift+S on Windows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Name**, and an optional **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where it appears">
    **Limit to** your company is checked, so the skill appears only while that company is active. Uncheck it to use the skill in every company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create**. Mist creates the skill as a Markdown file. Write its instructions, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also ask Mist in the chat to write a skill for you.

## Edit a skill

Open the skill and click **Edit**. Make your changes, then click **Save**, or press Cmd+S.

For a skill **From Fluid** or **Built into Mist**, Mist asks **Duplicate to edit?** first. Click **Duplicate & edit** to copy the skill to **Yours**, and you edit the copy.

To copy a skill's instructions, click **Copy the instructions**.

## Schedule a skill

You can run a skill on a timer, such as every weekday at 9am.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open its schedules">
    Open the skill and click **Schedule this skill**, or **Run it on a schedule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a schedule">
    Click **New schedule**. Choose **Recurring** or **One-time**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the time">
    For a recurring schedule, pick a preset, such as **Every hour**, **Every day at 9am**, **Every weekday at 9am**, **Every Monday at 9am** or **First of the month**, or enter a **Cron expression**. For a one-time run, pick a date and time under **Run at**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what happens if Mist was closed">
    Under **If Mist was closed at the scheduled time**, choose **Run once on next launch** or **Skip the missed run**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Click **Create schedule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each schedule shows when it runs next and when it last ran. Use **Run now**, **Pause**, **Resume** and **Delete** to manage it. **Recent scheduled runs** lists the latest runs.

<Note>
  Scheduled skills run on your computer. A schedule runs only while Mist is open and its company is the active company. If Mist was closed, it catches up at most once.
</Note>

## Use Mist's skills with other AI agents

The skills **From Fluid** are public, and many of them work with other coding agents too. See [Mist Skills](/guides/mist-skills).
