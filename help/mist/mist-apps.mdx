Everything you build in Mist is a project. Projects are listed by type in the **Code** group of the sidebar.

## Project types

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Mist Apps** | A hosted web app with its own backend and database. Fluid hosts it for you. |
| **Themes** | Your storefront theme, built with Liquid. |
| **MySites** | A rep's MySite. |
| **Portals** | A portal: the signed-in experience for your reps. |
| **Widgets** | A component for the portal builder. |
| **Skills** | A local skill. See [Skills](/help/mist/skills). |
| **Databases** | A connection to a database. See [Databases](/help/mist/databases). |

## Create a project

In the **Code** group, hover over a type and click **+**, such as **New Mist**. You can also ask Mist in the chat, for example "Build me an app that lets reps log event attendance". Mist shows an **Approve** and **Dismiss** card before it creates anything.

Shortcuts create the most common types:

- Cmd+Shift+M: a Mist app
- Cmd+Shift+T: a theme
- Cmd+Shift+W: a widget
- Cmd+Shift+S: a skill

On Windows, use Ctrl instead of Cmd.

## Create a Mist app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open New Mist">
    In the **Code** group, click **+** next to **Mist Apps**, or press Cmd+Shift+M.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a name under **Name (optional)**, such as "Customer Portal".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create Mist**. You see **Setting up your Mist Cloud environment…** while Fluid sets up the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Fluid creates a code repository, a database and hosting for the app. The app starts as a Next.js web app, with sign-in to Fluid already built in. Ask Mist to build what you need, then run it on your computer and publish it. See [Run and publish](/help/mist/run-and-publish).

For how a Mist app works under the hood, see [Mist apps](/mist-apps/overview) in the developer docs.

To create a Mist app, your role needs the **Mist** permission that starts with **Create new Mist hostings**. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).

If creating fails, you see **We couldn't finish creating your Mist right now.** This is usually temporary, so try again in a moment. If it keeps happening, contact Fluid support.

## Manage a Mist app

Right-click a Mist app in the sidebar for:

- **Rename…**: change the app's name.
- **Environment variables…**: view and set the app's environment variables, such as API keys.
- **Domain**: connect your own domain to the app. See [Connect a domain](#connect-a-domain).
- **Compute**: the app's server settings, saved in the app's code. It appears once the app is on your computer.
- **Add integration point…**: connect the app to Fluid, as a droplet, a drop zone or a mobile embed. See [Integration points](/mist-apps/integration-points).
- **Delete…**: delete the app. See [Delete a Mist app](#delete-a-mist-app).

You can also see and create your company's Mist apps in the admin, on [Mist Apps](/help/admin/settings/mist-apps).

### Connect a domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Domain">
    Right-click the Mist app and click **Domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your domain">
    Under **Add a domain**, enter the domain, such as `shop.example.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the DNS records">
    Add the DNS records Mist shows at your domain provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it">
    The domain shows **Pending verification** or **Needs DNS routing** until the records work, then **Live**. Click **Re-check verification** to check again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To disconnect a domain, click **Remove domain**.

### Delete a Mist app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Delete">
    Right-click the Mist app and click **Delete…**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Type the app's name, then click **Delete Mist**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Fluid schedules the app to be torn down in 15 days. Until then, you can still restore it, and you aren't charged for it. Mist doesn't have a restore button yet. If you use the Fluid CLI, run `fluid mist restore` with the app's slug. Otherwise, contact Fluid support.

## What a Mist app costs

A Mist app is billed by use. Fluid charges for what the app's server and database use each day, plus a small monthly price for its code repository. The AI that Mist uses while it builds counts as AI usage. Fluid draws these charges from your company's usage credits.

If your credits run out, your live apps keep running, but you can't create a new one until you buy more. To stop paying for an app, delete it. See [Mist app billing](/mist-apps/billing).

## If something goes wrong

Ask Mist first, for example "Why did my app crash?". Mist reads the app's logs and tells you what it finds. For common problems and their fixes, see [Troubleshoot a Mist app](/mist-apps/troubleshooting).
