Staging is a private copy of a theme. You publish your changes to staging, check them on your real storefront through a preview link, and then **promote** staging to your live theme when it's right. Shoppers don't see anything on staging until you promote it.

Each theme has one staging copy, and everyone on your team who works on that theme shares it.

## Before you start

- **Mist 1.11.0 or later.** Mist updates itself; restart it to get the latest version. See [Download Mist](/help/mist/download).
- **Permission to update themes**, to publish to staging, promote, and undo.
- **A draft or live theme.** Other themes can't have staging.
- **The theme downloaded to your computer.** See [Run and publish](/help/mist/run-and-publish#before-you-start).

## Create staging

Open the theme project. In the preview header, click **Request staging**. Mist copies the theme, which usually takes under a minute, and the header shows **Staging** when it's ready.

You only do this once per theme. Staging stays until the theme is deleted.

## Publish to staging

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change the theme in the chat or in your files, and check the **Local** preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish to staging">
    Click **Publish to staging**, next to **Publish**. Your live theme isn't touched. When it's done, the header shows **Published to staging · revision N**, and the revision goes up by one with each publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check staging">
    Click the **Staging** tab to see staging on your real storefront.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Next to the staging pill, Mist shows whether your files are **Up to date with local** or have **Local changes not on staging**.

## Share a preview link

Click **Copy link** on the staging pill, and send the link to anyone who should review the change. They don't need a Fluid login. The link lasts 7 days; copy a new one when you need it.

<Warning>
  The cart and checkout in a staging preview use your real store. Adding to the cart or checking out can create real orders. Share staging links for review, not for test purchases.
</Warning>

## When a teammate published first

Publishing to staging sends your theme files, apart from a few kinds it skips and lists. If a teammate published to staging after you last did, and your copies of their files are different, publishing would put your copies back over their work. Mist asks before that happens:

> **Staging has changes you don't have**
>
> Someone published files to staging (revision 12) that this project has differently. Publishing replaces them with your copies. Get their changes into this project first, or replace them if you mean to.

The dialog lists their files under **Their changes**.

- **Get their changes** copies their files into your project and publishes nothing. Mist then shows **Got their changes**, listing each file that came in as **Updated** and each file it left alone as **Kept yours**, with the reason. A file is kept when you changed it too since your last publish to staging. Click **Publish to staging** again to send your work with theirs. If you kept any of your own files, Mist asks again before replacing theirs.
- **Replace anyway** publishes your copies over theirs. It replaces only what you were shown: if someone publishes again before yours lands, Mist asks you again.
- **Cancel** publishes nothing.

Files your teammate published that you don't have at all are never removed.

## Promote staging to your live theme

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Promote to live">
    Mist lists every file that would change on your live theme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Promote to live**. The dialog shows the promotion running, then **Promoted to live** with the files that changed. Shoppers see them straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Promoting copies exactly what staging shows, including what your teammates published to it, so read the list before you confirm.

If someone publishes to staging while the dialog is open, nothing is promoted. The dialog says **Staging changed while you were reviewing**; click **Review again** to see the new changes.

When **Promote to live** is greyed out, hover over it to see why:

| Reason | What to do |
|---|---|
| The live theme already matches staging | Nothing; there's nothing to promote |
| The live theme changed since staging was made | Someone changed the live theme another way, such as **Publish** or the theme editor. Promoting would overwrite that change, so use **Publish** instead |

## Undo a promotion

Click **Undo promote**, then **Undo promotion**. Your live theme goes back to how it was before the promotion, and the dialog shows **Promotion undone**. Staging keeps its changes, so you can promote them again later.

You can undo only the latest promotion, once. If someone edited a promoted file on the live theme since, undo is refused so their edit isn't lost; the dialog says why.

## Publish or promote?

There are two ways to change your live theme:

- **Promote to live** puts exactly what staging shows onto your live theme. Use it when the change was reviewed on staging, especially when several people work on the theme.
- **Publish** sends the files on your computer straight to your live theme. Before it does, Mist warns you if your files differ from staging.

<Warning>
  Once the live theme is changed with **Publish** or the theme editor, promoting that staging is refused from then on, because it would overwrite that change. If your team reviews on staging, promote every change instead of mixing Publish and promote.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see staging in the preview header?" id="no-staging">
    Only draft and live themes can have staging. Check that the project is one of those, and that you have permission to update themes. If it still doesn't show, update Mist and restart it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I delete staging?" id="delete-staging">
    No. Staging goes away when its theme is deleted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use the terminal instead?" id="terminal">
    Yes. Mist runs the same Fluid CLI commands. See [Theme branches](/themes/branches).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
