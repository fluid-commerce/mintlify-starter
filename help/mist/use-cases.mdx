These examples show what teams do with Mist. Each one lists something you could type, and the Mist features it uses. Try them with [Safe Mode](/help/mist/safe-mode) on first, to see what Mist would do without changing anything live.

## Build an app for your reps

> Build an app where reps can log who came to their events, and show each rep their own list.

Mist creates a Mist app, builds it, and runs it on your computer so you can try it. When you're happy, publish it, and connect your own domain if you want one. You can then connect the app to Fluid, for example as a drop zone or a mobile embed.

Uses: [Mist Apps](/help/mist/mist-apps), [Run and publish](/help/mist/run-and-publish).

## Copy your current website into a theme

> Clone https://harborlinewellness.com into a new theme. Don't publish it.

The **Theme Clone** skill rebuilds each page of the site as theme sections and compares screenshots against the original. Follow it with **Theme Refine** to fix what doesn't match. You review the result in the preview before anything goes live.

Uses: [Skills](/help/mist/skills), [Run and publish](/help/mist/run-and-publish).

## Change part of your storefront

> Make this banner shorter on small screens, and use our brand blue for the button.

Run your theme, pick the banner in the preview, then type what you want. Mist edits the section, and the preview updates as it works.

Uses: [Pick an element](/help/mist/pick-an-element), [Run and publish](/help/mist/run-and-publish).

## Open a new country

> Open Canada.

The **Open a Country** skill looks up what Fluid already knows about the country, then asks only what it needs, such as how you'll operate there. It then runs the **Open a Country** workflow, which sets up the country step by step and checks each step.

Uses: [Skills](/help/mist/skills), [Workflows](/help/mist/workflows).

## Translate your store

> Translate the theme into every language we sell in.

The **Translate theme languages** skill translates your theme's text into each language your company has turned on.

Uses: [Skills](/help/mist/skills). For the languages themselves, see [Languages](/help/admin/settings/languages).

## Answer questions about your sales

> How did last month's revenue compare with the month before, by country?

With your reporting databases turned on, Mist writes the query, runs it and shows the answer in the chat. Skills such as **Monthly close report**, **Promo code summary**, **Top customers by lifetime value** and **Stalled order revenue recovery** answer common questions the same way every time. Type `/dashboard` to build a live dashboard of your business.

Uses: [Databases](/help/mist/databases), [Skills](/help/mist/skills).

## Bring in social videos

> /rip https://www.tiktok.com/@harborlinewellness/video/7412345678901234567

`/rip` saves a public video into your DAM and Media library. The **UGC Discovery** skill finds popular posts about your brand, and **Brand Social Content Import** brings in your own brand's videos.

Uses: [Slash commands](/help/mist/slash-commands), [Skills](/help/mist/skills).

## Write down your brand voice

> Set up our brand voice.

The **Brand Setup** skill asks you about your brand, then saves a brand voice document. Mist, and your themes, portals and widgets, read it to match your tone.

Uses: [Skills](/help/mist/skills).

## Build a portal or a widget

> Create a portal for our reps with a home screen that shows their recent orders.

Mist creates the portal with starter screens, navigation and a theme, then builds what you asked for. Widgets work the same way, for components you add in the portal builder.

Uses: [Mist Apps](/help/mist/mist-apps), [Run and publish](/help/mist/run-and-publish).

## Run a report on a schedule

Open the **Stalled order revenue recovery** skill and schedule it for **Every weekday at 9am**. Each morning, Mist ranks your stalled orders by the revenue you can recover and hands you a call list. Schedules run only while Mist is open.

Uses: [Skills](/help/mist/skills#schedule-a-skill).
