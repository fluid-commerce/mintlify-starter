Every conversation with Mist is a chat. Mist saves each chat, so you can come back later and pick up where you left off, or see how something was built.

## Start a chat

Click **New chat** at the top of the **Chats** pane, or press Cmd+N (Ctrl+N on Windows).

If a project is open, **New chat** starts a chat in that project. The **Chats** pane lists every chat, from every project, in one place. Each chat shows its project's icon.

## Find an earlier chat

- In the **Chats** pane, look under **Pinned**, your folders or **Recent**.
- Type in **Search chats** at the top of the pane. It finds chats by title, or by the name of their project.
- Press Cmd+K (Ctrl+K on Windows) to open the command menu, then type the chat's title.

Search doesn't match the messages inside a chat.

## Organize your chats

Hover over a chat in the **Chats** pane and click **⋯** for:

- **Pin to the top** or **Unpin**: move the chat to **Pinned**, or back.
- **Move to a folder**: pick a folder, or click **New folder…** to make one with this chat in it.
- **Close chat**: remove the chat from the pane.

You can also drag a chat onto **Pinned**, onto a folder, or back to **Recent**.

To change a folder, hover over it, click **⋯**, then click **Rename** or **Delete folder**. When you delete a folder, its chats go back to **Recent**.

## Manage a chat

Click **Chat options** at the top of an open chat for:

- **Rename**: give the chat a new title.
- **Close chat**: remove the chat from the **Chats** pane.

To pin a chat, use **Pin to the top** in the **Chats** pane. See [Organize your chats](#organize-your-chats).

<Note>
  Closing a chat hides it, but Mist keeps the transcript on your computer. Mist doesn't have a way to reopen a closed chat.
</Note>

## Edit, rewind or delete a message

Hover over a message you sent to see its actions:

- **Edit message**: change the message and run it again.
- **Rewind to here**: drop everything after this message. Mist asks you to confirm.
- **Delete message**: delete this message and everything after it. This can't be undone.

To clear the whole chat, type `/clear`. See [Slash commands](/help/mist/slash-commands).

## Limits

- Mist keeps the latest 200 messages of each chat. Older messages drop off.
- Chats are saved on your computer, not in your Fluid account. If you sign out, your chat history stays on this computer.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is chat history the same as History in the preview?" id="history-difference">
    No. **History** in the preview toolbar is the version history of a project's files and publishes, with a way to roll back. See [Run and publish](/help/mist/run-and-publish#roll-back). Changes Mist made to your company through the Fluid API are in [Time Machine](/help/mist/time-machine).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My chat is very long. How do I keep Mist on track?" id="long-chat">
    Type `/compact` and send it. Mist writes a short summary of the goals, decisions and current state. Start a new chat and paste the summary in, so the new chat starts with what matters.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
