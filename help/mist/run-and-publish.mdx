You build in two places:

- **Local**: the project runs on your computer. Changes you make here aren't live.
- **Prod**: production, the live version your customers, reps or users see.

You work locally, check the preview, then publish to production.

## Before you start

The project must be on your computer. If it isn't, **Run** and **Publish** show **Clone this project locally first**. Open the project and click **Download to this machine** in the preview.

## Run locally

In the preview toolbar, click **Run**. The button shows **Starting…**, then **Stop** once the project is running. The preview shows your local version.

You can also press Cmd+Shift+Enter, or Ctrl+Shift+Enter on Windows. To stop every running project at once, press Cmd+Shift+K, or Ctrl+Shift+K.

Locally, a Mist app uses its own database on your computer, not the production database. Changes you make to data while you test locally don't reach production.

## Switch between Local and Prod

The preview header has two tabs, **Local** and **Prod**. Click one to preview that version.

<Note>
  For a MySite, **Prod** shows your draft on the production servers, not the published MySite.
</Note>

## Read the logs

Click **Logs** in the preview toolbar. It appears once there's output to show.

- **Dev server output** shows what the project prints while it runs locally.
- **Publish output** shows what happens during a publish.
- For a Mist app, the **Production** tab streams the live app's logs as requests arrive. The **Local** tab shows your computer's output.

Lines with errors show in red. Mist keeps the latest 2,000 lines.

You can also ask Mist to read the logs for you, for example "Why did my app crash?". Mist checks the logs and the preview console, and tells you what it finds.

## Publish

<Warning>
  Publishing changes what's live, and Mist doesn't ask you to confirm. Check the **Local** preview first.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your changes">
    Run the project locally and check the preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    In the preview toolbar, click **Publish**, or press Cmd+Shift+P (Ctrl+Shift+P on Windows). The button shows **Publishing…** and the logs open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    The status shows **Live** when the publish is done, or **Build failed** if it isn't.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What **Publish** does depends on the project:

| Project | What Publish does |
| --- | --- |
| Mist app | Saves your changes, sends them to the app's repository and builds the live app. |
| Theme | Pushes your theme files to Fluid. |
| Portal | Pushes the portal to Fluid and makes it the active version. |
| Widget | Pushes the widget to Fluid. |
| MySite | Publishes your changes to the live MySite. |

To cancel, hover over **Publishing…** and click **Cancel publish**. For a Mist app, the changes may already have been sent, so the app can still go live.

Publishing is blocked while [Safe Mode](/help/mist/safe-mode) is on.

### If a publish fails

When a publish fails, Mist tries one fix, then publishes again. You see **Publish failed — Mist is making one fix. Publishing will retry once when the fix is done.**

If that also fails, you see **Publish stopped:** with the reason and what to do. Mist won't publish again for the same failure on its own. A bar under the preview offers **Retry**, **Ask Mist** and **Dismiss**.

| Reason | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Validation rejected** | Check the project's settings and fix the issue the logs report. |
| **Remote theme changed** | Nothing. Mist merges the changes made elsewhere and publishes again. |
| **Merge conflicts to resolve** | Someone changed the same files. Resolve the conflicts, then publish again. Ask Mist to help. |
| **Authentication failed** | Sign in to Mist again, then publish again. |
| **Network unavailable** | Check your internet connection, then publish again. |
| **Publish tooling failed** | Ask Mist to check the project's publish setup before you retry. |
| **Publish timed out** | Check your connection, then publish again. |
| **Publish cancelled** | Nothing failed. Publish again when you're ready. |

## Roll back

**History** in the preview toolbar lists earlier versions of the project's files and publishes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open History">
    In the preview toolbar, click **History**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a version">
    Find the version you want and click **Roll back**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Mist saves your current state to a backup first. Then it brings back the older version as a new change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Rolling back changes only your local files. Click **Publish** to make the older version live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To undo changes Mist made to your company through the Fluid API, such as product updates, use [Time Machine](/help/mist/time-machine).
