Mist can show and query three kinds of database:

- **Reporting databases**: your company's Fluid data, such as orders, customers and products, plus data from Fluid Connect connectors. An admin turns these on in the admin.
- **Mist app databases**: the database behind each Mist app you build.
- **Your own Postgres databases**: any Postgres database you connect.

Every database session in Mist is read-only. You and Mist can look at data, but not change it.

## Before you start

- **Reporting databases** appear only after an admin turns them on, on the [Databases](/help/admin/settings/databases) screen in the admin. Your role must also be one of the roles listed there under **Who has access**.
- To see a Mist app's production database, your role needs the same reporting-database access.

<Warning>
  Database contents can include personal and financial data. Queries and results may be processed by AI model providers when you ask Mist about them. Read the risk notice on the [Databases](/help/admin/settings/databases) screen.
</Warning>

## Open a database

In the sidebar, open the **Code** group and find **Databases**. Click a database to open the database viewer.

The viewer has two tabs:

- **Browse**: the tables in the database. Type in **Filter tables…** to find one, then click it to see its rows.
- **SQL**: write and run your own query.

For a Mist app's database, a **Local** and **Production** switch picks which copy you see. It starts on **Local** when the app is on your computer. **Local** is the app's database on your computer. **Production** is the live app's database.

## Ask Mist about your data

In the chat, ask a question in plain words, such as "Which 10 products sold the most last month?". Mist writes the query, runs it and shows the answer in the chat. Click **Open in SQL editor** to see and change the query Mist wrote.

To build a live dashboard from your data, type `/dashboard`. See [Slash commands](/help/mist/slash-commands).

<Note>
  When Mist queries a Mist app's database for you, it uses the production database unless you ask for the local one.
</Note>

## Run your own query

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the SQL tab">
    Open the database, then click **SQL**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the query">
    Write a query that reads data, such as a `SELECT`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run it">
    Click **Run**, or press Cmd+Enter (Ctrl+Enter on Windows).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To keep a query, click **Save**. Your queries are under **Saved queries**, on this computer. To download the results, click **Export result to CSV**.

## Connect your own Postgres database

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    In the **Code** group, click **+** next to **Databases**. The **Add Database Connection** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Enter a **Name**, then the **Host**, **Port**, **Database**, **User** and **Password**. Turn on **Require SSL** if your database needs it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Click **Add Connection**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Mist stores the password encrypted in your computer's keychain. To copy the connection details later, right-click the database and click **Copy connection string**.

## Limits

- Only read-only queries run: `SELECT`, `EXPLAIN` and `SHOW`. Any other query is rejected with **Only read-only queries are allowed**.
- A query stops after 60 seconds.
- On a production database, avoid slow, expensive queries. They run against the live database.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see my company's Fluid data?" id="no-fluid-data">
    An admin has to turn on the Fluid database on the [Databases](/help/admin/settings/databases) screen, and your role has to be one that can query reporting databases. Ask an admin to check **Who has access** on that screen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did a Mist app's production database disconnect on my other computer?" id="credential-revoked">
    Mist gets you a personal sign-in for a Mist app's production database. Each time Mist gets a new one, the previous one stops working, on every computer. When the sign-in on one computer stops working, Mist gets a new one the next time it connects there, which ends the sign-in on your other computer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I talk with Mist by voice about a database?" id="voice">
    No. Voice conversations need a project folder on your computer, and databases don't have one. You can still dictate a message. See [Voice](/help/mist/voice).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
