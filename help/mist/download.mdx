Mist is a desktop app called **Fluid Mist**. Fluid publishes builds for:

- macOS on Apple silicon
- macOS on Intel
- Windows

## Download the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the download banner">
    In the admin, click the **Developer Hub** code icon in the top bar. You can also click the **Settings** gear, then select **Developer** under **System**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the banner">
    Click the **Meet Mist Desktop** banner. Its button reads **Download for** and the name of your computer's system, such as **Download for macOS (Apple Silicon)** or **Download for Windows**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the file">
    Your browser downloads the latest build for your computer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To open the Developer Hub or the **Developer** screen, your role needs at least **View only** for **Developer**, in the **Settings** card on the role's **Permissions** tab. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).

<Note>
  The banner assumes a Mac has Apple silicon when the browser can't tell. On an Intel Mac, click **Intel Mac? Get the x64 build** under the banner. It opens the release page, where you download the file that ends in `x64.dmg`.
</Note>

If the banner can't find a build for your computer, it opens the latest release page instead. Download the file for your system from there:

| Your computer | File to download |
| --- | --- |
| Mac with Apple silicon | The file ending in `arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | The file ending in `x64.dmg` |
| Windows | The file ending in `Setup.exe` |

## Install the app

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="macOS">
    1. Open the `.dmg` file you downloaded.
    2. Drag **Fluid Mist** into your **Applications** folder.
    3. Open **Fluid Mist** from **Applications**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Windows">
    1. Run the `Setup.exe` file you downloaded.
    2. The installer installs Fluid Mist and opens it.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Sign in

The first time you open Mist, it asks you to sign in. You can't use the app until you do.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Under **Log in to your Fluid account**, enter the email address you use for Fluid. Click **Send code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    Fluid emails you a 6-digit code. Enter it, then click **Verify**. If the code doesn't arrive, click **Resend code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a company">
    If your account belongs to more than one company, pick the one you want to use first. If it belongs to only one, Mist picks it for you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The code expires after a few minutes. Mist shows how long you have on the code screen.

If you see **No companies are associated with that account.**, your email address isn't on any Fluid company. Ask an admin at your company to add you.

### Switch company

Click the company name in the title bar. Search for the company, then click it.

### Sign out

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click **Log Out**. Mist asks you to confirm. Projects you downloaded and your chat history stay on your computer.

## Updates

Mist updates itself. While it's open, it checks for a new version when it starts and every 10 minutes after that, and downloads it in the background.

When an update is ready, the **Fluid Mist Update** message appears:

- Click **Restart** to install the update now.
- Click **Later** to keep working. The update installs when you quit and reopen Mist.

If a workflow is running, you see **Update ready — waiting for workflows to finish** instead. Mist doesn't restart while a workflow runs. The **Restart** and **Later** message comes back when every workflow has finished or been cancelled.

On macOS, you can also check by hand. In the menu bar, open the **Fluid Mist** menu and click **Check for Updates…**. You see **You're up to date.** or **Update found — downloading now.**

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a Linux version?" id="linux">
    No. Fluid publishes builds for macOS and Windows only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Mist in a web browser?" id="web">
    No. Mist is a desktop app. In the admin, you can manage the apps you build with Mist on [Mist Apps](/help/admin/settings/mist-apps).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why didn't my sign-in code work?" id="code-failed">
    The code expires after a few minutes. Click **Resend code** to get a new one. Make sure you entered the email address you use for Fluid.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
