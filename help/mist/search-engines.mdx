A search engine, such as Google, may list a Site in its results. Your Sites are not listed unless you turn that on. Use **Show in search engines** in Mist to turn it on for a Site you own.

## What turning it on does

- **It asks search engines to list your Site.** The Site's pages stop telling search engines to stay away, and your company's sitemap lists the Site.
- **It doesn't promise a listing.** Search engines decide what they list, and they take time to find a Site.
- **It only works while the Site is open to everyone.** A search engine is a visitor who isn't signed in, so a Site must be public to be listed.
- **It stays in your hands.** Only you, as the Site's owner, can turn it on or off. A person you shared the Site with, even one who can edit it, can't.

Every Site starts with it off, including Sites you copy.

## Turn it on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Site">
    In Mist, open the Site you own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open its settings">
    In the Site's settings rail, open the **Details** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Show in search engines">
    Switch on **Show in search engines**. Mist saves it. To stop, switch it off again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The switch is on your own Sites only. A Site your company owns has its own search engine settings, set in the admin. See [Sites in the admin](/help/admin/member-sites).

<Note>
  Mist doesn't save the change while [Safe Mode](/help/mist/safe-mode) is on. Turn Safe Mode off first.
</Note>

## When a Site isn't listed

A Site is listed only when all of these are true:

- **Show in search engines** is on, or your company has set the Site to always allow.
- The Site is public. A private Site, or a Site behind a [password](/sites/password), is never listed.
- The Site is published and switched on.
- Your company hasn't taken it down.
- Your company doesn't block search engines for its whole storefront.

When the switch is on but one of these isn't true, the switch shows that search engines list a Site only while it's live and public. Your choice is kept. The Site is listed again once everything is true, without you turning the switch on again. For example, make a private Site public again, and it's listed again if the switch is still on.

## Why the switch can be locked

Your company's admins can override your choice for a Site. The switch is then off-limits and reads **Your company controls this setting**. There are two kinds of override:

- **Always allow.** Search engines may list the Site whatever your switch says. The Site still has to be public and live.
- **Never allow.** Search engines never list the Site, even with your switch on.

Your own choice isn't lost. While an override is set, your company's choice decides. When an admin removes the override, the switch is yours again, set the way you left it.

To ask for a change, contact an admin of your company. You can't change it from Mist.
