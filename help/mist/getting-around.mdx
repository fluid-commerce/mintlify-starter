The Mist window has three resizable panels. From left to right:

- The **sidebar**, with your chats, projects, skills and workflows.
- The **chat**, where you talk with Mist.
- The **main pane**, which shows the live preview of the project you're working on, or a page such as **Skills** or **Time Machine**.

To take the tour again, click the **?** menu in the title bar, then click **Replay Product Tour**.

## Sidebar

At the top of the sidebar:

- **Home**: the start page.
- **Tasks**: the task board.
- **Time Machine**: the changes Mist made to your company. See [Time Machine](/help/mist/time-machine).
- **Skills**: the skills library. See [Skills](/help/mist/skills).
- **Workflows**: the workflows library and your runs. See [Workflows](/help/mist/workflows).

Below them:

- **Chats**, with a **New chat** button. See [Chats](/help/mist/chats).
- **Code**, a group that starts collapsed. It holds your projects by type: **Mist Apps**, **Themes**, **MySites**, **Portals**, **Widgets**, **Skills** and **Databases**. Click **+** on a type to create one. See [Mist Apps](/help/mist/mist-apps).

The **Search projects** box at the top filters projects and chats by name.

At the bottom of the sidebar, click your name to open a menu:

- **Settings**: **Appearance**, **MCP servers**, **Workspace** and your company's **AI usage**.
- **Preferences**: settings such as the **Default chat model**.
- **Docs**: opens the Fluid docs.
- **Shortcuts**: the list of keyboard shortcuts.
- **Contact Us**: opens an email to Fluid support.
- **Log Out**: signs you out.

## Chat

Type in the box at the bottom of the chat. Its placeholder reads "Plan, Build, / for commands, @ for context".

- Press Enter to send. Press Shift+Enter for a new line.
- Type **/** to pick a command, a skill or a workflow. See [Slash commands](/help/mist/slash-commands).
- Click the model name to choose an AI model. See [Model selection](/help/mist/models).
- Use the buttons on the right of the box to dictate or to talk with Mist. See [Voice](/help/mist/voice).

As Mist works, the chat shows each step it takes, such as reading files, calling the Fluid API or checking logs. When Mist wants to make a change that needs your approval, it shows a card with **Approve** and **Dismiss**.

## Preview

When you open a project, the main pane shows its preview. The toolbar above the preview has the controls you use to work on the project, including **Run**, **Publish**, **History**, **Logs** and the element picker. See [Run and publish](/help/mist/run-and-publish) and [Pick an element](/help/mist/pick-an-element).

## Title bar

The title bar has:

- **Back** and **Forward**.
- Buttons that hide or show the sidebar and the chat.
- The company switcher. Click your company's name to switch to another company.
- The **Safe Mode** switch. See [Safe Mode](/help/mist/safe-mode).
- The **?** menu, with **Ask a Question**, **Leave Feedback**, **Report a Bug**, **Replay Product Tour**, **See what Mist can do** and **Start from an idea**.

## Check your company's AI usage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find AI usage">
    Under **AI usage**, **Today** shows your company's spend so far today against its daily cap. **This month** shows the month's total, with a breakdown by AI provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Admins can see the full history on the [AI Spend](/help/admin/settings/ai-spend) screen in the admin.

## Keyboard shortcuts

On Windows, use Ctrl where this table shows Cmd.

| Shortcut | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Cmd+N | New chat |
| Cmd+K | Search projects |
| Cmd+, | Settings |
| Cmd+Shift+/ | Keyboard shortcuts |
| Cmd+Shift+A | See what Mist can do |
| Cmd+Shift+M | Create a Mist app |
| Cmd+Shift+T | Create a theme |
| Cmd+Shift+W | Create a widget |
| Cmd+Shift+S | Create a skill |
| Cmd+Shift+Enter | Run or stop the project |
| Cmd+Shift+P | Publish the project |
| Cmd+Shift+K | Stop all dev servers |
