The Mist window has three parts. From left to right:

- The **left nav**, with Mist's pages and a section for each kind of project you build.
- The **Chats** pane, where you find your chats and talk with Mist.
- The **page**, which shows the project you're working on, or a page such as **Skills** or **Time Machine**.

To take the tour again, click **Help and support** (the **?** button) in the title bar, then click **Replay Product Tour**.

## Left nav

At the top of the left nav:

- **Home**: the start page.
- **Time Machine**: the changes Mist made to your company. See [Time Machine](/help/mist/time-machine).
- **Skills**: the skills library. Your pinned skills are listed under it. See [Skills](/help/mist/skills).
- **Workflows**: the workflows library. Your open runs are listed under it. See [Workflows](/help/mist/workflows).

Below them is a section for each kind of project: **Themes**, **Apps**, **Portals**, **Widgets**, **MySites** and **Databases**. Click a section to open its page, which shows all your projects of that kind. Your projects are also listed under the section, newest first. See [Mist Apps and other projects](/help/mist/mist-apps).

To create a project, click its section, then click the button at the top of the page, such as **New app** or **New theme**.

Press Cmd+B (Ctrl+B on Windows) to fold the left nav to its icons, or to open it again. You can also click **Collapse sidebar** or **Expand sidebar** next to the window controls. While the nav is folded, hover over an icon to see what's in its section.

### Account menu

At the bottom of the left nav, click your name to open a menu:

- **Preferences**: settings such as the **Default chat model**.
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: the list of keyboard shortcuts.
- **Settings**: **Appearance**, **MCP servers**, **Workspace** and your company's **AI usage**.
- **Sign out**: signs you out.

Next to your name, one button switches between light and dark mode, and another opens **Settings**.

## Chats pane

The **Chats** pane lists every chat, including each project's chats, in three groups:

- **Pinned**: chats you pinned to the top.
- **Folders**: folders you made to group chats.
- **Recent**: everything else, newest first.

Click **New chat** at the top of the pane to start a chat. Type in **Search chats** to find one. See [Chats](/help/mist/chats).

Click a chat to open it in the pane. Click **Chats** at the top to go back to the list. To make more room for the page, click **Hide chats**. Click **Show chats** to bring the pane back.

### Talk with Mist

Type in the box at the bottom of the chat. Its placeholder reads "Plan, build, / for commands, @ for context".

- Press Enter to send. Press Shift+Enter for a new line.
- Type **/** to pick a command, a skill or a workflow. See [Slash commands](/help/mist/slash-commands).
- Click the model name to choose an AI model. See [Model selection](/help/mist/models).
- Use the buttons on the right of the box to dictate or to talk with Mist. See [Voice](/help/mist/voice).

As Mist works, the chat shows each step it takes, such as reading files, calling the Fluid API or checking logs. When Mist wants to make a change that needs your approval, it shows a card with **Approve** and **Decline**.

## Page

When you open a project, the page shows its preview. The bar at the top names the project. Click **More** (**⋯**) in that bar for the project's actions, such as **Rename…** or **Delete…**.

The toolbar above the preview has the controls you use to work on the project, including **Run**, **Publish**, **History**, **Logs** and the element picker. See [Run and publish](/help/mist/run-and-publish) and [Pick an element](/help/mist/pick-an-element).

## Title bar

The title bar has:

- The company switcher. Click your company's name to switch to another company.
- **Back** and **Forward**.
- A tab for each page you have open.
- The **Safe Mode** switch. See [Safe Mode](/help/mist/safe-mode).
- **Search**, which opens the command menu. Type to find pages, projects, chats and actions, such as **New app** or **Open settings**. You can also press Cmd+K (Ctrl+K on Windows).
- The notifications bell.
- **Help and support** (the **?** button), with **Replay Product Tour**, **What's new** and **See what Mist can do**.

## Check your company's AI usage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your name at the bottom of the left nav, then click **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find AI usage">
    Under **AI usage**, **Today** shows your company's spend so far today against its daily cap. **This month** shows the month's total, with a breakdown by AI provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Admins can see the full history on the [AI Spend](/help/admin/settings/ai-spend) screen in the admin.

## Keyboard shortcuts

On Windows, use Ctrl where this table shows Cmd.

| Shortcut | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Cmd+N | New chat |
| Cmd+K | Search: open the command menu |
| Cmd+B | Fold or open the left nav |
| Cmd+, | Settings |
| Cmd+Shift+/ | Keyboard shortcuts |
| Cmd+Shift+A | See what Mist can do |
| Cmd+Shift+M | Create a Mist app |
| Cmd+Shift+T | Create a theme |
| Cmd+Shift+W | Create a widget |
| Cmd+Shift+S | Create a skill |
| Cmd+Shift+Enter | Run or stop the project |
| Cmd+Shift+P | Publish the project |
| Cmd+Shift+K | Stop all dev servers |
