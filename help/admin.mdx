The admin portal at [admin.fluid.app](https://admin.fluid.app) is where your team runs your Fluid company: products and orders, your storefront and website, reps and customers, payments and settings. These guides follow the admin's own sidebar, so each guide sits where its screen does.

    Products, enrollment packs, categories, collections, pages, playlists and media.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Page Editor" icon="pen-to-square" href="/help/admin/page-editor">
    Edit your theme's page templates visually.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sitemap" icon="sitemap" href="/help/admin/sitemap">
    Control your sitemap, and add custom and country-specific routes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="URL Redirects" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left" href="/help/admin/url_redirects">
    Send marketing and legacy URLs to the right product or page.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Forms" icon="clipboard-list" href="/help/admin/forms">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Settings" icon="gear" href="/help/admin/settings">

</CardGroup>
