Your company's mobile app is where you do your day-to-day selling: sharing content, following up with contacts, messaging customers and running your MySite. Each company publishes the app under its own name, so these guides call it "the app".

Your company chooses which features appear in its app, and how its tabs and **Home** screen are laid out. If something described here isn't in your app, your company hasn't turned it on.
