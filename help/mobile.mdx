The Fluid mobile app is where reps do their day-to-day selling: sharing content, following up with contacts, messaging customers and managing their MySite.

Guides for the app are moving here from the previous help center. They cover:

- Getting around the app
- MySite
- Sharing content
- Contacts
- Messaging
- Playlists and content
- Settings and troubleshooting

Until the one you need is here, [Getting help](/help/getting-help) explains how to reach Fluid support.

If you set up your company's app in the admin portal, start from the [Admin portal](/help/admin) guides instead.
