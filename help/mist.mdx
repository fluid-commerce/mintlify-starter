Mist is Fluid's AI assistant, and the admin calls it "Fluid's agentic command center." It's a desktop app for macOS and Windows. You describe what you want in a chat, and Mist plans it, writes it and connects it to your Fluid company.

With Mist you can:

- Build and edit storefront themes, portals, widgets, MySites and hosted apps, called Mist apps.
- Preview your work on your computer, then publish it.
- Ask questions about your company's data, and query your reporting databases.
- Run skills and workflows that automate multi-step jobs, such as opening a country.
- Undo changes Mist made through Time Machine.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Mist">
    Download the app from the admin, then install it. See [Download Mist](/help/mist/download).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in with your Fluid email address, then pick a company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a chat">
    Type what you want in the chat, or pick a skill. See [Getting around](/help/mist/getting-around).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I download Mist?" id="where-download">
    In the admin, open **Settings** > **Developer**, or click the **Developer Hub** code icon in the top bar. Click the **Meet Mist Desktop** banner to download the build for your computer. See [Download Mist](/help/mist/download).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Mist change my live store?" id="change-live-store">
    Yes. Mist can publish themes and apps and make changes through the Fluid API, as you. Turn on [Safe Mode](/help/mist/safe-mode) when you want to explore without any risk of changing production.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who pays for what Mist does?" id="who-pays">
    Mist's AI usage is charged to your company, up to a daily cap that Fluid sets. Admins can see the spend on the [AI Spend](/help/admin/settings/ai-spend) screen. Mist apps also use hosting, which appears as Mist usage on [Billing](/help/admin/settings/billing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I undo something Mist did?" id="undo">
    For a change Mist made through the Fluid API, use [Time Machine](/help/mist/time-machine). For a project's files, use **History** in the preview to roll back. See [Run and publish](/help/mist/run-and-publish#roll-back).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I manage my company's Mist apps?" id="manage-apps">
    In the admin, on [Mist Apps](/help/admin/settings/mist-apps) under **Settings** > **System**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Mist's skills with other AI agents?" id="skills-elsewhere">
    Yes. Fluid publishes Mist's community skills and workflows in a public repository. Many skills work with other coding agents too. See [Mist Skills](/guides/mist-skills).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
