The Playbook is training for brand teams and reps: practical guidance on content, apps, leads and conversions that helps you get more out of Fluid.

Its articles are moving here from the previous help center, in three sections:

- **Business development**: video and shareable content, app engagement, and customer experience.
- **Sales and marketing**: conversations that convert, landing pages, cart abandonment, warming up leads, and playlists.
- **Playbook FAQ**: quick answers on messaging, leads, events, metrics and more.

Until the article you need is here, [Getting help](/help/getting-help) explains how to reach Fluid support.
