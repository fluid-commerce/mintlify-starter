The **Website** screens control how your storefront and your reps' MySites look, and how visitors and search engines find their pages.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, select **Website**. It opens to show its screens. You see only the screens your role can view.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Theme" icon="palette" href="/help/admin/themes">
    See your current theme, keep others in your library, and publish the one your store uses.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mysite Theme" icon="id-badge" href="/help/admin/mysite-themes">
    Add and rename the themes behind your reps' MySites, and open their code.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sitemap" icon="sitemap" href="/help/admin/sitemap">
    Control your sitemap, and add custom and country-specific routes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Page Editor" icon="pen-to-square" href="/help/admin/page-editor">
    Edit your theme's page templates visually. Open it with **Customize** on the **Theme** screen.
  </Card>
  <Card title="URL Redirects" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left" href="/help/admin/url_redirects">
    Send marketing and legacy URLs to the right product or page.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**Navigation**, where you set up your website's menus, doesn't have a guide yet.
