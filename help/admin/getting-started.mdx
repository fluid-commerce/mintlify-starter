**Getting Started** is the screen the admin opens on. Use it to check your website and mobile app at a glance, start the first setup tasks, and open each area you need to set up.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, select **Getting Started**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Getting Started**. To use **Connect Now**, the **Schedule a Call** button at the top, and the **Set Up Payments**, **Enrollments** and **Droplets** cards, it needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Getting Started** is in the **Commerce** card. Open the card and set access from the **Getting Started** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

A card for another area, such as **Set Up Payments** or **Products**, appears only if your role can view that area.

### Header

The screen opens with **Welcome to We-Commerce** and a greeting. Under it:

- **Learn About We-Commerce** opens the We-Commerce website in a new tab.
- **Schedule a Call** opens a booking page in a new tab, where you can book a call with Fluid Studios.

### Commission engine card

The **Your Platform. Ready in Minutes.** card is for connecting your commission engine. **Connect Now** opens the **Connect Data Source** panel. See [Connect your commission engine](#connect-your-commission-engine).

### Site and app previews

**Live Site** shows a preview of your website's home page, with the site's address above it. The preview is for viewing only.

- Scan the QR code to open your site on a phone, or click it to open the site in a new tab.
- **Customize** opens the [Theme](/help/admin/themes) screen.
- **Connect domain** appears while your site uses a Fluid web address instead of your own domain. It opens the [Domains](/help/admin/settings/domains) screen.

**Mobile App** shows a preview of your app's home page. If you have more than one home page, it shows the default one, or the first active one if none is the default.

- Scan the QR code with a phone to download your app, or click it to open the download page in a new tab.
- **Customize** opens the [Home Tabs](/help/admin/mobile/home) screen.

### Setup cards

Three cards sit under the previews. Click a card to use it.

- **Set Up Payments** opens the **Set Up Payments** panel. See [Set up payments](#set-up-payments).
- **Enrollments** opens the **Enrollment Packages** panel. See [Set up enrollment](#set-up-enrollment).
- **Droplets** opens the Droplets **Marketplace**. Its button reads **Explore** until your company has connected a droplet, and **Manage** after that.

### Quickstart Console

The **Quickstart Console** has a card for each area you set up. Most cards show a count. Click a card to open its screen.

**Set up We-Commerce** covers the items that sync through Fluid Connect when you connect a commission engine. If you don't have one, you add them yourself.

- **Products**: how many products you have, counting active, draft and archived ones. Opens [Products](/help/admin/products).
- **Customers**: how many members your company has, customers and reps alike. Opens [Customers](/help/admin/customers).
- **Countries**: how many countries you've added. Opens [Countries](/help/admin/settings/countries).
- **Warehouses**: how many warehouse locations you have. Opens [Warehouses](/help/admin/settings/warehouses).
- **Inventory**: how many warehouses you have. Opens [Inventory](/help/admin/inventory-levels).
- **Categories**: how many categories you have. Opens [Categories](/help/admin/categories).
- **Collections**: how many collections you have. Opens [Collections](/help/admin/collections).

The next section's title starts with **Configure Fluid for** and ends with your company's name. It covers areas Fluid has set up for you that you should double-check.

- **APMs**: how many active payment methods you have. Opens [Alternative Payment Accounts](/help/admin/settings/apm).
- **Shipping**: your company's shipping setting, or **Not Set**. Opens [Shipping](/help/admin/settings/shipping).
- **Tax**: your company's tax setting, or **Not Set**. Opens [Tax](/help/admin/settings/taxes).
- **Admins**: how many admins your company has. Opens [Admins](/help/admin/admins).
- **Agreements**: how many **Active Agreements** you have. Opens [Agreements](/help/admin/settings/agreements).
- **Languages**: how many **Active Languages** your company has. Opens [Languages](/help/admin/settings/languages).
- **Subscription Plans**: how many subscription plans you have. Opens [Subscription Plans](/help/admin/settings/subscription-plans).
- **Promotions**: how many **Active Codes** you have. Opens [Promo Codes](/help/admin/promo-codes).
- **Developer**: opens [Developer](/help/admin/settings/developer).
- **Brand Guidelines**: opens [Brand Guidelines](/help/admin/settings/brand-guidelines).

In this section, a green dot on a card means the area is set up: its count is above zero or, for **Shipping** and **Tax**, a setting is chosen. **Developer** and **Brand Guidelines** don't show one.

**Get even more out of We-Commerce** has two more cards:

- **Advanced settings & configurations for power users** opens the [Brand Guidelines](/help/admin/settings/brand-guidelines) screen in Settings.
- **Get personalized help from Fluid Studios experts** opens the same booking page as **Schedule a Call**, in a new tab.

## Tasks

### Connect your commission engine

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    On the **Your Platform. Ready in Minutes.** card, click **Connect Now**. The **Connect Data Source** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your engine">
    Under **Connect Your Commission Engine**, click your engine's tile. The panel has a tile for each of these that's available as a droplet: Exigo, ByDesign, Pillars, Shopify and Infotrax. A **Connected** badge marks one your company has already connected. The droplet's page opens, and the panel closes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your engine has no tile, click **Custom** to browse all integrations in the **Marketplace**. If you don't have a commission engine, click **I Need One** to open a booking page for a call with Fluid Studios.

For guided setup of these connections, see [Connect Exigo](/setup/connect-exigo), [Connect ByDesign](/setup/connect-bydesign), [Connect Pillars](/setup/connect-pillars) and [Connect InfoTrax](/setup/connect-infotrax) in the developer docs.

### Set up payments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    Click the **Set Up Payments** card. The **Set Up Payments** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how you take payments">
    Under **Choose Your Payment Experience**, choose one:

    - **Activate Fluid Payments** opens the [Onboarding](/help/admin/settings/onboarding) screen.
    - **Connect to existing provider accounts** opens the [Alternative Payment Accounts](/help/admin/settings/apm) screen, for using your own provider accounts instead of Fluid Payments.
    - **Need Help?** opens a booking page in a new tab, where you can book a call with a payment specialist.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Set up enrollment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    Click the **Enrollments** card. The **Enrollment Packages** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to set up">
    - On the **Enrollment Forms** card, click **Set Up Forms** to open the [Forms](/help/admin/forms) screen.
    - On the **Enrollment Packs** card, click **Create Packs** to open the [Enrollments](/help/admin/enrollments) screen.
    - **Need Help Getting Started?** opens a booking page in a new tab, where you can book a call with an enrollment specialist.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips

- The counts on the cards can take up to five minutes to catch up with changes you make elsewhere in the admin.
- The **Mobile App** preview reloads each time you open this screen, so changes to your app's home page show when you come back.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why are some buttons and cards grayed out?" id="grayed-out">
    Your role has **View only** for **Getting Started**. **Connect Now**, the **Schedule a Call** button at the top, and the **Set Up Payments**, **Enrollments** and **Droplets** cards need **Full access**. The **Quickstart Console** cards still open their screens. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see a card?" id="missing-card">
    A card for another area appears only if your role can view that area. Ask an admin who manages roles to give your role access to it. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why doesn't the Live Site card show my store?" id="store-preview-pending">
    If the card says **Your store preview appears once you confirm your email.**, your company's signup email isn't confirmed yet. After you confirm it, the preview and the site's QR code appear.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the Mobile App preview say No home page configured yet?" id="no-home-page">
    Your app doesn't have a home page yet. Click **Customize** on the **Mobile App** card to open the [Home Tabs](/help/admin/mobile/home) screen and set one up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the Some features may be limited banner mean?" id="profile-banner">
    The admin couldn't load your full profile. Click **Retry** on the banner to load it again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
