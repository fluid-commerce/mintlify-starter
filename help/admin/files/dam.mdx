Use the **Files** screen to manage the files your company stores in Fluid: images, videos, audio, text files, documents and more.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Content**, select **Files**.

<Note>
  To open this screen and see your files, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for both **File Resources** and **Media**. To upload, rename, replace or delete files, it needs **Full access** for both. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **File Resources** and **Media** are in the **Content & Website** card. Open the card and set access from each area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

The top of the page has **Upload File(s)**. Below it is the file list:

- The **All**, **Images**, **Videos**, **Audio**, **Text**, **Documents** and **Other** tabs narrow the list. **All** doesn't list files in the **Text** or **Other** categories.
- Search with the **Search...** box.
- Click the filter button (funnel icon) to filter by **Tags**, then click **Apply Filters**.
- Columns: **Preview**, **Name**, **Category**, **Created** and **Size**. A file's tags show under its name. The preview shows a thumbnail for images, the first frame for videos and the first page for PDFs.
- Click a row to open the file's details page.

Each row also has these buttons. Hover over a button to see its name.

- **Copy asset link** (link icon): copies the file's link.
- **Transform asset** (wand icon): opens the transform window. See [Transform a file](#transform-a-file).
- **Edit tags** (tag icon): adds or removes tags. See [Tag files](#tag-files).
- The **⋮** menu: **Replace asset** and **Delete**.

## Upload files

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the upload window">
    Click **Upload File(s)**. The upload window opens on the option to upload from your computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your files">
    Select up to 10 files. The size limit is 100 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the upload">
    Confirm your selection in the upload window. A message confirms how many files were uploaded.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View a file's details

Click a file in the list. Its details page shows the file's title at the top, a preview, and these cards:

- **Variations** (images and videos only): ready-made versions of the file, each with a link you can copy.
- **Details**: the **Title**, the **Default URL** with a copy button, the **MIME type** and the **Size**.
- **Actions**: **Transform** and **Replace**.
- **Tags**: the file's tags.

The preview shows images, plays videos and displays PDFs and text files. For other files, it shows **No preview available**.

## Copy a link

- To copy the file's own link, click **Copy asset link** in its row, or the copy button next to **Default URL** on its details page.
- To copy a variation's link, open the file and, in the **Variations** card, click the copy icon on that variation's thumbnail.

A message confirms each copy. These variations are available:

| File type | Variations |
|:---|:---|
| Image | **Square (512×512)**, **Widescreen (1280×720)**, **Low quality preview**, **High quality**, **Grayscale**, **Blurred** |
| Video | **Low quality**, **High quality**, **Poster frame**, **Short clip** |

## Transform a file

Click **Transform asset** in the file's row, or **Transform** in the **Actions** card on its details page. The transform window opens. When you finish, the link to the transformed version is copied to your clipboard.

## Rename a file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the file">
    Click the file in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the title">
    In the **Details** card, click **Edit title** (pencil icon) and type the new title. To discard your change, press Escape or click **Cancel** (X icon).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** at the top of the page. **Save** is available once the title has changed, and the title can't be blank.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Replace a file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Replace">
    In the file's row, open the **⋮** menu and choose **Replace asset**. On the details page, click **Replace** in the **Actions** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the new file">
    Pick the new file in the window that opens. An **Asset replaced** message appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The file keeps the same link after you replace it.

## Tag files

- In the list, click **Edit tags** (tag icon) in the file's row. Type a tag in the **Add tag...** box and press Enter.
- On the details page, type a tag in the **Tags** card and press Enter. A tag is also added when you click away from the box.

To remove a tag, click the **×** next to it. Each change saves right away. Tags are saved in lowercase, and a tag the file already has isn't added again.

## Delete a file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    In the file's row, open the **⋮** menu and choose **Delete**. On the details page, open the **⋮** menu at the top of the page and choose **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Are you sure?** or **Delete asset** dialog, click **Delete**. If you deleted the file from its details page, you return to the file list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a file is permanent, and its links stop working.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see my text files under All?" id="text-files-not-in-all">
    The **All** tab doesn't list files in the **Text** or **Other** categories. Open the **Text** or **Other** tab to see them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I find files that have several tags?" id="filter-several-tags">
    Click the filter button and enter the tags in **Tags**. Separate them with semicolons, such as `summer;sale`, to show files that have all of them. Separate them with commas, such as `summer,sale`, to show files that have any of them. Then click **Apply Filters**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I upload, replace or delete files?" id="missing-edit-controls">
    Your role doesn't have **Full access** for both **File Resources** and **Media**. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
