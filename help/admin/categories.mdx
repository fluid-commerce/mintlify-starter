Use the **Categories** screen to group products into categories for your storefront. Each category has its own title, description, image, product list and storefront settings.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Storefront**, select **Categories**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Categories**. To create, edit or delete categories, it needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Categories** is in the **Commerce** card. Open the card and set access from the **Categories** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

The **Add Category** button is at the top of the page. Below it is the list of categories.

- The **All**, **Published**, **Scheduled**, **Draft** and **Archived** tabs filter the list by status.
- Use the search box to find a category by name.
- Sort the list by **Name** or **Date Modified**. Until you choose a sort, the newest categories are first.

The list has these columns:

- **Preview**: the category's image, or an empty frame if it has none.
- **Name**: the category's title.
- **Status**: **Published**, **Draft**, **Archived**, or **Scheduled** with the date and time it goes live.
- **Countries**: **All** when the category is available in every country you sell in. Otherwise, the flags of the countries it's limited to, with a count such as **+3** for the rest.
- **Date Modified**: when the category last changed.

Click a category to open it. Each row's actions menu has **Edit** and **Delete**. To delete several categories, select their rows; the selection's menu shows the count, such as **Delete 3 categories**.

A banner above the list offers a new version of the table. Click **Try it** to use it, or **Switch back** to return. The new table adds **Status** and **Country** filters. Your choice is remembered on that device.

## Create a category

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new category">
    On the **Categories** screen, click **Add Category**. The **Create Category** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    In the **Category Details** card, enter a **Title**. Add a **Description** if you want one; the editor has formatting tools such as bold, lists and links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an image">
    Under **Media**, click **Select Image** and choose or upload an image. JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP and SVG files up to 10 MB are supported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add products">
    In the **Associated Products** card, click **Add Products** and select the products you want. There's no limit on how many products a category holds. Drag products to set the order they appear in on your storefront.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the status">
    In the **Status** card, choose a status. A new category starts as **Published**, so choose **Draft** if it isn't ready. See [Status options](#status-options).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the other cards">
    Optionally, limit the **Countries**, choose a **Theme Template**, and edit **SEO And Link Sharing**. See [Cards on the category page](#cards-on-the-category-page).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the category">
    Click **Create Category**. A confirmation message appears and the category's page opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Fill in the **Metafields** card after you create the category, from the category's page.
</Tip>

## Edit a category

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the category">
    In the list, click the category, or choose **Edit** from its actions menu. The **Category Details** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change the fields and cards you need, add or remove products, or drag products into a new order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. A confirmation message appears when the category is saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The title at the top of the page shows the category's name and a badge with its current status.

### Status options

The **Status** card has four options:

- **Published**: live and visible to the public.
- **Draft**: hidden from public view.
- **Scheduled**: goes live automatically at a date and time you choose. Pick the date and time, then click **Schedule Activation**. The card then shows the scheduled date.
- **Archived**: retired and hidden from public view.

<Warning>
  Scheduling isn't a privacy setting. A scheduled category is reachable by direct link before its scheduled date. The date controls when it appears in listings and becomes available for search engines to index.
</Warning>

### Cards on the category page

- **Metafields**: values for any metafields your company has defined for categories. Each value has its own **Save** button. To define metafields, see [Metadata Definitions](/help/admin/settings/metadata-definitions).
- **Countries**: the countries where the category is available. Leave it on **All Countries** to make it available in every country you sell in, including countries you add later. Choose countries to limit it to those.
- **Theme Template**: the template from your active theme that displays the category. If none is chosen, the theme's default category template is selected for you. If your theme has no category templates, choose **Create Template** to add one.
- **SEO And Link Sharing**: how the category appears in search results and shared links. See [Edit SEO and link sharing](#edit-seo-and-link-sharing).
- **Fluid Connect**: appears only when your company has a connected [Exigo](/setup/connect-exigo), [InfoTrax](/setup/connect-infotrax), [ByDesign](/setup/connect-bydesign) or [Pillars](/setup/connect-pillars) droplet, and links to that droplet.
- **Analytics**: the category's **Shares**, **Page Speed** and **Content Compliance**. Click a metric to see its details. You can request a new page speed or compliance scan from its details; results can take a few minutes.

### Edit SEO and link sharing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    In the **SEO And Link Sharing** card, click **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the search text">
    Edit the **Title Tag** and **Meta Description**. They start from the category's title and description. The suggested lengths are 70 characters for the title tag and 160 for the meta description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the image and preview">
    Add or replace the **SEO Image**. Use the **Preview** menu to see the category as a **Google Search Result**, a **Text Message** or on **X (formerly Twitter)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the URL and visibility">
    The **URL** field shows the category's address. Turn on **Use custom slug** to edit the **URL Slug**; otherwise the slug is made from the title. Turn off **Search engine visibility** to keep the category out of Google and other search engines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the panel and save">
    Click **Done**, then click **Save** on the category page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Done** is grayed out until the **Title Tag** has at least 3 characters and the **Meta Description** has at least 5.

### Other actions on a saved category

- **Preview** opens the category on your storefront in a new tab.
- **Translations** opens the category's **Category Title**, **Description** and **Category Image** in each of your active languages. Save your translations from that panel. To manage languages, see [Languages](/help/admin/settings/languages).
- **Reset Cache**, in the **Actions** menu, clears the storefront's cached copy of the category.

## Delete a category

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    In the list, choose **Delete** from the category's actions menu. To delete several, select their rows and choose the delete option that shows the count, such as **Delete 3 categories**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the confirmation dialog, click **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also open a category and open the **Actions** menu and choose **Delete Category**, then click **Delete Category** to confirm. You return to the list.

<Warning>
  Deleting a category can't be undone.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is Create Category grayed out?" id="create-category-disabled">
    Enter a **Title** in the **Category Details** card. **Create Category** is available once the category has a title.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Preview?" id="no-preview">
    **Preview** isn't on the **Create Category** page. It appears at the top of the category's page after you create it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does All mean in the Countries column?" id="countries-all">
    The category isn't limited to particular countries. It's available in every country your company sells in, including countries you add later.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Add Category, or why can't I edit a category?" id="no-add-or-edit">
    Your role can view categories but doesn't have **Full access** for **Categories**. Without it, a category's page opens read-only, with no **Save** button. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
