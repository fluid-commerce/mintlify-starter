Use the **Portal Builder** screen to build your portal, the signed-in experience for your reps, and to choose which of your portal apps is live. The portal is separate from your mobile app: the [Mobile App](/help/admin/mobile) screens set up the app's tabs, while the portal opens in a web browser and has its own desktop and mobile layouts.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, select **Portal**.

<Note>
  To open this screen and the builder, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Mobile Pages**. To create, change, publish or delete apps, it needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Mobile Pages** is in the **Content & Website** card. Open the card and set access from the **Mobile Pages** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

Each portal you build is an app. You can keep several, but only one is live at a time, marked **Current App**.

The card at the top shows where your portal stands:

- **You haven't built a portal yet**: you have no apps. The header button is **Create Your App**.
- **Nothing published yet**: you have apps, but none is live.
- Otherwise, desktop and mobile previews of your live app, with its **Last published** date and **Customize**, which opens it in the builder. Widgets with member data, such as points, to-dos, activity and products, show sample data in these previews.

Once you have an app, the header button is **View Your App**, which opens your portal in a new tab.

The **Apps Library** lists your apps, 15 at a time (click **Load More** for the rest). Each row has an actions menu with **Update Name** and **Delete**, **Customize**, and, except on the current app, **Publish**. If another app is live, the **Publish this app?** dialog warns that this app will replace it. Click **Publish**.

To rename an app, choose **Update Name**, change the **App Name** and click **Save**. To delete one, choose **Delete** and confirm in the **Delete App** dialog. You can't undo a delete, and you can't delete the current app: publish a different app first.

## Create an app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create Your App**, or **Create New App** in the **Apps Library**. Fluid names the app after you, with the date and time, such as `Jane Doe's New App 14/10/26 09:30`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the starter setup">
    The app opens in the builder with a **Home** screen, a navigation with **We Commerce** and **Marketing Assets** sections, a **Company Theme** made from your [brand colors](/help/admin/settings/brand-guidelines), and a **Default Profile** for admins and reps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The app isn't live until you publish it.

## The builder

- **Header**: a menu button with **Return to Dashboard**, which goes back to the Portal Builder screen; sidebar toggles; the app's name and save status (**Unsaved Changes**, **Last Saved At** and a time, or **No Changes Yet**); **Undo** and **Redo** (⌘Z or Ctrl+Z, ⌘⇧Z or Ctrl+Shift+Z); **History**; **App Settings**; **Preview**; and **Publish**.
- **Profile column**: a button with each profile's initials. The default profile has a green dot. The **+** button (**Create new profile**) adds one.
- **EXPERIENCES**: **Desktop** and **Mobile** switch the canvas between a desktop and a phone frame. Each has its own navigation. **Access** opens the selected profile's menu.
- **Screens** and **Widgets** tabs.
- **Canvas**: the selected screen, with zoom buttons, a fit-to-screen button and **Hide Preview Navigation** (**Show Preview Navigation** once hidden). Click a widget to select it.
- **Right panel**: the selected widget's settings, **App Settings** or **History**.

**Preview** shows the app full screen without editing tools. **Exit Preview** takes you back.

How changes save:

- Screen and widget edits save about a second after you stop. While you type in a text field, they save when you leave the field.
- Navigation changes save as you make them. Profile changes save when you click **Create Profile** or **Save Changes**.
- Theme and app name changes save only when you click **Save Changes**.
- Nothing goes live in your portal until you [publish](#publish-your-changes).

## Screens and navigation

The **Screens** tab has a search box and three sections:

- **Navigation**: the tabs and section headers in the selected profile's menu for this experience.
- **System Screens**: built-in screens not in the navigation yet. In the builder they show a **System Screen** placeholder.
- **Available Screens**: your screens that aren't in the navigation.

Click an item to show it on the canvas.

A few system screens have extra rules in your live portal:

- **Messaging** and **Contacts** show only when they're in the viewer's navigation.
- **Messaging** and **MySite** show only to members whose member type has **Can act as rep** turned on. See [Member Types](/help/admin/settings/member-types). Everyone else doesn't see them in the menu, and gets **Page not found** if they open the page.
- **Profile**, **Orders** and **Subscriptions** are core items. You can't remove them from the navigation.

### Add a screen

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the screen">
    Under **Available Screens**, click **Create New Screen**. Fluid adds an empty screen named **New Screen** with a number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to the navigation">
    Drag the screen into **Navigation**, where you want it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add widgets">
    See [Add a widget](#add-a-widget).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Create from Template**, in the **+** menu next to **Navigation**, creates a screen from a template and puts it straight into the navigation.

### Change the navigation

- **Reorder**: drag items within **Navigation**. Drop an item onto another to nest it there. Section headers can't be nested, and built-in items can't hold other items. If the target is a tab, it becomes a section header and its screen moves into the section as the first item.
- **Remove**: hover over an item, open its ⋮ menu and choose **Remove Navigation**, then confirm in the **Delete Navigation Item** dialog. You can also drag an item back to **System Screens** or **Available Screens**. The screen itself isn't deleted, and core items can't be removed. Removing a section header also removes the items inside it, so move out any you want to keep first.
- **Add a tab**: click **+** next to **Navigation**, choose **Add Screen**, then pick a screen under **Available Screens** or an item under **System Navigation**. It goes to the end of the navigation.
- **Section headers**: click **+** next to **Navigation**, choose **Add Header**, enter a **Name** and click **Add Header**.
- **Empty navigation**: the canvas shows **No navigation items yet**. Add **Products**, **Account** or **Shop**, or click **Add All**.

To copy the other experience's navigation, hover over **Navigation**, open the ⋮ menu (**Navigation actions**), choose **Sync from mobile navigation** (on **Mobile**, **Sync from desktop navigation**), and click **Replace**. The option is unavailable when the other navigation is empty.

<Warning>
  **Replace** deletes this experience's navigation items and puts a copy of the other navigation in their place. You can't undo it.
</Warning>

## Add a widget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the screen">
    On the **Screens** tab, click the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the widget">
    Open the **Widgets** tab. Widgets are grouped by category. Widgets from your installed droplets have categories named after the droplet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag it onto the canvas">
    Drop it where you want it. Fluid selects it and opens its settings on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it up">
    Change its settings. The canvas updates as you go.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The settings panel's top bar has **Move Up** and **Move Down** (within the widget's column), **Duplicate**, **Delete** and **Close**. For a container widget it shows only **Close**.

## Theme and app name

Click **App Settings**. **Appearance** edits the theme:

- **Light** and **Dark**: the mode you're editing. In **Dark**, colors marked **Auto** are set for you. **Reset** returns a changed color to auto.
- **Use brand colors**: applies your brand guideline colors. **Manage** opens Brand Guidelines. Changing a color yourself turns this off.
- **Colors** (**Primary**, **Surface** and **Text**), **Heading** and **Body** fonts, **Text scale**, **Radius** and **Spacing**.
- **Style**: brand presets to start from. **Shuffle palette** makes a random palette.

The canvas previews changes right away. Click **Save Changes** to keep them, or **Cancel**.

**Name** has the app's **Title**. Change it and click **Save Changes**.

## Profiles

Each profile has its own desktop and mobile navigation, and rules for who can access it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a profile">
    Click **Create new profile** (+).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to start">
    Choose **Start from Scratch** or **Start from Template**, then click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it up">
    Change the **Profile Name** if you like. Turn on **Default Profile** if needed. Under **Rules**, choose **Ranks**, **Member Types** and **Countries**. The hint under **Member Types** says whether leaving it empty allows everyone or only admins. With member types selected, turn on **Visible to admins** to let company admins in too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create Profile**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your only profile is always the default. To change a profile, select it and click **Access**: **Edit Profile**, **Duplicate Profile** or **Delete Profile**. Deleting a profile also deletes its navigations.

### Who sees which profile

When someone opens your live portal, Fluid picks one profile for them:

1. It compares each profile's **Ranks**, **Member Types** and **Countries** with the person's rank, member type and country. Company admins count as admins, not as a member type. Someone without a country gets your company's default country.
2. An empty rule allows everyone. A rule that leaves the person out rules that profile out.
3. If several profiles fit, the most specific one wins. A rule with one value beats a rule with several, and both beat an empty rule.
4. If no profile fits, the person gets the **Default Profile**.
5. If no profile fits and none is the default, the person sees no screens, only Fluid's built-in menu.

For **Member Types**:

- Left empty, everyone can access the profile, admins included.
- With member types selected, only those members can. Turn on **Visible to admins** to let company admins in too.
- **Visible to admins** is available only once you've selected at least one member type.

<Warning>
  Nothing tells someone they've fallen back to the default profile. If a person doesn't see the screens you expect, check that their rank, member type and country fit the profile's rules.
</Warning>

People only get the screens that their profile's navigation includes.

## Publish your changes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Publish">
    In the builder header, click **Publish**. It's available when the app has unpublished changes. Pending edits save first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm if asked">
    If this app isn't live, the **Publish this app?** dialog warns that it will replace the live app. Click **Publish**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## History and earlier versions

**History** has two tabs:

- **Local edits**: up to 50 changes since you opened the builder, newest first. Click an entry to undo or redo to that point. Entries you went back past show **Redo available**.
- **Published**: your published versions, newest first. The active one has a **Live** badge.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the version">
    In **History**, open **Published**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Republish it">
    Click **Republish** on the version, then **Republish** in the dialog. That version becomes active. Fluid doesn't create a new version. If the app isn't your current app, republishing also makes it the live app.
  </Step>
</Steps>
