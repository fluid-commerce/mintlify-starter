TikTok Shop lets shoppers buy your products without leaving TikTok. Fluid publishes your products to your TikTok Shop, keeps their price and stock up to date, and brings every TikTok order into Fluid so you can fulfill it like any other order.

TikTok Shop is a closed loop: the shopper pays TikTok, and TikTok holds the money and handles refunds. Fluid records the order, its credit and its refunds.

<Note>
  TikTok Shop on Fluid is in beta, for US shops only. Fluid's team sets up each shop with you. The limits that apply during the beta are listed in [Beta limits](#beta-limits).
</Note>

## Before you start

- A US TikTok Shop seller account that has finished TikTok's seller verification.
- Someone who can sign in to TikTok Shop as the shop owner or an admin. They approve Fluid's access during setup.
- At least one shipping warehouse in TikTok Seller Center, linked to one of your Fluid warehouses.
- A list of the products you want to sell on TikTok, checked against [What can sell on TikTok Shop](#what-can-sell-on-tiktok-shop).

## How Fluid sets up your shop

Fluid's team connects your TikTok Shop with you. You don't connect it yourself from the admin.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Approve Fluid on TikTok">
    Fluid's team sends you two TikTok approval links. The person who can sign in as the shop owner or an admin opens each one and approves it:
    - **Fluid We-Commerce**, which lists your products and imports your orders.
    - **Fluid Creator Sales**, which tells Fluid which creator sold each order, so the right rep gets credit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the warehouse">
    Tell Fluid's team which Fluid warehouse ships TikTok orders. Fluid publishes that warehouse's stock to TikTok.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who gets credit by default">
    Choose the member who gets credit for TikTok orders that no rep sold, usually your house account. See [Who gets credit for a TikTok order](#who-gets-credit-for-a-tiktok-order).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree how TikTok volume reaches your back office">
    Decide with Fluid's team whether TikTok orders count toward your reps' volume in your back office. Fluid itself pays no commission on TikTok orders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your products">
    Fluid's team publishes the products you chose. After that, you can publish, retry or unpublish each product from its page. See [Publish a product](#publish-a-product).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What can sell on TikTok Shop

TikTok decides what can be sold on TikTok Shop, and its rules change. Check your catalog against TikTok's current rules before anything is published. A product TikTok rejects shows **Rejected by TikTok** on its page in Fluid.

| Product type | On TikTok Shop | What TikTok requires |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Weight management | Not allowed | Includes diet pills, fat burners, diet shakes and meal-replacement bars and drinks |
| Dietary supplements | By invitation | TikTok's invitation, an FDA-registered facility, cGMP and a recent lab test |
| Essential oils | Allowed with documents | The manufacturer's safety data sheet (SDS) |
| Skincare and cosmetics | Allowed | Qualification documents if TikTok asks; no whitening, bleaching or CBD |

Also check:

- **Claims.** Titles, descriptions and images can't claim to treat, cure or prevent a disease, or promise weight loss.
- **Your business model.** Listings sell products only. Nothing on TikTok Shop can promote joining your business, earning income or recruiting.
- **Your own policies.** Some companies' policies and procedures don't allow selling on marketplaces. Check yours before you ask for a shop.

TikTok's own pages have the full rules: [prohibited and restricted products](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=1399532709988097&lang=en) and [dietary supplements](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=1411277869713198&lang=en).

## Publish a product

Once your shop is connected, a product's page has a **Sales Channels** card with a **TikTok Shop** row. The card appears only when your role can view company settings, and the buttons need permission to update products.

- **Publish** sends the product to TikTok Shop.
- **Retry** sends it again after a failed or rejected publish.
- **Unpublish** deactivates the listing on TikTok Shop. Shoppers there can't buy it until you publish it again.

If the row says to set the TikTok warehouse first, contact Fluid support: no product can publish until a warehouse is set.

Price and stock changes you make in Fluid are sent to TikTok automatically. You don't need to publish the product again.

### What the status means

| Status | What it means |
|:---|:---|
| **Not published** | The product isn't on TikTok Shop. |
| **Publishing…** | Fluid is sending the product to TikTok Shop. |
| **Live · In review** | The product is on TikTok Shop, and TikTok is still reviewing it. |
| **Live** | TikTok approved the product, and shoppers can buy it. |
| **Rejected by TikTok** | TikTok's review rejected the product. Fix what TikTok names, then click **Retry**. |
| **Publish failed** | Fluid couldn't send the product. The error shows on the row. Click **Retry** after you fix it. |
| **Unpublished** | You unpublished the product. |
| **Deactivated by TikTok** or **Frozen by TikTok** | TikTok took the product down. Check TikTok Seller Center for the reason. |

## TikTok orders in Fluid

Every TikTok Shop order is imported into Fluid. It appears on the [Orders](/help/admin/orders) screen like any other order, and you fulfill it from Fluid. When you add tracking in Fluid, Fluid sends it to TikTok.

The order's page has a **TikTok Shop order** card with:

- **TikTok order ID** and **TikTok status**.
- **Ingestion**: whether Fluid imported the order cleanly. If it failed, **Import error** says why.
- **Creator**: the TikTok creator who sold the order, if one did. Click the handle to open their TikTok profile.
- **Commission paid**: the commission TikTok paid that creator.
- **Buyer** and **Imported**.

TikTok hides each shopper's real email address. The shopper becomes your customer in Fluid under the private email address TikTok gives them.

## Who gets credit for a TikTok order

TikTok orders have no Fluid rep link, so Fluid credits them this way:

- **A creator sold the order and matches one of your reps:** the rep gets credit. The **Creator** row shows **Credited to** and the rep's name. Fluid matches creators to reps by the TikTok handle on the rep's profile, so ask your reps to add theirs. In the Fluid mobile app, it's under [Settings](/help/mobile/settings) > **Social Media**.
- **A creator sold it but matches no rep:** the order credits your house account for now. To credit the right rep, contact Fluid support with the creator's handle and the rep. Fluid moves the credit on that creator's open orders.
- **No creator sold it** (a shopper found the product on their own): the order credits your house account.

Whether that credit counts toward volume in your back office is the setting you agreed during setup. TikTok pays creators their commission directly, so the same sale is never paid twice.

## Cancellations, returns and refunds

TikTok holds the shopper's money, so TikTok issues every refund.

- **The shopper or TikTok cancels:** Fluid cancels the order, records the refund, and sends the cancellation to your back office.
- **You cancel in Fluid:** Fluid asks TikTok to cancel the order too. TikTok can refuse, for example once the order has shipped. If it does, contact Fluid support.
- **A completed return:** Fluid records it as a refund on the order.

## Beta limits

- **TikTok orders don't lower your Fluid stock yet.** Your Fluid stock and your TikTok stock can drift apart. Choose products for TikTok that you won't oversell, and check their stock by hand.
- **US shops only.**
- **Fluid's team handles setup and creator mapping.** There's no admin screen for either yet.
