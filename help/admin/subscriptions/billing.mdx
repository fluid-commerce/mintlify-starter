Use **Subscription Billing** to work through a month of subscription renewals. You can spot the days with failed or at-risk renewals, open a day to see who's affected, and retry payments or email those customers from the same screen.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Payments**, select **Subscription Billing**.

To open the screen, your role needs **Reports** set to **On**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Reports** is in the **Commerce** card. Open the card and turn it on from the **Reports** area's menu. The same permission opens [Home](/help/admin/home), [Traffic](/help/admin/traffic), [Performance](/help/admin/payments/performance) and [Transactions](/help/admin/company-payment-reports).

To retry payments or email customers, your role also needs **Full access** for **Subscriptions**, or **Custom** access with **Edit subscription details, pause, or cancel** turned on, in the same **Commerce** card. Without it, you can read every figure, but the selection boxes, the retry buttons and **Recommended actions** don't appear.

## What's on the screen

The page header reads **Subscription Billing**, under a **Subscriptions** breadcrumb. The breadcrumb is a link only if your role can open the **Subscriptions** screen.

The screen has two tabs, **Calendar** and **Analytics**, which report on the same month. It opens on **Calendar** and the current month.

### Calendar

Above the month grid, a summary row shows the month's figures. Use the **Renewals** and **Recovery** switch to change what the summary and each day show, **Today** to go back to the current month, and the arrows to move between months. Only days with renewals show figures.

| View | Each day shows |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Renewals** | The money collected (days that have run, including today) or scheduled (later days), and how many renewals |
| **Recovery** | The money you could still recover and, on days that have run, how many renewals **need action** (failed payments that a retry can't fix, because the customer needs a new payment method). On a later day, the amount is the at-risk total. |

A dot marks a day that needs a look: amber for **Elevated risk**, red for **High risk**. Point at a day for more figures, and click it to open the day panel.

### The day panel

The day panel opens beside the calendar, or over it on a narrow screen. The X closes it. It has two tabs.

**Overview** sums up the day:

- **Collected** (a day that has run) or **Expected** (a later day), with the number of **Orders**. Click the card for a breakdown by charges, customers and products.
- **Lost to failures**, when renewals failed, with how many **Failed** and how many are **Recoverable**. Click it to see **Why they failed**. On a later day, this card is **Projected loss**.
- **Recommended actions**: what you can do about the day. See the tasks below.

**Subscriptions** lists the day's renewals, up to 100 at a time:

- **Status filters**: **All**, then whichever of **Failed**, **Retrying**, **At risk**, **Scheduled** and **Cleared** the day has. Pick one at a time. **Failed** includes renewals already queued for a retry; **Retrying** shows only those.
- **Search**: the magnifying glass searches by customer or product.
- **Rows**: each row shows the customer, the product and the amount. A yellow pill gives the reason a renewal is at risk, for example **Repeated declines**. A blue pill shows a booked retry, for example **Retry 14:30**. **asked to update card** means the customer was recently sent a payment update email. **Retryable** marks a failed payment that **Retry now** can charge.
- **Pages**: if the day has more than 100 renewals, use the arrows at the bottom to move between pages.

Click a customer to open the renewal's details: why it's at risk, the decline reason, the amount, product and market, what the customer has paid to date, past failed charges, the last attempt, any scheduled retry and the customer's **Lifetime value**. **Couldn't be billed** with **Not a card decline** means the payment failed before it reached a payment gateway. **View full subscription** opens the subscription.

### Analytics

**Analytics** reports on the whole month: a summary (**Cleared first time**, **Revenue to date**, **Renewals** and **Failure rate**), **Revenue by day**, **When charges clear** by time of day and time of month, **Orders by market**, **Where failures concentrate** and **Top failure reasons**.

## Find a day's failed renewals

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to Recovery">
    On **Calendar**, choose **Recovery**. Each day shows the money you could still recover. Look for days with a red or amber dot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the day">
    Click the day. The panel opens on **Overview**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See why payments failed">
    Click **Lost to failures** to see **Why they failed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="List the failed renewals">
    Open **Subscriptions** and choose **Failed**. Click a customer to see the renewal's details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Retry failed payments

**Retry now** and **Schedule** charge failed renewals again, on the payment method already on file. They only reach payments that failed in a way a retry can fix. A hard decline needs a new payment method, so it isn't retried. **Retry now** also skips renewals that already have a retry queued.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from the day's overview">
    In **Recommended actions**, click **Retry now** or **Schedule retries**. These cards appear when the day has payments a retry can fix. The panel switches to **Subscriptions**, with the retryable renewals on the first page selected. You can also tick rows yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the selection">
    The bar at the bottom of the list appears once you select a row. It shows how many are selected and, when some can't be retried or scheduled, how many each action can reach. If the day has more than one page, only the rows on the page you're on are included. Turning a page, changing the status filter or searching clears your selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry now">
    Click **Retry now**. The dialog shows how many payments it covers and, if they share a currency, their total. Click **Retry N payments** (for example **Retry 12 payments**) to queue them to be charged again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or pick a time">
    Click **Schedule**. Choose **In 4 hours** (the default), **In 8 hours**, **In 12 hours** or **In 20 hours**, or set **Retry at**. The time must be at least 5 minutes and at most 23 hours away, in your own time zone. Nothing is charged now. Click **Schedule N retries** to book it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    The dialog shows how many renewals were queued or scheduled, and names any that weren't, with the reason. Click **Done**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a renewal you schedule already has a retry booked, scheduling moves it to the new time instead of adding a second retry. The dialog names the existing booking first. When every selected renewal is already booked, the button reads **Move N retries**.

You can't close a retry dialog while it's sending.

<Warning>
  If a retry dialog shows **Retry status unknown** or **Scheduling status unknown**, don't retry straight away. Click **Reload the day** and check the renewals first. Retrying again can charge the same card twice.
</Warning>

## Email customers about a payment

**Recommended actions** offers two emails. Each opens a confirmation that says how many customers it will email.

- **Payment failed email**: for renewals whose charge ran and failed, including those queued for a retry. It tells the customer the charge didn't go through and asks them to update their payment method.
- **Payment update email**: for renewals that haven't run yet and are flagged at risk. It asks the customer to update their payment method before the renewal runs. Nothing is charged. It skips customers who were asked recently; their rows show **asked to update card**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the email">
    Open the day on **Calendar**. In **Recommended actions**, click **Payment failed email** or **Payment update email**. Each card shows how many of the day's renewals it applies to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide about resends (Payment failed email only)">
    **Payment failed email** skips customers the automatic dunning emails already wrote to, and customers already sent this email in the last day. To include them, tick **Also tell customers who have already been told**. This also waives the one-day quiet period, so some customers hear about the same charge twice. It's off each time the dialog opens. Only tick this the first time you send this email for a day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Check the number on the **Email N customers** button before you send. That number is how many get the email. Then click it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the results">
    The dialog shows how many emails were sent, and how many customers weren't emailed, with the reasons. Click **Done**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a **Send status unknown** notice appears, close the dialog with **Cancel**, reload the day and check before you send again. Sending again can email the same customer twice.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Recommended actions or the selection boxes?" id="actions-missing">
    Retrying payments and emailing customers need **Full access** for **Subscriptions**, or **Custom** access with **Edit subscription details, pause, or cancel** turned on, in the **Commerce** card on the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen. Without it, the screen is read-only. **Recommended actions** also appears only when the day has failed or at-risk renewals.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Retry now greyed out?" id="retry-now-disabled">
    None of the selected rows is a payment that **Retry now** can charge: it cleared, it hasn't run yet, it was a hard decline, or a retry is already queued. **Schedule** can still move a retry that's already booked for a set time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
