Use the **Themes** screen to manage your storefront's look. One theme is live at a time. The others wait in your library until you publish one.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Website**, select **Theme**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Theme**. To import, add, customize, publish, rename, upgrade or delete themes, it needs **Full access**. To add or edit global themes, it also needs **Full access** for **Global Themes**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Theme** and **Global Themes** are in the **Content & Website** card. Open the card and set access from each area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

At the top of the page:

- **View your site** opens your storefront in a new tab. It's grayed out until your company's signup email is confirmed.
- The three-dot button next to it holds **Clear Cache** and **Connect from GitHub**. It appears only if your role has the **Theme** switch **Customize and edit theme settings**.

### Current theme

The first card shows a desktop-sized and a phone-sized preview of your storefront, with your current theme's row below them. Until your signup email is confirmed, the previews say **Your store preview appears once you confirm your email.**

### Theme Library

**Theme Library** lists your company's themes, including the current one. Other themes in the library aren't live until you publish one.

- Use the search box to filter the list.
- **Import** uploads a theme from a ZIP file. It appears only if your role has the **Theme** switch **Install new themes**.
- The list loads 10 themes at a time. Click **Load More** for the next 10.

### Theme rows

The current theme card and the library show each theme as a row with its thumbnail, name, **Version** and **Last save** date and time. Two badges can appear:

- **Current theme**: the theme your storefront uses.
- **Outdated**: you can upgrade the theme with **Upgrade**.

A row can also show:

- **Customize**: opens the theme's home page in the [Page Editor](/help/admin/page-editor). If the theme has no default home page template, it opens the theme's code editor instead.
- **Publish**: on draft themes in the library. See [Publish a theme](#publish-a-theme).
- **Importing**: the theme is still importing.
- **Error Importing Theme**: the theme's import failed.

The row's three-dot button, hidden while the theme imports, has:

- **Preview**: opens your storefront with this theme in a new tab. It isn't offered for the current theme.
- **Open in Editor**: opens the theme's code editor in a new tab.
- **Upgrade**: on outdated themes. In the **Upgrade Theme** dialog, click **Upgrade**. The dialog warns that you may lose your changes to the theme.
- **Rename**: turns the name into a text box. Press Enter or click away to save, or press Esc to cancel.
- **Download**: saves the theme as a ZIP file named after the theme.
- **Delete**: on draft themes in the library and on themes whose import failed. In the **Delete Theme** dialog, click **Delete**.
- **Copy Preview URL**: copies a link that opens the theme's preview.

**Customize**, **Publish**, **Open in Editor**, **Upgrade** and **Rename** appear only with the **Customize and edit theme settings** switch, and **Delete** only with **Remove installed themes**. **Preview** and **Copy Preview URL** don't appear until your signup email is confirmed.

### Fluid Studios

This banner offers Fluid's design and marketing services. **Let's get started** opens a scheduling page in a new tab.

### Trending Themes

**Trending Themes** lists ready-made themes you can add to your library. Each card shows the theme's name and version, with an **Add** button. Click **Load More** to see more. This section appears only if your role has the **Install new themes** switch.

### Global Themes

The last card's title is your company's name followed by **Global Themes**. It lists the global themes your company has created, each with its name, version and a status such as **Draft** or **Published**. **View Demo** opens a theme's demo, when it has one.

**Add Global Theme** creates a global theme, and a card's **Edit** opens it. Both open the global theme's own screen. **Add Global Theme** appears only with the **Install new themes** switch.

## Tasks

### Publish a theme

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the theme">
    In **Theme Library**, find the draft theme you want. Use the search box or **Load More** if you need to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview it">
    Optional: open the row's three-dot button and choose **Preview** to see the theme on your storefront.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**. A **Published successfully!** message appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Publishing takes effect right away, with no confirmation. The theme becomes your current theme, and the theme that was current becomes a draft in your library.

### Import a theme from a ZIP file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the import">
    In **Theme Library**, click **Import**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the ZIP file">
    Click **Choose Files**, pick the theme's `.zip` file and confirm it. You can import one file at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the theme">
    In the **Import Theme** dialog, **Theme Name** starts as the file's name. Change it if you like. It can't be empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    Click **Import Theme**. A message confirms that the import has started.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The import runs in the background. When it finishes, the theme is a draft in your library. If it fails, its row shows **Error Importing Theme**, and you can delete the theme.

### Add a theme from Trending Themes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a theme">
    In **Trending Themes**, find the theme you want. Click **Load More** to see more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Click **Add**. A **Theme import started!** message appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The theme imports into your library in the background and goes through the same statuses as a ZIP import.

### Connect a theme from GitHub

**Connect from GitHub** imports a theme from a branch of a GitHub repository. Click the three-dot button at the top of the screen and choose **Connect from GitHub**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a GitHub account">
    Under **GitHub Account**, select an account. To add one, click **Connect to GitHub**, or **Add Another Account** if one is already connected. In the GitHub window that opens, choose which repositories to grant access to. If your browser blocks the window, allow pop-ups for the admin and try again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the repository">
    Under **Select Repository**, search for the repository and select it. Each one is marked **Private** or **Public**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the branch">
    Under **Choose Branch**, select a branch. The repository's default branch, marked **default**, is selected for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. A message confirms that the theme is connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The theme imports into your library in the background, named after the repository.

**Connect from GitHub** appears only if your role also has the **GitHub** switch **View GitHub integration status**. Adding a GitHub account also needs **Configure GitHub integration**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **GitHub** is in the **Settings** card; set it from the **GitHub** area's menu, not the card's. If a **Failed to connect GitHub account** message appears, check your role's access to **GitHub**.

### Clear the cache

Click the three-dot button at the top of the screen and choose **Clear Cache**. It clears the cache for every page of your storefront. The item reads **Clearing Cache** while it works, and a message confirms when it's done.

To clear the cache, your role also needs the **Developer** switch **Manage webhooks and API configuration (Callbacks, Developer, Webhooks)**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Developer** is in the **Settings** card; set it from the **Developer** area's menu, not the card's. If a **Failed to clear cache** message appears, check your role's access to **Developer**.

## Tips

<Tip>
  Before you upgrade a theme, use **Download** to save a copy of it.
</Tip>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I import a theme?" id="import-a-theme">
    You can add a theme to your library in three ways:

    - Upload a ZIP file with **Import**. See [Import a theme from a ZIP file](#import-a-theme-from-a-zip-file).
    - Add a ready-made theme from **Trending Themes**. See [Add a theme from Trending Themes](#add-a-theme-from-trending-themes).
    - Import a branch of a GitHub repository. See [Connect a theme from GitHub](#connect-a-theme-from-github).

    Imported themes start as drafts. Customize and preview the theme, then publish it when it's ready.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I delete a theme?" id="cannot-delete-theme">
    **Delete** appears only on draft themes in **Theme Library** and on themes whose import failed. You can't delete your current theme. To remove it, publish another theme first. The old theme becomes a draft that you can then delete.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is View your site grayed out?" id="view-your-site-disabled">
    Your company's signup email isn't confirmed yet. Until it is, **View your site** is grayed out, the previews show a message instead of your storefront, and **Preview** and **Copy Preview URL** don't appear in the theme menus. Confirm the email to turn them on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Import, Customize or Trending Themes?" id="missing-theme-actions">
    Your role can view themes but doesn't have **Full access** for **Theme**. Without the **Install new themes** switch, **Import**, **Trending Themes** and **Add Global Theme** don't appear. Without **Customize and edit theme settings**, **Customize**, **Publish** and the three-dot button at the top of the screen don't appear. Ask an admin who manages roles to give your role **Full access** for **Theme**. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
