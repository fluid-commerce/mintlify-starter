The Droplets screen is the droplet marketplace. Use it to browse droplets, install the ones you want, and manage the droplets you've installed.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Droplets**, select **Marketplace**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Droplets**. To install droplets, change their permissions, uninstall them or use **New Droplet**, it needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Droplets** is in the **Tools & Messaging** card. Open the card and set access from the **Droplets** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

The page header reads **Droplets**. If your role has **Full access** for **Droplets**, the header also has **New Droplet**. **New Droplet**, and the **Edit** button on a droplet your company built (also **Full access**), are for building and publishing your own droplet. See [Build and publish a Fluid droplet](/guides/creating-droplets).

### Fluid Connect

The **Fluid Connect** section at the top holds the back-office connector droplets: ByDesign, InfoTrax, Exigo and Pillars. It appears only when at least one of them is available. When there are more cards than fit, scroll through them with the arrow buttons. On a wide screen they appear when you point at the section; on a narrow screen, such as a phone, they're always shown. Click a card to open the connector's details.

These droplets also appear in the **Droplets** list below. To connect one to your back office and manage its sync, see [Back-office connectors](/help/admin/droplets/integrations).

### Droplets list

The **Droplets** section shows the marketplace's droplets as cards. Droplets your company has installed come first.

- The search box, which reads **Search droplets...**, matches a droplet's name, its short summary, its publisher or its category names.
- Below the search box, category chips filter the list. **All** shows every droplet, and each chip shows how many droplets are in that category. Click a chip to show only its droplets.

If nothing matches your search or category, the section shows **No droplets found**.

Each card shows:

- The droplet's logo and title, and **by** followed by the publisher's name.
- **Installed**, with a check mark, if your company has installed the droplet.
- Up to three lines of summary, or **No description yet.** if the droplet has none.
- For a droplet built with Mist, a **Mist app:** line with the Mist app's name and its slug in brackets, and a **Mist** badge.
- A category badge: the droplet's first category, or the category you've filtered by. If the droplet has more categories, a badge such as **+2** shows how many more; point at it to see them all.
- **Theme blocks**, if the droplet adds blocks to your theme editor.

Click a card to open the droplet's details.

### A droplet's details

The page header shows the droplet's name. On a droplet your company hasn't installed, the header also has **Install** if your role has **Full access** for **Droplets**. For a droplet built with Mist, the header also has **Mist app**. It opens **Built with Mist**, which shows the **Mist app** name and the **Mist app ID**. Click the ID to copy it.

Until you install a droplet, its screen shows its marketplace listing. At the top are the droplet's logo and name, **by** and the publisher, a short summary, and its categories. Below them, the main column has:

- Screenshots and videos, one at a time with its name. Use the arrows or the strip of thumbnails to move between them. Videos play in place, and you can open an image full screen.
- **Overview**: the droplet's description, followed by **Features**.

A panel beside the main column, or below it on small screens, has:

- **Developer**: the company that publishes the droplet.
- **Permissions**: each permission the droplet asks for, with a short description, or **Asks for no access to your store's data.** See [Droplet permissions](#droplet-permissions).
- **Network access**, if the droplet connects to external systems, such as your back office, through Fluid's network.
- **Theme blocks**, if the droplet adds blocks to your theme editor, with its version when it has one.
- **Theme extension status**, only on a droplet your company built, while its theme blocks aren't published yet.

## Install a droplet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the droplet">
    In the **Droplets** list or the **Fluid Connect** section, click the droplet's card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Install">
    Click **Install** in the page header. An install page titled **Install** followed by the droplet's name opens. Every droplet installs from this page, so one click never installs a droplet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review what the droplet can access">
    The page shows your store and the droplet, the droplet's developer, and the permissions it asks for, each with a short description. If it asks for none, you see **No store data**. It also shows **Network access** and **Theme blocks** when they apply. You grant all of the permissions or none of them. To grant less, don't install the droplet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the install">
    Click **Install**. The button reads **Installing...** while the droplet installs. You see **Droplet installed successfully**, and the page shows the droplet's own screen. To back out instead, click **Cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your company has already installed the droplet, the install page says so and shows **Open** followed by the droplet's name. Click it to go to the droplet.

If the install fails, you see **Error installing droplet**.

### Droplet permissions

The standard permissions a droplet can ask for, and that you can grant or remove in **Edit Permissions**:

| Permission | Description on screen |
|:---|:---|
| **Main** | Access to core application features and dashboard |
| **Prospects** | View and manage prospect records |
| **Members** | View and manage member profiles and data |
| **Web** | Access to web content and page management |
| **Settings** | Read and modify application settings |
| **Products** | View and manage product catalog |
| **Orders** | View and manage customer orders |
| **Users** | View and manage user accounts |
| **Website** | Access to website configuration and content |
| **Header Menus** | Manage header navigation menus |
| **Payments** | Access to payment processing and transactions |

## Open an installed droplet

In the sidebar, under **Droplets**, each installed droplet is listed below **Marketplace** with its logo. Click the droplet's name to open it. A name longer than 16 characters is cut short; point at it to see the full name. You can also click the droplet's card in the **Droplets** list, where it shows **Installed**.

An installed droplet's screen shows the droplet's own screen below the page header, in place of the marketplace listing. If your role has **Full access** for **Droplets**, the page header has a three-dot menu with **Edit Permissions** and **Uninstall**.

If the droplet's screen shows **This app couldn’t connect to Fluid. Reload to try again.**, reload the page.

## Change a droplet's permissions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the droplet">
    In the sidebar, under **Droplets**, click the droplet's name, or click its card in the **Droplets** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Edit Permissions">
    In the page header, open the three-dot menu and choose **Edit Permissions**. The **Edit Permissions** panel opens on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check or uncheck permissions">
    The panel lists the permissions you can grant, and the ones the droplet has now are checked. Check a permission to grant it, or uncheck it to remove it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the change">
    Click **Save**. It's available once you've changed something, and reads **Saving...** while it works. You see **Permissions updated successfully**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

As the panel says, changes take effect immediately after saving. To close the panel without saving, click **Cancel**. If the save fails, you see **Failed to update permissions**.

## Uninstall a droplet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the droplet">
    In the sidebar, under **Droplets**, click the droplet's name, or click its card in the **Droplets** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Uninstall">
    In the page header, open the three-dot menu and choose **Uninstall**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The **Uninstall Droplet?** dialog asks whether you're sure you want to uninstall the droplet, by name. Click **Uninstall**, or click **Cancel** to keep the droplet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You see **Droplet disconnected successfully**. The droplet's installation is removed: its screen shows the marketplace listing and **Install** again, and its card no longer shows **Installed**. You can install it again later. If the uninstall fails, you see **Error disconnecting droplet**.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Install or the three-dot menu?" id="install-or-menu-missing">
    **Install** shows only on a droplet your company hasn't installed; on an installed droplet, the three-dot menu takes its place. Installing a droplet, and the three-dot menu, need **Full access** for **Droplets**. Ask an admin who manages roles to edit your role: on the role's **Permissions** tab, open the **Tools & Messaging** card, then set **Droplets** to **Full access**. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why isn't the Fluid Connect section showing?" id="fluid-connect-missing">
    The section appears only when at least one of the ByDesign, InfoTrax, Exigo or Pillars connector droplets is available. Other droplets are in the **Droplets** list.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
