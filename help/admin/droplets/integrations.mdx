A back-office connector keeps Fluid in step with your ByDesign, InfoTrax, Exigo or Pillars system. On its screen you connect the two, choose what syncs, and then follow each sync and fix the records that didn't go through.

Which tabs, cards and settings you see depends on the connector. If your connector only sends data out of Fluid, the screen says export where it would say import: for example, **Import preferences** reads **Export preferences**.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Droplets**, select **Marketplace**, then click your connector's card.

The connector's screen appears once its droplet is installed. Until then, the card opens the droplet's marketplace listing, where **Install** adds it. See [Droplets](/help/admin/droplets).

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Droplets**. Installing the connector's droplet needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Droplets** is in the **Tools & Messaging** card.
</Note>

## Connect your back office

A connector that isn't connected yet opens on **Credentials**. The fields depend on the connector:

- **Name for your integration**: at least 3 characters.
- **Company**: if your connector asks for it.
- **Type**: **Production**, the default, or **Sandbox**. Some connectors, such as Exigo, also ask for a **Sandbox ID** when you choose **Sandbox**.
- **API Credentials**: depending on the connector, an **API URL** (if the connector asks for it), plus either a **Username** and **Password** or an **API Token**. The eye button shows or hides what you typed.
- **Database Credentials**: if the connector also reads your back-office database, its **Data Server**, **Database**, **Username** and **Password**. The section lists the addresses to allow on your database server.

If the screen shows **Setting up your company…**, wait a few moments for the form to appear.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the connector">
    Open the connector's screen as described in [Where to find it](#where-to-find-it).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the connection details">
    Enter a name for the connection and the details your connector asks for. Fields marked with an asterisk are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    Click **Test Connection**. **Testing Connection...** shows while Fluid checks your credentials. If they work, you see **Integration connected successfully!**. If not, you see **Error testing connection:** followed by the reason. Correct your details and try again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the connection to start">
    The screen reads **Please wait while the integration is being initialized…** and checks again every few seconds. When it's ready, **Integration Connected!** appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue">
    If your connector has setup steps, **What's next?** names them. Click **Continue to Setup**. Otherwise, click **View Integration** to open the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Finish setup

Setup has up to two steps after **Connect**, depending on the connector: **Import Preferences** and **Data Mapping**. Click **Import Preferences** or **Data Mapping** at the top to move between setup steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose what to import">
    On **Import Preferences**, switch on the record types to sync, such as **Orders**, **Products** and **Customers**. Only the types your connector supports appear. Each card lists what it includes. Under **Sync Direction**, choose **Import**, **Bidirectional** or **Export**. Only the directions your connector supports appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your mappings">
    Click **Save & Continue**. On **Data Mapping**, use **Sections** to jump between mapping sections and check how your back office's values match Fluid's. **Export** saves your mappings as a JSON file. **Import** loads a saved file and, once you confirm with **Replace All**, deletes every existing mapping and replaces it with the file's. This can't be undone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete setup">
    Click **Complete Setup**. Fluid saves your choices and opens the connector's dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The dashboard

The header shows the connector's name and the direction data flows (point at it for details). If a banner says **Setup hasn't finished — this integration isn't syncing yet.**, click **Retry setup**. If the banner doesn't clear, check your credentials.

The tabs, in screen order, if your connector uses them:

- **Overview**: sync health for the range you pick, the **Needs attention** card and the activity log. See [Check sync health](#check-sync-health).
- **Reconciliation**: compares Fluid with your back office by external ID. Each record type shows **In Fluid**, the count in your back office and the **Difference**. **Check now** refreshes the counts; it doesn't sync or repair records. When a record type has differences, **View records** lists the affected records, and you can download them as a CSV file. See [Check reconciliation](#check-reconciliation).
- **Import preferences**: what syncs and in which direction. See [Change what syncs](#change-what-syncs).
- **Data Mapping**: how your back office's values match Fluid's, grouped by record type, such as Customers, Products and Orders, with a **Search mappings…** box. A group can also have a settings card for that record type.
- **Rules**: the rules your connector offers, such as what happens to **Duplicate records** (customers and orders), **Refund orders** and **Products without a warehouse**. Each rule marks its recommended option, and your pick saves as soon as you make it.
- **Settings**: **Edit credentials**, plus any settings your connector supports, such as **Bundle export** for Exigo, which sets how product bundles are written to Exigo orders.
- **Reports**: CSV exports of your sync activity.

## Check sync health

On **Overview**:

- Pick **24h**, **7d** or **30d**. The counts, the chart and the activity log follow the range. The log shows 10 rows a page; use **Next** and **Previous** to move through the most recent events.
- Cards show how many of each record type synced. Click one to filter the activity log to it.
- **Needs attention** has a tile for each kind of problem: **Held** (waiting on a mapping), **Failed**, **Skipped** (filtered by a rule) and **Quarantined** (held for review after repeated failures). When there's nothing to fix, it reads **All clear**.
- The activity log opens on **Needs attention**; **All** adds successful syncs. **Trends** groups errors that repeat, and **Inbox** groups failed, held and skipped syncs by category. In both, expand a group and click **view all** to list its records.
- To find a record, type at least two characters in **Search…** and press Enter. The search covers all time. Press Esc to clear it.
- Click a row to see its details: its Fluid and external IDs, its retries, and the error, missing mapping or rule behind it. In the row's three-dot menu, **Open record in Fluid** opens the record in the admin in a new tab. If the record has no admin link yet, you see **Not linkable yet**.

### What failure groups mean

The **Inbox** groups syncs that failed, were held or were skipped by the reason behind them. Some groups need a different fix:

- **Source data needs fixing**: the record in the system it came from is missing something the other system needs, such as a blank email address, so the sync was skipped on purpose. Replaying it won't help. Correct the record in the system it came from.
- **Rejected by target system**: the receiving system turned the record down as invalid. For example, Exigo can answer `400 Invalid CurrencyCode`. Fix the mapping or the value the error names, then replay the record.
- **Stalled with no job running**: the sync started but nothing is processing it any more.
- Other groups are named after their cause, such as **Missing mapping**.

A group's count is the number of sync events, not the number of different records. One record that failed five times counts five times.

## Fix a held record

A record is held when one of its values has no mapping. Fixing it needs the **Data Mapping** tab, so it's available only if your connector shows it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Fix mappings">
    On **Overview**, in **Needs attention**, click **Fix mappings** on the **Held** tile. The **Fix mappings** panel lists each unmapped value, how many events it's holding, and a suggestion when there is one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the mapping">
    Click **Open mapping**. The **Data Mapping** tab opens at that mapping.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the match">
    Set the value's match in that section. In most sections, you choose it from the value's row menu or click the value after **Suggested:**. The change saves right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replay the held records">
    In the activity log, click the filter button and check **Held**. Check the rows and click **Replay**. You see **Replay started**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Retry a failed record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the failed records">
    On **Overview**, in the activity log, click the filter button and check **Errors**. Click a row to see the error.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replay them">
    Click the **Replay sync** button on the row, or check several rows and click **Replay**. You see **Replay started**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Which records you can replay

A record can be replayed when:

- It failed, was held or was skipped. A record that synced, or is still syncing, can't be replayed.
- It hasn't reached the limit of 10 replays.
- Fluid still has the data it needs to send it again.

Records Fluid sends to your back office can be replayed only when a change in Fluid started the sync. Records the connector's own sync created, rather than a change in Fluid, can't be replayed. Records an admin excluded can't be replayed until they're unexcluded. Records coming into Fluid from your back office can be replayed for customers and orders only.

If you replay several rows, rows that can't be replayed are skipped and the rest start. You see **Replay partially started**, and the dialog lists each skipped row with its reason, such as **Reached the 10 replay limit** or **No stored payload to replay**. If none can be replayed, you see **Nothing to replay**.

## Review quarantined records

Records go to quarantine after their retries run out.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the quarantine queue">
    On **Overview**, in **Needs attention**, click **Review** on the **Quarantined** tile. The queue lists the first eight records; handle them and reopen it to see the rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry or exclude each record">
    Click **Retry now** to queue the record again. To stop a record from ever syncing, click **Exclude**, enter a **Reason** and click **Confirm exclusion**. Right after you confirm, the dialog changes and offers **Unexclude** to undo it. After you close the dialog, the record no longer appears in the queue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check reconciliation

On **Reconciliation**, each record type compares the records in Fluid with the records in your back office and shows a status, such as **Missing in Fluid**, **SKU drift** or **In sync**. For Exigo, products are matched by SKU: Exigo's item code against the product's SKU in Fluid, ignoring case and spaces at either end. Products with a blank or repeated SKU are flagged and never matched.

Reconciliation runs when you click **Check now**. It doesn't run on a schedule yet. **Checking...** shows while it runs. **Last checked** gives the time the screen last loaded the counts.

Saved results older than 15 minutes refresh in the background when you open a record type's records, and you see the previous results until the refresh finishes. If a refresh fails, the screen says **The last refresh didn't complete** and keeps showing the previous results. Click **Check now** to try again.

## Check the connector's webhooks

A connector learns about changes in Fluid through webhooks. To see whether they're arriving, open the connector's screen, click **Actions** in the header, then click **View Health**. In the **Droplet health** panel, **Event webhooks** shows a verdict, such as **Healthy**, **Needs attention** or **Failing**, how many of the connector's webhooks are active, and how many deliveries succeeded, failed or are pending over the last few hours. **View Health** appears only if your role has **View only** or **Full access** for **Developer**, in the **Settings** card on the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen.

For Exigo, Mist can also check the connector itself: test the connection to Exigo, compare the connector's webhook subscriptions with the ones it needs, check your mappings for gaps and look-alike values, and, with your approval, register missing webhooks or turn inactive ones back on. See [Fix a connector with Mist](/help/mist/integration-actions).

## Change what syncs

On **Import preferences**:

- **Sync direction**: import from your back office, **Bidirectional** or export to it, as your connector supports.
- A switch for each record type your connector supports, such as **Orders**, **Products** and **Customers**.
- For Exigo, **Points** syncs Fluid Points with Exigo point accounts. Set up the accounts on **Data Mapping**.

Each change saves as soon as you make it. If **These settings are saved, but nothing is importing yet** appears, the connection isn't live yet. If **Sync is paused — the source changed underneath it** appears, importing has stopped. If either message stays:

## Update credentials

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Edit Credentials">
    On **Settings**, click **Edit credentials**. The dialog opens with your saved details; sensitive values can appear masked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the details">
    Change what you need. Fields marked with an asterisk are required, including **Sandbox ID** if you use a sandbox. **Name for your integration** can't be changed here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update Credentials**. You see **Credentials updated**. If the save fails, you see **Failed to update credentials** with the reason.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Download a sync report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the range">
    On **Reports**, under **Generate sync activity report**, pick a **Start date** and **End date**. They start at the last 7 days. Choose an **Entity type**, or leave **All entity types**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the report">
    Leave **Hide system-skipped and waiting rows (recommended)** checked unless you need those rows, then click **Generate report**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download it">
    Under **Your reports**, the report shows **Preparing…** while it builds. When it's ready, click **Download CSV**. If a report fails, **Failed** appears instead; generate it again. Reports are kept for a limited time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see a tab or setting described here?" id="missing-tab-or-setting">
    Each connector's configuration decides which tabs, cards and settings appear, and some settings exist for only one back office. A tab your connector doesn't use isn't shown.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The setup steps didn't come back. How do I finish setup?" id="finish-setup-later">
    Setup steps show once, right after you connect. If you leave before you complete them, the connector opens on the dashboard next time. Make the same choices on the **Import preferences** and **Data Mapping** tabs.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
