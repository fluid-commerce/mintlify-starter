Use the **Navigation** screen to set up your company's mobile app navigation: the screens on the app's bottom bar, their order, and any custom links. You can create more than one navigation and use access rules to choose which members see each one. Your website's menus are on a separate screen, under **Website** › **Navigation**. For how reps use the app, see [Mobile app](/help/mobile).

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Mobile App**, select **Navigation**.

<Note>
  To open this screen and its editor, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Mobile Pages**. With **View only**, navigations open read-only. To create, edit, publish or delete navigations, it needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Mobile Pages** is in the **Content & Website** card. Open the card and set access from the **Mobile Pages** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

The **New navigation** button is at the top of the page, above the list of your company's navigations. Your default navigation is always in the list, with a **Default** badge. It starts out named **Navigation**. Members who don't match any other navigation see it.

### Navigations list

- The **All**, **Active** and **Inactive** tabs filter the list by status.
- Use the search box to find a navigation by name.
- The sort button sorts by **Name** or **Status**. Until you sort, the newest navigations come first.
- The filter button opens **Filters**: **Status**, **Countries**, **Ranks** and **Languages**. The last three find navigations whose access rules include any value you pick. Click **Apply Filters**, or **Reset All** to clear the filters.

The list has these columns:

- **Name**: the navigation's name.
- **Status**: **Active** or **Inactive**. Members only see active navigations.
- **Audience**: a chip for each kind of access rule (**Member Type**, **Country**, **Language** or **Rank**), with its values. Countries show as flags. **All members** means the navigation has no access rules.
- **Items**: how many items the navigation has, including hidden ones.

Click a navigation to open it in the editor. Each row's actions menu has **Edit**, **Publish** or **Unpublish**, and **Delete**. The default navigation's menu has only **Edit**.

### Navigation editor

The editor fills the screen. Its top bar has a close button (X), the navigation's name, a settings (gear) button and **Save**. Below are the item list, a phone preview and the **Settings** panel. To close **Settings**, click its X. The gear button opens it again.

**Item list.** Items are the screens and links you can put on the app's bottom bar:

- System screens: **Home**, **Share**, **Messaging**, **Contacts** and **Shop**. They're part of the app. You can hide and show them, but you can't rename them, change their icons or delete them.
- Custom links: web pages you add, marked **Custom**. They open inside the app.

The list has two sections:

- **In navigation**: the items on the bottom bar. Top to bottom here is left to right on the bar. The bar shows up to six items. With more than six, the first five stay on the bar and the rest go to a **More** menu, which starts at the **More menu** divider.
- **Hidden**: items that aren't on the bar. Hidden system screens stay here so you can show them again.

**Phone preview.** The preview shows the bottom bar as icons, with the previewed screen's icon in your company's brand color. Click an icon, or an item in **In navigation**, to preview that screen. The three-dot icon opens the **More** menu.

- **Home**, **Share** and **Shop** show a published page from your Home Tabs, Share Tabs or Shop Tabs, with its top tabs.
- **Messaging** and **Contacts** show sample screens with a **Built into the app** notice. The content isn't your company's data, and you can't change it here.
- A custom link shows its web page. Some sites can't be previewed here but still open normally in the app.

**Settings panel.** With no item selected, **Settings** shows the navigation's settings:

- **Name**: required.
- **Active**: when it's on, members who match the access rules see this navigation. The default navigation always stays active.
- **Access rules**: who sees the navigation. See [Choose who sees a navigation](#choose-who-sees-a-navigation). The default navigation applies to all members and can't have access rules.

**Item settings.** Click an item in the list to see its settings, and **Done** to go back. For a system screen:

- **Icon**: the icon when the screen isn't selected (**Inactive**) and when it is (**Active**). The app sets these icons.
- **Content**: for Home, Share and Shop, a button such as **Edit home page content** opens [Home Tabs](/help/admin/mobile/home), [Share Tabs](/help/admin/mobile/share) or [Shop Tabs](/help/admin/mobile/shop). Build those screens' pages there, including the tabs across the top. For Messaging and Contacts, a link opens the [Messaging](/help/admin/messages) or [Contacts](/help/admin/contacts) screen. If you have unsaved changes, you're asked to save them first.

For a custom link:

- **Label**: the link's name in the app. Required.
- **URL**: the page to open. Enter a full address that starts with `https://` or `http://`, such as `https://example.com/rewards`.
- **Open in full screen**: off by default. When it's on, the link opens as a full-screen page instead of inside a tab.
- **Icon**: to change the link icon, click **Change icon** and pick one.

If someone else saved the navigation after you opened it, saving opens a **Someone saved newer changes** message. Click **Reload** to get their version, then make your changes again.

## Tasks

### Create a navigation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new navigation">
    Click **New navigation**. Fluid creates an inactive navigation named **New navigation**, with a copy of the default navigation's items, and opens it in the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    In **Settings**, change the **Name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who sees it">
    Add at least one access rule. You can't save without one. See [Choose who sees a navigation](#choose-who-sees-a-navigation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the items">
    Show, hide, order or add items. See [Change the items](#change-the-items).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Turn on **Active** when you want members to see it. Leave it off to keep the navigation as a draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The confirmation message says your changes appear when members next open the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The new navigation stays in the list even if you close the editor without saving. Delete it if you don't need it.

### Change the items

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the navigation">
    In the list, click the navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show or hide items">
    Use the switch on an item's row, or drag the item between **In navigation** and **Hidden**. Items you show again go to the end of **In navigation**. Home can't be hidden: it's the app's landing screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the order">
    Drag items by the handle on the left of their rows to reorder them in **In navigation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a custom link">
    Click **Add custom link**. A link named **Custom link** appears at the end of **In navigation**, with its settings open. Enter its **Label** and **URL**, and set **Open in full screen** and **Icon** if you need them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove a custom link, select it, click **Delete**, then click **Remove link**. It's removed when you save. System screens can only be hidden.

### Choose who sees a navigation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the navigation's settings">
    Open the navigation. If an item's settings are showing, click **Done**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a rule">
    Under **Access rules**, click **Add rule** and choose **Rank**, **Country**, **Language** or **Member Type**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the values">
    In the rule's card, select one or more values.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add more rules if you need them">
    Each rule gets its own card, joined by **And**. To remove a rule, click the X on its card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

How Fluid chooses each member's navigation:

- A member can see an active navigation if they match every rule card. Within a card, any one selected value is enough. For example, a **Country** rule for Canada and a **Language** rule for French reach members in Canada whose language is French.
- If a member matches more than one navigation, the most specific wins. A **Language** rule counts most, then **Country**, then **Rank**, then **Member Type**. Between equally specific navigations, the most recently updated one wins.
- Members who match no other navigation see the default navigation.

### Publish or unpublish a navigation

Set the navigation's access rules in the editor before you publish it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the actions menu">
    In the list, open the navigation's actions menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Publish or Unpublish">
    Choose **Publish** to make the navigation active, or **Unpublish** to make it inactive. A confirmation message appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also turn **Active** on or off in the editor's **Settings** and click **Save**. When you publish a navigation, or save one that's active, any other published navigation with exactly the same access rules is unpublished. You can't unpublish the default navigation.

### Delete a navigation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    In the list, choose **Delete** from the navigation's actions menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Delete Navigation?** dialog, click **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can't undo this. Members who saw the navigation then get the next one that matches them, or the default navigation. You can't delete the default navigation.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I save?" id="cannot-save">
    **Save** stays grayed out until you change something. It also stays off while the **Name** is empty, while a navigation other than the default has no access rules, or while a custom link has no **Label** or no valid **URL**. Click **Save** anyway to open the setting that needs fixing.

    If there's no **Save** button, your role doesn't have **Full access** for **Mobile Pages**. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't members see my navigation?" id="members-dont-see-navigation">
    Check that:

    - The navigation is **Active**. Publishing or saving another active navigation with exactly the same access rules unpublishes it.
    - The members match every access rule card.
    - No more specific navigation matches them. See [Choose who sees a navigation](#choose-who-sees-a-navigation).

    Saved changes appear when members next open the app.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
