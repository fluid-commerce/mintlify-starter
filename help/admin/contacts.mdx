Use the **Contacts** screen to keep your company's contact records up to date: each person's name, contact details and status, and the rep they're assigned to.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **People**, select **Contacts**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Contacts**. **View only** lets you search the list, open contacts and export them. To add, edit or delete contacts, your role needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Contacts** is in the **People** card. Open the card and set access from the **Contacts** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

The **Export** and **Add Contact** buttons are at the top of the page. Below them is the list of contacts.

Use the search box above the list to find contacts by first name, last name, email or phone number. If no contacts match, **No contacts found** appears. More contacts load as you scroll down.

The list has these columns:

- **ID**: the contact's ID number.
- **Name**: the contact's full name.
- **Email** and **Phone**: the contact's email address and phone number.
- **Status**: the contact's status, such as **New** or **Lead**.

A dash means the contact has no value in that column.

Click a contact to open its page. Each row's actions menu has **Edit**, which also opens the contact's page, and **Delete**.

To select contacts, check the boxes at the start of their rows. The checkbox in the table header selects every contact loaded so far. When you select contacts, the header shows how many, such as **3 selected**, and its actions menu has a delete option for them, such as **Delete 3 rows**.

On small screens, the list shows each contact's name, email and status, with no checkboxes or actions menus. Tap a contact to open it.

## Tasks

### Add a contact

The **New Contact** page has the **Assign Rep** card at the top, followed by the contact's fields:

- **First Name** and **Last Name**: required.
- **Email**: optional. If you enter one, it must be a valid email address.
- **Phone**
- **Status**: **New**, **Active**, **Inactive**, **Cold**, **Lead** or **Customer**. A new contact starts as **New**.
- **Full Address**, **City**, **State/Province** and **Postal Code**
- **Country**: choose the country from the list.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new contact">
    On the **Contacts** screen, click **Add Contact**. The **New Contact** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a rep">
    This step is optional. In the **Assign Rep** card, type a rep's name or email, then select the rep from the results. Only reps appear in the results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Enter the **First Name** and **Last Name**, and fill in any other fields you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the contact">
    Click **Add Contact** at the top of the page. A confirmation message appears and the contact's page opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If **First Name** or **Last Name** is empty, a message appears under the field and the contact isn't added. To leave without adding the contact, click **Cancel**.

### Edit a contact

A contact's page is titled with the contact's name. On the left, the **Contact Information** card shows the contact's saved name, status, email, phone and address, and the **Assign Rep** card is below it. On the right are the same fields as on the **New Contact** page. **Save** and an actions menu are at the top of the page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact">
    In the list, click the contact, or choose **Edit** from its actions menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change the fields you need. **Save** stays grayed out until you change something.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. A confirmation message appears and you stay on the contact's page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If **First Name** or **Last Name** is empty when you click **Save**, a **Please fix the form errors before saving** message appears and nothing is saved.

### Assign or change a rep

The **Assign Rep** card shows the rep the contact is assigned to, with **Clear** and **Edit** buttons. If the contact has no rep, the card shows a search box instead.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact">
    In the list, click the contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the rep">
    In the **Assign Rep** card, click **Edit** if the contact already has a rep. Type the rep's name or email, then select the rep from the results. To keep the current rep instead, click **Back**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** at the top of the page. The new rep isn't saved until you do.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove the contact's rep, click **Clear** in the **Assign Rep** card, then click **Save**.

### Export contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the export">
    On the **Contacts** screen, click **Export**. The **Export Contacts** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the export">
    Click **Export** in the dialog. A message says your file will be emailed shortly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the file">
    Fluid emails you a message titled "Your Contacts export is ready". Click **Download** in the email to get the CSV file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each row in the file is a contact, with the contact's avatar URL, first name, last name, email, phone and status.

### Delete contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    In the list, choose **Delete** from the contact's actions menu. To delete several contacts, select their rows and choose the delete option from the table header's actions menu, such as **Delete 3 rows**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Delete contact?** dialog, click **Delete**. A confirmation message appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also delete a contact from its page. Open the actions menu at the top of the page and choose **Delete**. In the **Delete Contact** dialog, click **Delete**. You return to the contacts list.

<Warning>
  Deleting a contact can't be undone.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I add a new contact?" id="how-do-i-add-a-new-contact">
    On the **Contacts** screen, click **Add Contact**. Enter the contact's **First Name** and **Last Name**, which are required, and any other details, such as **Email**, **Phone**, **Status**, the address and **Country**. To assign the contact to a rep, choose the rep in the **Assign Rep** card. Then click **Add Contact**. See [Add a contact](#add-a-contact).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I manage contacts and leads?" id="how-do-i-manage-contacts-and-leads">
    Use each contact's **Status** and rep. **Status** can be **New**, **Active**, **Inactive**, **Cold**, **Lead** or **Customer**, and the list's **Status** column shows it for every contact. To update a contact, open it, change **Status** or the rep in the **Assign Rep** card, and click **Save**. See [Edit a contact](#edit-a-contact) and [Assign or change a rep](#assign-or-change-a-rep).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I add, edit or delete contacts?" id="cannot-add-edit-or-delete">
    Adding, editing and deleting contacts needs **Full access** for **Contacts**. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
