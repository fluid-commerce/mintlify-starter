Use the **Announcements** screen to post news for your reps and let them know about it with a push notification. To show announcements in your mobile app, add the **Announcements** widget to a Home tab, as described in [Home Tabs](/help/admin/mobile/home).

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Tools**, select **Announcements**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Announcements**. With **View only**, you can open announcements but not change them. To add, edit or delete announcements, or send notifications, your role needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Announcements** is in the **Tools & Messaging** card. Open the card and set access from the **Announcements** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

The **Add Announcement** button is at the top of the page. Below it is the list of announcements.

### Announcements list

- The **All**, **Active** and **Inactive** tabs filter the list by status.
- Use the search box to find an announcement.
- Click the filter button to open **Filters**. Choose a **Status** (**All Statuses**, **Active** or **Inactive**, the same choice as the tabs), then click **Apply Filters**. **Reset All** clears it.

The list has these columns:

- **Title**: the announcement's title.
- **Preview**: a thumbnail of the announcement's media, when it has one.
- **Status**: **Active** or **Inactive**.
- **Date Created**: the date the announcement was created.

The newest announcements are listed first. Click the **Title** or **Date Created** header to sort by that column.

Click an announcement to open it. Each row's actions menu has **Edit** and **Delete**. The table header has a menu for the rows you select, labeled with the count, such as **Delete 3 rows**.

### Announcement page

The announcement page opens when you click **Add Announcement** or an announcement in the list. Its breadcrumb ends in **New Announcement** for a new announcement and **Edit Announcement** for a saved one.

The buttons at the top of the page:

- **Send Notification**: opens the push notification dialog. It's grayed out until the announcement is saved.
- **Delete**: on a saved announcement only.
- **Cancel**: appears when a saved announcement has changes you haven't saved. Click it to put back the saved version.
- **Save**: grayed out until there's something to save.

On the left is the media card, and below it the card with the text:

- **Media**: on a new announcement, this is the **New Announcement Media Upload** card. Click it and choose one file in the window that opens. The card lists the supported formats, MP4, MOV, AVI, JPG, PNG, PDF, PPT and PPTX, and a maximum file size of 35MB. After you choose a file, the card shows the file's name, and **Change** lets you choose a different one. On a saved announcement, the **Preview** card shows the current media instead.
- **Title**: required.
- **Body**: the announcement's text, in an editor with formatting.

On the right is the **Settings** card:

- **Ranks**: choose one or more of your company's ranks.
- **Search Engine Indexing**: **True** or **False**. A new announcement starts at **True**.
- **Allow Comments**: **True** or **False**. A new announcement starts at **True**.
- **Countries**: the countries where the announcement shows. Choose from your company's countries, or leave it empty to show the announcement in all countries.
- **Status**: **Active** or **Inactive**. A new announcement starts as **Inactive**. Only active announcements appear in the **Announcements** widget.

## Tasks

### Create an announcement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new announcement">
    On the **Announcements** screen, click **Add Announcement**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the file">
    Click the **New Announcement Media Upload** card and choose a video, image, PDF or PowerPoint file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the announcement">
    Enter a **Title**. Add **Body** text if you want it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the settings">
    In the **Settings** card, choose **Ranks** and **Countries**, and set **Search Engine Indexing** and **Allow Comments**. Set **Status** to **Active** when you want the announcement to appear in the **Announcements** widget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. A confirmation message appears, and the page reopens as the saved announcement, where you can send a notification.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Save** stays grayed out until you've entered a **Title** and added a file.

### Edit an announcement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the announcement">
    In the list, click the announcement, or choose **Edit** from its actions menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change the fields you need. To replace the file, click **Edit Media** in the **Preview** card, click **Change**, and choose the new file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. A confirmation message appears and you stay on the announcement's page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To discard changes you haven't saved, click **Cancel** at the top of the page.

### Send a push notification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the announcement">
    In the list, click a saved announcement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    Click **Send Notification**. The **Notify Users about updates** dialog opens. It sends a push notification to active, licensed users.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose countries">
    Under **Send a notification to people in**, choose one or more countries. These are separate from the announcement's **Countries** setting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send Push**. A confirmation message appears, and the dialog lists the countries the notification went to. Click **Cancel** to close it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After a notification goes out, **Send Push** stays grayed out for 5 minutes and shows how long ago the notification was sent.

<Tip>
  Before you send a notification, set **Status** to **Active** and save your changes. The notification uses the saved announcement, and only active announcements appear in the **Announcements** widget.
</Tip>

### Delete an announcement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    In the list, choose **Delete** from the announcement's actions menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Delete announcement?** dialog, click **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To delete several announcements, select their rows and choose the delete option, such as **Delete 3 rows**, from the table header's menu. You can also open an announcement and click **Delete** at the top of the page, then **Delete** in the **Delete Announcement?** dialog.

<Warning>
  Deleting an announcement can't be undone.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What does an announcement need before I can save it?" id="what-an-announcement-needs">
    A **Title** and a file: a video, image, PDF or PowerPoint file of up to 35MB. **Body**, **Ranks** and **Countries** are optional. If you leave **Countries** empty, the announcement shows in all countries.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Send Notification grayed out?" id="send-notification-unavailable">
    The announcement isn't saved yet. Click **Save**. The page reopens as the saved announcement, and **Send Notification** becomes available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I click Send Push?" id="send-push-unavailable">
    Choose at least one country first. If a notification for this announcement went out in the last 5 minutes, the button shows how long ago it was sent and stays grayed out until 5 minutes have passed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Add Announcement?" id="no-add-announcement">
    Your role doesn't have **Full access** for **Announcements**. With **View only**, announcements open with their fields grayed out, and **Save**, **Delete** and **Send Notification** don't appear. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
