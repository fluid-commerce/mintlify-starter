Use the **Events** screen to create and manage your company's events. Each event has a title, a description and dates in a time zone you choose, and it can have media, a web link and a location.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Tools**, select **Events**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Events**. To create, edit, duplicate or delete events, it needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Events** is in the **Tools & Messaging** card. Open the card and set access from the **Events** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

The **Create Event** button is at the top of the page. Below it is the list of events. A banner above the list offers the new Events table, which keeps your place in the list as you move around the admin: click **Try it** to use it, or **Switch back** to return.

### Events list

- The **All**, **Active**, **Draft** and **Out of Date** tabs filter the list by status. **Active** shows active events that haven't ended, **Draft** shows inactive events, and **Out of Date** shows active events that have ended.
- Use the search box to find events by title.
- Use the sort menu to sort by **Title** or **Start**, **Ascending** or **Descending**. Until you choose, the list is sorted by title, A to Z.
- Click the filter icon to open **Filters**. Choose a **Country** to show only the events whose **Available Countries** include it, then click **Apply Filters**. While the filter is on, a **1 Filter Applied** label shows. To clear the filter, click the × on that label, or click **Reset All** in **Filters**.

The list has these columns:

- **Title**: the event's title.
- **Status**: **Active**, **Draft** or **Out of Date**, as in the tabs.
- **Notification**: **On** or **Off**, matching the event's **Event Notifications** switch.
- **Start** and **End**: when the event starts and ends, in the event's time zone.

Click an event to open it. Each row's actions menu has **Edit**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**. When you select rows, the table header has a menu that changes their status, labeled with the count, such as **Set 3 events as active** and **Set 3 events as draft**.

### Event page

The event page opens when you click **Create Event** or an event in the list. A new event's page is titled **Create New Event**. A saved event's page is titled **Edit Event**.

The main card holds the event's details:

- **Title**: required.
- **Media**: images for the event. Click **Upload Media**, or **Add More Media** once the event has images, to open the file picker. You can add up to 10 images at a time, and each can be up to 10 MB. To remove an image, point to it and click the × in its corner.
- **Time Zone**: required. The dates and times you enter are in this time zone. A new event is set to UTC until you change it.
- **URL**: a web address for the event, such as a link to an online meeting. Enter the full address, for example `https://meet.example.com/spring-launch`.
- **Location**: where the event takes place.
- **Start Date** and **End Date**: required. **Start Time** and **End Time** are optional. Without a start time, the event starts at the beginning of its start date. Without an end time, it ends at the end of its end date.
- **Description**: required.

The **Settings** card:

- **Available Countries**: the countries the event is available in. Choose from your company's countries.
- **Event Notifications**: a switch, on by default. When it's on, Fluid sends a reminder to reps in the event's **Available Countries** about an hour before an active event starts.
- **Status**: **Active** or **Inactive**. A new event starts as **Active**. In the list, an inactive event shows as **Draft**.

The **Event Preview** card shows the event as you fill in the form, with its start and end, a status (**Upcoming**, **In Progress** or **Concluded**), its URL and its location.

## Tasks

### Create an event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new event">
    On the **Events** screen, click **Create Event**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Enter a **Title** and a **Description**. Choose the **Time Zone**, then set the **Start Date** and **End Date**, and the times if you need them. Add **Media**, a **URL** and a **Location** if you want them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the settings">
    Choose the **Available Countries**. Turn off **Event Notifications** if you don't want the reminder. To save the event without making it active, set **Status** to **Inactive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the event">
    Click **Create Event**. A confirmation message appears and the event's page opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Edit an event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the event">
    In the list, click the event, or choose **Edit** from its actions menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change the fields you need. **Save** is grayed out until you change something. To undo your changes, click **Cancel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. A confirmation message appears and you stay on the event's page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Activate events or set them as draft

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the events">
    In the list, select the rows of the events you want to change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the status">
    Open the menu in the table header and choose **Set 3 events as active** or **Set 3 events as draft**. The number matches the rows you selected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also open an event, change its **Status**, and click **Save**.

### Duplicate an event

In the list, choose **Duplicate** from the event's actions menu. The copy has the same details, countries and images, and its title ends in `(copy)`. It's saved as a draft. When it's ready, open it, set **Status** to **Active**, and click **Save**.

### Delete an event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the event">
    In the list, click the event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    Click **Delete** at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Delete Event?** dialog, click **Delete**. You return to the events list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also choose **Delete** from an event's actions menu in the list.

<Warning>
  Deleting an event can't be undone. If you close the **Delete Event?** dialog without deleting, reload the page before you delete another event from the list.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  In the mobile app's tab editor, the **Calendar** widget shows your active events. To add it to a tab, see [Home Tabs](/help/admin/mobile/home).
</Tip>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why won't my event save?" id="event-wont-save">
    Check that **Title**, **Time Zone**, **Start Date**, **End Date** and **Description** are filled in. A description with only spaces counts as empty.

    The **End Date** can't be before the **Start Date**. If the event starts and ends on the same day and you set both times, the **End Time** must be after the **Start Time**. If you enter a **URL**, it must be a full web address, such as `https://meet.example.com/spring-launch`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does my event show as Draft or Out of Date?" id="draft-or-out-of-date">
    **Draft** means the event's **Status** is **Inactive**. To make it active, open it, set **Status** to **Active**, and click **Save**.

    **Out of Date** means the event is active and its end has passed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Create Event?" id="no-create-event">
    Your role can view events but doesn't have **Full access** for **Events**. Without it, you can open events, but there's no **Save** button. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
