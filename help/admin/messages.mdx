Use the **Messaging** screen to read and answer your conversations, set up channels, and plan messages to send later.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, select **Messaging**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Messaging**. To start conversations, send, schedule or pin messages, add reactions, and create or manage channels, it needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Messaging** is in the **Tools & Messaging** card. Open the card and set access from the **Messaging** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

The screen opens your most recent conversation. Your conversations are listed on the left, grouped in sections. Sections with no conversations are hidden once your conversations have loaded:

- **SMS** and **Emails**: SMS and email conversations sent and received through Fluid.
- **Public Channels**: channels whose members come from filters, which can include anyone in your organization.
- **Private Channels**: channels that only their members can view and post in. The team channels you create are listed here.
- **Broadcast Channels**: channels where only certain members can post. Your company's announcements channel is listed first.
- **Downline**: messages to an entire downline. If you have a downline of your own, your downline conversation is listed first. It's a Pro feature: without Pro, clicking it opens **Unlock Downline Messaging** instead.
- **Bots**: conversations with automated bots.
- **Direct Messages**: messages sent to you, or to a group you're in.

Each section shows up to 10 conversations, then **Show All**. Click a section's name to collapse it. Conversations with unread messages stay visible. A pencil icon marks a conversation with an unsent draft.

At the bottom of the list are a menu button, with **Contacts** and **Scheduled Messages**, and **New**, which starts a message or a channel.

Above an open conversation are **Back** and **Forward**, the conversation's name, and the **More options** menu. The menu has **Pinned Messages** and, in a channel, **Channel Members** and, with **Full access**, **Channel Settings**.

## Send a new message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a message">
    Click **New**, then **Message**. The **New Message** screen opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who gets it">
    In **To:**, start typing a name. Type `@` first to search only people, or `#` to search only channels. You can pick several people or one channel, but not both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and send">
    Type your message and press Enter, or click the send button. If you already have a conversation with the people you picked, the message is added to it. The conversation opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To reply, open the conversation and type in the message box at the bottom.

### Write messages

- Click into the message box to show the formatting toolbar: **Bold**, **Italic**, **Strikethrough**, **Add Link**, **Bullet List** and **Numbered List**. SMS and email conversations don't have the toolbar.
- **Add Link** opens the **Add link** dialog. Enter the **Text** to show and the full web address in **Link**, including `https://`, then click **Save**. Web addresses you type or paste also become links.
- Click **Attach file**, or drag files onto the message box. A file larger than 1 GB shows **File is too large** and isn't attached. You can send attachments without text.
- Click **Add emoji** to insert an emoji, or type `@` to mention a member of the conversation.
- By default, Enter sends and Shift+Enter starts a new line. Once you've typed something, click **Enter to Send** next to the send button to swap them.

## Schedule a message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    In the conversation, type the message and add any attachments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the schedule menu">
    Click the arrow next to the send button. The **Schedule send** menu lists the next four weekday times at 9:00 AM or 1:00 PM, such as **Tomorrow at 9:00 AM**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a time">
    Choose a suggested time. Or choose **Custom date & time…**, pick a date and a time at least a minute from now, and click **Schedule message**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Times are in your computer's time zone. A banner above the message box shows when the conversation's next scheduled message goes out. Click the banner to open **Scheduled Messages**.

## Manage scheduled messages

To see the messages you've scheduled, click the menu button at the bottom of the conversation list and choose **Scheduled Messages**. Each row shows the conversation, the start of the message and its send time. **Overdue** means the send time passed more than 10 minutes ago, and **Failed** means more than 2 hours ago. A **Failed** message won't be sent. Use **Send now**, or delete it.

Each row's **More options** menu has **Go to conversation**. With **Full access**, it also has:

- **Edit message**: opens the conversation with the message in the message box. Make your changes and click the check button (**Save scheduled message**). The send time stays the same, and you return to **Scheduled Messages**.
- **Send now**: in **Send Message Now?**, click **Send Now**. The message is sent and the conversation opens.
- **Delete message**: see [Delete a scheduled message](#delete-a-scheduled-message).

## Create a channel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a channel">
    Click **New**, then **Channel**. The **New Channel** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the channel">
    Enter a **Channel Name** and, if you like, a **Channel Description** of up to 120 characters. To change the channel's icon, click it and pick an emoji.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the visibility">
    Under **Visibility**, choose **Public**, **Team** or **Broadcast**. You can't change the type after you create the channel. See [Which channel type should I use?](#choose-channel-type) Then click **Add Users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members">
    For a public or broadcast channel, use **Filter Users** to add people by **Country**, **Language**, **Rank** or **Member Type**, or **Add All Users** to add everyone in your organization. Each has a switch to add future members automatically. For a team channel, use **Search Users** to add people by name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the channel">
    Click **Create Channel**. The new channel opens. **Create Channel** stays unavailable until you've added at least one person, chosen a filter, or chosen **Add All Users**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A filter appears only if your company has options for it. Channel names show in lowercase, with hyphens in place of spaces. If you pick exactly the same people as an existing team channel or group message, Fluid doesn't create a new one, and the panel shows **This conversation already exists**. Click **Open Conversation** to go to it.

## Manage a channel

Open the channel and choose **Channel Members** from **More options**, or **Channel Settings** if your role has **Full access**. With **Full access**, a **Channel Settings** button also appears at the start of the channel's history. The channel panel has two tabs.

**Members** lists the channel's members, with a count on the tab. Use **Search members** to find someone. With **Full access**:

- To remove someone, hover over their row, open the **Member options** menu, choose **Remove From Channel**, then click **Remove**. You can't remove yourself. On a public or broadcast channel, you can't add a removed member back from this screen.
- On a team channel, click **Add Members**, search for people, and click **Add**. Public and broadcast channels don't have **Add Members**.

**Settings** shows the **Channel Icon**, **Channel Name**, **Channel Description**, and a **Channel Type** card with the type and who created the channel. With **Full access**, change the icon, name or description and click **Save Changes**. The name must not already be taken. Without **Full access**, the tab is read-only.

## Pin messages

Hover over a message and open its menu to choose **Pin message**, **Unpin message** or **Copy link to message**. Pinned messages are marked **Pinned**. Next to the menu, you can add an emoji reaction, except in SMS and email conversations.

To see a conversation's pinned messages, choose **Pinned Messages** from **More options**. The menu shows how many there are. Click a pinned message to jump to it in the conversation.

## Delete channels and scheduled messages

You can delete a whole channel, or a scheduled message before it's sent. This screen has no archive option, and you can't delete a message once it's sent.

### Delete a channel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the channel's settings">
    In the channel, choose **Channel Settings** from **More options**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete the channel">
    In the **Delete Channel** card, click **Delete Channel**, then click **Delete**. You return to the **Messaging** screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a channel removes it and all of its messages for every member. Delete a channel only when nobody needs its history, such as a test channel or a duplicate.
</Warning>

Deleting a channel needs **Full access**. Your company's announcements channel can't be deleted, renamed or given a new icon. You can change its description.

### Delete a scheduled message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the message">
    Open **Scheduled Messages** and find the message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete it">
    From the row's **More options** menu, choose **Delete message**, then click **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A deleted scheduled message is gone for good. You can't undo it.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which channel type should I use?" id="choose-channel-type">
    - **Public**: members come from the filters you set, or from everyone in your organization, and every member can post. Listed under **Public Channels**.
    - **Team**: only the people you add can view the channel. Listed under **Private Channels**.
    - **Broadcast**: admins post, and everyone else can only read, as in your company's announcements channel. Listed under **Broadcast Channels**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I start a new message?" id="start-new-message">
    Click **New** at the bottom of the conversation list, then **Message**. In **To:**, type `@` and a name to find people, or `#` and a name to find a channel. See [Send a new message](#send-a-new-message).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I add people to a channel?" id="cannot-add-channel-members">
    **Add Members** is only on team channels, and only for roles with **Full access** for **Messaging**. A public or broadcast channel gets its members from the filters or the **Add All Users** choice made when it was created.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I send messages?" id="cannot-send-messages">
    If there's no message box, your role doesn't have **Full access** for **Messaging**. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).

    If the conversation shows **You don't have permission to send messages to this conversation.**, sending is turned off for you in that conversation.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
