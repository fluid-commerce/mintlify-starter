Use the **Media** screen to manage your company's media library: images, videos and PDF documents that you can tag with products and embed.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Storefront**, select **Media**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Media**. To add, duplicate, edit or delete media, it needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Media** is in the **Content & Website** card. Open the card and set access from the **Media** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

The top of the page has a view switcher with three views: **Table view**, **Grid view** and **Grouped by product**. Next to it are **Export**, **Bulk Upload** and **Add Media**. Grid view is the default, and the screen remembers the view you last used. On small screens, the view switcher, **Export** and **Bulk Upload** are hidden.

**Export** downloads a CSV file of your media with each item's title, type, file URL and status.

Each media item has a status:

- **Active**: published and live.
- **Inactive**: a draft, hidden from public view.
- **Scheduled**: set to go live at a future date and time.

### Grid view

- Search with the **Search media...** box.
- Use the sort menu to sort by **Title**, **Type** or **Date Created**, **Ascending** or **Descending**. The default is **Date Created**, newest first. Sorting by **Type** groups the page into **Images**, **PDFs**, **Videos** and **Other**.
- Filter with **Type** (**Image**, **Documents** or **Videos**) and **Status** (**Active** or **Inactive**). **Clear Filters** removes both.
- Click a card to open it.
- Hover over a card to open its menu: **Preview**, **Edit** and **Delete**.
- Hover over a card and tick its checkbox to select it. Once you've selected items, **Select all** selects every item on the page, and the **selected** menu offers **Associate to Products**, **Change Status**, **Clear Selection** and **Delete**.
- At the bottom, choose **Items per page** (25, 50 or 100) and move with **Previous** and **Next**.

### Table view

- The **All**, **Videos**, **Images** and **Documents** tabs filter by type.
- Use the search box, and the filter button for **Type** and **Status**.
- Columns: **Preview**, **Title**, **Type**, **Status**, **Countries** and **Date Modified**. You can sort by **Title** and **Date Modified**. **Countries** shows **All** when the item is available in every country you sell in.
- Click a row to open the item.
- Each row's actions menu has **Edit**, **Associate to Products**, **Change Status** (with **Set Active** and **Set Inactive**) and **Delete**. Select rows to use the same actions on several items at once from the table header's menu, where the labels include the count, such as **Set 3 Active** or **Delete 3 rows**.

### Grouped by product

This view lists your products, 12 per page, with the media tagged to each. Search with **Search products...**, and use the **Media Status** filter to show products **With Media** or **Without Media**. Click a product's header to see all its media, and click a thumbnail to open that media item.

## Add a media item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new item">
    Click **Add Media**. The **New Media** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the file">
    In the **Media** card, click **Select Media** and choose one image, video or PDF.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    In **Media Details**, enter a **Title** (required) and, if you want, a **Description**. For a video, you can set its thumbnail with **Select Video Frame** or **Select New Image**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag products">
    In **Tagged Products**, click **Add Product** to tag up to 100 products.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the options on the right">
    Set the **Status**, **SEO And Link Sharing**, **Theme Template**, **Countries** and **Tags**. A new item starts as **Published**. Leave **Countries** empty to make the item available in every country you sell in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The page changes to the new item's **Media Details** page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your active theme has no default media template and you haven't chosen a **Theme Template**, a **Your theme has no media template** dialog appears when you save. Click **Create template** to create one and save the item. If you click **Not now**, the item isn't saved.

To add a call-to-action button, click **CTA Button: Off** (or **On**) in the **Media** card, turn on **Enable call-to-action button**, choose the action (**Page**, **Cart**, **Email** or **Phone**), set it up, and click **Done**. Then save the item.

## Upload several files at once

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open bulk upload">
    Click **Bulk Upload**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the files">
    Click **Select Assets** and choose up to 20 assets from your asset library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review each file's settings">
    A table shows one column per file. Each file gets a **Title** from its file name, with **Active** and **Search engine listing** turned on and all your countries selected. You can change the **Title**, **Active**, **Search engine listing**, **Description**, **Products**, **Media Theme**, **Countries** and **Tags** for each file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit several files together (optional)">
    Tick the checkbox at the top of several columns, or **Select All**. A change you make in one ticked column then applies to all of them. **Title** and **Description** can't be edited while a column is ticked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the media">
    Click **Create Media**. A confirmation message appears and you return to the media list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your active theme has no default media template, the template dialog appears after the media are created. Your media are saved either way.

## Edit a media item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    Click the item in any view. Its **Media Details** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change the file with **Replace Media**, or change any field or card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. **Save** is available once you've changed something.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On a saved item, the top of the page also has **Preview**, which opens the item's storefront page in a new tab, and an **Actions** menu with:

- **Duplicate**: creates a copy titled with **- Copy** added, as a **Draft**, with the same tagged products. The copy includes any changes you haven't saved, and opens when it's created.
- **Embed**: shows code you can copy to embed the item on a website or blog.
- **Translations**: opens a panel where you translate the **Media Title** and **Description**, and choose a different file for each language. On very wide screens, **Translations** is a separate button.
- **Delete**: deletes the item.

### Set the status

In the **Status** card, choose **Published**, **Draft** or **Scheduled**, then click **Save**. For **Scheduled**, pick a date and time and click **Schedule Activation**. In the media list, **Published** items show as **Active** and **Draft** items as **Inactive**.

<Warning>
  Don't use **Scheduled** to keep an item private. Use **Draft** until you want it to be reachable.
</Warning>

### Set the SEO and link preview

In the **SEO And Link Sharing** card, click **Edit**. Set the **SEO Image**, **Title Tag** and **Meta Description**, which control how the item looks in search results and when its link is shared. The **Preview** shows how the item looks as a **Google Search Result**, a **Text Message** or on **X (formerly Twitter)**. Turn off **Search engine visibility** to keep search engines from listing the item. Click **Done**, then click **Save**. **Done** stays unavailable until the **Title Tag** has at least 3 characters and the **Meta Description** at least 5; **Cancel** closes the panel without keeping your changes. When you first add an image file, it's also used as the SEO image unless you've already set one.

## Change the status of several items

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the items">
    In grid view, select the items' checkboxes. In table view, select the rows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the new status">
    In grid view, open the **selected** menu, choose **Change Status**, then **Active** or **Inactive**. In table view, open the table header's menu, choose **Change Status**, then **Set 3 Active** or **Set 3 Inactive** (the number matches your selection).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Change status to Active?** or **Change status to Inactive?** dialog, click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tag products to several items

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the items">
    Select the items in grid or table view, then choose **Associate to Products** from the selection or actions menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add products">
    Add each product you want. Each product you add is tagged to all the selected items right away. Click **Done** when you're finished.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove a tagged product, open the media item and remove it from **Tagged Products**, then click **Save**.

## Delete media

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    Choose **Delete** from a card's menu, a row's actions menu, or the selection menu. On an item's page, choose **Delete** from the **Actions** menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm in the dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting media can't be undone.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why won't my media item save?" id="media-wont-save">
    Check that you've chosen a file in the **Media** card and entered a **Title**. If you're creating an item and the **Your theme has no media template** dialog appeared, choose **Create template**. If you choose **Not now**, the item isn't saved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Add Media or Bulk Upload?" id="no-add-media-button">
    Your role doesn't have **Full access** for **Media**. **Bulk Upload** is also hidden on small screens. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
