Use the **Menus** screen to create and manage navigation menus. A menu is a list of links, and each link can have its own sub-menu items.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Website**, select **Navigation**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Menus**. To create, edit or delete menus, it needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Menus** is in the **Content & Website** card. Open the card and set access from the **Menus** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

The **Add Menu** button is at the top of the page.

- The **All**, **Active** and **Draft** tabs filter the list by status. **Draft** lists menus whose status is **Inactive**.
- Use the search box to find a menu.
- Filter the list by **Status** (**Active** or **Draft**) or by **Country**. The **Status** filter and the tabs stay in step.

The list has these columns:

- **Title**: the menu's name. Click it, or anywhere on the row, to open the menu.
- **Status**: **Active** or **Inactive**.
- **Menu Items**: the titles of the menu's top-level items, up to five, then a count of the rest, such as **+3 more**. A menu with no items shows **No items**.
- **Items**: how many top-level items the menu has.
- **Date**: when the menu was created.

Click the **Title** or **Date** header to sort by that column.

Each row's actions menu has **Edit** and, if your role can delete menus, **Delete**. To delete several menus, select their rows with the checkboxes (shown only if your role can delete menus), then click **Delete** in the bar that shows how many rows are selected.

## Create a menu

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new menu">
    On the **Menus** screen, click **Add Menu**. The **New Menu** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the menu">
    In the **Menu Details** card, the **Title** starts as **New Menu**. Replace it with your menu's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add menu items">
    Under **Menu Items**, add each link. See [Add a menu item](#add-a-menu-item).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose countries and status">
    In the **Available Countries** card, select at least one of your company's countries. In the **Status** card, choose **Active** or **Inactive**. A new menu starts as **Active**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the menu">
    Click **Create Menu**. A confirmation message appears and the menu's page opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Create Menu** stays grayed out until you change something on the page. If no country is selected, the menu isn't created and a message asks you to select at least one country.

Once the menu is saved, **Handle** under the **Title** field shows the menu's handle, which is made from its title.

## Add a menu item

You add items the same way under **Menu Items** on a menu's page and under **Sub Menu Items** on a menu item's page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the item title">
    In **Item Title**, enter the text the link shows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where it links">
    Click the **Enter URL** field to open the **Select Type** list. **Home Page**, **Categories**, **All Collections**, **Blog**, **Join Page**, **Shop** and **Cart** are selected as soon as you click them. For **Products**, **Media**, **Pages**, **Enrollments**, **Collections**, **Posts** and **Playlists**, click the type, then pick the item from its list. Use the search box to find it, or click the back arrow to choose another type. To link anywhere else, type the full address in the field instead, such as `https://shop.example.com/sale`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the item">
    Click **Add**. The item appears in the table below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Add** stays grayed out until the item has a title and a link. When you click **Add**, a typed address must be a full URL, or a message asks for a valid one.

The table shows each item's **Title**, **Link** and number of **Sub Menu Items**. In the table you can:

- **Reorder** items: drag a row up or down.
- **Edit** an item: click the pencil icon, change the title or link, then click the check mark to apply the change in the table. To cancel, click the X or press Escape.
- **Remove** an item: click the trash icon.
- **Open** an item: click its title to open its page, where you add sub-menu items. See [Add sub-menu items](#add-sub-menu-items).

Adding, editing, removing and reordering items change the table only. Click **Save** (**Create Menu** on a new menu) to keep them. A new item opens only after it's saved; before that, a message asks you to save first.

## Edit a menu

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    In the list, click the menu, or choose **Edit** from its actions menu. The **Edit Menu** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change the title, items, countries or status. The badge next to the menu's name shows **Active** or **Inactive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. A confirmation message appears. **Save** is grayed out when there's nothing to save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your role can only view menus, there's no **Save** button.

## Add sub-menu items

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    On the menu's page, click a saved item's title. The item's page opens, titled **Edit Menu Item** followed by the item's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change the **Menu Item Title** if you need to. Under **Sub Menu Items**, add, edit, reorder and remove items the same way as on the menu's page. To go a level deeper, click a sub-menu item's title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. A confirmation message appears. **Save** is grayed out when there's nothing to save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back to the menu">
    Click **Back to Menu**. If you have unsaved changes, you're asked to confirm that you want to leave without saving.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Back to Menu** always returns to the menu's page, even from a deeply nested item. When you reach the maximum nesting depth, clicking an item shows a message instead of opening it.

If your role can only view menus, an item's page has its fields grayed out and no **Save** button.

## Translate menu item titles

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    On a saved menu's page, click **Save** if you have unsaved changes. Translations don't open while there are changes to save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    Click **Translations**. The **Menu Translations** panel opens with each top-level item's **Title**. Enter the title in each of your company's other active languages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Translate sub-menu items">
    For an item that has sub-menu items, click **View Sub-items**. Click **Back to Menu** or **Back to Parent** to go back up a level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the translations">
    Click **Save** in the panel. A confirmation message appears and the panel closes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The panel translates item titles only, not the menu's own title. At the top level, **Language Settings** opens your company's language settings in a new tab. To close the panel without saving, click the close button or press Escape.

## Delete a menu

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    In the list, choose **Delete** from the menu's actions menu. To delete several menus, select their rows and click **Delete** in the selection bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Delete Menu** dialog (**Delete Menus** for several), click **Delete**. A confirmation message appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can delete menus only from the list, not from a menu's page.

<Warning>
  Deleting a menu deletes its items too and can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  The **Navigation** item under **Mobile App** is a different screen. See [Mobile app navigation](/help/admin/mobile/navigation).
</Tip>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why isn't my menu created or saved?" id="select-a-country">
    A menu needs a **Title** and at least one country in **Available Countries**. Select a country, then click **Create Menu** or **Save** again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why doesn't a page or product appear in the link list?" id="item-missing-from-picker">
    The **Products**, **Pages**, **Enrollments**, **Collections**, **Posts** and **Playlists** lists show only items that are active or published. Publish or activate the item, then pick it again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I change where a menu item links?" id="change-item-link">
    An item's own page has no link field. In its parent's table (the menu's page for a top-level item), click the pencil icon next to the item, choose a new link, click the check mark, then click **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does clicking a new item show a message instead of opening it?" id="save-before-opening">
    A new item opens only after it's saved. Click **Save** (or **Create Menu**), then click the item's title.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
