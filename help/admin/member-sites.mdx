Use the **Member Sites** screen to see every member site in your company, a member's own sites included. Open a site to see who made it, its address, and what it uses of a viewer's data. If the site keeps a database, you can also read the rows its members wrote and delete the ones that shouldn't be there.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Website**, select **Member Sites**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View** on **Application Themes**. To read a site's data, it also needs **View** on **Member Site Data**. To delete rows, it needs **Delete** on **Member Site Data**. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles). Fluid turns member sites on for your company. If you don't see **Member Sites**, [contact support](/help/getting-help).
</Note>

## The site list

The list shows every site in your company, company-owned and member-owned.

- The **All**, **Draft**, **Published** and **Taken down** tabs filter the list by status.
- The **Owner** filter narrows it to **Company** or **Member** sites.
- The box labelled **Filter this page…** narrows the page you're on by what you type, such as a site's name or handle, or a creator's name or email.

The list has these columns: **Name**, **Handle**, **Created by**, **Status**, **Visibility** and **Date Modified**. Click a row to open the site. A published site also has an **Open** link, which opens the site itself in a new tab.

**Created by** shows who made each site:

- A member's site shows the member who owns it.
- A company site shows the admin who created it, marked **Company site**. When there's no creator to show, such as an admin who has left, it shows **Not available**.

To see a member who made a site, your role needs the **Customers** action **View the unified Customers screen (/customers) and member detail pages**. To see an admin who made one, it needs **View company administrator accounts** for **Company Admins**. Without them, a member's site shows only the owner's name, and a company site shows only **Company site**.

## A site's page

A site's page shows its **Details**, an **Access** card that lists what the site uses of a viewer's data, who the site is shared with, and, when the site keeps a database, a **Data** card.

In **Details**:

- **URL** is the address members open the site at. Click **Copy** to copy it.
- **Owner**, on a member's site, shows the member with their username and email. **Copy** copies the email.
- **Created by**, on a company site, shows the admin who created it.

Click **Preview** to open the site in a new tab. Only a published site opens. On a draft or a taken-down site, **Preview** is turned off.

## Take down or bring back a site

Take a site down to stop it opening. Its owner can't publish it again until you bring it back. Both need the **Application Themes** switch **Customize and edit theme settings**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the site">
    On **Member Sites**, click the site's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Take down">
    In the **Take down** dialog, click **Take down**. The site's status becomes **Taken down**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To undo it, open the site, click **Bring back**, and confirm with **Bring back**. A site that was published comes back published. A site that never was goes back to a draft. Its owner can publish it again.

Taking a site down also takes down its branch. Bringing the site back doesn't bring the branch back: bring it back on its own page, after the site it was made from.

## Review a site's data

A member site can keep its own data, such as RSVPs or guestbook entries. The **Data** card shows it. You see every row, whoever wrote it, whatever the site's rules say about who may read it.

<Info>
  The **Data** card appears only when your role has **View** on **Member Site Data** and the site has an available database. Without the permission, or when the site has no available database, the page has no **Data** card. See [If you don't see the Data card](#if-you-dont-see-the-data-card).
</Info>

To read a site's data:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the site">
    On **Member Sites**, click the site's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a table">
    In the **Data** card, click a table's name. The first table is chosen when the card opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the rows">
    The table lists the newest rows first, in groups of 25. Each row shows the table's columns, then the `id`, `created_by`, `created_at` and `updated_at` columns every table has. `created_by` is the id of the person who wrote the row. An empty value shows as a dash.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move between pages">
    When a table has more than 25 rows, **Previous** and **Next** appear under it, with the range you're on, such as **26 to 50 of 61**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A long value is cut short in the table. Hold your pointer over it to see the whole value. A value that holds nested data is shown as JSON.

The card can also say **This site's database has no tables.** or **This table has no rows.** If it says **Could not load the data**, click **Try again**.

## Delete a row

Delete a row to remove something a member wrote, such as a spam entry. You can only delete here. You can't add a row or change one.

<Warning>
  A deleted row can't be restored. Your name is recorded with the deletion.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the row">
    Choose the table, then use **Next** and **Previous** to find the row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete">
    Click **Delete** at the end of the row. You see this button only if your role has **Delete** on **Member Site Data**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In **Delete this row?**, click **Delete row**. Click **Cancel** to keep it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A message, **Row deleted**, confirms it. If it fails, you see **Failed to delete the row**. Try again, or check that your role still has **Delete** on **Member Site Data**.

## Give a role access to a site's data

Reading and deleting rows are separate permissions, because the rows are what members wrote. Having access to **Member Sites** or to themes is not enough.

1. In the admin, click the **Settings** gear and, under **Company**, select **Roles**.
2. Open the role and type **Member Site Data** in **Search permissions...**.
3. Turn on **View** to let the role read rows. Turn on **Delete** to let it delete them.
4. Click **Save**.

A role also needs **View** on **Application Themes** to open the **Member Sites** screen. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) for how roles work.

## If you don't see the Data card

| What you see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No **Data** card | Your role doesn't have **View** on **Member Site Data** | Ask an admin who manages roles to turn it on |
| No **Data** card | The site has no database yet. A site gets one when its owner first publishes it with a `db/schema.json` | Nothing. The card appears after the owner publishes |
| No **Delete** button | Your role has **View** but not **Delete** on **Member Site Data** | Ask an admin who manages roles to turn on **Delete** |
| **Could not load the data** | The request failed | Click **Try again**. If it keeps failing, [contact support](/help/getting-help) |

## Download everything

The screen doesn't download a site's data. To save every table and row of a site as one file, use the API. See [Download the data](/member-sites-v1/database#download-the-data) for the file and [Download a site's database](/api-reference/member-site-database/download-a-sites-database) for the request. It needs a signed-in admin with **View** on **Member Site Data**. A company API token on its own can't use it.

A member who owns a site can also inspect its data through the read-only Member API, separately from this admin screen. See [Inspect your site's data](/member-sites-v1/database#inspect-your-sites-data).
