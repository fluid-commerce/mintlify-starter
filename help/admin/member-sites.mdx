Use the **Sites** screen to see every Site in your company, a member's own Sites included. Sites used to be called member sites. You can create and edit company Sites, take any Site down, set a company Site's search engine settings, make an earlier version live, and review the rows members wrote in a Site's database. For what a Site is, see [Sites](/sites/overview).

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Website**, select **Sites**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View** on **Application Themes**. To create, edit, take down or delete a Site, it needs **Update** on **Application Themes**. To read a Site's data, it also needs **View** on **Member Site Data**, and to delete rows, **Delete** on **Member Site Data**. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles). Fluid turns Sites on for your company. If you don't see **Sites**, [contact support](/help/getting-help).
</Note>

## The site list

The list shows every Site in your company, company-owned and member-owned.

- The **All**, **Draft**, **Published** and **Taken down** tabs filter the list by status.
- **Owner** narrows it to **Company** or **Member** Sites. **Visibility** narrows it to public or private Sites.
- **Search sites…** finds Sites by what you type.
- Sort by **Name** or **Date Modified**.

The columns are **Name**, **Handle**, **Created by**, **Status**, **Visibility** and **Date Modified**. Click a row to open the Site. Each row's menu, and the bulk action for selected rows, deletes Sites.

- **New site** creates a company Site. Give it a **Name**, and a **Handle** or leave the handle blank to have one made from the name. A new Site has nothing published until someone publishes a build with the Fluid CLI, Mist or an AI agent. See [Publish a Site](/sites/publishing).
- **Visitor data** opens the list of what Sites may use of a visitor's data. See [What your company allows](/sites/declarations#what-your-company-allows).

## A site's page

At the top:

- **Preview** opens the published Site.
- **Edit** changes a company Site's **Name**, **Handle**, **Visibility**, **Required** (members must have this Site) and **Position** (where it sits among the company's Sites). A member's Site can't be edited here.
- **Take down** hides the Site from everyone but its owner and admins, with an optional **Reason**, up to 1000 characters. Its owner can't publish it, switch it on or make it public until you bring it back. **Bring back** undoes it. See [When a site is taken down](/storefront/sites#when-a-site-is-taken-down).
- **Delete** deletes the Site and all of its pages. It can't be undone.

The cards:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Details** | The name, handle, URL with a copy button, owner, who created it, visibility, status, **Required**, **Position**, and when it was created and last changed |
| **Access** | What the Site uses of a visitor's data, from its declaration |
| **Storefront** | Company Sites only. **Active**, **Publish at**, **Search engine title**, **Search engine description** and **Show in search engines**, with **Archive** or **Restore**. See [SEO and indexing](/sites/seo) |
| **Deployments** | Company Sites only. Each published version. **Make live** makes an earlier one live again. Only the 25 newest published versions are kept, and a version with a database can't be made live |
| **Shares** | Who the Site is shared with. **Copy link** copies a share's link, and **Remove share** removes it |
| **Data** | The rows members wrote in the Site's database, when it has one. See below |

**Show in search engines** is off for a new Site. Turn it on when a public company Site is ready to be indexed.

## Review a site's data

A Site can keep its own data, such as RSVPs or guestbook entries. The **Data** card shows it. You see every row, whoever wrote it, whatever the site's rules say about who may read it.

<Info>
  The **Data** card appears only when your role has **View** on **Member Site Data** and the site has an available database. Without the permission, or when the site has no available database, the page has no **Data** card. See [If you don't see the Data card](#if-you-dont-see-the-data-card).
</Info>

To read a site's data:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the site">
    On **Sites**, click the site's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a table">
    In the **Data** card, click a table's name. The first table is chosen when the card opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the rows">
    The table lists the newest rows first, in groups of 25. Each row shows the table's columns, then the `id`, `created_by`, `created_at` and `updated_at` columns every table has. `created_by` is the id of the person who wrote the row. An empty value shows as a dash.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move between pages">
    When a table has more than 25 rows, **Previous** and **Next** appear under it, with the range you're on, such as **26 to 50 of 61**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A long value is cut short in the table. Hold your pointer over it to see the whole value. A value that holds nested data is shown as JSON.

The card can also say **This site's database has no tables.** or **This table has no rows.** If it says **Could not load the data**, click **Try again**.

## Delete a row

Delete a row to remove something a member wrote, such as a spam entry. You can only delete here. You can't add a row or change one.

<Warning>
  A deleted row can't be restored. Your name is recorded with the deletion.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the row">
    Choose the table, then use **Next** and **Previous** to find the row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete">
    Click **Delete** at the end of the row. You see this button only if your role has **Delete** on **Member Site Data**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In **Delete this row?**, click **Delete row**. Click **Cancel** to keep it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A message, **Row deleted**, confirms it. If it fails, you see **Failed to delete the row**. Try again, or check that your role still has **Delete** on **Member Site Data**.

## Give a role access to a site's data

Reading and deleting rows are separate permissions, because the rows are what members wrote. Having access to **Sites** or to themes is not enough.

1. In the admin, click the **Settings** gear and, under **Company**, select **Roles**.
2. Open the role and type **Member Site Data** in **Search permissions...**.
3. Turn on **View** to let the role read rows. Turn on **Delete** to let it delete them.
4. Click **Save**.

A role also needs **View** on **Application Themes** to open the **Sites** screen. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) for how roles work.

## If you don't see the Data card

| What you see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No **Data** card | Your role doesn't have **View** on **Member Site Data** | Ask an admin who manages roles to turn it on |
| No **Data** card | The site has no database yet. A site gets one when its owner first publishes it with a `db/schema.json` | Nothing. The card appears after the owner publishes |
| No **Delete** button | Your role has **View** but not **Delete** on **Member Site Data** | Ask an admin who manages roles to turn on **Delete** |
| **Could not load the data** | The request failed | Click **Try again**. If it keeps failing, [contact support](/help/getting-help) |

## Download everything

The screen doesn't download a site's data. To save every table and row of a site as one file, use the API. See [Download the data](/sites/database#download-the-data) for the file and [Download a site's database](/api-reference/member-site-database/download-a-sites-database) for the request. It needs a signed-in admin with **View** on **Member Site Data**. A company API token on its own can't use it.

A member who owns a site can also inspect its data through the read-only Member API, separately from this admin screen. See [Inspect your site's data](/sites/database#inspect-your-sites-data).
