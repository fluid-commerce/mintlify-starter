Use the **Sites** screen to see every Site in your company, a member's own Sites included. Sites used to be called member sites. You can create and edit company Sites, take any Site down, set a company Site's search engine settings, make an earlier version live, and review the rows members wrote in a Site's database. For what a Site is, see [Sites](/sites/overview).

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Website**, select **Sites**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View** on **Application Themes**. To create, edit, take down or delete a Site, it needs **Update** on **Application Themes**. To read a Site's data, it also needs **View** on **Member Site Data**, and to delete rows, **Delete** on **Member Site Data**. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles). Fluid turns Sites on for your company. If you don't see **Sites**, [contact support](/help/getting-help).
</Note>

## The site list

The list shows every Site in your company, company-owned and member-owned.

- The **All**, **Draft**, **Published** and **Taken down** tabs filter the list by status.
- **Owner** narrows it to **Company** or **Member** Sites. **Visibility** narrows it to public or private Sites.
- **Search…** finds Sites by what you type.
- Sort by **Title**.

The columns are **Image**, **Title**, **Status**, **Visibility** and **Created by**. Click a row to open the Site. Each row's menu has **Edit**, which opens the Site, and **Delete**. The bulk action for selected rows also deletes Sites.

**Created by** shows who made each Site, with their email under the name when there is one:

- A member's Site shows the member who owns it.
- A company Site shows the admin who created it, marked **Company site**. When there's no creator to show, such as an admin who has left, it shows **Not available**.

To see a member who made a Site, your role needs the **Customers** action **View the unified Customers screen (/customers) and member detail pages**. To see an admin who made one, it needs **View company administrator accounts** for **Company Admins**. Without them, a member's Site shows only the owner's name, and a company Site shows only **Company site**.

- **Create Site** creates a company Site. Give it a **Name**. Its address is made from the name, such as `fall-launch` for Fall Launch, and shown under the field as you type. If a Site already has that address, or the address is reserved, choose a different name. You can also set its **Visibility** and **Required**. A new company Site goes after the others. To change its **Handle** or **Position**, edit the Site. A new Site has nothing published until someone publishes a build with the Fluid CLI, Mist or an AI agent. See [Publish a Site](/sites/publishing).
- **Visitor data** opens the list of what Sites may use of a visitor's data. See [What your company allows](/sites/declarations#what-your-company-allows).

## A site's page

At the top:

- **Actions** has **Publish Now** for a company Site that isn't published, **Archive** or **Restore** for a company Site, **Take down** or **Bring back**, and **Delete Site**. **Delete Site** deletes the Site and all of its pages. It can't be undone.
- **Preview** opens the published Site. It's unavailable until the Site is published.
- **Save** saves your changes to a company Site. A member's Site can't be edited here, except its password and its search engine setting.

**Take down** hides the Site from everyone but its owner and admins, with an optional **Reason**, up to 1000 characters. Its owner can't publish it, switch it on or make it public until you bring it back. See [When a site is taken down](/storefront/sites#when-a-site-is-taken-down).

The left column:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Site Details** | A company Site's **Title**, **Description** and **Media** |
| **Deployments** | Company Sites only. Each published version, ten at a time, newest first. **Make live** makes an earlier one live again. Only the 25 newest published versions are kept, and a version with a database can't be made live |
| **Shared with** | Who the Site is shared with, and each share's access. **Copy link** copies the Site's link. **Add share**, **Change access** and **Remove** manage the shares. While the Site has a password, each share shows **Paused while password is set**. See [Share a site](#share-a-site) |
| **Data** | The rows members wrote in the Site's database, when it has one. See below |

The right column:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | Company Sites only. **Published**, **Draft**, **Scheduled** or **Archived**, with the date for a scheduled Site |
| **Visibility** | Company Sites only. **Public** or **Private**. A Site behind a password shows **Public, password protected** |
| **Password** | Whether visitors are asked for a password, and when it was set. A public Site behind a password shows **Protected**, and a private Site that kept its password shows **Kept**. Shown once the Site has a password, or once Site passwords are on for your company. You can add, change or remove it on a company Site or a member's; until Site passwords are on, only remove. A taken-down Site that asks for a password keeps it until you bring the Site back. See [Password-protect a Site](/sites/password) |
| **SEO And Link Sharing** | Company Sites only. Click **Edit** to set the **Title Tag**, **Meta Description** and **SEO Image**, and **Block search engine crawlers**. See [SEO and indexing](/sites/seo) |
| **Search engines** | Member Sites only. Whether search engines may list the Site, with the owner's choice and your override. See [Control whether a member's Site is listed](#control-whether-a-members-site-is-listed) |
| **Settings** | The owner, who created a company Site, **Required** for a company Site (every member is given it and can't remove it), the visibility of a member's Site, and when the Site was taken down |
| **Access** | What the Site uses of a visitor's data, from its declaration |

**Block search engine crawlers** is on for a new Site. Turn it off when a public company Site is ready to be indexed, then click **Save**.

## Control whether a member's Site is listed

A search engine, such as Google, may list a Site in its results. A member's Site is not listed unless its owner turns that on. You can override the owner's choice for any member's Site, so the Site is always listed or never listed whatever the owner chose. A company Site isn't affected: it keeps the **SEO And Link Sharing** card.

Your role needs **Update** on **Application Themes** to see the **Search engines** card and change the setting.

The **Search engines** card has three options:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **The owner decides** | No override. The owner's switch decides. This is the default, and a Site's owner switch starts off |
| **Always allow** | Search engines may list the Site, whether or not the owner turned it on. The owner's switch is locked |
| **Never allow** | Search engines never list the Site, whether or not the owner turned it on. The owner's switch is locked |

While **Always allow** or **Never allow** is set, the owner can't change their switch. Their switch shows that the company controls the setting. The owner's own choice is kept, and it takes effect again when you choose **The owner decides**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Site">
    On **Sites**, click the member's Site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an option">
    In the **Search engines** card, choose **The owner decides**, **Always allow** or **Never allow**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Site's page also shows the owner's own choice. The card shows whether search engines may list the Site now, and who set the override.

<Warning>
  **Always allow** doesn't make a Site public. A search engine is a visitor who isn't signed in, so it lists a Site only while the Site is public, published and switched on, and while your company doesn't block search engines for the whole storefront. A private Site, a Site behind a password, and a Site you took down aren't listed, whatever this card says.
</Warning>

A change takes effect within 30 seconds, with no need to publish the Site again. Search engines may take time to list or drop a Site. Changing the override doesn't send the owner a message.

To set the override through the API, see [Search engine indexing](/api/member-sites#search-engine-indexing).

## Share a site

Share a Site to let members open it, or work on its drafts. You can share any Site in your company, a member's own Site included. You need **Update** on **Application Themes** to see **Add share**, **Change access** and **Remove**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Site">
    On **Sites**, click the Site's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add share">
    In the **Shared with** card, click **Add share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who">
    Under **Share with**, pick **A member** and search by name or email, **Every member of the company**, or **Members of a type or country** and choose a **Member type**, a **Country**, or both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose their access">
    Under **Access**, pick **View** to let them open the Site, **View and copy** to also let them copy it to start a Site of their own, or **Edit** to let them work on its drafts. Publishing, sharing, deleting and copying stay with the Site's owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Share">
    Giving **Edit** to every member, or to members of a type or country, asks you to confirm first. If the share is new, everyone it matches is sent a message in Flow with a link to the Site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the Site is already shared with that audience, the share stays as it is, and its access changes to the one you picked. To change a share's access later, click **Change access** on its row. To stop sharing, click **Remove**.

## Take down or bring back a site

Take a Site down to stop it opening. Its owner can't publish it again until you bring it back. Both need the **Application Themes** switch **Customize and edit theme settings**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Site">
    On **Sites**, click the Site's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take it down">
    Click **Actions**, then **Take down**. Add a **Reason** if you like, then click **Take down** in the dialog. The Site's status becomes **Taken down**, and its owner is told in Flow, with your reason.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To undo it, open the Site, click **Actions**, then **Bring back**, and confirm with **Bring back**. A Site that was published comes back published. A Site that never was goes back to a draft. Its owner can publish it again.

Taking a Site down also takes down its branch. Bringing the Site back doesn't bring the branch back. Branches aren't listed on **Sites**, so bring one back through the API with [Bring back a member site that was taken down](/api-reference/member-sites/bring-back-a-member-site-that-was-taken-down), after the Site it was made from is back.

## Review a site's data

A Site can keep its own data, such as RSVPs or guestbook entries. The **Data** card shows it. You see every row, whoever wrote it, whatever the site's rules say about who may read it.

<Info>
  The **Data** card appears only when your role has **View** on **Member Site Data** and the site has an available database. Without the permission, or when the site has no available database, the page has no **Data** card. See [If you don't see the Data card](#if-you-dont-see-the-data-card).
</Info>

To read a site's data:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the site">
    On **Sites**, click the site's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a table">
    In the **Data** card, click a table's name. The first table is chosen when the card opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the rows">
    The table lists the newest rows first, in groups of 25. Each row shows the table's columns, then the `id`, `created_by`, `created_at` and `updated_at` columns every table has. `created_by` is the id of the person who wrote the row. An empty value shows as a dash.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move between pages">
    When a table has more than 25 rows, **Previous** and **Next** appear under it, with the range you're on, such as **26 to 50 of 61**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A long value is cut short in the table. Hold your pointer over it to see the whole value. A value that holds nested data is shown as JSON.

The card can also say **This site's database has no tables.** or **This table has no rows.** If it says **Could not load the data**, click **Try again**.

## Delete a row

Delete a row to remove something a member wrote, such as a spam entry. You can only delete here. You can't add a row or change one.

<Warning>
  A deleted row can't be restored. Your name is recorded with the deletion.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the row">
    Choose the table, then use **Next** and **Previous** to find the row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete">
    Click **Delete** at the end of the row. You see this button only if your role has **Delete** on **Member Site Data**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In **Delete this row?**, click **Delete row**. Click **Cancel** to keep it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A message, **Row deleted**, confirms it. If it fails, you see **Failed to delete the row**. Try again, or check that your role still has **Delete** on **Member Site Data**.

## Give a role access to a site's data

Reading and deleting rows are separate permissions, because the rows are what members wrote. Having access to **Sites** or to themes is not enough.

1. In the admin, click the **Settings** gear and, under **Company**, select **Roles**.
2. Open the role and type **Member Site Data** in **Search permissions...**.
3. Turn on **View** to let the role read rows. Turn on **Delete** to let it delete them. **Create** and **Update** let the role add and change rows through the API. This screen can't add or change a row.
4. Click **Save**.

A role also needs **View** on **Application Themes** to open the **Sites** screen. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) for how roles work.

## If you don't see the Data card

| What you see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No **Data** card | Your role doesn't have **View** on **Member Site Data** | Ask an admin who manages roles to turn it on |
| No **Data** card | The site has no database yet. A site gets one when its owner first publishes it with a `db/schema.json` | Nothing. The card appears after the owner publishes |
| No **Delete** button | Your role has **View** but not **Delete** on **Member Site Data** | Ask an admin who manages roles to turn on **Delete** |
| **Could not load the data** | The request failed | Click **Try again**. If it keeps failing, [contact support](/help/getting-help) |

## Download everything

The screen doesn't download a site's data. To save every table and row of a site as one file, use the API. See [Download the data](/sites/database#download-the-data) for the file and [Download a site's database](/api-reference/member-site-database/download-a-sites-database) for the request. It needs a signed-in admin with **View** on **Member Site Data**. A company API token on its own can't use it.

A member who owns a site can also inspect its data through the read-only Member API, separately from this admin screen. See [Inspect your site's data](/sites/database#inspect-your-sites-data).
