Use this screen to build your company's Default MySite. While **Auto Populate Status** is **Active**, each new member's MySite starts with a copy of it. Any member's MySite that has no content items of its own shows your Default MySite content, and one with no buttons of its own shows your buttons.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Company**, select **Default Mysite**.

To see **Default Mysite** in the sidebar and change anything on it, your role needs **Full access** for **Default Mysite**, in the **Content & Website** card on the role's **Permissions** tab. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).

## What's on the screen

The screen has a content panel and four cards: **Instant Redirect**, **Default Theme**, **Auto Populate Status** and **Buttons**. Both **Save** buttons, at the top of the screen and in the **Buttons** card, save everything on the screen.

### Content

The content panel lists the items on your Default MySite, in order.

- The type menu sets what kind of item you search for. It starts on **Products**. The other types are **Pages**, **Media**, **Promotions** and **Enrollments**. **Enrollments** lists active enrollments only.
- The **Search content...** field finds items of the selected type. Items you've already added don't appear in the results.
- **Add** puts the item you selected in the results at the bottom of the content list. It stays unavailable until you select an item.
- The content list shows each item's title and type, with a drag handle and a trash icon.

### Instant Redirect

When **Enable instant redirect** is checked, a **Forwarding URL** field appears.

### Default Theme

Select the theme new members' MySites get when they're copied from your Default MySite. That copy happens only while **Auto Populate Status** is **Active**. The menu lists **Default** and your company's MySite themes. To manage those themes, in the admin sidebar, under **Platforms**, click **Website** > **Mysite Theme**.

### Auto Populate Status

This card has two options:

- **Active**: when a new member is added, their MySite gets a copy of your Default MySite content, buttons and theme. Nothing is copied if the member has no country, or if none of your content is available in the member's country.
- **Inactive**: new members' MySites get no copy.

A Default MySite that has never been saved starts as **Inactive**. Either way, a MySite with no content or buttons of its own shows your current Default MySite content and buttons.

### Buttons

The **Custom Buttons** list holds the buttons on your Default MySite.

- **Add** creates a new button at the top of the list and opens it for editing.
- Each button has a row that opens and closes when you click it, and a trash icon. A button with no text shows as **Untitled button**.
- Every button needs both **Button Text** and **Button Link**.

## Set up your Default MySite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add content">
    Choose a type from the type menu, click **Search content...**, and select an item. Click **Add**. Repeat for each item you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order the content">
    Drag items by their handle into the order you want. To remove an item, click its trash icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add buttons">
    In the **Buttons** card, click **Add**, then enter the **Button Text** and **Button Link**. To change a button later, click its row and edit the fields. To remove one, click its trash icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the theme new members start with">
    In the **Default Theme** card, select a theme. New members get it only while **Auto Populate Status** is **Active**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the auto populate status">
    In the **Auto Populate Status** card, select **Active** if new members' MySites should start with a copy of this setup. Select **Inactive** if they shouldn't.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the redirect setting">
    If **Enable instant redirect** is checked and **Forwarding URL** is empty, uncheck **Enable instant redirect**. The save stops while the box is checked and the field is empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save**. When the save succeeds, a confirmation message appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do my changes update existing members' MySites?" id="changes-existing-members">
    Only MySites that have none of their own. A MySite with no content items of its own shows your current content, and one with no buttons of its own shows your current buttons. Content and buttons copied to a member's MySite when they joined count as their own, as do items the member added, so your later changes don't reach them.

    A member's MySite page can keep showing the earlier version until you clear the MySite cache. To clear it, in the admin sidebar, under **Platforms**, click **Website** > **Mysite Theme**, then click **Reset Cache**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I save?" id="cannot-save">
    - **Save** stays unavailable until you change something on the screen.
    - There's no **Save** button at the top and you can't change anything: your role doesn't have **Full access** for **Default Mysite**. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
    - A button is missing its **Button Text** or **Button Link**. The save stops with a message asking you to fill in all button fields. Open the button and fill in the empty field, or remove the button with its trash icon.
    - **Enable instant redirect** is checked and **Forwarding URL** is empty. Uncheck **Enable instant redirect**, then save again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
