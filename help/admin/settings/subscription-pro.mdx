Subscription Pro is a set of extra subscription features. It helps you keep subscribers longer and ship their orders together. Fluid turns it on for your company. You then turn on each feature you want to use.

## Features

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Autoship profiles](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro/autoship-profiles) | Groups a customer's subscriptions so they renew as one order. Customers can add items and join an autoship at checkout. |
| [SMS renewal reminders](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro/sms-reminders) | Texts customers before their subscription renews. |
| [Cancel flow and win-back](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro/cancel-flow) | Offers a save offer, a pause or a skip before a customer cancels, and emails an offer after they cancel. |
| [Loyalty pricing](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro/loyalty-pricing) | Lowers a subscription's price the longer a customer keeps it. Its switch is called **Tenure pricing**. |
| [Milestone items](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro/milestone-items) | Adds a free item to an order when a customer reaches a milestone, and lets you add a one-time item to a subscription's next order. |
| [Renewal webhook](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro/renewal-webhook) | Sends a webhook to your systems before each subscription renews. |
| [Annual renewal notice](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro/sms-reminders#annual-renewal-notice) | Emails subscribers on plans with a yearly term before the renewal. Its lead time is set by **Annual renewal notice (days before renewal)** in the **Options** card. |
| [Blackout dates](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro/blackout-dates) | Keeps monthly and yearly renewals off the days of the month you block, and moves them to the day you choose. Plans that renew every few days or weeks aren't changed. |

The table lists eight features. Every feature starts off.

A feature works only when all of these are true: Fluid has turned on Subscription Pro for your company, you turned on the feature's switch in the **Features** card, and its setup is ready. If one isn't true, the feature is off and customers see no change.

To stop one feature, turn its switch off. To stop all of them, ask Fluid to turn off Subscription Pro.

## How Subscription Pro is turned on

Fluid staff turn Subscription Pro on for your company. You can't turn it on yourself. Fluid records who turned it on and why. To ask for it, contact Fluid support.

<ContactSupport />

When Fluid turns Subscription Pro off, the features stop working, and the **Subscription Pro** screen is hidden again. Your settings stay saved, and if Fluid turns it on again, your earlier choices come back. What you already promised to customers stays in force:

| Feature | After Subscription Pro is turned off |
| --- | --- |
| Autoship profiles | The profile screens go away. Subscriptions keep their dates and keep renewing together while bundling is on. |
| Cancel flow | Cancel goes back to the standard dialog. Offers that customers accepted stay in force. |
| Renewal webhook | No new webhooks go out. A manage link already sent keeps working until it expires. |
| SMS reminders | Texts stop at once. Consent records stay. Reminder emails go back to 3 days before renewal. |
| Loyalty pricing | No new tier changes. Tier discounts already on subscriptions stay while each subscription's count is inside its tier's range. |
| Milestone items | No new items are planned. An item already on an unpaid renewal order stays on it. |
| Annual renewal notice | No new annual renewal notices go out. |
| Blackout dates | Renewal dates stop moving, and blocked days can be picked again. Dates already moved stay, and each subscription goes back to its billing day at its next renewal. Your rules stay saved. |

## What it costs

- While Subscription Pro is active, Fluid charges 0.9% + $0.25 per transaction. For example, a $100.00 transaction carries a $1.15 Subscription Pro fee.
- This fee is on top of your current transaction fee, which doesn't change.
- There is no monthly price and no setup fee.
- It applies to every settled payment while Subscription Pro is active, not only to subscription orders. A declined or failed payment carries no fee.
- The fee starts when Fluid turns Subscription Pro on, even before you turn on a feature. If your company isn't billed through Fluid platform billing yet, the fee starts when that billing starts.
- It appears on your monthly Fluid platform invoice as one **Subscription Pro** line under **Payments**, next to the **Transaction fee** line.
- The fee is billed in US dollars. Fluid converts a transaction in another currency first.
- A refund doesn't reverse the fee.
- Transactions before Subscription Pro is turned on, or after it is turned off, don't carry the fee.

SMS renewal reminders send text messages. Fluid may bill SMS segments separately, at a price Fluid sets.

See [Billing](/help/admin/settings/billing).

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Commerce**, select **Subscription Pro**. It's next to **Subscription Plans**.

The screen shows in the sidebar only when Subscription Pro is active for your company. If you open it while Subscription Pro isn't active, you see **Subscription Pro is not active for your company**.

The screen has a **Settings** tab and a **Retention** tab. The Retention report works only when **Cancel flow** is on. See [Cancel flow and win-back](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro/cancel-flow).

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs the **Access order settings and configuration (Checkout, Shipping, Taxes)** switch under **Orders**. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
</Note>

## What's on the screen

### Features

The **Features** card has a switch for each of the eight features. Turn on the features you want to use, then click **Save**.

Some features need something set up first. Until then, the switch is unavailable and a message says why:

- **Autoship profiles** needs **Bundle subscriptions automatically** on. See [Subscription Plans](/help/admin/settings/subscription-plans#bundle-subscriptions-automatically).
- **SMS reminders** needs a verified SMS sender, and Fluid must publish the final consent wording first. See [SMS renewal reminders](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro/sms-reminders).
- **Tenure pricing** needs no droplet that discounts subscription orders. See [Loyalty pricing](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro/loyalty-pricing).
- **Milestone items** needs your renewals on Fluid's current renewal system. Contact Fluid support to move them. See [Milestone items](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro/milestone-items).

**Annual renewal notice** also needs the **Subscription Annual Renewal Notice** email type on in [Communications](/help/admin/settings/communications). It is on by default. See [Annual renewal notice](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro/sms-reminders#annual-renewal-notice).

**Blackout dates** needs no setup first. If you use a blackout dates droplet, contact Fluid support before you turn it on, because the droplet must be removed first. See [Blackout dates](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro/blackout-dates).

You can always turn a feature off.

### Options

- **Reminder lead time (days)**: how many days before a renewal customers hear from you. Enter 1 to 14. The default is 3. It sets when SMS reminders and the renewal webhook go out, and the renewal reminder email while SMS reminders are on. With **Blackout dates** on, a renewal due within this lead time (or within 3 days, if that is longer) isn't moved.
- **Annual renewal notice (days before renewal)**: how many days before a renewal the annual renewal notice goes out. It applies to plans with a yearly term, and it works only when the **Annual renewal notice** switch is on. Enter 15 to 90. The default is 30.
- **Save offer cooldown (days)**: how long a customer who accepted a discount offer waits before they can see one again. Enter 30 to 730. The default is 365.

</Frame>

Click **Save** to keep your changes. **Save** saves the **Features** and **Options** cards and the **Blackout dates** section together. If someone else changed the settings while you worked, the screen reloads them and shows **Subscription Pro settings changed**. Check the settings and make your change again.

### Feature cards

The **Loyalty pricing**, **Cancel flow** and **Milestone items** cards and the **Blackout dates** section on this screen hold each feature's settings. Each of the three cards saves on its own, with its own buttons. The **Blackout dates** section has no save button of its own. It saves with the page's **Save** button, together with the **Features** and **Options** cards. When a feature is off or its setup isn't ready, its card says why. Each feature's page explains its card.

## Your customers and your copy

Subscription Pro gives you tools to text, email and make offers to your customers. You are responsible for the text you show customers, for the offers you make, and for how you collect and keep consent. Check that your practices meet the rules that apply to your business. Fluid doesn't review your text or your consent practices for legal compliance.
