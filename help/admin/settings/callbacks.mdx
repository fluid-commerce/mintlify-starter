Use the **Callbacks** screen to have Fluid call your endpoint during store events, such as a cart that needs its tax calculated. For most events, the store action waits for your response, so a slow endpoint slows it down.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **System**, select **Callbacks**.

You can also click the **Developer Hub** code icon in the top bar, then select **Callbacks** in the Developer Hub sidebar.

If you don't see **Callbacks**, your role doesn't have access to **Developer**. An admin who manages roles can give your role **View only** or **Full access** to **Developer** on the role's **Permissions** tab, in the **Settings** card. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).

## The callbacks list

The list shows your company's callbacks. Click **Create Callback** to add one.

- The **All**, **Active** and **Inactive** tabs filter the list by status. **All** is selected when the screen opens.
- Sort the list by **Event**, **Status** or **Created At**.

The list has these columns:

- **Event**: the store event the callback responds to, for example `update_cart_tax`.
- **URL**: the endpoint Fluid sends the request to. The list shortens long URLs. Hover over one to see the full address.
- **Timeout**: you can ignore this column. Fluid sets the time limit for each event.
- **Installed By**
- **Status**: **active** or **inactive**.
- **Created At**: the date and time the callback was created.

Click a row to open the callback, or use its actions menu to **Edit** or **Delete** it.

If your role has **View only** for **Developer**, **Create Callback** is hidden and the actions menu has **View** instead. You can open a callback to see its settings, but the panel has no **Save** or **Update** button.

<Warning>
  Some callbacks in this list may belong to droplets you've installed. Check with your team before you change or delete a callback you didn't create.
</Warning>

## The callback panel

The panel opens on the right when you click **Create Callback** or open a callback.

- **Definition**: the event that triggers the callback. Select it from the **Definition** menu, which lists event names such as `update_cart_tax`, `update_cart_shipping` and `redirect_cart_payment`. This field is required.
- **URL**: the address of your endpoint, for example `https://shop.example.com/fluid/callbacks`. It must start with `https://`.
- **Timeout (seconds)**: new callbacks start at 5. Fluid sets the time limit for each event, and this value doesn't change it, so you can leave it as is.
- **Active**: turn this on to have the callback receive events. New callbacks start with **Active** on.

The panel has a **Cancel** button and a **Save** button, which reads **Update** when you edit an existing callback.

<Note>
  Fluid uses a tax callback (`update_cart_tax`) only when your tax settings use **Custom Tax Integration** or **Droplet**, or, in **Per Country** mode, when a country's **Tax Mode** is **Custom Tax Integration** or **Tax Droplet**. You choose this on the [Taxes](/help/admin/settings/taxes) screen.

  The Taxes screen mentions callback country filters. You can't set them on the **Callbacks** screen. Your developer sets them through the [Fluid API](/api/overview).
</Note>

## Create a callback

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new callback">
    On the **Callbacks** screen, click **Create Callback**. The **Create Callback** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the event">
    In **Definition**, select the event you want your endpoint to handle. If you already created a callback for this event, edit it instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your endpoint">
    In **URL**, enter the address of your endpoint. It must start with `https://`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Leave **Active** on so the callback starts receiving events, then click **Save**. The new callback appears in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit a callback

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the callback">
    In the list, click the callback, or choose **Edit** from its actions menu. The **Edit Callback** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Change the fields you need, for example the **URL**, then click **Update**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  To pause a callback without deleting it, open it, turn off **Active** and click **Update**. Turn **Active** back on when you're ready to use it again.
</Tip>

## Delete a callback

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    In the list, choose **Delete** from the callback's actions menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Delete Callback?** dialog, click **Delete**. You can't undo this.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between a callback and a webhook?" id="callbacks-vs-webhooks">
    A webhook tells your service that something happened, without holding up the store action. A callback calls your service during the event, and for most events Fluid waits for your response before it continues. If you only need to know that an event happened, use [Webhooks](/help/admin/settings/webhooks) instead. Developers can find the callback API alongside webhooks in [Register and handle webhooks](/api/guides/webhooks#related-surfaces).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I save my callback?" id="callback-save-fails">
    Check these things:

    - The **URL** must start with `https://`.
    - You can create only one callback for each event. If you already created a callback for this event, open it and change its **URL** instead.
    - The `deliver_email` event accepts only one active callback for your company. If another callback for it is already active, such as one a droplet installed, Fluid rejects the save while **Active** is on.

    When Fluid rejects a save for one of these reasons, the panel closes and an error message appears, so you need to enter your details again.

    If an error appears under a field instead, the panel stays open. Fix the field and save again. For example, **Timeout (seconds)** must be a number from 1 to 30. If it shows 0, enter a number such as 5.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
