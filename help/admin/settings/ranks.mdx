Use the Ranks screen to keep your company's list of rep ranks.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Commerce**, select **Ranks**.

To open the screen, your role needs at least **View only** for **Ranks**, in the **Settings** card on the role's **Permissions** tab.

## What's on the screen

The **Create Rank** button is in the page header.

Above the table are a sort menu and a search box. Sort by **Name**, **External ID** or **Created**, or click a column header. Search finds ranks by name or by external ID.

The table lists one row per rank:

- **Name**: the rank's name.
- **External ID**: the identifier you gave the rank.
- **Created**: the date the rank was created.

Click a row to open the rank. Each row's three-dot menu has **Edit** and **Delete**.

If there are no ranks, or none match your search, the table shows **Nothing to show**.

### Rank panel fields

**Create Rank** opens a side panel titled **Create Rank**. Clicking a row or choosing **Edit** opens it as **Edit Rank**, with the rank's current details filled in. In order, it has:

- **Name** (required). No two ranks can share a name, and names that differ only in capital letters count as the same.
- **External ID**: an identifier you choose for the rank. No two ranks can share an external ID.

The panel ends with **Cancel** and **Save**. If **Name** is empty when you click **Save**, you see **Please fill in all required fields** and nothing is saved.

## Create a rank

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    Click **Create Rank**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the rank details">
    Enter a **Name** and an **External ID**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The panel closes and the rank appears in the table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit a rank

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the rank">
    Click the rank's row, or choose **Edit** from its three-dot menu. The **Edit Rank** panel opens with the rank's current details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the details">
    Change the **Name** or the **External ID**. Keep both filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The panel closes and the table shows the new details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete a rank

<Warning>
  Deleting a rank can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    In the rank's three-dot menu, choose **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Your browser asks you to confirm that you want to delete the rank. Confirm, and the rank disappears from the table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see Ranks in the Settings sidebar?" id="ranks-missing-from-settings">
    The **Ranks** item appears only if your role can view ranks. Ask an admin who manages roles to edit your role's permissions: open the **Settings** card, then set **Ranks** to **View only** or **Full access**. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I create, edit or delete ranks?" id="rank-actions-missing">
    Each action depends on the **Ranks** permissions on your role:

    - **Create Rank** needs **Create ranks** and **Edit ranks**. Without **Create ranks**, the **Create Rank** button doesn't appear.
    - **Save** on an existing rank needs **Edit ranks**. Without it, you can still open a rank, but the panel has no **Save** button.
    - **Delete** needs **Delete ranks** and **Edit ranks**. Without **Delete ranks**, **Delete** doesn't appear in the three-dot menu.

    Ask an admin who manages roles to edit your role's permissions: open the **Settings** card, then set **Ranks** to **Full access**. To grant only some actions, they can click **Ranks** and turn on the permissions listed above. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
