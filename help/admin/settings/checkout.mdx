The Checkout screen controls how your checkout looks to customers and what its form asks them for.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Commerce**, select **Checkout**.

To see **Checkout** in the sidebar and open the screen, your role needs the **Access order settings and configuration (Checkout, Shipping, Taxes)** switch under **Orders**. To load your checkout settings, it also needs the **Company Settings** switch that starts with **View global company configuration**. Without it, the screen shows default values, such as a black button color and every toggle off, instead of your saved settings, so don't save from it. To save changes, your role also needs the **Company Settings** switch that starts with **Edit company-wide settings**. Those two **Company Settings** switches don't mention Checkout, but this screen uses them. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).

## What's on the screen

The header at the top holds the **Save** button. Below it, a banner has a **Setup payment methods** button that opens [APM](/help/admin/settings/apm), where you manage alternative payment accounts.

Below the banner are the **Preview** and two collapsible sections, **Styling** and **Checkout Form**. On a wide screen, the preview sits on the left and the sections on the right. Both sections start closed, and only one opens at a time.

<Note>
  Your changes show in the preview right away, but they take effect only when you click **Save**.
</Note>

### Preview

The preview is a sample checkout that updates as you change settings. It shows:

- Your company logo, which is the **Primary Logo** from [Brand Guidelines](/help/admin/settings/brand-guidelines). Without one, a placeholder with your subdomain appears.
- One of your active products, with its image, name and price. Without an active product, a placeholder product appears.
- A **Discount Code** field with an **Apply** button.
- **Subtotal**, **Taxes**, **Shipping** and **Total** lines. Taxes and shipping are sample amounts.
- A **Contact** section with **Name** and **Email** fields, plus **Phone** when **Collect phone number** is on.
- The email and SMS marketing checkboxes, when **Collect email marketing** or **Collect SMS marketing** is on.
- A **Pay now** button.

### Styling

**Primary Button Color** sets the color of your checkout's main buttons.

You don't pick a text color. The button text turns black on light colors and white on dark colors.

### Checkout Form

This section has five toggles:

- **Collect phone number**: adds a phone number field to guest checkout.
- **Require phone number**: makes that field mandatory. Checkout asks signed-in customers without a valid phone number on file to add one.
- **Order on behalf**: when a rep is signed in and checking out their own cart, checkout shows an **Order credit** section where they choose which rep gets credit for the order.
- **Collect email marketing**: shows an **Email me with news and offers** checkbox at checkout.
- **Collect SMS marketing**: shows a **Text me with news and offers** checkbox at checkout.

## Tasks

### Change the button color

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Styling">
    Click **Styling** to expand it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the color">
    Under **Primary Button Color**, click the swatch and choose a color. You can also type a six-digit hex value, such as `#2E6DB4`, in the field next to it. The **Apply** and **Pay now** buttons in the preview change color.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. A **Checkout settings saved successfully** message appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Require a phone number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Checkout Form">
    Click **Checkout Form** to expand it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Collect phone number">
    Turn on **Collect phone number**. A **Phone** field appears in the preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it required">
    Turn on **Require phone number**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Add marketing checkboxes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Checkout Form">
    Click **Checkout Form** to expand it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the checkboxes">
    Turn on **Collect email marketing**, **Collect SMS marketing** or both. The matching checkboxes appear in the preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I save?" id="save-disabled">
    **Save** is unavailable when nothing has changed, including after you change a setting back to its saved value. It's also unavailable while the screen is loading or saving. If a **Failed to update checkout settings** message appears when you save, Fluid didn't save your changes. If it keeps failing, check that your role has the permissions listed under [Where to find it](#where-to-find-it).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I turn on Require phone number?" id="require-phone-unavailable">
    **Require phone number** needs **Collect phone number** turned on first. Until then, the toggle is unavailable and its description reads **You must enable 'Collect phone number' first**. Turning off **Collect phone number** also turns off **Require phone number**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
