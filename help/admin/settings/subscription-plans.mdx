Use the **Subscription Plans** screen to create and manage your company's subscription plans. You can also turn on subscription bundling for your whole company here.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Commerce**, select **Subscription Plans**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Subscription Plans**. To create, edit or delete plans, or change their status, it needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Subscription Plans** is in the **Commerce** card. Open the card and set access from the **Subscription Plans** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

The **New Subscription Plan** button is at the top of the page. Further down are the **Bundle subscriptions automatically** card and then the list of plans.

### Bundle subscriptions automatically

This switch applies to your whole company. When it's on, Fluid combines a customer's subscriptions that bill on the same date and ship to the same address into one order. Clicking the switch saves the setting right away.

To change it, your role needs **Full access** for both **Subscription Plans** and **Companies**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Companies** is in the **Settings** card; set it from the **Companies** area's menu, not the card's. If a **Failed to update bundle subscriptions setting** message appears and the switch turns back, check your role's access to **Companies**.

### Plans list

- The **All**, **Active** and **Inactive** tabs filter the list by status.
- Use the search box to find a plan by name.

The list has these columns:

- **Plan Name**: the plan's name. Your company's default plan has a **Company Default** badge.
- **Billing Frequency**: how often the plan bills, for example **1 Month** or **3 Months**.
- **Discount**: the plan's percentage discount, such as **10%**, or **Per Variant** when the plan's pricing is defined on each product.
- **Associated Products**: how many products the plan is linked to. The count includes products where you've turned the plan off in the product's **Manage Subscription Plans** panel.
- **Subscribers**: how many subscriptions use the plan. The count includes cancelled subscriptions.
- **Status**: **Active** or **Inactive**.

Click a plan to open it. Each row's actions menu has **Delete** and **Toggle Status**. The table header has the same menu for the rows you select, labeled with the count, such as **Delete 3 rows**.

### Plan page

The plan page opens when you click **New Subscription Plan** or a plan in the list. A new plan's page is titled **New Subscription Plan**. A saved plan's page shows the plan's name.

Badges next to the title show:

- **Active** or **Inactive**, matching the **Status** field.
- **Company Default**, when the **Company Default** switch is on.
- **Unsaved Changes**, when you've changed something you haven't saved.

On a new plan, **Create** is at the top of the page. On a saved plan, **Delete** and **Save Changes** are there instead. **Save Changes** reads **Saved** when there's nothing to save.

The main card holds the plan's schedule and pricing:

- **Subscription Plan Name**: required.
- **Billing Interval**: how often the subscription is billed. Choose a number, **Every 29** or less, and a unit (**Day**, **Week**, **Month** or **Year**). A new plan starts at 1 month.
- **Fulfillment Frequency**: how often the products in the subscription are shipped. It has the same choices as **Billing Interval** and also starts at 1 month.
- **Volume Frequency**: how often the products' volume credit (CV/QV) for commissions is assigned. It has the same choices and also starts at 1 month.
- **Pricing Strategy**: how the discount is calculated.
  - **Percent Off** applies one percentage to all variants. Enter the percentage, from 0 to 100, in the field next to it. A new plan starts at **Percent Off** with no discount.
  - **Defined on product** uses each variant's own subscription price, which you set in the product details. The percentage field is grayed out.
- **Show Savings As**: how the savings appear to customers on shop pages, product detail, cart and checkout. Choose **Percent off (Save 10%)** or **Dollar amount (Save \$5.00)**. A new plan starts at **Percent off (Save 10%)**.
- **Apply Discount To Volume**: a switch, off by default. When it's on, Fluid reduces each subscribed line's CV/QV by the same percentage as the plan's price discount. Existing subscriptions pick up the change on their next renewal. Past orders don't change.

The cards on the right:

- **Status**: **Active** or **Inactive**. A new plan starts as **Active**.
- **Company Default**: a switch that sets this plan as the default subscription plan for your company. Only an active plan can be the default.
- **Allow Skips**: a switch, off by default. When it's on, choose **Max Skips Allowed**, from 1 to 10.
- **Max Billing Cycles**: a switch, off by default, that sets a maximum number of billing cycles for the plan. When it's on, choose the number of cycles, from 1 to 100.
- **Stats**: the plan's **Subscribers** and **Associated Products** counts. They're counted the same way as the matching columns in the plans list.

## Tasks

### Create a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new plan">
    On the **Subscription Plans** screen, click **New Subscription Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the plan">
    Enter a **Subscription Plan Name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the schedule">
    Choose the **Billing Interval**, **Fulfillment Frequency** and **Volume Frequency**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the pricing">
    Choose a **Pricing Strategy**. For **Percent Off**, enter the percentage. Then choose how to **Show Savings As**, and turn on **Apply Discount To Volume** if you want it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set status, default and limits">
    Set the **Status**. Turn on **Company Default**, **Allow Skips** or **Max Billing Cycles** if you need them. For each of **Allow Skips** and **Max Billing Cycles** that you turn on, choose a number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the plan">
    Click **Create**. A confirmation message appears and you return to the plans list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To offer the plan on a product, associate it from the product's page. See [How do I offer a plan on a product?](#add-plan-to-product).

### Edit a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan">
    In the plans list, click the plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change the fields you need. The **Unsaved Changes** badge appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. You stay on the plan's page, and the button changes to **Saved**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Once a plan has subscriptions that aren't cancelled, you can change only **Company Default**, **Show Savings As**, **Allow Skips**, **Max Skips Allowed** and **Apply Discount To Volume**. You can also raise **Max Billing Cycles** or turn it off. If you change only other fields, or lower **Max Billing Cycles** or turn it on, an error message appears and nothing is saved. Changes to other fields aren't saved, even when the save succeeds, so reload the plan to check what was saved.
</Warning>

### Activate or deactivate plans

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the actions menu">
    In the plans list, open a plan's actions menu. To change several plans, select their rows and open the actions menu in the table header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Toggle Status">
    Choose **Toggle Status**. Each plan switches to the opposite status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also open a plan, change its **Status**, and click **Save Changes**.

Some plans can't be deactivated. See [Why can't I delete or deactivate a plan?](#cannot-delete-or-deactivate).

### Make a plan the company default

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan">
    In the plans list, click the plan you want as the default. It must be **Active**. If it's **Inactive**, activate it first. See [Activate or deactivate plans](#activate-or-deactivate-plans).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Company Default">
    Turn on the **Company Default** switch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. This plan becomes the company default, and the plan that was the default before no longer is.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Delete a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    In the plans list, choose **Delete** from the plan's actions menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Delete Subscription Plan?** dialog, click **Delete Subscription Plan**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To delete several plans, select their rows and choose **Delete** from the table header's menu. You can also open a plan and click **Delete** at the top of the page.

<Warning>
  Deleting a plan can't be undone. Some plans can't be deleted. See [Why can't I delete or deactivate a plan?](#cannot-delete-or-deactivate).
</Warning>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I delete or deactivate a plan?" id="cannot-delete-or-deactivate">
    You can't delete or deactivate the company default plan. To move the default to another plan, see [Make a plan the company default](#make-a-plan-the-company-default).

    You'll see this in two places:

    - In the plans list, an error message asks you to set another plan as the company default first.
    - On the default plan's page, **Delete**, **Status** and the **Company Default** switch are grayed out.

    You can't delete a plan that has subscriptions, including cancelled ones. You also can't delete a plan that an enrollment uses as its subscription plan. If you try, an error message appears.

    You can't change a plan's status while it has subscriptions that aren't cancelled. For such a plan, **Toggle Status** shows an error message.

    When you select several plans and use the table header's menu: if the selection includes the company default plan, an error message appears and none of them change. Otherwise, the action applies to every plan it can. Each plan it can't change or delete shows its own error message.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is the percentage field grayed out?" id="percentage-field-disabled">
    The **Pricing Strategy** is **Defined on product**. With that strategy, the plan uses each variant's own subscription price, which you set in the product details. Choose **Percent Off** to enter a percentage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I offer a plan on a product?" id="add-plan-to-product">
    You associate plans with products from each product's page, not from this screen. Open the product, and on its **Subscription Plans** card click **Manage Subscription Plans**. Turn on the plans you want, click **Done**, then click **Save** on the product page.

    For a bundle, turn on the **Subscription Plans** switch on the bundle's page first. Then click **Manage Subscription Plans**, or click a plan in the list if the bundle already has plans, and continue as above.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why won't my plan save?" id="plan-wont-save">
    Check that **Subscription Plan Name** isn't empty. If **Allow Skips** or **Max Billing Cycles** is on, choose a number for it. A plan with **Company Default** turned on must also be **Active**.

    **Billing Interval**, **Fulfillment Frequency** and **Volume Frequency** can each be at most **Every 29**.

    If the plan has subscriptions that aren't cancelled, you can change only some fields. See [Edit a plan](#edit-a-plan).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see New Subscription Plan?" id="no-new-plan-button">
    Your role can view subscription plans but doesn't have **Full access** for **Subscription Plans**. Without it, plans also don't open when you click them. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
