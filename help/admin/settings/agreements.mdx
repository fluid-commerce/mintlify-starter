The **Agreements** screen holds the legal agreements your checkout shows customers. For each one, you choose whether customers must accept it, can choose to accept it, or only see it as a link in the checkout footer.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Company**, select **Agreements**. Agreements is also the page Settings opens first.

## The agreements list

The list shows your company's agreements. Click **New Agreement** to create one.

- The **All**, **Active** and **Inactive** tabs filter the list by status.
- Use the search box to find an agreement.
- Use the sort button (up and down arrows) to sort the list by **Title**, **Required**, **Status** or **Created At**.

The list has **Title**, **Required**, **Status**, **Created At** and **Countries** columns.

- **Required** shows **Yes** when the agreement uses **Required Checkbox**, otherwise **No**.
- **Countries** shows flags for the agreement's countries. When there are more than two, you see the first two and a count of the rest.

Click a row to open the agreement. Each row's three-dot menu has **Edit**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**. To duplicate several agreements at once, select their rows and choose the duplicate option (for example **Duplicate 3 rows**) from the three-dot menu in the table header. The copies are added to the list.

## The agreement editor

The editor opens when you click **New Agreement** or open an agreement from the list. On a wide screen, **Agreement Details** and **Responses** are on the left, and **Status**, **Checkout Display** and **Country** are in a column on the right.

### Buttons at the top

The top of the editor has **Preview** and **Save**. On a saved agreement, it also has a three-dot menu and, on wider screens, **Translations**.

- **Preview** opens the saved agreement in checkout in a new browser tab. On a new agreement, it's grayed out until you save.
- The three-dot menu has **Duplicate** and **Delete**. On smaller screens, it also has **Translations**.

### Agreement Details

This card has the agreement's **Title**, its **Content** in a rich-text editor, and a **Preview**. The preview is a mock-up of the bottom of your checkout page with your current settings. It's different from the **Preview** button at the top.

Click the agreement's title in the preview to read its content. When you select **Required Checkbox**, the preview adds a sample **Terms and Conditions** agreement to show how required agreements combine. It isn't one of your agreements.

### Responses

A saved agreement also shows a **Responses** table below **Agreement Details**. It lists the acceptances recorded for this agreement, with **Recorded Date**, **Customer Name**, **Email** and **Status** columns.

- **Customer Name**: when the name is a link, click it to open the customer's record.
- **Status**: **Accepted**, or **Agent-confirmed** when an admin confirmed the agreement for the customer while placing their order.

Use the search box to find a response. Until someone accepts the agreement, the table shows **No responses recorded yet**.

### Status

Choose **Active** or **Inactive**. New agreements start as **Inactive**.

On your global checkout (every checkout that isn't an enrollment checkout), an active agreement appears the way you set it under **Checkout Display**. An inactive agreement is hidden. Enrollment checkouts follow different rules. See [Which agreements appear on enrollment checkouts?](#enrollment-checkout-agreements)

### Checkout Display

This card sets how the agreement appears at checkout. Choose one of three options:

- **Required Checkbox**: the customer must agree before placing the order. New agreements start with this option.
- **Optional Checkbox**: the customer can choose to accept.
- **Footer Link Only**: no checkbox. The agreement appears as a link in the checkout footer.

Below the options, a list of checkboxes adds extra settings:

- **Show on its own line**: applies to **Required Checkbox**. It gives this agreement its own checkbox. Otherwise, the agreement shares one required checkbox with your other required agreements.
- **Already checked when the page loads**: applies to **Optional Checkbox**. The box starts checked.
- **Only show when the cart has a subscription**: shows the agreement only on carts that include a subscription. It works with any of the three options.

An extra setting that doesn't apply to the selected option is grayed out. Hover over the info icon next to it to see why.

### Country

Choose the countries for this agreement. The list shows the countries your company has set up in [Countries](/help/admin/settings/countries) settings.

Once checkout has the customer's country, customers see the agreement only if that country is one you chose. Leave **Country** empty to show the agreement in every country.

## Create an agreement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new agreement">
    On the **Agreements** screen, click **New Agreement**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the title and content">
    Fill in **Title** and **Content**. Check the **Preview** in **Agreement Details** to see how the agreement will look at checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how it appears">
    Under **Checkout Display**, choose **Required Checkbox**, **Optional Checkbox** or **Footer Link Only**. Select any extra settings you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the status and countries">
    Set **Status** to **Active** when you're ready for customers to see the agreement. Under **Country**, choose the countries for the agreement, or leave it empty for every country.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The button is available once you fill in **Title** and **Content**. You return to the agreements list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit an agreement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open and edit the agreement">
    In the agreements list, click the agreement, or choose **Edit** from its three-dot menu. Make your changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. In the **Consider Creating a New Version** dialog, click **Save Changes** to update this agreement. You return to the agreements list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  In the **Consider Creating a New Version** dialog, **Duplicate** copies the last saved version, discards your unsaved edits and returns you to the agreements list. To make a new version, duplicate the agreement before you edit it. See [Create a new version of an agreement](#create-a-new-version-of-an-agreement).
</Warning>

## Create a new version of an agreement

The **Consider Creating a New Version** dialog recommends editing a copy, so you keep the earlier version.

A copy keeps the original's **Status**, **Checkout Display** settings and countries, but it isn't linked to any enrollment. Its title ends in a copy marker: `(Copy)`, or `(Copy 2)`, `(Copy 3)` and so on when you copy a copy.

<Warning>
  A copy of an active agreement is live on your global checkout right away, next to the original.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Duplicate the agreement">
    In the agreements list, choose **Duplicate** from the agreement's three-dot menu. Or open the agreement, open the three-dot menu and choose **Duplicate**. The copy opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and save the copy">
    Remove the copy marker from the **Title** and make your changes. To keep the copy off checkout until it's ready, set its **Status** to **Inactive**. Click **Save**, then **Save Changes**. You return to the agreements list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update translated titles">
    If the agreement has translations, open the copy and click **Translations**. Remove the copy marker from each language's **Agreement Title**, then click **Save** in the translations panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to the new version">
    When the copy is ready, open it, set its **Status** to **Active**, and click **Save**, then **Save Changes**. Open the old version, set its **Status** to **Inactive**, and save it the same way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update linked enrollments">
    Setting the old version to **Inactive** doesn't remove it from enrollment checkouts, so update each enrollment it's linked to. Open the enrollment (see [Which agreements appear on enrollment checkouts?](#enrollment-checkout-agreements)). In its **Agreements** section, turn on the toggle next to the new version (listed under **Available — toggle on to add**) and turn off the toggle next to the old version. Then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Translate an agreement

You can add translations after you save the agreement. The translations panel has a column for each of your company's active languages. The column marked **DEFAULT** is the agreement itself. Editing it changes the agreement's **Title** and **Content**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open translations">
    Open the agreement and click **Translations**. On smaller screens, open the three-dot menu and choose **Translations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the translations">
    For each other language, enter the **Agreement Title** and the **Description** (the translated **Content**). To fill a column automatically, click the translate icon in its header. Its tooltip reads **Translate to** followed by the language name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** in the translations panel. Changes to the **DEFAULT** column update the agreement directly, without the **Consider Creating a New Version** dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add or change your languages, click **Language Settings** in the translations panel. It opens in a new browser tab. See [Languages](/help/admin/settings/languages).

## Delete an agreement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    In the agreements list, choose **Delete** from the agreement's three-dot menu. Or open the agreement, open the three-dot menu and choose **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete** to confirm. You can't undo this.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To delete several agreements, select their rows, choose the delete option from the table header's three-dot menu (for example **Delete 3 rows**), then click **Delete** to confirm.

<Note>
  You can't delete an agreement that has responses. Fluid shows **Error deleting agreement**. When you delete several agreements and any of them has responses, Fluid deletes none of them and shows **Error deleting agreements**. To stop showing an agreement that has responses, set its **Status** to **Inactive** instead, and turn it off in the **Agreements** section of any enrollment it's linked to.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which agreements appear on enrollment checkouts?" id="enrollment-checkout-agreements">
    An enrollment checkout shows the agreements linked to its enrollment, even inactive ones. Other agreements don't appear there. If the cart has a subscription, it also shows your active agreements set to **Only show when the cart has a subscription**.

    Linked agreements still follow their **Country** and **Only show when the cart has a subscription** settings. You link agreements on the enrollment itself: in the admin sidebar, under **We-Commerce**, click **Storefront** > **Enrollments**, click the enrollment's title, and use its **Agreements** section.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
