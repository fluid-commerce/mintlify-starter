Email change history is a read-only record of who turned each of your company's emails on or off, and when. Use it to find out why an email stopped going out, or to check a change someone else made.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Company**, select **Communications**. At the top of the Communications screen, click **View Change History**.

To see **View Change History** and open the page, your role needs at least **View only** for **Company Settings**, in the **Settings** card of the role's **Permissions** tab. If your role is missing it, the page says you don't have access to email change history. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).

## What's on the screen

The page is titled **Email change history**. Its breadcrumb, **Communications** > **Change history**, takes you back to the Communications screen.

Each row is one change, newest first:

- **When**: how long ago the change happened, for example **2 hours ago**. Hover over it to see the exact date and time.
- **Who**: the admin, droplet or API token that made the change, or **System**. Under the name, **via** shows how they made it.
- **What**: the email's name, with a **Turned on** or **Turned off** badge.

Click **Load more** at the bottom to see older changes. If nobody has changed an email yet, the page says **No email changes recorded yet.**

## Filter the history

Use the filters above the list:

- **Email**: show changes to one email. The list can include emails that the Communications screen doesn't show.
- **From** and **To**: show changes made between two dates.

Click **Clear filters** to show everything again. The filters are saved in the page's address, so you can copy the link to share the same view with your team or with support.
