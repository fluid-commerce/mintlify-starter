Use the Warehouses screen to keep a list of your company's warehouse locations and their addresses. Products and bundles track their stock by warehouse.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Commerce**, select **Warehouse**.

## What's on the screen

The **Add Warehouse** button is in the page header.

Above the table are the **All**, **Active** and **Inactive** tabs, a sort menu and a search box. Sort by **Warehouse Name** or **Address**, or click either column header. Search finds warehouses by name or by any part of the address, including the country code.

The table lists one row per warehouse:

- **Warehouse Name**: the name you gave the warehouse.
- **Address**: the street address, city, state, postal code and country code.
- **Status**: an **Active** or **Inactive** badge.

Click a row to open the warehouse. Each row's three-dot menu has **Edit** and **Delete**.

If the table is empty, it shows **No warehouses yet** with an **Add Warehouse** button below it. This also happens when no warehouse matches your tab or search. Check the **All** tab or clear the search.

### Warehouse panel fields

**Add Warehouse** and **Edit** open a side panel titled **Add Warehouse** or **Edit Warehouse**. In order, it has:

- **Warehouse Name** (required). Each warehouse needs its own name.
- **Country** (required): choose the country from the list. You can type to search the list.
- **Street Address** (required).
- **Street Address 2**: an optional second address line.
- **City** (required).
- **State** (required).
- **Postal Code** (required).
- **Active**: on by default for a new warehouse. Turn it off to make the warehouse inactive.

The panel ends with **Cancel** and **Save**. If you leave a required field empty, a message such as **Postal code is required** appears under it when you click **Save**.

## Add a warehouse

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    Click **Add Warehouse**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the warehouse details">
    Choose a **Country** and fill in the other fields. You can leave **Street Address 2** empty. To add the warehouse as inactive, turn off **Active**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. You see **Warehouse created successfully** and the warehouse appears in the table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit a warehouse

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the warehouse">
    Click the warehouse's row, or choose **Edit** from its three-dot menu. The **Edit Warehouse** panel opens with the current details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the details">
    Change the fields you need. To stop using the warehouse without deleting it, turn off **Active**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. You see **Warehouse updated successfully**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete a warehouse

You can delete a warehouse only if it has no stock levels and no orders. A stock level counts even when its quantity is zero. If you try to delete a warehouse that's in use, you see an error that starts with **Failed to delete warehouse**, and nothing is deleted.

To retire a warehouse that's in use, turn off **Active** instead. It stays on the list under the **Inactive** tab.

<Warning>
  Deleting a warehouse can't be undone. The shipping methods that use the warehouse are deleted with it, and any country that used it as its **Fulfillment Warehouse** is left without one.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    In the warehouse's three-dot menu, choose **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The **Delete warehouse?** dialog asks you to confirm. Click **Delete**. You see **Warehouse deleted successfully**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What changes when a warehouse is inactive?" id="inactive-warehouses">
    The stock table on a product's page and on a bundle's page lists only active warehouses. A variant's own page can still show an inactive warehouse if the variant already has stock levels recorded there. On the **Inventory** screen, the warehouse menu still lists inactive warehouses.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which settings use my warehouses?" id="where-warehouses-are-used">
    Three settings screens let you pick a warehouse from this list. All three lists include inactive warehouses.

    - **Countries**: each country has an optional **Fulfillment Warehouse**. When you add a warehouse, it becomes the **Fulfillment Warehouse** of its country if that country is on your **Countries** screen and has no warehouse yet. See [Countries](/help/admin/settings/countries).
    - **Entities** (under **Company**): on an entity's **Entity Information** tab, each country under **Operating Countries** needs a **Warehouse**, the one that fulfills orders for that country.
    - **Shipping**: each shipping method needs a **Warehouse**. If you use **Fluid Rates**, click **Manage Shipping Methods** to see your methods. See [Shipping](/help/admin/settings/shipping).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if a save fails?" id="save-failed">
    You see **Failed to save warehouse**, sometimes with the reason. The panel closes and your details aren't saved. Open the panel again, re-enter the details and click **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see Warehouse in the Settings sidebar?" id="warehouse-missing-from-settings">
    The **Warehouse** item appears only if your role can view warehouses. Ask an admin who manages roles to edit your role's permissions: open the **Settings** card, then set **Warehouses** to **View only** or **Full access**. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I add, edit or delete warehouses?" id="warehouse-actions-missing">
    Each action depends on the **Warehouses** permissions on your role:

    - **Add Warehouse** needs **Add new warehouses** and **Edit warehouse details**. Without **Add new warehouses**, the **Add Warehouse** button doesn't appear, and an empty table shows **Nothing to show** instead of **No warehouses yet**.
    - **Save** on an existing warehouse needs **Edit warehouse details**. Without it, you can still open a warehouse, but the panel has no **Save** button.
    - **Delete** needs **Delete warehouses** and **Edit warehouse details**. Without **Delete warehouses**, **Delete** doesn't appear in the three-dot menu.

    Ask an admin who manages roles to edit your role's permissions: open the **Settings** card, then set **Warehouses** to **Full access**. To grant only some actions, they can click **Warehouses** and turn on the permissions listed above. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
