This screen controls how your company's emails go out. The **Email Delivery Provider** card sets who delivers them. The table below it lists each email type with its status.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Company**, select **Communications**.

<Note>
Your role needs these permissions, as named on the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen:

- At least **View only** for **Company Settings**, in the **Settings** card, to open this screen.
- **Full access** for **Company Settings** to set emails to **Active** or **Inactive**. Without it, **Save** doesn't appear, and the switches, tabs, search box, page buttons and **View Droplets** are disabled, so you see only the first page of emails.
- **Full access** for **Company Settings**, plus the **Integrations** switch **Configure integration settings** in the **Settings** card, to save a provider change.
- The **Droplets** switch **Browse the droplet marketplace and installed droplets**, in the **Tools & Messaging** card, to open the **Droplets** page.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

### Email Delivery Provider

Pick one option:

- **Fluid Email (Default)**: sends your emails through Fluid's email service.
- **Use a Droplet**: sends your emails through your own provider using a droplet integration. While this option is selected, a **View Droplets** button appears. It opens the **Droplets** page.

With **Use a Droplet** selected, Fluid hands your emails to your provider only when your company has an active `deliver_email` [callback](/help/admin/settings/callbacks), such as one that an email droplet sets up. Without one, Fluid keeps sending your emails itself.

<Warning>
With **Use a Droplet** selected and that callback active, if handing an email to your provider fails, the email isn't sent. Fluid doesn't send it itself instead.
</Warning>

### Email table

Each row is one email type. The table has three columns:

- **Type**: the email's name, for example **Order Confirmation** or **Subscription Reminder**.
- **Description**: a short note on what the email is for.
- **Status**: an **Active** or **Inactive** badge, next to a switch.

Above the table, use the **All**, **Active** and **Inactive** tabs to filter the list. Use the search box to find an email by its type or description.

Most types start with a word that names their group:

- **Order**: includes confirmations, notifications for your admins, refund receipts, fulfillment notices and cancellation notices.
- **Subscription**: renewal reminders, invoices, confirmations, and notices when a subscription skips its next billing, is paused, is cancelled or resumes. It also includes emails about a failed payment, a past-due subscription and a request to update a payment method.
- **Messaging**: new message notices, unread conversation reminders, and notices about premium messaging, phone numbers and TrustHub profiles.
- **Notification**: cart activity and abandonment, announcements, overdue tasks, comment replies, mentions on theme preview comments, page views, video activity and upcoming events.
- **Export**: notices that a product export or a data export is ready.
- **Enrollment**: enrollment completion reminders, and the portal invitation sent when a member completes enrollment.

<Warning>
The **Notification Cart Activity** switch also controls other notification emails, such as a rep's alerts about new orders and new leads, and mentions in messages. Setting it to **Inactive** stops those emails too. Mentions on theme preview comments have their own row, **Notification Preview Comment Mention**.
</Warning>

Rows outside those groups include:

- **Auth Signup Confirmation**: email address confirmation for new sign-ups.
- **Comment Reply Notification**: emails a contact when someone replies to a comment the contact left. **Notification Comment Reply** is a separate row, for reply notices that go to people on your team.
- **Promotion Promo Code**: delivery of promotional codes.

## Set an email to Active or Inactive

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the email">
    Use the tabs or the search box above the table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the switch on or off">
    In the **Status** column, turn the switch on to make the email **Active**, or off to make it **Inactive**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The change saves right away, and a confirmation message appears. You don't need to click **Save**.

## Change the email delivery provider

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a provider">
    Under **Email Delivery Provider**, select **Fluid Email (Default)** or **Use a Droplet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your choice">
    **Save** becomes available once you pick a different option. Click **Save** at the top of the page. A confirmation message appears when Fluid saves the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
**View Droplets** takes you away from this screen. If you switched to **Use a Droplet**, click **Save** first so your choice isn't lost.
</Tip>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit an email's content or timing here?" id="edit-content-or-timing">
    No. This screen doesn't change what an email says or when it's sent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the sender or reply-to address here?" id="sender-address">
    No. The banner at the top of this screen mentions sender details, but the screen has no sender or reply-to setting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I set up abandoned checkout emails here?" id="abandoned-checkout-emails">
    No. The banner at the top of this screen mentions abandoned checkout emails, but the screen doesn't set up follow-up emails to customers who abandon a checkout. The **Notification Cart Activity** row covers the cart activity and abandonment notifications that go to your reps.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I turn off sign-in or user invitation emails?" id="turn-off-sign-in-emails">
    No. Passwordless sign-in links, multi-factor sign-in emails and user invitations aren't listed on this screen, so you can't turn them off here. The portal invitation sent after a member completes enrollment is a different email, listed under **Enrollment**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
