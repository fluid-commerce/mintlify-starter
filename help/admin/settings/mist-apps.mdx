Use the Mist Apps screen to see the hosted apps your company has deployed with Mist and to create new ones. Apps you build and ship with the Fluid CLI or Mist Desktop appear here too.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **System**, select **Mist Apps**.

To open the screen, your role needs at least **View only** for **Mist**, in the **Other** card on the role's **Permissions** tab. To see the **Create Mist** button, your role also needs the **Mist** permission that starts with **Create new Mist hostings**.

## What's on the screen

The **Create Mist** button is in the page header. Below the header, a banner describes Mist and has a **View Mist Documentation** link, which opens the Fluid docs in a new tab.

Above the table is a search box. Search matches any part of an app's name, slug, vendor or URL, or the droplet name shown under **Hosts**.

The table lists one row per Mist app:

- **App**: the app's name, with its slug below it. If the app has no name, the slug shows in its place.
- **Status**: one of **Live**, **Provisioning**, **Pending**, **Failed**, **Pending Destroy** or **Archived**.
- **Hosts**: what the app wraps. A droplet app shows the droplet's name with **Droplet App** below it. Other hosted resources show their type, such as **Mobile Widget** or **Drop Zone**, and, when there is one, the resource's ID, such as `#123`. An app that hosts nothing shows **Standalone**.
- **URL**: the app's public address. Click it to open the app in a new tab. A dash (—) means the app has no public URL.
- **Vendor**: the app's vendor, or a dash (—) if it has none.
- **Created**: the date the app was created, such as Oct 1, 2026.

If you have no Mist apps, the table shows **No Mist apps yet**, with a reminder of the ways to deploy one. You see the same message when no app matches your search. Clear the search to see your apps again.

### Create Mist panel fields

**Create Mist** opens a side panel with the same title. In order, it has:

- **Name** (required): the app's name.
- **Host a resource**: leave this set to **None — standalone mist**.

The panel ends with **Cancel** and **Create**.

## Create a Mist app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    Click **Create Mist**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the app">
    Enter a **Name**, and leave **Host a resource** set to **None — standalone mist**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create**. You see **Mist created successfully**, the panel closes and the app appears in the table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you leave **Name** empty, you see **Give your mist a name.**

You can also deploy apps with the Fluid CLI (`fluid mist create`) or Mist Desktop. Developers can list and create Mist apps through the API too. See the [API overview](/api/overview).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens if creating a Mist app fails?" id="create-failed">
    You see **Failed to create mist**, with the reason when one is available, or **Something went wrong. Please try again.** The panel stays open with your entries, so you can fix them and click **Create** again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see Mist Apps in the Settings sidebar?" id="mist-apps-missing-from-settings">
    The **Mist Apps** item appears only if your role can view Mist hostings. Ask an admin who manages roles to edit your role's permissions: open the **Other** card, then set **Mist** to **View only** or **Full access**. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).

    The same permission also shows **AI Spend** under **Payments**. See [AI Spend](/help/admin/settings/ai-spend).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see the Create Mist button?" id="create-mist-button-missing">
    **Create Mist** appears only if your role can create Mist hostings. Ask an admin who manages roles to open the **Other** card on your role's **Permissions** tab, click **Mist**, and turn on the permission that starts with **Create new Mist hostings**. Setting **Mist** to **Full access** also works, but it turns on every Mist permission, including reading and changing environment variables.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
