Use the **Languages** screen to turn languages on or off for your company. The languages you turn on are the language options in much of the admin, such as the **Language** field when you add or edit an admin, the page editor's language preview, and translations.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Company**, select **Languages**.

## What's on the screen

The screen lists every language Fluid supports, one row per language. Above the list:

- **All**, **Active**, and **Inactive** tabs show every language, only the languages that are on, or only the languages that are off.
- The sort button (the up-and-down arrows icon) opens **Sort By**. Choose **Name** or **Users**, then **Ascending** or **Descending**.
- The search box finds a language by name in the current tab.

Each row shows:

- **Name**: the language's name.
- **Users**: how many people in your company have this as their language, including reps, customers, and admins.
- A switch at the end of the row that shows whether the language is on for your company.

## Turn a language on or off

<Warning>
  You can't turn off a language while a mobile app page or mobile app navigation has a **Language** access rule that uses it, even if the page or navigation is inactive. The change fails, and the message names the pages and navigations that use the language. If your role can't view mobile app pages, the message doesn't name them. Remove the language from those access rules first, then turn it off. See [Home Tabs](/help/admin/mobile/home) and [Navigation](/help/admin/mobile/navigation).
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the language">
    Click **Inactive** to find a language to turn on, or **Active** to find one to turn off. You can also type its name in the search box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click its switch">
    The change saves right away, and you see **Language updated successfully**. If you're on the **Active** or **Inactive** tab, the language moves to the other tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a change fails, **Failed to update language** appears and the switch returns to its saved position.

<Note>
  You can't turn off your company's default language. The change fails with a note that the default language can't be deleted. New companies start with English as their default, and this screen doesn't mark which language is the default.
</Note>

## Regional languages

A regional language is a regional version of a language, such as **Français (Canada)** next to **Français**. Use one to give shoppers and members in one region their own wording, such as "courriel" instead of "e-mail". Fluid offers three:

| Regional language | Base language |
| --- | --- |
| **Français (Canada)**, Canadian French | **Français** |
| **Español (México)**, Mexican Spanish | **Español** |
| **Português (Brasil)**, Brazilian Portuguese | **Português** |

You can turn a regional language on with its base language on or off.

<Note>
  Regional languages may not be available to your company yet. They appear in the list only after Fluid turns them on for your company. To ask for them, contact support.

  <ContactSupport />
</Note>

### Turn on a regional language

Find the regional language, such as **Français (Canada)**, and turn its switch on. You don't need to turn on **Français** first.

### Offer only the regional language

You can offer a regional language without its base language. A store for Canada, for example, can offer **Français (Canada)** and **English**, with **Français** off.

- Your storefront's language selector offers **Français (Canada)**, but not **Français**. The checkout page's language menu still lists **Français**.
- A shopper whose browser or link asks for French gets **Français (Canada)**. So does a shopper who picks French at checkout.
- Where you have no Canadian French text, shoppers still see any French text you already have, then English. Your theme's French text and Fluid's own French text fill gaps too.
- Anything you limit to **Français** still reaches people whose language is **Français (Canada)**.
- If you have no French text, translate your store into **Français (Canada)** like any other language.

### What shoppers and members see

- The regional language appears wherever people choose a language: your storefront's language selector, the language menu on the checkout page, and your portal's language picker.
- A shopper whose browser asks for Canadian French gets it on their first visit, once your company has it on.
- Where you've written Canadian French text, they see it. Everywhere else, they see your French text. Where there's no French either, they see English.
- Prices look the same as in the base language. Checkout shows dates in the regional style, such as 2026-11-20 for Canadian French.

### Give Canadian, Mexican or Brazilian wording

You don't need to translate everything again. A regional language shows its base language wherever it has no text of its own, so translate only what reads differently in that region.

- **Products, pages and other storefront content:** translate the text that differs into the regional language, the same way you translate into any language. In the **Translations** sheet, a regional language's column header says, for example, "Empty fields use Français", and an empty cell shows the base language's text as its placeholder.
- **Your theme's text,** such as button labels: type the wording that differs into the regional language's column in the page editor's **Template Translations** sheet. Saving creates the theme's language file for the regional language if it doesn't have one. See [Translate a template](/help/admin/page-editor#translate-a-template). Your theme developer can also add the file. Point them to [Regional languages in themes](/themes/developer-guide#regional-languages).
- **Fluid's own text,** such as checkout and system emails: Fluid shows its regional wording where it has one, and the base language's wording elsewhere. You don't need to do anything.

Nothing is copied from the base language. When you change your French text, Canadian French shoppers see the change at once. The exception is text you've already translated into Canadian French: from then on, that text has its own Canadian wording, and you update the two separately.

<Warning>
  Don't machine-translate your whole store into a regional language. Everything would get a Canadian French copy, and later changes to your French text would no longer reach Canadian French shoppers.
</Warning>

### Who sees what you limit by language

A person whose language is **Français (Canada)** also sees anything you limit to **Français**, such as mobile app pages, mobile app navigation and widgets. To show Canadian members something different, limit it to **Français (Canada)**. Where a mobile app page or navigation for each applies, the one for **Français (Canada)** wins. A person whose language is **Français** doesn't see what you limit only to **Français (Canada)**.

### Turn off a regional language

You can turn off a regional language and its base language in any order. When you turn off **Français**, **Français (Canada)** stays on and keeps showing your French text where it has none of its own.

When you turn a regional language off, shoppers who chose it see one of your other languages. Its saved translations are kept, so they come back if you turn it on again.

For developers, [Regional languages](/storefront/regional-languages) covers how pages pick a language, caching, and the API.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What if the language I need isn't listed?" id="language-not-listed">
    Search only looks in the tab you're on, so click **All** first, then type the language's name. The list already includes every language Fluid supports, and you can't add a new language from this screen. To ask about a language that isn't listed, contact support.

    <ContactSupport />
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are the switches grayed out?" id="switches-grayed-out">
    Your role has **View only** access to **Languages**. Ask an admin who manages roles to set **Languages** to **Full access** on your role. **Languages** is in the **Settings** category on the role's **Permissions** tab. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Français (Canada) or another regional language?" id="regional-language-missing">
    Regional languages appear only after Fluid turns them on for your company. Check the **All** tab first, then contact support to ask for them.

    <ContactSupport />
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I turn off a language?" id="cannot-turn-off">
    One of these applies:

    - It's your company's default language.
    - A mobile app page or mobile app navigation is limited to it by a **Language** access rule. Remove the language from that rule first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do Canadian French shoppers see my French text?" id="regional-shows-base">
    That's expected. A regional language shows its base language wherever you haven't written regional text. Translate into **Français (Canada)** only the text that should read differently in Canada.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see Languages in the Settings sidebar?" id="languages-missing">
    The **Languages** item appears only if your role has **Languages** set to at least **View only**. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
