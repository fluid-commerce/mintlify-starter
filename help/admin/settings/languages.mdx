Use the **Languages** screen to turn languages on or off for your company. The languages you turn on are the language options in much of the admin, such as the **Language** field when you add or edit an admin, the page editor's language preview, and translations.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Company**, select **Languages**.

## What's on the screen

The screen lists every language Fluid supports, one row per language. Above the list:

- **All**, **Active**, and **Inactive** tabs show every language, only the languages that are on, or only the languages that are off.
- The sort button (the up-and-down arrows icon) opens **Sort By**. Choose **Name** or **Users**, then **Ascending** or **Descending**.
- The search box finds a language by name in the current tab.

Each row shows:

- **Name**: the language's name.
- **Users**: how many people in your company have this as their language, including reps, customers, and admins.
- A switch at the end of the row that shows whether the language is on for your company.

## Turn a language on or off

<Warning>
  Turning off a language also removes it from the **Language** rule in the access rules of any mobile app page or mobile app navigation that uses it. Turning the language back on doesn't add it back to those rules, so you need to add it again.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the language">
    Click **Inactive** to find a language to turn on, or **Active** to find one to turn off. You can also type its name in the search box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click its switch">
    The change saves right away, and you see **Language updated successfully**. If you're on the **Active** or **Inactive** tab, the language moves to the other tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a change fails, **Failed to update language** appears and the switch returns to its saved position.

<Note>
  You can't turn off your company's default language. The change fails with a note that the default language can't be deleted. New companies start with English as their default, and this screen doesn't mark which language is the default.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What if the language I need isn't listed?" id="language-not-listed">
    Search only looks in the tab you're on, so click **All** first, then type the language's name. The list already includes every language Fluid supports, and you can't add a new language from this screen. To ask about a language that isn't listed, contact support.

    <ContactSupport />
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are the switches grayed out?" id="switches-grayed-out">
    Your role has **View only** access to **Languages**. Ask an admin who manages roles to set **Languages** to **Full access** on your role. **Languages** is in the **Settings** category on the role's **Permissions** tab. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see Languages in the Settings sidebar?" id="languages-missing">
    The **Languages** item appears only if your role has **Languages** set to at least **View only**. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
