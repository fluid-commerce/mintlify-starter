Use the Member Types screen to manage your company's member types. For each type, you set the price type its members pay and whether they can act as a rep.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Commerce**, select **Member Types**.

To open the screen, your role needs at least **View only** for **Member Types**, in the **Other** card on the role's **Permissions** tab. To create, edit or archive member types, your role needs **Full access** for **Member Types**.

## What's on the screen

The **Member types** card lists your company's member types, 10 to a page, with a search box. System member types come first. Within each group, types are sorted by name. Archived types are hidden until you turn on **Show archived**.

- **Name**: the member type's name, with a **System** or **Custom** badge. An **Archived** badge marks an archived type.
- **Pays**: the price type that members of this type pay. A type with no price type of its own pays your company's default, shown as its name followed by **(company default)**.
- **Can act as rep**: **Yes** or **No**.

With **Full access**, the card has an **Add type** menu, and each row has an actions menu with **Edit** and **Duplicate**. **Archive** is in the menu only for a type you can archive. You can't archive a system member type or a type that's already archived. You can also click a row to edit it.

If your company has no member types, you see **No member types found**. If the list can't load, you see an error such as **Couldn't load member types.** and a **Try again** button.

If your company can create its own price types, the screen is called **Member & Price Types** in the Settings sidebar, and a second card on the same page manages the price types.

### Member type panel fields

Adding, editing and duplicating a type all open the same side panel. It has three cards:

- **Details**: **Name** (required), plus a **System** or **Custom** badge. A new member type can't reuse the name of an existing one. When you create a type, the panel tells you the slug is generated automatically from the name. When you edit one, it shows the slug read-only. Renaming a type doesn't change its slug.
- **Pricing**: **Pays**, the price type that members of this type are charged. The first option is your company's default price type, for example **Retail (company default)**. Choosing it leaves the type without a price type of its own, so it follows the company default. Custom price types can't be assigned yet, so only system price types are listed.
- **Rep access**: **Can act as rep**. Turn it on to let members of this type act as a rep.

If you leave **Name** empty, you see **Name is required** when you save.

## Create a member type

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add type menu">
    In the **Member types** card, click **Add type**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start from a recommendation or a blank type">
    Choose a recommended type, **Preferred Customer**, **Affiliate**, **Employee** or **Pre-enrollee**, to prefill the panel with its name, price type and rep setting. Or choose **Blank custom type**. Nothing is created until you save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Enter or change the **Name**, for example `Ambassador`. Choose what the type **Pays**, and turn on **Can act as rep** if its members act as reps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create type**. You see **Member type created**, and the new type appears in the table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To start from an existing type instead, open its actions menu and choose **Duplicate**. The panel opens with its price type and rep setting, and a name such as **Ambassador copy**. Nothing is created until you click **Create type**.

## Edit a member type

You can also edit system member types, for example to change the price type they pay.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    Click the member type's row, or open its actions menu and choose **Edit**. The **Edit member type** panel opens with the current details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the details">
    Change the **Name**, what the type **Pays**, or **Can act as rep**. The slug stays the same.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. You see **Member type updated**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Archive a member type

Archive a custom member type you no longer use. Fluid archives a type only when nothing uses it: no member has it, and no enrollment pack, open cart or form audience points to it. Your company's default shopper type can't be archived either. An archived type is hidden from the table until you turn on **Show archived**. You can't archive a system member type.

<Warning>
  You can't unarchive a member type from this screen. Archive a type only when you're sure you no longer need it.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Archive">
    In the member type's row, open the actions menu and choose **Archive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The **Archive member type** dialog asks you to confirm. Click **Archive**. You see **Member type archived**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If Fluid refuses, the reason appears in the dialog and nothing changes. For example, **Member type is still in use and cannot be archived: 3 members** means you need to move those members to another type first. The table refreshes, so a type that can't be archived any more loses its **Archive** option.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between the company default and choosing the default price type itself?" id="company-default-vs-retail">
    Members of the type pay the same either way, and the **Pays** list shows them as one option. The company default leaves the type without a price type of its own, so it follows your company's default if that changes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I choose a price type or save?" id="pricing-tiers-unavailable">
    The panel loads your company's price types when it opens. Until they load, the **Pays** list shows **Loading price types...** and you can't save. If they can't load, you see **Price types could not be loaded.** Click **Try again**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if a save fails?" id="save-failed">
    If the name is taken, the error appears under **Name**. The panel stays open with your entries, so you can fix them and save again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see Member Types in the Settings sidebar?" id="member-types-missing-from-settings">
    The **Member Types** item appears only if your role can view member types. Ask an admin who manages roles to edit your role's permissions: open the **Other** card, then set **Member Types** to **View only** or **Full access**. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I create, edit or archive member types?" id="member-type-actions-missing">
    The **Add type** menu and each row's actions menu appear only if your role has **Full access** for **Member Types**. With **View only**, you can see the table but not change it. Ask an admin who manages roles to open the **Other** card and set **Member Types** to **Full access**. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
