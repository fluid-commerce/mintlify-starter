A metafield definition adds a custom field to one kind of record, such as products or orders. You set the field's name, identifier and data type on this screen, then fill in a value on each record. The admin calls these both metadata definitions and metafield definitions.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Commerce**, select **Metadata Definitions**.

## Permissions you need

What you can do here depends on your role's access to **Company Settings** and **Developer**. To see these permissions, open the role on the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen. On the **Permissions** tab, click the **Settings** card to open it, then click **Company Settings** or **Developer**.

- To open this screen and browse definitions, the role needs at least **View only** for both areas.
- To add, edit, pin or delete definitions, the role also needs the **Company Settings** permission that starts with **Edit company-wide settings** and the **Developer** permission that starts with **Manage webhooks and API configuration**.

<Note>
  The **Developer** permissions also let a role view API keys and manage webhooks.
</Note>

## Metadata categories

The **Metadata Definition Categories** card lists the kinds of records you can add fields to. Click a category to open its definitions:

- **Product Metadata**
- **Variant Metadata**
- **Orders Metadata**
- **Customers Metadata**
- **Pages Metadata**
- **Posts Metadata**
- **Media Metadata**
- **Enrollments Metadata**
- **Collections Metadata**
- **Categories Metadata**
- **Playlists Metadata**

## Definitions list

The category screen's header names the category, for example **Settings** › **Metadata** › **Product Metadata**. Click **Add Definition** to create a definition.

- The **All** and **Pinned** tabs filter the list. **Pinned** shows only pinned definitions.
- Use the **Search...** box to find a definition by its name, namespace, key or description.

The list has five columns: **Definition Name**, **Type**, **Namespace**, **Used In** and **Pinned**.

- **Type** shows the field's data type, for example **Single Line Text Field**.
- **Namespace** shows the definition's full identifier, its namespace and key joined by a dot, for example `custom.care_instructions`.
- In the **Pinned** column, click the pin icon to pin or unpin the definition.

Click a row to open the definition.

If translatable metafields are available to your company, a **Translatable** badge appears next to the name of each definition that is translatable. On the **Product Metadata** list, those rows also have a **Translate values** button. See [Translate field values](#translate-field-values).

## The definition form

For a new definition, the form's title names the category, for example **New Product Metafield Definition**. For a saved definition, the title shows its name. To return to the list, click the back link above the title, for example **Product Definitions**.

- **Name**: the field's label, up to 100 characters.
- **Namespace and key**: two boxes that together make the definition's unique identifier. The screen shows the identifier under the boxes as `namespace.key`. You can't change them after you create the definition.
  - The namespace starts as `custom`. It can use letters, numbers and underscores, up to 20 characters.
  - The key fills in from **Name** as you type, for example "Care instructions" becomes `care_instructions`. It can use lowercase letters, numbers and underscores, up to 30 characters.
  - You can use each namespace and key pair only once for the same kind of record, for example once among your **Product Metadata** definitions.
- **Description**: optional text that explains what the field is for.
- **Select Type**: the field's data type. It starts as **Single Line Text Field**. When you pick a type, a box shows what the type holds and which validations it supports. You can't change the type after you create the definition. See [Field types](#field-types).
- **Validations**: rules for the field's values. The options depend on the type. See [Validations](#validations).

The form also has these settings:

- **Translatable**: turn this on to give each language its own value for the field. This card is on the right side of the form. See [Translate field values](#translate-field-values).
- **Pinned**: turn this on to list the definition on the **Pinned** tab.
- **Locked**: turn this on to make the field read-only on records once it has a value. A locked field that has a value shows a lock icon, and you can't change or clear it in the admin.
- **Position**: a whole number, 1 by default.
- **Category Assignments**: product categories from your catalog, not the metadata categories above. Click **Select Categories** to open **Assign Categories**. Search your product categories, select the ones you want, then click **Done**.

## Field types

The **Select Type** list includes these types, grouped here for reference.

| Group | Types |
| --- | --- |
| Text | **Single Line Text Field** (up to 5,000 characters), **Multi Line Text Field** (up to 65,535 characters), **Rich Text Field** (text with HTML formatting) |
| Numbers | **Number Integer** (whole numbers), **Number Decimal**, **Rating** (a value on a scale) |
| Dates | **Date** (no time), **Date Time** (date and time with time zone) |
| Other values | **Boolean** (true or false), **Json** (structured JSON data), **Color** (hex colors such as `#RRGGBB`), **Url** (HTTP or HTTPS addresses) |
| Measurements and money | **Weight**, **Volume** and **Dimension** (a value and a unit), **Money** (an amount and a currency) |
| References | **File Reference** (an uploaded file), **Page Reference**, **Product Reference**, **Variant Reference**, **Collection Reference**, **Mixed Reference** (any kind of record) |

Many types also have a list version that holds several values. Its name starts with **List Of**, for example **List Of Single Line Text Field**. List versions exist for **Single Line Text Field**, **Number Integer**, **Number Decimal**, **Date**, **Date Time**, **Color**, **Url** and every reference type.

## Validations

The **Validations** section shows only the rules the selected type supports.

- **Minimum** and **Maximum**: for text types, these read **Minimum character count** and **Maximum character count**. For number types, they read **Minimum value** and **Maximum value**. Text, number, measurement, rating and money types support them.
- **Limit to preset choices**: turn this on, then enter options under **Preset choices**. Click **Add choice** for each extra option. While it's on, the form hides the **Minimum**, **Maximum** and **Regular expression** rules. **Single Line Text Field**, **Multi Line Text Field**, **Number Integer**, **Number Decimal**, **Color** and **Url** support it, and so do their list versions. Before you turn it on, clear any **Minimum**, **Maximum** or **Regular expression** values. Before you turn it off, clear every preset choice.
- **Regular expression**: a pattern that values must match. **Single Line Text Field**, **Multi Line Text Field** and **List Of Single Line Text Field** support it.
- **Maximum decimal places**: for **Number Decimal** and its list version.
- **Accept specific file types**: for file reference types. Select one or more of **Images**, **Videos**, **Documents** or **Accept all file types**.
- **Required JSON keys** and **Allowed JSON keys**: for **Json**. Click **Add key** for each extra key.

**Boolean**, **Page Reference**, **Product Reference**, **Variant Reference**, **Collection Reference** and **Mixed Reference** have no validations.

## Where fields show up

Each definition appears as a field in the **Metafields** card on the matching record's page in the admin, for example on a product, an order or a customer. On a variant's page, the card is called **Variant metafields**. Enter a value there to fill in the field for that record.

If you can edit the record, the card also has a **Manage Definitions** button. It opens that category's definitions on this screen.

## Translate field values

A translatable field can hold a different value for each language your company sells in. Shoppers then see the value in their language, and the original value stays the one you entered.

<Note>
  This feature may not be available to your company yet. If you don't see the **Translatable** card on the definition form, it isn't on for your company.
</Note>

### Which fields can be translatable

A definition can be translatable when all of these are true:

- It belongs to **Product Metadata**, **Variant Metadata** or **Enrollments Metadata**.
- Its type is **Single Line Text Field**, **Multi Line Text Field**, **Rich Text Field**, **List Of Single Line Text Field** or **Url**.
- **Limit to preset choices** is off.

If a definition doesn't qualify, the **Translatable** switch is grayed out and the text under it gives the reason, for example "Only text fields on products, variants, or enrollment packs can be translated." or "Fields with preset choices can't be translated."

### Make a field translatable

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the definition">
    On the **Metadata Definitions** screen, click the category, then click the definition. To make a new field translatable, click **Add Definition** and fill in the form as in [Add a definition](#add-a-definition).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Translatable">
    In the **Translatable** card, turn on the switch. The card's text reads "Lets each language have its own value for this field."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes** on a saved definition, or **Create Definition** on a new one. The definition now has a **Translatable** badge in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Developers and agents can do the same through the API. See [Translate with the API](/storefront/metafields#translate-with-the-api).

Turning the switch off keeps every translation you saved. Shoppers see the original value again, because translations only show while the definition is translatable. Turn it back on and the saved translations show again.

### Where you translate the values

You translate the values in these places:

- **One record at a time.** Open a product, a variant or an enrollment pack, then click **Translations**. The sheet has a **Metafields** section. See [Translate product metafields](/help/admin/products#translate-product-metafields), [Translate variant metafields](/help/admin/products#translate-variant-metafields) and [Translate enrollment pack metafields](/help/admin/enrollments#translate-enrollment-pack-metafields).
- **One field across all products.** On the **Product Metadata** list, click **Translate values** on a translatable row. The [Translate metafields](/help/admin/settings/metadata-definitions/translate) page lists every product that has a value for the field, so you can translate, export or AI-translate them together. Variant and enrollment pack fields have no such page. Translate them on the record.

Shoppers see a translated value only where your theme prints the field. See [Theme variables](/themes/theme-variables#translated-metafields). To read and save translations from an agent or a script, see [Translate metafields with the API](/storefront/metafields#translate-metafields).

## Add a definition

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the category">
    On the **Metadata Definitions** screen, click the category you want to add a field to, for example **Product Metadata**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new definition">
    Click **Add Definition**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the field">
    Enter a **Name** and, if you want, a **Description**. If you need a different namespace or key, change it after you finish the name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the type and validations">
    In **Select Type**, choose the data type before you set any **Validations**. If you change the type after you set validations, go back to the list and start the definition again. You can't change the type or validations after you create the definition.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the options">
    Set **Pinned**, **Locked**, **Position** and **Category Assignments** if you need them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the definition">
    Click **Create Definition**. You return to the category's list, where the new definition appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit a definition

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the definition">
    In the category's list, click the definition.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change **Name**, **Description**, **Pinned**, **Locked**, **Position** or **Category Assignments** as needed. If the definition has validations, **Validation Rules** shows them as read-only code under **Current rules**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. The button reads **Saved** when there's nothing new to save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete a definition

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the definition">
    In the category's list, click the definition.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    Click **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Delete Metafield Definition?** dialog, click **Delete Definition**. You can't undo this.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I change a definition's type or key?" id="change-type-or-key">
    Create a new definition with the type, namespace and key you want.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why won't my definition save?" id="definition-save-fails">
    Check for these common causes:

    - The key is longer than 30 characters, or the namespace is longer than 20. A long name can make a long key, so shorten the key.
    - Another definition for the same kind of record already uses the same namespace and key.
    - You changed **Select Type** after you set validations. Start again and choose the type first, as in [Add a definition](#add-a-definition).
    - Your role doesn't have the **Developer** permission that starts with **Manage webhooks and API configuration**. See [Permissions you need](#permissions-you-need).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my key change?" id="key-changed">
    On a new definition, the key fills in again from **Name** each time you edit the name. Finish the name first, then change the key.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Add Definition?" id="no-add-definition-button">
    Your role doesn't have the **Company Settings** permission that starts with **Edit company-wide settings**. Without it, you can open definitions but can't change them. See [Permissions you need](#permissions-you-need) for what your role needs, and ask an admin who manages roles to update your role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is the Translatable switch grayed out?" id="translatable-grayed-out">
    The definition can't be translated. The text under the switch says why: its category isn't products, variants or enrollment packs, its type isn't one of the five text types, or **Limit to preset choices** is on. You can't change the type or the choices after you create the definition. Create a new definition instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see the Translatable card?" id="no-translatable-card">
    Translatable metafields aren't on for your company yet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why doesn't the screen show any categories?" id="categories-dont-load">
    If the **Metadata Definition Categories** card is empty, or you see **Failed to load metadata categories**, your role may not have access to **Developer**. See [Permissions you need](#permissions-you-need) for what your role needs, and ask an admin who manages roles to update your role.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
