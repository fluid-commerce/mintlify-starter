Tax change history is a read-only record of changes to your tax settings: who changed what, and when. Use it to find out why tax on your orders changed, or to check a change someone else made.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Commerce**, select **Taxes**. At the top of the Tax screen, click **View Change History**.

To see **View Change History** and open the page, your role needs both of these, in the **Settings** card of the role's **Permissions** tab:

- **View company details** under **Companies**
- **View company country settings** under **Company Countries**

If your role is missing either, ask an admin who manages roles to update it. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).

## What's on the screen

The page is titled **Tax change history**. Its breadcrumb, **Tax** > **Change history**, takes you back to the Tax screen.

Each row is one change, newest first:

- **When**: how long ago the change happened, for example **2 hours ago**. Hover over it to see the exact date and time.
- **Who**: the admin or droplet that made the change, or **System**. Under the name, **via** shows how they made it.
- **What**: what changed, such as **Company tax mode changed**, **Country tax settings updated** or **Avalara account updated**. Changes to your Avalara account show a **Production** or **Sandbox** badge for the environment.
- **Where**: **Company** for a company-wide change, or the country's flag and name.
- **Changes**: the first two fields that changed, from the old value to the new one, for example **Tax mode: Fluid's Tax Solution → Avalara**. If more fields changed, the row says how many more. Click **Show details** to see every field, and **Hide details** to close them.

Credentials and tax IDs are never shown. Those fields read **changed (hidden)**.

Click **Load more** at the bottom to see older changes.

## Filter the history

Use the filters above the list:

- **Country**: show changes for one of your countries.
- **Action**: show one kind of change, such as **Avalara settings updated** or **Avalara connection tested**.
- **From** and **To**: show changes made between two dates.

Click **Clear filters** to show everything again. The filters are saved in the page's address, so you can copy the link to share the same view with your team or with support.
