Use the Fair Share screen to decide how commission is attributed for orders that come through shared links. To learn how Fair Share works out which rep's link a customer came from, see [FairShare: sales rep attribution](/concepts/fair-share).

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Company**, select **Fair Share**.

To open the screen, your role needs at least **View only** for **Company Settings**, in the **Settings** card on the role's **Permissions** tab. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).

## What's on the screen

Below the **Fluid Fair Share** banner, the screen has four setting cards and an **Attribution Placement** reference card. Each setting card has an **Attribution Target** list where you pick one option.

### Shared links (multi-rep)

The **Shared Links (multi-rep)** card decides who gets commission when a customer clicks two or more reps' links before buying.

- **First Touch**: Attribution goes to the first rep whose link was clicked before purchase.
- **Last Touch** (recommended): Attribution goes to the final rep whose link was clicked before purchase.
- **Most Activity**: Attribution goes to the rep whose shared link generated the most activity from the customer.

### Sponsored customer re-orders

The **Sponsored Customer Re-Orders** card decides who earns commission when a sponsored customer buys through another rep's link.

- **Sponsor** (recommended): Commission goes to the customer's sponsor, even if they click another rep's link.
- **Sharing Rep**: Commission goes to the rep whose link the customer used for the re-order, regardless of sponsor.
- **Share Rep & Reassign**: Commission goes to the rep whose link was used, and the customer's sponsor record is reassigned to that rep.

### Orphaned orders

The **Orphaned Orders** card decides how commission is attributed when a sale occurs through a non-representative shared link. An orphaned order is one with no sponsor and no rep's link to credit.

- **Leave Orphaned**: The sale remains unattributed and no rep receives commission.
- **Select single rep for all orphans**: Every unattributed sale is assigned to one rep that you choose. When you select this option, a **Select Rep** field appears under it.
- **Random Top Sharer** (recommended): Unattributed sales are randomly distributed among the reps with the highest recent share activity.

### Enrollment orders

The **Enrollment Orders** card decides who earns commission on enrollment orders.

- **New Rep**: Commission goes to the customer who placed the enrollment order and became a new rep.
- **Current Sponsor**: Commission on enrollment orders goes to the customer's current sponsor.

### Attribution placement

The **Attribution Placement** card has nothing to set. It explains that Fair Share detects a rep's username for attribution whether it's in the subdomain or in the path of a link. It shows these examples:

- `username.example.com/home/shop`
- `example.com/username/shop`

The card's **Attribution Placement Documentation** link opens the [FairShare SDK overview](/sdk/overview).

## Change an attribution setting

<Note>
  To change these settings, your role needs **Full access** for **Company Settings**. Without it, the options are disabled and **Save** doesn't appear.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select an option">
    In the card for the situation you want to change, select an option under **Attribution Target**.

    If you select **Select single rep for all orphans** on the **Orphaned Orders** card, a **Select Rep** field appears. Search by name or email, then select the rep from the results. Choose a rep before you click **Save**. If you save without one, orphaned orders stay unattributed. To pick a different rep later, click **Edit** on the **Representative** card and search again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** in the top-right corner of the screen. One save applies your selections in all four cards. A **Fair Share settings saved** message confirms the save. If you leave the screen without saving, your selections aren't applied.
  </Step>
</Steps>
