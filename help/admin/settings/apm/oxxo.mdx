OXXO lets shoppers in Mexico pay for an order in cash at an OXXO convenience store. Fluid connects to OXXO through PPRO, a payment provider.

| Detail | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Country | Mexico |
| Currency | MXN only |
| Order amount | From MXN 1.00. PPRO allows up to MXN 10,000. |

## How it works for your shopper

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose OXXO">
    At checkout, the shopper chooses **OXXO** and places the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the voucher">
    The shopper gets a voucher with a barcode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay in cash">
    The shopper pays the voucher in cash at any OXXO store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order confirmed">
    Fluid confirms the order automatically when the payment arrives. This can take hours or days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While the shopper waits, checkout shows **Payment in progress**.

## Stock

Fluid holds the items while the voucher is open, so they stay reserved for the shopper.

## Refunds

Fluid can't refund OXXO payments automatically. To refund an OXXO order, use PPRO's manual refund process. PPRO collects the shopper's bank details.

If you try to refund an OXXO order in Fluid, Fluid tells you this.

## Set up OXXO

<Note>
  PPRO must have OXXO enabled on your live contract. Ask PPRO before you add the account.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open APM">
    Click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Payments**, select **APM**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the account">
    Click **Add Payment Account**. Enter a **Display Name** and a **Reporting Name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the payment type">
    Select **OXXO (PPRO)** as the **Payment Type**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign Mexico">
    Under **Available Countries**, select **Mexico**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your PPRO details">
    Fill in the fields the form shows for the payment type, and turn on **Active**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add Account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For the full form, see [APM](/help/admin/settings/apm).
