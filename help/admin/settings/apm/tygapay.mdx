Shoppers can subscribe and pay with crypto (USDC) through TygaPay. At the first checkout, the shopper approves a recurring agreement in TygaPay. Fluid then charges each renewal against that agreement. The shopper does nothing at renewal time.

<Warning>
  TygaPay crypto subscriptions work on TygaPay **sandbox** accounts only. TygaPay does not yet allow recurring orders on live accounts. Live TygaPay accounts are not offered on subscription carts, and you cannot set a live TygaPay account to recurring-only.
</Warning>

## How it works for your shopper

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose TygaPay">
    At checkout, the shopper pays for a cart with a subscription item by choosing TygaPay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the agreement">
    The shopper approves a recurring agreement in TygaPay. The agreement sets the limits Fluid can charge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Renewals are charged automatically">
    Fluid charges each renewal against the agreement. The shopper takes no action.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The limits the shopper approves

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Per charge | 2 times the larger of the cart total before points and the first payment. |
| Per period | The per-charge limit times the number of subscription items in the cart. |
| Schedule | The shortest subscription cadence in the cart. |
| Length | The agreement ends after 5 years at most. |

## Renewals that stay pending

A renewal can be pending. If the shopper did not approve a wallet, TygaPay sends a pay link, and the renewal waits up to 24 hours for the shopper.

Fluid settles a pending renewal when TygaPay sends its webhook. Fluid also re-checks pending renewals on a schedule.

## When the agreement stops working

The next renewal declines if the shopper does any of these, or if the agreement expires:

- Revokes the agreement in their wallet.
- Removes the spending limit.

After a decline, the normal dunning flow asks the shopper for a new payment method.

## When Fluid cancels the agreement

Fluid cancels the agreement at TygaPay in either of these cases:

- The last Fluid subscription that uses the agreement is cancelled.
- The subscription moves to another payment method.

TygaPay does not email the shopper about this. Fluid's subscription cancelled email does.

## Points

A subscription cannot be paid fully with points on TygaPay, or on another redirect payment method. The shopper must pay part of it with the payment method.

## Set up TygaPay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the payment account in Fluid">
    On [APM](/help/admin/settings/apm), click **Add Payment Account** and choose the TygaPay payment type. Use your TygaPay **sandbox** account, because live accounts are not offered on subscription carts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the webhook events">
    In the TygaPay portal, open the webhook endpoint for Fluid. Select the `order.*`, `refund.*` and `mandate.*` events.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Subscription emails">
    In the TygaPay portal, go to **Settings** › **Customers** and turn on **Subscription emails**. A shopper who did not approve a wallet then gets the renewal pay link.
  </Step>
</Steps>
