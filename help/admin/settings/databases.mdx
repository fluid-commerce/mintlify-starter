The Databases screen controls which reporting databases admins with reporting access can view in the Mist desktop app and query with Mist agents. It holds your company's Fluid database, which syncs on a schedule you set, and any databases from connectors you install.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **System**, select **Databases**.

To open the screen, your role needs **Full access** for **Reporting Databases**, in the **Other** card on the role's **Permissions** tab.

## What's on the screen

The introduction at the top of the screen has a **View Mist Documentation** link, which opens the Fluid docs in a new tab.

Below it, the **Enabling a database carries risk** notice explains the risks of turning on a database.

<Warning>
  Turning on a database makes its contents queryable by Mist agents and by every admin who holds a reporting-database role, including any personal, financial, health or otherwise confidential data it holds. Query content and results may be processed by third-party AI model providers. Read the full notice on the screen before you turn on a database.
</Warning>

If your settings can't load, the screen shows **Couldn't load reporting database settings** and a **Try Again** button.

### Fluid card

The **Fluid** card is for syncing your company's Fluid data, such as orders, customers and products, into a dedicated reporting database. It has:

- **Sync Now**: requests a sync. It's available only while the Fluid database is turned on. While the request is being sent, the button reads **Syncing...**.
- **Last synced**: the date and time of the last sync, shown next to **Sync Now** once there's a sync to show.
- **Sync every**: the number of hours between syncs, as a whole number of 1 or more.
- A switch at the right of the card, next to **Sync every**, that turns syncing of the Fluid reporting database on or off.

At the bottom of the card, a note says that Fluid charges per sync for database storage and transfer. It gives the cost range for each sync, which depends on the size of your database.

### Connectors

The **Connectors** section lists the databases from your installed connectors. Each row shows the connector's name, a **via Fluid Connect** badge and a switch. The switch lets admins with reporting access view that connector's data in the Mist desktop app, with access tracked per user.

If the list is empty, it shows **No connector databases yet** and a **Browse the Droplet Marketplace** button.

### Who has access

The **Who has access** section shows, as badges, the roles that can view and query reporting databases in Mist. Each admin connects separately, and every connection is tracked by user. If no role is listed, the section says that only full-access admin roles hold the reporting-database permissions.

**Manage Roles** opens the **Roles** screen. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).

## Turn on the Fluid database

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the risk notice">
    Read the **Enabling a database carries risk** notice near the top of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the switch">
    On the **Fluid** card, turn on the switch at the right of the card. You see **Fluid reporting database updated**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To turn the Fluid database off, turn off the same switch. **Sync Now** is unavailable while the switch is off.

## Change how often Fluid data syncs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the number of hours">
    On the **Fluid** card, enter a whole number of 1 or more in **Sync every**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the change">
    Press Enter, or click outside the field. You see **Fluid reporting database updated**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you enter a number that isn't whole, or is less than 1, the field goes back to the saved value and nothing is saved.

## Request a sync now

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Sync Now">
    On the **Fluid** card, click **Sync Now**. The Fluid database must be turned on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the confirmation">
    You see **Sync requested**, or **Sync requested. It will run on the next scheduler tick.** If the request fails, you see **Failed to request sync**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Turn a connector database on or off

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the risk notice">
    Read the **Enabling a database carries risk** notice near the top of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the connector's switch">
    Under **Connectors**, turn the switch at the end of the connector's row on or off. You see a message with the connector's name and **viewing data in Mist enabled** or **viewing data in Mist disabled**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see Databases in the Settings sidebar?" id="databases-missing-from-settings">
    The **Databases** item appears only if your role can manage reporting databases. Ask an admin who manages roles to edit your role: on the role's **Permissions** tab, open the **Other** card, then set **Reporting Databases** to **Full access**. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I add a connector database?" id="add-connector-database">
    Fluid Connect brings data from your other platforms into Fluid as reporting databases. Install a database-capable connector from the droplet marketplace, and its database appears under **Connectors**. If you have no connector databases yet, click **Browse the Droplet Marketplace** to open the marketplace.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Sync Now unavailable?" id="sync-now-unavailable">
    **Sync Now** works only while the Fluid database is turned on. Turn on the switch on the **Fluid** card first. The button is also unavailable while a sync request is being sent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if a change doesn't save?" id="change-not-saved">
    You see **Failed to update setting**, and the screen reloads your saved settings, so the switch or field shows its saved value again. Make the change again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
