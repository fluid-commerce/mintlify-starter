When a customer cancels a subscription, Fluid can ask why. With the cancel flow, Fluid then shows up to two save offers that fit the reason, such as a skip or a pause. The **Retention** tab shows how many customers each reason and offer saves. Win-back emails offer a discount to customers after they cancel.

Cancellation reasons are for every company. The cancel flow, experiments, the **Retention** tab and win-back emails are [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro) features.

## Cancellation reasons

Every company records why a subscription was cancelled. Fluid is adding an optional reason list to the customer's cancel dialog and turns it on for each company. With it on, a customer who cancels from their subscription page can pick one of these reasons:

- It costs too much
- I have too much product
- It arrives too often
- I am not seeing results
- I did not like the product
- I am switching to another brand
- Shipping or delivery problems
- I want to take a break
- I am moving or my life changed
- Something else

With **Something else**, the customer can add a note of up to 500 characters.

Fluid also records a reason when someone other than the customer cancels. For example, Fluid records it when an admin cancels, when a subscription reaches its last billing cycle, or when a customer is deleted. When a customer gives no reason, Fluid records that no reason was given.

The reason is sent with the subscription cancelled webhook. See [Register and handle webhooks](/api/guides/webhooks). The admin subscription screens don't show the reason yet.

## Before you start

To use the cancel flow:

- Subscription Pro is active for your company.
- **Cancel flow** is on in [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro).

## Where to find it

Open [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro). The **Cancel flow** card is on the **Settings** tab. The **Retention** tab is next to it.

## How the cancel flow works for customers

The cancel flow runs in the cancel dialog on the customer's subscription page.

1. The customer clicks **Cancel subscription**. Fluid asks for a reason. This step is optional.
2. After the customer clicks **Continue**, Fluid shows the save offers for that reason, up to two. With no offers, the customer sees a short goodbye.
3. The customer accepts an offer, keeps the subscription, or cancels.

<Frame caption="Two save offers for the reason the customer chose.">
  <img src="/images/help/subscription-pro/cancel-flow-offers.png" alt="A cancel dialog that offers a one-month pause or a skip" />
</Frame>

The customer can cancel on every step, also before they choose a reason. A customer who cancels from the reason step sees no offers. There is only one offer screen, and no offer shows after the customer confirms the cancel. Each offer shows the date of the next order, and its total when Fluid can work it out in advance.

- Only the customer who owns the subscription sees the cancel flow. When a rep or an admin cancels for a customer, the plain cancel runs, with no offers.
- The cancel flow runs on the checkout subscription page. The customer portal, the member storefront and the reminder manage link page keep the plain cancel for now.
- A cancel session lasts 30 minutes. After that, the customer can still cancel, but can't accept an offer.
- If you turn the cancel flow off, offers that customers already accepted stay in force.

## Edit the cancel flow

The flow starts as the **Default flow**. It has no discounts:

- **I have too much product**: skip the next order, or delay it 30 days.
- **It arrives too often**: delay the next order 30 days, or skip it.
- **I want to take a break**: pause for 1 month, or skip the next order.
- **I am moving or my life changed**: pause for 2 months.

One flow applies to all your subscriptions. Until you publish your first version, customers get the **Default flow**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the reasons to show">
    In the **Cancel flow** card, use each reason's **Shown** switch to show or hide it. Use **Up** and **Down** to change the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Translate the labels">
    To show a reason in another language, click **Add a label** under **Labels**, enter the language code, such as `es` or `pt-BR`, and enter the text. A label has up to 100 characters. Without a label, customers see Fluid's standard wording in their language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add save offers">
    Under **Save offers**, click **Add an offer**, choose the type, and set its terms. Each reason has up to 2 offers, of different types. Offer types that cost you money say so in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish version**. The button shows the number of the new version. The new version applies to customers who start cancelling from then on. To undo your edits before you publish, click **Discard changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can't change a published version. Each publish makes a new version, and older versions stay. If someone else published while you edited, the card reloads the latest version. Make your change again.

You can hide reasons, but you can't add, rename or delete them. At least one reason must show.

### Save offer types

| Offer | Terms |
| --- | --- |
| **Skip the next order** | None. |
| **Delay the next order** | **Days to delay**, 7 to 60. |
| **Change how often it ships** | **Plan to move to**: another plan of the same product. The price doesn't change. |
| **Swap to another variant** | 1 to 50 variants: another variant of the same product. The price doesn't change. |
| **Pause** | **Months to pause**, 1 to 3. |
| **Discount** | **Percent off**, 1 to 100. **Applies to** **The next order** or **Several orders**, 2 to 12. |
| **Contact support** | **Support page link**, an `https` address of up to 2,048 characters. This offer shows a link. It doesn't keep the subscription. |

**Free gift** shows in the list but isn't available yet.

Fluid leaves out an offer that the subscription can't take. For example, it leaves out a skip when the plan doesn't allow skipping, and a delay or a pause that goes past the plan's date limit.

A discount is the only offer that costs you money. A customer who accepted a discount, from the cancel flow or from a win-back email, sees no discount offer for the number of days in **Save offer cooldown (days)** on the Subscription Pro screen. The default is 365 days. The cooldown covers all of that customer's subscriptions with your company. Offers that cost nothing have no cooldown.

On the customer's subscription page, a discount from a save offer shows as **Special discount**, not as a code.

## Test two flows with an experiment

An experiment shows one published version to some customers and another version to the rest. Use it to find which offers save more subscriptions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the experiment card">
    Open the **Retention** tab. Find the **Experiment** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the versions">
    Choose the **Control version** and the **Variant version**. Both must be published versions, not the **Default flow**. Set **Customers on the variant (%)** from 1 to 99.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **Start experiment**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each customer always sees the same version during an experiment. Only one experiment runs at a time. To end it, click **Stop experiment** and confirm. New sessions then run the latest published version. You can't restart a stopped experiment, but you can start a new one. The card lists your latest 20 experiments.

Each version needs 200 sessions with a reason before its save rate means much. Until then, the card says there is no winner yet.

## Read the Retention tab

The **Retention** tab shows only when Fluid has made the cancel flow available to your company.

Choose a range: **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, or your own **From** and **To** dates. The default is the last 30 days, and the range can be up to 366 days. Days are in UTC, and a session counts on the day it started.

<Frame caption="The Retention tab for the last 30 days.">
  <img src="/images/help/subscription-pro/retention-report.png" alt="The Retention tab with sessions, save rate, cancellations and the Reasons table" />
</Frame>

- **Sessions** counts customers who started to cancel, and how many gave a reason.
- **Save rate** is the saved sessions divided by the sessions with a reason. Only a customer who gave a reason can see an offer.
- **Cancellations** counts customers who cancelled, and how many left without deciding. A session with no outcome after 30 minutes counts as left without deciding.
- The **Reasons** table shows sessions, saves, cancellations and save rate for each reason.
- The **Offers** table shows how often each offer type was shown and accepted.
- **Results by arm** compares the two versions in an experiment.

## Win-back emails

A win-back email offers a discount to a customer some days after they cancel. The customer clicks **Reactivate my subscription** in the email. On the page that opens, they click **Reactivate my subscription** again to restart the subscription with the discount. Nothing happens when the page opens.

### Set up the win-back email

The win-back settings are part of the cancel flow, so you publish them with it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Win-back section">
    In the **Cancel flow** card, scroll to the **Win-back** section at the bottom. Turn on **Send win-back**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the timing">
    In **Days after cancel**, set how many days after the cancel the email goes out, 1 to 90. In **Link valid for (days)**, set how long the offer lasts, 1 to 30 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the discount">
    Under **Discount offer**, set **Percent off**, 1 to 100. Under **Applies to**, choose **The next order**, or **Several orders** and a **Number of orders** from 2 to 12.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish version**. The win-back settings apply from the new version.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The Win-back section: the email goes 21 days after the cancel, with 25% off the next order for 10 days.">
  <img src="/images/help/subscription-pro/win-back-settings.png" alt="The Win-back section of the Cancel flow card with Send win-back on, Days after cancel 21, Link valid for 10 days, Percent off 25 and Applies to The next order" />
</Frame>

- A flow that never had a win-back starts with the switch off, 14 days after the cancel, a link valid for 7 days, and 10% off the next order.
- A value out of range shows an error under its field, and you can't publish until you fix it.
- The offer is always a percent-off discount.
- If you turn **Send win-back** off and publish, Fluid keeps your settings. When you turn it on again, they come back.

### Who gets the email

- Fluid sends one win-back email for each cancellation.
- It goes only to customers who cancelled themselves, for one of the ten reasons above. It doesn't go out when an admin cancels, when payments fail, when the plan's last cycle ends, or when a customer is deleted.
- The customer must have agreed to marketing emails from your company. Fluid checks this again just before it sends.
- It isn't sent while the customer is in the save offer cooldown.
- The subscription must still be cancelled, its plan must be active, and its product must still exist.
- Your company needs a postal address on file, because the email shows it.
- The **Subscription Win Back** email type must be on in [Communications](/help/admin/settings/communications). It's on by default.
- If you turn the cancel flow off, no win-back emails go out, and open offers can't be accepted. Discounts that customers already accepted stay.

### What the customer sees

The email comes from your company. It shows the offer, the date the offer ends, a **Reactivate my subscription** button, and the product, quantity and price of the subscription.

<Frame caption="The page that the email opens. The customer must click to reactivate.">
  <img src="/images/help/subscription-pro/win-back-page.png" alt="The Your subscription offer page with 25% off the next order, the product, the offer end time and a Reactivate my subscription button" />
</Frame>

- The page shows the offer, the product and the exact time the offer ends, with the time zone. It shows no address or saved card.
- Reactivating restarts the subscription on its current plan and schedule, and adds the discount. The offer works once.
- Accepting a win-back offer starts the customer's save offer cooldown.
- The email has an **Unsubscribe** link, and the page has a **Stop these emails** link. Either one turns off the customer's marketing emails from your company. Mail apps that support one-click unsubscribe also show their own unsubscribe button.
- The link in the email works for 60 days, so the customer can still unsubscribe after the offer ends.
