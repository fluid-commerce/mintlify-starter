When a customer cancels a subscription, Fluid can ask why. With the cancel flow, Fluid then shows up to two save offers that fit the reason, such as a skip or a pause. The **Retention** tab shows how many customers each reason and offer saves. Win-back emails offer a discount to customers after they cancel.

Cancellation reasons are for every company. The cancel flow, experiments, the **Retention** tab and win-back emails are [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro) features.

## Cancellation reasons

Every company records why a subscription was cancelled. When a customer cancels from their subscription page, they can pick one of these reasons. The reason is optional.

- It costs too much
- I have too much product
- It arrives too often
- I am not seeing results
- I did not like the product
- I am switching to another brand
- Shipping or delivery problems
- I want to take a break
- I am moving or my life changed
- Something else

With **Something else**, the customer can add a note of up to 500 characters.

Fluid also records a reason when someone other than the customer cancels. For example, Fluid records it when an admin cancels, when a subscription reaches its last billing cycle, or when a customer is deleted.

The reason is sent with the subscription cancelled webhook. See [Register and handle webhooks](/api/guides/webhooks).

## Before you start

To use the cancel flow:

- Subscription Pro is active for your company.
- **Cancel flow** is on in [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro).

## Where to find it

Open [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro). The **Cancel flow** card is on the **Settings** tab. The **Retention** tab is next to it.

## How the cancel flow works for customers

The cancel flow runs on the customer's subscription page.

1. The customer clicks to cancel. Fluid asks for a reason. This step is optional.
2. Fluid shows the save offers for that reason, up to two. With no offers, the customer sees a short goodbye.
3. The customer accepts an offer, keeps the subscription, or cancels.

<Frame caption="Two save offers for the reason the customer chose.">
  <img src="/images/help/subscription-pro/cancel-flow-offers.png" alt="A cancel dialog that offers a one-month pause or a skip" />
</Frame>

The customer can cancel on every step. When an offer changes the next order, the screen shows the date and the total of that order before the customer accepts.

## Edit the cancel flow

The flow starts as the **Default flow**. It has no discounts:

- **I have too much product**: skip the next order, or delay it 30 days.
- **It arrives too often**: delay the next order 30 days, or skip it.
- **I want to take a break**: pause for 1 month, or skip the next order.
- **I am moving or my life changed**: pause for 2 months.

One flow applies to all your subscriptions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the reasons to show">
    In the **Cancel flow** card, use each reason's **Shown** switch to show or hide it. Use **Up** and **Down** to change the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Translate the labels">
    To show a reason in another language, click **Add a label** under **Labels**, choose the language, and enter the text. A label has up to 100 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add save offers">
    Under **Save offers**, click **Add an offer**, choose the type, and set its terms. Each reason has up to 2 offers, of different types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish version**. The button shows the number of the new version. The new version applies to customers who start cancelling from then on. To undo your edits before you publish, click **Discard changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can't change a published version. Each publish makes a new version.

### Save offer types

| Offer | Terms |
| --- | --- |
| **Skip the next order** | None. |
| **Delay the next order** | **Days to delay**, 7 to 60. |
| **Change how often it ships** | **Plan to move to**. |
| **Swap to another variant** | 1 to 50 variants. |
| **Pause** | **Months to pause**, 1 to 3. |
| **Discount** | **Percent off**, 1 to 100. **Applies to** **The next order** or **Several orders**, 2 to 12. |
| **Contact support** | **Support page link**, an `https` address. |

**Free gift** shows in the list but isn't available yet.

A customer who accepts a discount doesn't see another discount for the number of days in **Save offer cooldown (days)** on the Subscription Pro screen. A win-back offer counts toward the same cooldown.

On the customer's order, a discount from a save offer shows as a discount, not as a code.

## Test two flows with an experiment

An experiment shows one published version to some customers and another version to the rest. Use it to find which offers save more subscriptions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the experiment card">
    Open the **Retention** tab. Find the **Experiment** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the versions">
    Choose the **Control version** and the **Variant version**. Set **Customers on the variant (%)** from 1 to 99.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **Start experiment**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each customer always sees the same version during an experiment. Only one experiment runs at a time. To end it, click **Stop experiment**. You can't restart a stopped experiment, but you can start a new one.

Each version needs 200 customers who started to cancel and gave a reason before its save rate is reliable. Until then, the card says there is no winner yet.

## Read the Retention tab

Choose a range: **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, or your own **From** and **To** dates. The range can be up to 366 days. Days are in UTC.

<Frame caption="The Retention tab for the last 30 days.">
  <img src="/images/help/subscription-pro/retention-report.png" alt="The Retention tab with sessions, save rate, cancellations and the Reasons table" />
</Frame>

- **Sessions** counts customers who started to cancel, and how many gave a reason.
- **Save rate** is the share of sessions where the customer kept the subscription.
- **Cancellations** counts customers who cancelled, and how many left without deciding.
- The **Reasons** table shows sessions, saves, cancellations and save rate for each reason.
- The **Offers** table shows how often each offer was shown and accepted.
- **Results by arm** compares the two versions in an experiment.

## Win-back emails

A win-back email offers a discount to a customer some days after they cancel. The customer clicks **Reactivate my subscription** to restart the subscription with the discount.

- Fluid sends one win-back email for each cancellation.
- It goes only to customers who cancelled themselves and who agreed to marketing emails.
- It isn't sent while the customer is in the save offer cooldown.
- Your company needs a postal address on file, because the email shows it.
- The offer works once and ends after the number of days you set.
- The email has an **Unsubscribe** link. Unsubscribing turns off the customer's marketing emails from your company.

Win-back has no controls in the admin yet. To turn it on and set the offer and timing, contact [Fluid support](/help/getting-help).
