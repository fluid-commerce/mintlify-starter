Loyalty pricing gives a subscription a bigger discount the longer the customer keeps it. You build a program with tiers, such as 5% off from the third order and 10% off from the sixth. Fluid moves each subscription up as it reaches the next tier.

Loyalty pricing is a [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro) feature. In the **Features** card its switch is called **Tenure pricing**.

## Before you start

- Subscription Pro is active for your company.
- **Tenure pricing** is on in [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro).
- No droplet discounts subscription orders. If one does, the card shows **A droplet already discounts subscription orders**, and you can't create or publish a program.

## Where to find it

Open [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro). The **Loyalty pricing** card is on that screen. Your role needs the same access as for the rest of the screen.

## How a program counts

Each program counts in one of two ways. You choose it when you create the program and can't change it later.

- **Paid orders.** Every successful order counts, including the first one. Skipped orders and failed payments don't count. While a subscription is paused, its count stays the same.
- **Months subscribed.** Fluid counts whole months from the day the subscription started. Months keep counting through a skip, a failed payment or a pause.

A customer who cancels and comes back keeps their progress. A refund doesn't change progress.

## Create a program

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a program">
    In the **Loyalty pricing** card, click **New program**. The **New loyalty program** form opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and choose what it covers">
    Enter a **Program name**. Under **Count by**, choose **Paid orders** or **Months subscribed**. Under **Applies to**, choose **All subscriptions**, **Specific subscription plans** or **Specific products**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the tiers">
    Under **Tiers**, click **Add tier** for each tier. For each one, set **From order** or **From month**, a **Label**, and the **Discount type**: **Percent** with **Percent off**, or **Amount** with **Amount off**. A tier runs until the next tier starts. The last tier has no end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the effect on volume">
    Turn on **Discounts reduce CV and QV** if the discount should lower the subscription's CV and QV. It's off by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the draft">
    Click **Create program**. The program is a **Draft** until you publish it. To change a draft or a published program later, click **Edit** next to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Tier rules:

- A program has 1 to 10 tiers.
- On **Paid orders**, the first tier starts at order 2 or later. On **Months subscribed**, it starts at month 1 or later. Each tier starts after the one before it.
- A tier label has 1 to 50 characters.
- A percent is more than 0 and at most 100. An amount is more than 0.

## Publish a program

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the publish preview">
    Click **Publish**. The **Publish loyalty pricing** dialog shows how many subscriptions move up, move down and stay where they are.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Confirm and publish**. The program's status changes to **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After you publish, Fluid places existing subscriptions in their tiers in the background. From then on, Fluid checks after each successful renewal and once a day. When you change a published program, a subscription that moves down keeps its current tier until its next renewal.

## End a program

Click **Archive program**. Choose what happens to discounts that subscriptions already have:

- **Keep current discounts** leaves each subscription at its current tier. This is the default.
- **Remove at next renewal** takes the discount off at each subscription's next renewal.

The program's status changes to **Archived**.

## How it combines with other discounts

A loyalty discount compounds with another percent discount. For example, a 10% loyalty discount with another 10% discount is 19% off in total, not 20%.

Loyalty discounts don't appear in the main **Promo Codes** list, and you can't edit or delete them there. Customers can't enter them as codes.

## What customers see

On their subscription page, customers see a **Loyalty pricing** section. It shows the tier label and a line such as "Order 5 saves 10%. 2 more orders to unlock 15%." A **How loyalty pricing works** section explains how orders or months count. On the price, the discount shows as **Loyalty discount**.

These customer texts are in English only for now.

## Bring over tenure from another platform

If your customers had subscriptions on another platform, their earlier orders or start dates can count toward their tier. When you create subscriptions through the API, you can send each subscription's earlier order count and start date. Fluid support can also set them for subscriptions that already exist. Contact [Fluid support](/help/getting-help) to arrange it.
