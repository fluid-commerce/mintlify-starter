Milestone items add a free item to a subscription order when the customer reaches a milestone. For example, you can give a free bottle on the first order, or a watch on each anniversary. You can also add a one-time item to one subscription's next order.

Milestone items is a [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro) feature.

## Before you start

- Subscription Pro is active for your company.
- **Milestone items** is on in [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro).
- Your renewals run on Fluid's current renewal system. If they don't, the card shows **Milestone items cannot run yet**. Contact [Fluid support](/help/getting-help) to move them.

## Where to find it

Open [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro). The **Milestone items** card has a **Rules** tab and a **Report** tab. One-time items are on each subscription's page.

## Milestone types

| Type | When the item is given |
| --- | --- |
| **First order** | On the order that creates the subscription. |
| **A given order** | On order number N of the subscription. N is 2 or more. |
| **Every few orders** | On orders N, 2N, 3N and so on, after the first order. N is 1 or more. With 1, every renewal gets the item. |
| **Anniversary** | On the first renewal on or after each anniversary, every N months. N is 1 or more, and the default is every 12 months. |

Orders count when they are paid. The checkout order is order 1. Anniversaries count from the day the subscription started, in the subscription's time zone.

Every rule item is free. Each subscription gets an item only once for each milestone, even if a renewal is retried.

A milestone item never stops a renewal from billing. If Fluid can't add the items, the order bills without them and the items wait for the next order.

## Create a rule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a rule">
    In the **Milestone items** card, click **New rule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and choose the milestone">
    Enter a **Rule name**. Customers never see it. Under **When it is given**, choose the milestone type and its number. You can't change the type later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who gets it">
    Under **Given to**, choose **Each matching subscription** or **Once per order**. Choose which subscriptions the rule covers: all subscriptions, or specific plans, products or variants.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the items">
    Click **Add an item**, choose the variant, and set the **Quantity**. The price is fixed at **Gift (free)**. To have a backup when the item is out of stock, click **Add a fallback**. Fluid tries fallbacks in the order listed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the options">
    Set **When an item is out of stock**, **Volume** and **Visibility**, described below. To cap how often one subscription gets the items, set **Most times one subscription gets the items**. To limit the rule to a period, set **Starts** and **Ends**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and publish">
    Click **Create draft**. The rule is a draft until you click **Publish**. A published rule starts giving its items.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Rule options

- **Given to**: with **Each matching subscription**, each covered subscription on a shared order gets its own items. With **Once per order**, the order gets the items once.
- **When an item is out of stock**: **Use a fallback, otherwise give it on the next order**, **Use a fallback, otherwise skip it**, or **Skip it**. **Skip it** uses no fallbacks. The order always goes through. An item that waits for the next order and is still out of stock is skipped.
- **Volume**: **No CV or QV (gift)** or **Keep the catalog CV and QV**. A rule item is a $0 gift, so it earns no points.
- **Visibility**: **Announced** items show to the customer before the order. **Surprise** items show only on the order.
- **Most times one subscription gets the items**: leave it blank for no limit.

### Rule limits

- A rule has at most 20 items.
- The quantity is a whole number from 1 to 100.
- A **First order** item is given once, at checkout, with quantity 1, and takes no fallbacks.
- To rename a published rule or change its dates, archive it and create a new one.
- To change the items, scope or options of a published rule, click **Edit** and publish the new version. Items already given stay as they are.
- An archived rule gives nothing from then on and can't be published again. Items already given stay as they are, and items already owed to a coming order are still delivered.
- If you publish an **Anniversary** rule for subscribers who passed anniversaries already, each of them gets the items for every anniversary they passed on their next renewal. For example, a subscriber 30 months in gets the year 1 and year 2 items together. **A given order** and **Every few orders** rules match only the exact order number, so they never give past milestones.
- A **First order** gift goes only on a checkout order. A subscription that an admin creates without a checkout never gets it.
- A milestone item adds weight to the order, so a weight-based shipping rate can go up. Tax applies to the order as usual. A $0 gift adds no tax.
- A physical item can't ship on an order where every subscription is digital. It waits for a later order that ships.

## Add a one-time item to the next order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the subscription">
    Open the subscription from [Subscriptions](/help/admin/subscriptions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    On the **Milestone items** card, click **Add item to next order**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the item">
    Choose the variant and the **Quantity**, 1 to 100. Under price, choose **Catalog price** or **Gift**. Under volume, choose **Keep the catalog CV and QV** or **No CV or QV**. The volume follows the price choice, and you can change it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Click **Add item**. The item shows on the card and goes on the next renewal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To take an item off before the order is built, click **Remove** next to it. Only one-time items show **Remove**. To stop a rule gift, archive the rule. You can't add or remove items while the next order is being built.

- The item goes on the next renewal order once. It isn't repeated. If it is out of stock, it waits one order and then is skipped. It has no fallbacks.
- **Catalog price** is the variant's one-time price in the subscription's market, read when you add the item. You can't type a price. A **Gift** is $0.
- A one-time item at **Catalog price** earns points like any one-time item on the order. A **Gift** earns no points.
- You can't add a physical item to a subscription that ships nothing.
- You can't add an item to a cancelled subscription.
- One-time items work even if you have no milestone rules.

To add or remove one-time items, your role needs **Full access** for **Subscriptions**.

## Read the report

The **Report** tab lists the items given in a date range. The default is the last 30 days, and the range can be up to 366 days. Filter by **Rule**, **Status** and **Source**.

The summary shows totals **By status**, **By source**, **Deferred, by reason**, **Substitutions** and **By rule**. The **Milestone grants** table lists each item with its **Date**, **Subscription**, **Rule**, **Occurrence**, **Variant**, **Quantity**, **Unit price** and **Status**.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Planned** | The item is owed to an upcoming order. |
| **On an order** | The item is on an order that isn't paid yet. |
| **Fulfilled** | The order with the item is paid. |
| **Deferred** | The item waits for the next order. |
| **Skipped, out of stock** | The item and its fallbacks were out of stock. |
| **Removed** | Someone removed a one-time item. |

**Source** is **Rule**, **One-time** or **Save offer**. **Save offer** has no items until the free gift save offer in the cancel flow is available.

## What customers see

On their subscription page, customers see **Coming with your next order** with each announced item and one-time item, its milestone, and **Free** or its price. A line such as "Order 6 brings Classic Watch. 3 more orders to go." shows the next milestone. Surprise items don't show until they are on an order. When several subscriptions renew together, the list covers the whole next order. This text is in English.

On the order, a rule item is a line priced at $0. The line names its milestone, such as order 4 or the first anniversary. Customers never see the rule's name.
