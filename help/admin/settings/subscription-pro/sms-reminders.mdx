SMS renewal reminders text a customer a few days before their subscription order renews. The text says when the order ships and links to a page where the customer can manage it. Fluid texts only customers who opted in.

SMS renewal reminders is a [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro) feature. Fluid may bill SMS segments separately, at a price Fluid sets.

## Before you start

- Subscription Pro is active for your company.
- Your company has a verified SMS sender. Until it does, the **SMS reminders** switch is unavailable and shows **A verified SMS sender is needed before this feature can be turned on.**
- **SMS reminders** is on in [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro).

## Get an SMS sender

Fluid sets up a toll-free number for your company and sends texts from it. The carrier must verify the number before texts can go out. Fluid asks you for your business details, such as your business registration number, for this verification. To start, contact [Fluid support](/help/getting-help). The time the carrier takes to verify the number is outside Fluid's control.

## How customers opt in

Fluid texts a customer only after they opt in. There are two places to opt in:

- **At checkout.** When the cart has a subscription, a checkbox shows below the phone field. It is unchecked by default.
- **On the subscription page.** The customer turns on the **SMS reminders** switch. The disclosure shows while the switch is off. Turning the switch off stops all reminder texts, the same as replying STOP.

The checkbox and the switch show the opt-in disclosure. The disclosure says that the customer agrees to automated texts about their subscription, that message and data rates may apply, how to reply STOP and HELP, and that consent isn't a condition of purchase. It links to your terms and privacy pages.

Fluid writes the disclosure. You can't edit it. Fluid fills in your company name and the links to your terms and privacy pages. When a customer opts in, Fluid keeps a record of the phone number, the choice, the disclosure text they saw, and the time.

After a customer's first opt-in, Fluid sends one text that confirms it.

Only US and Canadian phone numbers can opt in.

## What the reminder says

The reminder names your company, the date the order ships, and a link to manage it. When it fits in one text, it also gives the item count and the order total. For example:

> Acme Wellness: your subscription order (2 items, $48.98) ships on Nov 12. Manage: *link* Reply STOP to opt out.

The text is in English. You can't edit it.

The link opens a page where the customer can skip, pause, resume or cancel that order's subscriptions.

## When reminders go out

- **Lead time.** Fluid texts the number of days before the bill date that you set in **Reminder lead time (days)** on the [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro) screen. The default is 3 days, and you can set 1 to 14. Renewal reminder emails use the same lead time while SMS reminders are on.
- **Send window.** Fluid sends texts only between 09:00 and 20:00 in the customer's local time. A reminder that comes due before 09:00 goes out when the window opens. A reminder that comes due after 20:00 isn't sent.
- **Local time.** Fluid uses the subscription's time zone. If the subscription has none, Fluid uses the shipping address's state or province, when that region is in one time zone. If Fluid can't tell the local time, it doesn't send the reminder.
- **Daily cap.** One phone number gets at most 3 texts from your company in 24 hours. Fluid doesn't send a fourth text and doesn't send it later.
- **One per order.** Fluid sends one reminder per order and bill date.

## Replies: STOP, HELP and START

| The customer replies | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, REVOKE or OPTOUT | Fluid stops texting that number. The carrier sends the opt-out confirmation. A STOP or CANCEL reply doesn't cancel the subscription. |
| HELP or INFO | Fluid replies with your company name, what the texts are for, and how to opt out. The reply also gives your support email when your company has one. |
| START or UNSTOP | Fluid starts texting again a number that opted in before. |

The reply must be only the keyword. Case doesn't matter.

## Annual renewal notice

For subscriptions that renew once a year or less often, Fluid emails the customer 30 days before the renewal. The email is titled "Your subscription renews on *date*" and has a **Manage or cancel** button. This email doesn't need Subscription Pro.

If SMS reminders are on and the customer opted in, Fluid also sends a text with the renewal date, the total, and a link to manage or cancel the subscription.

## Your responsibilities

You are responsible for your customer messages and your consent practices. Check that how you collect consent, what your terms and privacy pages say, and how you use text messages meet the rules that apply to your business. Fluid doesn't give legal advice and doesn't promise that a setup meets any law.
