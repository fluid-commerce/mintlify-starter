SMS renewal reminders text a customer a few days before their subscription order renews. The text says when the order ships and links to a page where the customer can manage it. Fluid texts only customers who opted in. A customer who opts in at checkout must also confirm by text.

SMS renewal reminders is a [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro) feature. Fluid may bill SMS segments separately, at a price Fluid sets.

## Before you start

- Subscription Pro is active for your company.
- Your company has a verified SMS sender. Until it does, the **SMS reminders** switch is unavailable and shows **A verified SMS sender is needed before this feature can be turned on.**
- Fluid has published the final consent wording that customers agree to. Until then, SMS reminders stay unavailable for every company, and the switch shows **This feature cannot be turned on yet because a requirement is not met.**
- **SMS reminders** is on in [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro).

## Get an SMS sender

Fluid sets up a US toll-free number for your company and sends your reminder texts from it. The number is used only for Subscription Pro texts. To start, contact [Fluid support](/help/getting-help).

The carriers must verify the number before texts can go out. Fluid asks you for your business details and submits them for you:

- Business name, website, and an email for verification notices.
- At least one image link that shows how customers opt in, such as a screenshot of your checkout consent checkbox.
- Your expected message volume.
- Business street address, city, state or province, postal code and country.
- A business contact's name, email and phone.
- Business type: private for-profit, public for-profit, sole proprietor, non-profit or government.
- Business registration number, such as an EIN, with the registration authority and country. The carriers require it.
- Optional: a "doing business as" name, and links to your privacy policy and terms.

The number moves through these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Fluid set up the number. |
| **Submitted** | The carrier is reviewing your details. |
| **Verified** | Texts can go out. |
| **Rejected** | The carrier gave a reason. Correct your details, and Fluid submits them again. |

The time the carrier takes to verify the number is outside Fluid's control. A number can lose its verification later. Texts then stop at once, and your **SMS reminders** switch stays on. Customers in Canada get texts from the same US toll-free number.

## How customers opt in

Fluid texts a customer only after they opt in. Consent belongs to one phone number and your company only. Only US and Canadian phone numbers can opt in.

### At checkout

When the cart has a subscription and the checkout asks for a phone number, a checkbox shows below the phone field. It is unchecked by default.

1. The customer checks the box and places the order.
2. When the order completes, Fluid texts the phone a request to reply YES. The phone gets no reminders yet.
3. The customer replies **YES** or **Y** within 7 days. The phone is now opted in, and Fluid sends a text that confirms it.

If the customer doesn't reply within 7 days, the phone stays without consent and gets no reminders. The next time the customer opts in at checkout, Fluid asks again. Fluid sends at most 2 request texts to one phone from your company in any 7 days: the first request, and one more only when the customer opts in again at another checkout.

A phone that only Apple Pay or Google Pay supplied can't opt in, because the customer never saw it next to the checkbox.

### On the subscription page

A signed-in customer turns on the **SMS reminders** switch on their subscription page. The customer is signed in, so the phone is opted in at once, with no YES step. The disclosure shows while the switch is off. Turning the switch off stops all reminder texts, the same as replying STOP. A phone that is still waiting for YES shows the switch off.

### The disclosure

The checkbox and the switch show the opt-in disclosure. It says that the customer agrees to recurring automated texts about their subscription, up to 3 a day, that message and data rates may apply, how to reply STOP and HELP, and that consent isn't a condition of purchase. It links to your terms and privacy pages.

Fluid writes the disclosure. You can't edit it. Fluid fills in your company name and the links to your terms page (your domain + `terms-conditions`) and privacy page (your domain + `privacy-policy`). Make sure those pages exist and cover text messages. When Fluid changes the disclosure, customers must agree to the new wording again.

When a customer opts in or out, Fluid keeps a record of the phone number, the choice, where it was made, the disclosure text they saw, and the time. Records can't be edited.

Fluid sends the confirmation text once per phone for your company. A customer who opts out and opts in again doesn't get a second confirmation.

The SMS reminder consent is separate from your marketing text consent, such as a "Text me with news and offers" checkbox.

## What the reminder says

The reminder names your company, the date the order ships, and a link to manage it. When it fits in one text, it also gives the item count and the order total. For example:

> Primex: your subscription order (2 items, $54.00) ships on Oct 14. Manage: *link* Reply STOP to opt out.

When it doesn't fit in one text, or the total isn't known, Fluid sends a shorter text without the item count and the total.

The text is in English. You can't edit it. It has no offer, discount or upsell.

The link opens a page where the customer can skip, pause, resume or cancel that order's subscriptions without signing in.

## When reminders go out

- **Lead time.** Fluid texts the number of days before the bill date that you set in **Reminder lead time (days)** on the [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro) screen. The default is 3 days, and you can set 1 to 14. Renewal reminder emails use the same lead time while SMS reminders are on.
- **Send window.** Fluid sends texts only between 09:00 and 20:00 in the customer's local time, and checks every 15 minutes. A reminder that comes due before 09:00 goes out when the window opens. If the window closes before the text can go out, the text is skipped for that order and isn't sent the next day. The reminder email still goes out.
- **Local time.** Fluid uses the subscription's time zone. If the subscription has none, Fluid uses the shipping address's state or province, when that whole region is in one time zone. If Fluid can't tell the local time, it doesn't send the reminder.
- **Daily cap.** One phone number gets at most 3 texts from your company in 24 hours. Request, confirmation and HELP texts count toward the cap. Fluid doesn't send a fourth reminder and doesn't send it later.
- **One per order.** Fluid sends one reminder per order and bill date.
- **Checked just before sending.** Right before each text goes out, Fluid checks the phone's consent again. If the customer opted out a moment earlier, the text isn't sent. Fluid also checks that the feature is still on, that your number is still verified, and that the order and its bill date haven't changed.

## Replies: STOP, HELP, START and YES

| The customer replies | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, REVOKE or OPTOUT | Fluid stops texting that number for your company. The carrier sends the opt-out confirmation. A STOP or CANCEL reply doesn't cancel the subscription. |
| HELP or INFO | Fluid replies with your company name, what the texts are for, and how to opt out. The reply also gives your support email when your company has one. |
| START or UNSTOP | Fluid starts texting again a number that opted in before. It doesn't confirm a checkout opt-in that is waiting for YES. |
| YES or Y | Confirms a checkout opt-in within 7 days of the request text. YES does nothing for a phone that isn't waiting, or that opted out. |

The reply must be only the keyword. Case doesn't matter.

STOP works at any time, from any state. When a STOP and an opt-in arrive at the same moment, STOP wins. A YES or START sent before a later STOP never undoes it. When a carrier reports that a phone opted out, Fluid records the opt-out too.

## Annual renewal notice

For subscriptions that renew once a year or less often, Fluid emails the customer before the renewal. The email is titled "Your subscription renews on *date*". It gives the amount, how often the subscription renews, and a **Manage or cancel** button. This email is a [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro) feature. It goes out only when Subscription Pro is active for your company and you turn on **Annual renewal notice** in the **Features** card. The switch is off by default. Without Subscription Pro, customers get no annual renewal notice.

- The default is 30 days before the renewal. You can set **Annual renewal notice (days before renewal)** on the [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro) screen, from 15 to 90 days.
- The email type is **Subscription Annual Renewal Notice** in [Communications](/help/admin/settings/communications). It's on by default, and the notice needs it on. If you turn it off there, no annual renewal notice goes out.
- The email is in English.

If **Annual renewal notice** and SMS reminders are both on and the customer opted in, Fluid also sends a text with the renewal date, the total, and a link to manage or cancel the subscription. The text follows the same send window and daily cap as reminders.
