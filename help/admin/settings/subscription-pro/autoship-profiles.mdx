An autoship profile is a group of one customer's subscriptions that renew together as one order. Customers manage the group on their subscription page, add items to it, and can join it when they buy a new subscription at checkout. Admins can change the whole group at once.

Autoship profiles is a [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro) feature.

## Before you start

- Subscription Pro is active for your company.
- **Bundle subscriptions automatically** is on. See [Subscription Plans](/help/admin/settings/subscription-plans#bundle-subscriptions-automatically). The **Autoship profiles** switch stays unavailable until it is on.
- **Autoship profiles** is on in [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro).

## Which subscriptions form a profile

You don't create a profile. Fluid finds it. Two or more subscriptions form a profile when they belong to the same customer and match on all of these:

- The shipping address.
- The payment method.
- The bill date, in the same time zone.
- The plan's frequency.
- The currency and the rep who gets credit.

Only physical items count. Active and paused subscriptions stay in the profile. A cancelled subscription leaves it. A subscription with no next bill date isn't in a profile.

Subscriptions that bill on the same date to the same address, but differ only in payment method, frequency or time zone, show as **Shipping together**. They renew together on that date, but they can split later. You can't use profile actions on them.

## What customers see

### On their subscription page

When a subscription is in a profile, the customer's subscription page shows a **Your autoship** card. It lists the items, the **Next shipment** date, the **Frequency**, where it **Ships to**, and the **Payment** method. A paused item shows a **Paused** badge.

The card has these actions. Each one changes every subscription in the profile, except **Skip once**:

- **Change date** moves the next shipment.
- **Skip next shipment** skips the next order for the whole profile.
- **Skip once** skips one item for the next order. The item ships again with the others on the following date.
- **Pause** pauses the profile until a date the customer picks. **Resume** restarts it.
- **Change frequency**, **Change address** and **Change payment method** change all items.
- **Add item** adds a product to the profile.

### Add an item

**Add item** opens **Add an item**. The customer searches by product name or SKU, picks a product, and clicks **Review**. The review shows when the item ships, where, on which card, and at what price. The customer clicks **Add to autoship**.

The customer isn't charged when they add the item. It bills with the profile's next order.

The list shows only products that can ship with the profile:

- The product is live and public, and isn't a bundle.
- The variant is active, physical, and in stock in the profile's shipping country.
- The product offers the profile's subscription plan.
- The profile doesn't already hold that variant.

The price in the list is the plan price before automatic discounts and quantity breaks. The review shows the exact price.

### At checkout

A signed-in customer who buys a subscription sees **Ship with my autoship** at checkout when one of their profiles can take the new item. Guests don't see it. The customer chooses **Ship with my *date* autoship** or **Ship it on its own schedule**.

The order still ships right away. After that, the new subscription renews with the profile. If the profile has a different date or card, checkout shows the new date, address and card, and the customer clicks **Confirm**. The order summary then shows the profile's date as the start of the recurring subtotal.

If the profile changes before the order completes, the new subscription keeps its own schedule and the order still completes. The confirmation page tells the customer which of the two happened.

## Manage a profile in the admin

When the feature is on, the admin shows profiles in two places:

- A subscription's page has an **Autoship profile** card. It shows a **Profile** or **Shipping together** badge, the item count, the next ship date, the address, and the other subscriptions in the group with links to each.
- A customer's page has an **Autoship profiles** card that lists their profiles. It is hidden when the customer has none.

To change a profile, open the actions menu on the **Autoship profile** card. The menu appears only on a profile, not on a **Shipping together** group. It has the same actions as the customer's menu: **Change date**, **Skip next shipment**, **Skip one item**, **Pause**, **Resume**, **Change frequency**, **Change address**, **Change payment method** and **Add item**. Which actions show depends on whether the profile is active, paused or partly paused.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add an item">
    Open the actions menu and click **Add item**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the product and quantity">
    Pick the product in **Choose product**, set the **Quantity**, and click **Review**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check and confirm">
    **Review the new item** shows the ship date, the address, the payment method and the price. Click **Add to profile**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  To open the actions menu, your role needs **Full access** for **Subscriptions**. **Change payment method** also needs view access to payment methods, and **Change frequency** needs view access to **Subscription Plans**. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
</Note>

## Limits

- An action that would split the profile is refused. The dialog shows the reason.
- A pause needs a resume date.
- If someone else changes the profile while you edit it, the card reloads and shows a warning. Check the profile and make your change again.
- A new subscription can't join a profile at checkout if it has a different plan, currency, country or tax and shipping setup, if it's a digital item, or if the profile's renewal date has already arrived.
- Each subscription in the profile still sends its own subscription webhooks. Adding an item sends the same webhook and confirmation email as a new subscription.
