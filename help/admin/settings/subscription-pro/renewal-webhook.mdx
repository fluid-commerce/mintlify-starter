The renewal webhook tells your systems that a subscription order renews soon. Use it to send your own reminders, through your email or messaging tools, a few days before each renewal.

Renewal webhook is a [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro) feature.

## Before you start

- Subscription Pro is active for your company.
- **Renewal webhook** is on in [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro).

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the feature">
    In [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro), turn on **Renewal webhook** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the lead time">
    In **Reminder lead time (days)**, set how many days before the bill date the webhook goes out. The default is 3 days, and you can set 1 to 14. SMS reminders use the same lead time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the webhook">
    On [Webhooks](/help/admin/settings/webhooks), click **Create Webhook**. For **Resource**, choose **Subscription**. For **Event**, choose **Upcoming Renewal**. Enter your **Destination URL** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="A webhook for the Upcoming Renewal event of the Subscription resource.">
  <img src="/images/help/subscription-pro/renewal-webhook-create.png" alt="The Create Webhook panel with Resource Subscription, Event Upcoming Renewal and a destination URL" />
</Frame>

To change the Subscription Pro settings, your role needs the order settings permission. See [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro).

## When it is sent

Fluid checks once a day, at 09:00 UTC. It sends the webhook for each subscription order whose bill date, in the subscription's time zone, is the lead time away. For example, with the default of 3 days, an order that bills on October 14 is announced on October 11. If a customer's subscriptions renew together as one order, Fluid sends one webhook for that order.

Fluid sends each order once for each bill date. When you turn the feature off, or Subscription Pro ends, no new webhooks go out.

## What it contains

The webhook names the subscription order that renews. It has the bill date, the customer's name, email and phone, the subscriptions in the order, the items, the amounts, and a manage link. It has no address and no saved card. For the exact fields, read the event's payload schema. See [Introspect payload schemas](/api/guides/webhooks#introspect-payload-schemas).

The amounts are an estimate, the same one the renewal reminder email shows. Fluid prices the order again when it bills.

Each order has its own ID in the payload. Delivery retries can send the same webhook again. If your endpoint gets the same order twice, use that ID to send only one message.

## The manage link

The manage link opens a page where the customer can skip, pause, resume or cancel the subscriptions in that order. The customer doesn't need to sign in. The page shows no name, address, saved card, email or phone, and the customer can't change those details there.

<Frame caption="The manage link page for an order with two subscriptions.">
  <img src="/images/help/subscription-pro/manage-link-page.png" alt="The Manage your subscription page with two subscriptions, each with Skip next order, Pause and Cancel buttons" />
</Frame>

- **Cancel** asks for a confirmation: the button changes to **Confirm cancel**. The page has no cancellation reason step and no save offer.
- **Pause** has no end date. A paused subscription shows **Resume**.
- After a skip, the button changes to **Undo skip**.

Keep the link safe:

- Send the link only to the customer it belongs to. Anyone with the link can change those subscriptions until it expires.
- The link expires at the end of the day after the bill date. It can be used many times until then. An expired link shows a message that asks the customer to use their latest reminder or to sign in.
- The link is long. Don't shorten it with a public link shortener.
- A link that was sent keeps working until it expires, even if Subscription Pro is turned off.

## Your responsibilities

You decide what your own reminders say and who gets them. You are responsible for your customer messages and for any consent your messages need.
