The renewal webhook tells your systems that a subscription order renews soon. Use it to send your own reminders, through your email or messaging tools, a few days before each renewal.

Renewal webhook is a [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro) feature.

## Before you start

- Subscription Pro is active for your company.
- **Renewal webhook** is on in [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro).

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the feature">
    In [Subscription Pro](/help/admin/settings/subscription-pro), turn on **Renewal webhook** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the lead time">
    In **Reminder lead time (days)**, set how many days before the bill date the webhook goes out. The default is 3 days, and you can set 1 to 14. SMS reminders use the same lead time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the webhook">
    On [Webhooks](/help/admin/settings/webhooks), click **Create Webhook**. For **Resource**, choose **Subscription**. For **Event**, choose the upcoming renewal event. Enter your **Destination URL** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When it is sent

Fluid checks once a day. It sends the webhook for each subscription order whose bill date is the lead time away. If a customer's subscriptions renew together as one order, Fluid sends one webhook for that order.

## What it contains

The webhook names the subscription order that renews. It has the bill date, the customer's name, email and phone, the subscriptions in the order, the items, the amounts, and a manage link. For the exact fields, read the event's payload schema. See [Introspect payload schemas](/api/guides/webhooks#introspect-payload-schemas).

Each order has its own ID in the payload. If your endpoint gets the same order twice, use that ID to send only one message.

## The manage link

The manage link opens a page where the customer can skip, pause, resume or cancel the subscriptions in that order. The customer doesn't need to sign in. The page shows no other customer details.

- Send the link only to the customer it belongs to. Anyone with the link can change those subscriptions until it expires.
- The link expires at the end of the day after the bill date.
- The link is long. Don't shorten it with a public link shortener.
- A link that was sent keeps working until it expires, even if Subscription Pro is turned off.

## Your responsibilities

You decide what your own reminders say and who gets them. You are responsible for your customer messages and for any consent your messages need.
