Use the **Social Media** screen to keep the addresses of your company's social media profiles in one list.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. In the Settings sidebar, under **Company**, select **Social Media**.

## What's on the screen

The list shows the links you've saved. Click **Add Social Media Link** at the top of the screen to add one.

The list has two columns:

- **Platform**: the platform's icon and name.
- **URL**: the full profile address.

Use the **Search...** box above the list to find a link by platform or URL. You can sort the list by **Platform**. When you haven't saved any links, the list shows **Nothing to show**.

The three-dot menu at the end of each row has **Edit** and **Delete**.

<Note>
  To open the screen, your role needs at least **View only** for **Company Settings**, in the **Settings** card. To add, edit or delete links, it needs **Full access** for **Company Settings**. Without it, you can see the list, but **Add Social Media Link** and the row's three-dot menu don't appear. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
</Note>

### Link panel fields

Clicking **Add Social Media Link** opens the **New Social Media Link** panel. Choosing **Edit** on a row opens the **Edit Social Media Link** panel. Both have these fields:

- **Platform**: **Facebook**, **Twitter**, **Instagram**, **Linkedin**, **Youtube**, **Tiktok** or **Pinterest**. New links start with **Facebook**. You can't change the platform when you edit a link. Each platform holds one link. Saving a link for a platform that already has one replaces it.
- **URL**: your profile on that platform. This field is required.

Click **Save** to save the link, or **Cancel** to close the panel without saving.

### What to enter in URL

In **URL**, enter only the part of your profile address after the domain, even though the field asks for a URL. Fluid adds the platform's address in front of it:

- For `https://www.facebook.com/example-shop`, enter `example-shop`.
- For `https://www.linkedin.com/company/example-shop`, enter `company/example-shop`.

## Add a link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    Click **Add Social Media Link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the platform">
    Under **Platform**, choose the platform for this link. If the platform already has a link, saving replaces it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the address">
    In **URL**, enter the part of your profile address after the domain (see [What to enter in URL](#what-to-enter-in-url)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The link appears in the list. If an error message appears instead, the link wasn't saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit a link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Edit">
    In the list, open the three-dot menu at the end of the link's row and choose **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the value">
    Update **URL**, then check that it matches the part of your profile address after the domain (see [Why doesn't URL match my address when I edit a link?](#edit-url-differs)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you saved a link under the wrong platform, delete it and add it again under the right one.

## Delete a link

<Warning>
  Deleting takes effect right away. There's no confirmation step.
</Warning>

In the list, choose **Delete** from the link's three-dot menu. The link disappears from the list. If it's still in the list, it wasn't deleted.

To delete several links, select the checkbox at the start of each link's row. Then open the three-dot menu that appears at the top of the list and choose the delete option, for example **Delete 3 rows**. On a small screen, that menu appears in a bar near the bottom of the screen instead.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does the URL column show the address twice?" id="url-repeats-address">
    The link was saved with the full profile address. Follow [Edit a link](#edit-a-link). **URL** shows the address you saved, so delete everything up to and including the domain and the slash after it. Check the rest against your profile address, then click **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why doesn't URL match my address when I edit a link?" id="edit-url-differs">
    When you edit a link, **URL** leaves out any part of your address that starts with `?` or `#`, and some characters can appear encoded, for example `%20` for a space. Before you click **Save**, make sure **URL** matches the part of your profile address after the domain, and add back anything that's missing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
