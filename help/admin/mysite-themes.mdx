The **Mysite Themes** screen lists the themes your reps' MySites can use. Here you add themes, rename them and open their code.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Website**, select **Mysite Theme**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Mysite Theme**. To add, rename or customize themes, or to reset the cache, it needs **Full access**. With **View only**, the screen is read-only: you see the themes, but not the buttons. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Mysite Theme** is in the **Content & Website** card. Open the card and set access from the **Mysite Theme** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

**Reset Cache** and **Add Theme** are at the top of the page. Below them, the **Mysite Theme Library** lists your company's MySite themes. If there aren't any, the screen shows **No Mysite themes found**.

Each theme's row shows:

- Its preview image. A theme without one shows a placeholder with the theme's name.
- Its name. A **Default** badge marks the theme a rep's MySite uses when it has no theme of its own.
- **Last save**, followed by a date and time.
- A pencil button next to the name, for renaming the theme, and **Customize**, which opens the theme's code.

## Tasks

### Add a theme

<Warning>
  Give each new theme a name that no MySite theme has ever used, even with different capitalization. To change an existing theme, use **Customize** instead.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    Click **Add Theme** at the top of the screen. The **Add Mysite Theme** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the theme details">
    In the **Theme Details** card, enter a **Theme name** of up to 120 characters. You can also add a **Description** and a **Preview Image**. To add an image, click **Select Image** and choose one. After that, **Change** replaces the image and the trash button removes it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the template">
    In the **Template** card, enter the theme's HTML and Liquid in **Content (HTML/Liquid)** and its CSS in **Stylesheet (CSS)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the theme">
    Click **Create Theme**. The button stays grayed out until the theme has a name. A **Theme created successfully** message appears and the dialog closes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

As soon as you create a theme, reps can choose it for their MySite. If the theme can't be created, an error message appears at the bottom of the dialog.

### Rename a theme

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start renaming">
    Click the pencil button next to the theme's name. Its tooltip reads **Rename theme**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the new name">
    Type the new name, then press Enter or click outside the field. To cancel, press Escape or click the X next to the field.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you leave the name empty or don't change it, the theme keeps its name. If another theme already uses the name, an error message appears under the field.

### Customize a theme

Click **Customize** on a theme to open its template in the [Page Editor](/help/admin/page-editor) in **Code** mode, where you edit the theme's code.

To open and save the template there, your role also needs **Full access** for **Theme**. On the Roles screen, **Theme** is in the same **Content & Website** card.

### Reset the cache

**Reset Cache** clears the cached copies of all your reps' MySites.

Click **Reset Cache** at the top of the screen. The button reads **Resetting...** while the request is sent, then a confirmation message appears.

Resetting the cache also needs the **Developer** switch that starts with **Manage webhooks and API configuration**. On the Roles screen, it's in the **Settings** card. Without it, you see **Failed to reset cache**.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Add Theme or Customize?" id="no-add-theme-button">
    Your role has **View only** for **Mysite Theme**, so the screen is read-only. Ask an admin who manages roles to give your role **Full access** for **Mysite Theme**. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the Default badge mean?" id="default-badge">
    It marks the theme a rep's MySite uses when the MySite has no theme of its own. This screen doesn't change which theme has the badge.

    The **Default Theme** on the [Default Mysite](/help/admin/settings/default-mysite) screen in Settings is a separate setting: it's the theme a rep's MySite gets when it's copied from your company's default MySite.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
