The **Mobile App** screens set up your company's mobile app: the screens on the app's bottom bar, and the pages, tabs and widgets your reps see on its Home, Share and Shop screens. For how reps use the app, see [Mobile app](/help/mobile).

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, select **Mobile App**. It opens to show its screens. You see only the screens your role can view.
