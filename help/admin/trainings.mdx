Use the **Trainings** screen to create and manage training content for your reps. Trainings show up for reps in your mobile app. On [Home Tabs](/help/admin/mobile/home), you can add a **Get Started** widget that shows trainings from a category you choose.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Tools**, select **Trainings**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Trainings**. With **View only**, you can open trainings but not save changes to them. To add, edit or delete trainings, your role needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Trainings** is in the **Tools & Messaging** card. Open the card and set access from the **Trainings** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

The **Add Training** button is at the top of the page. Below it is the list of trainings.

### Trainings list

- The **All**, **Active** and **Inactive** tabs filter the list by status. The list opens on **All**.
- Use the search box to find a training.
- Click the filter button and choose **Media Type** to show only **Image**, **Video**, **PDF** or **PowerPoint** trainings. Choose **All Types** to show every type again.

The list has these columns:

- **Title**: the training's title, with a thumbnail of its image when it has one. A training without a title shows as **Untitled**.
- **Status**: **Active** or **Draft**. A training whose status is set to **Inactive** shows as **Draft**.
- **Date**: the date the training was created.
- **Views**: the training's view count.

The newest trainings are listed first. Click the **Title**, **Date** or **Views** header to sort by that column.

Click a training to open it. Each row's actions menu has **Delete**. To delete several trainings at once, select their rows: a bar shows how many rows are selected, with a **Delete** button. The actions menu and row selection appear only if your role can delete trainings.

### Training page

The training page opens when you click **Add Training** or a training in the list. Its header reads **New Training** for a new training and **Training Details** for a saved one. On a saved training, the breadcrumb shows the training's title with an **Active** or **Draft** badge.

The **Save** button is at the top of the page. It's grayed out until you've entered a **Title** and, on a new training, chosen a file. You delete trainings from the list, not from this page.

On the left is the **Media** card, and below it the card with the text:

- **Media**: click **Select Media** and choose one image, video or PDF file in the window that opens. After you choose a file, the button reads **Replace Media**. The card shows a preview of the file. Click an image or video preview to see it larger. Until you choose a file, the card shows **No media uploaded**.
- **Title**: required.
- **Body**: the training's text, in an editor with formatting. It's optional.

On the right are three cards:

- **Status**: **Active** (make this training available for use) or **Inactive** (save it as a draft). A new training starts as **Active**.
- **Countries**: choose from your company's countries. Leave it empty, shown as **All Countries**, to make the training available in every country you sell in.
- **Category**: search for and choose one of your training categories.

## Tasks

### Create a training

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new training">
    On the **Trainings** screen, click **Add Training**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the file">
    In the **Media** card, click **Select Media** and choose an image, video or PDF file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the training">
    Enter a **Title**, and add the training's text in **Body**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the settings">
    Set **Status**, choose **Countries** and pick a **Category**. To save the training as a draft, set **Status** to **Inactive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. A confirmation message appears, and the page reopens as the saved training's **Training Details** page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Edit a training

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the training">
    In the list, click the training.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change the fields you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. A confirmation message appears and you stay on the training's page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Delete a training

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    In the list, open the training's actions menu and choose **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Delete training?** dialog, click **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To delete several trainings, select their rows and click **Delete** in the bar that appears, then confirm in the dialog.

<Warning>
  Deleting a training can't be undone.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is Save grayed out?" id="save-unavailable">
    Enter a **Title**. On a new training, also choose a file in the **Media** card. If **Save** doesn't appear at all, your role doesn't have **Full access** for **Trainings**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does a training show as Draft?" id="draft-status">
    **Draft** is how the list and the training page show a training whose **Status** is **Inactive**. The **Inactive** tab lists these trainings. To change it, open the training, set **Status** to **Active**, and click **Save**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Add Training?" id="no-add-training">
    Your role doesn't have **Full access** for **Trainings**. With **View only**, you can open trainings, but **Save** doesn't appear and you can't delete trainings. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
