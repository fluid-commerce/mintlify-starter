Settings holds your company's configuration in the Fluid admin, from agreements and branding to checkout, taxes, payment options and webhooks.

## Where to find it

In the admin, click the **Settings** gear in the top bar. Settings opens full screen on the **Agreements** screen.

## What's on the screen

- The sidebar on the left starts with a **Settings** heading and a **Search...** box, followed by the Settings screens listed under **Company**, **Commerce**, **Payments** and **System**.
- The header runs across the top of each screen. On a desktop it shows a breadcrumb that starts with **Settings** and ends with the screen's name. On the right are that screen's actions, such as **Save**, and the **X** that closes Settings.
- The main area shows the options for the screen you selected.

On a phone, the sidebar is hidden and the header shows the screen's name instead of a breadcrumb. Tap the menu button at the top left of the screen to open the sidebar, then tap a screen to open it.

The name in the header doesn't always match the sidebar label. For example, **Gateways** opens as **Payment Gateways**.

## Find a screen

On a desktop, type part of a screen's name in the **Search...** box under the **Settings** heading in the sidebar. The cursor is already in the box when Settings opens. The sidebar narrows to the matching screens and hides any group with no match. Select a screen to open it in the main area.

<Tip>
  Search matches screen names only, not the options inside a screen.
</Tip>

## Close Settings

Select the **X** at the top right of the screen, or press **Esc**. The admin returns to the page you had open before you opened Settings. If you opened Settings from a bookmark, reloaded the page while in Settings, or opened webhook logs with **View Webhook Logs**, the admin opens **Getting Started** instead.

<Warning>
  On a screen with a **Save** button in the header, leaving the screen doesn't save your changes. Select **Save** before you select another screen, select the **X** or press **Esc**. If a drawer or dialog is open, close it with its own buttons, not **Esc**.
</Warning>
