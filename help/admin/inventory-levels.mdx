Use the **Inventory** screen to see how much stock each product variant has in a warehouse and to correct those counts. You can also choose which variants Fluid tracks and whether they keep selling when they run out.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Commerce**, select **Inventory**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Inventory**. To change quantities, change tracking and selling settings, or delete inventory, it needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Inventory** is in the **Commerce** card. Open the card and set access from the **Inventory** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

Inventory is kept per warehouse. The screen opens on your company's first warehouse. The warehouse's name appears next to the **Inventory** title. Click it to open a list of your warehouses, with each one's city and country, and choose another.

At the top of the page:

- **Export** downloads the selected warehouse's inventory as a CSV file.
- **Save Changes** appears when you've changed a quantity that you haven't saved yet.

Use the **Search...** box to find items. It matches the product name, the variant name, the SKU, and the names of the product's tags and collections. The list shows 50 items per page.

The list has these columns:

- The product's image.
- **Name**: the product's name, with the variant's name as a badge when the variant has one.
- **SKU**: the variant's SKU, or **—** when it has none.
- **Unavailable**: split into four categories: **Damaged**, **Quality control**, **Safety stock** and **Other**.
- **Committed**: you can't edit this column.
- **Available**: type a new total, or adjust it from the field's menu.
- **On Hand**: the total of **Available**, **Unavailable** and **Committed**. You can also type a new total here.

Click a product's image or name to open the product. Rows for variants that Fluid doesn't track are grayed out, and their quantity columns are empty.

Each row's actions menu has **Track inventory** or **Stop tracking inventory**, **Continue selling when out of stock** or **Stop selling when out of stock**, and **Delete**. Select several rows to use the same actions from the table header's menu: **Start Tracking Inventory**, **Stop Tracking Inventory**, **Continue Selling When Out of Stock**, **Stop Selling When Out of Stock**, and **Delete**, labeled with the count, such as **Delete 3 Inventories**.

## Tasks

Quantity changes stay on the page until you click **Save Changes**. Tracking, selling and delete actions save as soon as you confirm them.

<Warning>
  Click **Save Changes** before you change page, search, or switch warehouse.
</Warning>

### Change available or on-hand stock

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the warehouse">
    Click the warehouse name next to the title and choose the warehouse.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the new quantity">
    In the item's row, click the **Available** or **On Hand** field, type the new total, and press Enter. When you change **Available**, **On Hand** updates to match. When you change **On Hand**, **Available** updates instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or adjust by an amount">
    Click the arrow next to the field instead. For **Available**, leave the first menu on **Adjust Available**. In **Adjust By**, enter the amount to add, or a negative amount to remove, and press Enter. Click outside the menu to apply it. **Available** and **On Hand** both change by that amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. A confirmation message appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Move stock to unavailable

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Available menu">
    Click the arrow next to the item's **Available** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Move to Unavailable">
    In the first menu, choose **Move to Unavailable**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the quantity and reason">
    Choose a **Reason** from the list every time: **Other**, **Damaged**, **Quality control** or **Safety Stock**. Then enter the **Quantity** and press Enter. You can't move more than the item's available stock.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply and save">
    Click outside the menu. **Available** goes down and **Unavailable** goes up by the same amount; **On Hand** doesn't change. Click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Manage unavailable stock

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the breakdown">
    Click the item's **Unavailable** value. The **Unavailable inventory** menu lists each category and its count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a category and action">
    Click a category's count, then choose an action:
    - **Add inventory** adds units to the category. **On Hand** goes up too.
    - **Move to Available** moves units from the category back to **Available**. It appears only when the category has units.
    - **Delete inventory** removes units from the category. **On Hand** goes down too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the quantity">
    Enter the **Quantity** and press Enter. To move or delete, you can't enter more than the category holds; the menu shows the limit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Turn tracking or out-of-stock selling on or off

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the action">
    Open a row's actions menu and choose the tracking or selling action. To change several items, select their rows and use the table header's menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the dialog, click **Start Tracking**, **Stop Tracking**, **Continue Selling** or **Stop Selling**. A confirmation message appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

These settings belong to the product variant, so they apply in every warehouse, not only the one you're viewing. **Continue selling when out of stock** lets customers buy the variant even when its inventory reaches zero.

### Export inventory

Click **Export**. Fluid downloads a CSV file named `inventory_export_` followed by today's date, with the selected warehouse's inventory.

### Delete inventory

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    Choose **Delete** from a row's actions menu, or select several rows and choose **Delete** from the table header's menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Delete Inventory?** dialog, click **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting removes the inventory record for the selected variants at this warehouse, and Fluid stops tracking their inventory there. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is a row grayed out with no quantities?" id="row-grayed-out">
    Fluid doesn't track inventory for that variant. To start tracking it, choose **Track inventory** from the row's actions menu.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I edit Committed?" id="committed-not-editable">
    **Committed** is shown for reference only. Change **Available**, **On Hand** or **Unavailable** instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Save Changes or the row actions?" id="no-save-button">
    **Save Changes** appears only after you change a quantity. If it still doesn't appear, or the rows have no tracking, selling or delete actions, your role doesn't have **Full access** for **Inventory**. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does my company need a warehouse?" id="no-warehouses">
    Yes. Inventory is kept per warehouse, so your company needs at least one warehouse. Set one up on the [Warehouses](/help/admin/settings/warehouses) screen.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
