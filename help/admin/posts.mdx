Use the **Posts** screen to write blog posts for your storefront and control when and where they appear.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Storefront**, select **Posts**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Posts**. To create, edit or delete posts, it needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Posts** is in the **Content & Website** card. Open the card and set access from the **Posts** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

**Add Post** is at the top of the screen.

- The **All**, **Published**, **Scheduled**, **Draft** and **Archived** tabs filter the list by status.
- Use the search box to find posts.
- **Filters** has **Status** and **Country**. The **Status** filter and the tabs stay in step. **Country** shows the posts available in the country you pick; **All Countries** clears it.

The list has these columns:

- **Preview**: the post's image, or an empty frame if it has none.
- **Title**: click it, or anywhere else on the row, to open the post. A post with no title shows **Untitled**. You can sort by this column.
- **Status**: **Published**, **Draft**, **Archived**, or **Scheduled** with its publish date and time.
- **Countries**: flags for the countries the post is available in, with **+** and a number when there are more than two. **All** means the post has no country restriction and is available everywhere you sell.
- **Publish Date**: the date set in the post's **Publish Date** field, or a dash if it has none.

Each row's actions menu has **Edit** and **Delete**. Select several rows to delete them together with **Delete**.

## Create a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new post">
    On the **Posts** screen, click **Add Post**. The **Create Post** screen opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the post">
    In the **Post Details** card, enter a **Title**. It's required. Enter the **Description** in the text editor. Under **Media**, click **Select Image** to choose the post's image: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP or SVG, up to 10MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the post's details">
    In the **Additional Details** card, enter a **Summary**, a **Publish Date** and an **Author** if you use them. **Publish Date** is the post's date, shown in the list; it doesn't schedule the post. Leave **Active** on (see [Status and the Active switch](#status-and-the-active-switch)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check countries and the template">
    In the **Countries** card, leave the field empty to make the post available everywhere you sell, or choose specific countries. In the **Theme Template** card, your active theme's default post template is selected for you. Choose another if you need to. If your theme has no post templates yet, choose **Create Template** from the list. This adds a template named **New Post Template**, with placeholder content, to your active theme and selects it for the post. You need an active theme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set SEO, if needed">
    In the **SEO And Link Sharing** card, click **Edit**. See [Set SEO and the post URL](#set-seo-and-the-post-url).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish or save a draft">
    A new post starts as **Published** in the **Status** card. Click **Publish** to create the post and make it live. To keep it hidden while you work on it, open the menu next to **Publish** and choose **Save as Draft**. The buttons are grayed out until the post has a title. A confirmation message appears and the post's detail screen opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Status and the Active switch

The **Status** card on the right sets where the post is in its life:

- **Published**: live and visible to the public.
- **Draft**: hidden from public view.
- **Scheduled**: goes live automatically at a date and time you choose. See [Schedule a post](#schedule-a-post).
- **Archived**: retired and hidden from public view.

The **Active** switch in **Additional Details** is separate. Turn it off to hide the post everywhere, whatever its status. A banner above the **Post Details** card tells you when the post is a draft, archived, scheduled or turned off.

## Edit a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the post">
    In the list, click the post's title, or choose **Edit** from its actions menu. The **Post Details** screen opens with the post's title and status badge at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change the fields in the **Post Details** and **Additional Details** cards and the cards on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. For a draft, the button reads **Save Draft**. A confirmation message appears and you stay on the post.

    To change a saved post's status, for example to **Archived**, choose another status in the **Status** card and click **Save** (**Save Draft** if you chose **Draft**).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To publish a draft, open the menu next to **Save Draft** and choose **Publish Now**. It saves your changes and publishes the post.

The top of a saved post also has:

- **Actions**: a menu with **Publish Now** (shown for scheduled posts) and **Delete Post**. **Publish Now** saves your changes and publishes the post right away.
- **Translations**: see [Translate a post](#translate-a-post).
- **Preview**: opens the post on your storefront in a new tab. It's grayed out until the post has a storefront URL.

To change a saved post's template, choose one in the **Theme Template** card and click **Save**.

The **Metafields** card below **Additional Details** shows the metafields defined for posts. **Manage Definitions** takes you to [Metadata Definitions](/help/admin/settings/metadata-definitions) in Settings.

<Tip>
  The **Posts** link at the top of a post takes you back to the list with the search, tab and filters you left it with.
</Tip>

## Schedule a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Scheduled">
    On the post, in the **Status** card, choose **Scheduled**. A date and time picker opens below it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the date">
    Choose a date and time in the future, then click **Schedule Activation**. The card shows **Scheduled for activation** with the date and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**, or **Schedule** for a new post.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you save a scheduled post with no date or a date in the past, an error message appears and nothing is saved. To publish a scheduled post early, choose **Publish Now** from the **Actions** menu.

<Warning>
  Scheduling isn't a privacy setting. The date controls when the post appears in listings and can be indexed by search engines, but anyone with the direct link can open it before then. To keep a post out of public listings while you work on it, use **Draft**.
</Warning>

## Set SEO and the post URL

In the **SEO And Link Sharing** card, click **Edit**. The panel has the same fields and limits as for pages: **Title Tag**, **Meta Description**, **Use custom slug** with **URL Slug**, and **Search engine visibility**. See [Set SEO and the page URL](/help/admin/pages#set-seo-and-the-page-url) for the details. **Done** stays grayed out until the **Title Tag** has at least 3 characters and the **Meta Description** at least 5. Click **Done**, then save the post.

The **URL** field shows the post's address on your storefront, under `/home/blog/`. If you don't set an SEO image, the post's image from **Media** is used.

## Translate a post

On a saved post, click **Translations**. You can translate the **Title**, **Description**, **Summary** and **Post Image** for each of your company's active languages. Click **Save** in the translations panel. Translations are saved there, separately from the post's **Save** button. **Language Settings** opens your [Languages](/help/admin/settings/languages) settings in a new tab. For more, see [Translate a page](/help/admin/pages#translate-a-page).

## Delete a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    In the list, choose **Delete** from the post's actions menu. To delete several posts, select their rows and choose **Delete**. On a post, choose **Delete Post** from the **Actions** menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the confirmation dialog, click **Delete** (or **Delete Post**). From a post, you return to the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a post can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  To take a post down but keep it, set its status to **Archived** instead.
</Tip>

## Analytics

Once a post is saved, the **Analytics** card on the right shows **Page Views**, **Shares**, **Page Speed** and **Content Compliance**, as it does for pages. Click a metric for details. See [Analytics](/help/admin/pages#analytics).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Publish Date schedule a post?" id="publish-date-vs-schedule">
    No. **Publish Date** is the post's date, shown in the list. It doesn't control when the post goes live. To publish a post later, choose **Scheduled** in the **Status** card. See [Schedule a post](#schedule-a-post).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my new post already live?" id="new-post-live">
    New posts start with the status **Published**. To create a post without publishing it, choose **Save as Draft** from the menu next to **Publish**. To unpublish a saved post, change its status to **Draft** and click **Save Draft**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I edit a post?" id="cant-edit">
    Your role can view posts but isn't allowed to edit them, so you can't change their fields and there's no **Save** button. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
