The **People** screens are where your team manages the people around your company: your customers and reps, the admins who run your admin, and the contacts your reps follow up with.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, select **People**. It opens to show its screens. You see only the screens your role can view.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Customers" icon="users" href="/help/admin/customers">
    Find, add and manage customers and reps, each on their own member card.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Admins" icon="user-shield" href="/help/admin/admins">
    Add and invite admins, and choose the roles that set what each one can do.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Contacts" icon="address-book" href="/help/admin/contacts">
    Keep your company's contact records and assign each contact to a rep.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
