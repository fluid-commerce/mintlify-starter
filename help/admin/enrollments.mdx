Use the **Enrollments** screen to manage your company's enrollment packs. A pack bundles a one-time product, a subscription product, legal agreements and an optional fee. When a shopper adds a pack to their cart, the cart becomes an enrollment.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Storefront**, select **Enrollments**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Enrollments**. To create, edit or delete packs, it needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Enrollments** is in the **Commerce** card. Open the card and set access from the **Enrollments** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

**Add Enrollment** is at the top of the page if your role can create packs. Below it is the list of packs.

- The **All**, **Published**, **Scheduled** and **Draft** tabs filter the list by status.
- Use the search box to find a pack.
- The filter panel has **Status** and **Country Availability**. Choose values and click **Apply Filters**. **Country Availability** takes one country at a time.

The list has these columns:

- **Preview**: the pack's primary image.
- **Title**: click it to open the pack. You can sort by this column.
- **Status**: **Published**, **Draft**, or **Scheduled** with the date and time it goes live.
- **Countries**: flags for the countries the pack is limited to. **All** means the pack has no country limit.
- **Date Modified**: when the pack was last changed. You can sort by this column.

Each row's actions menu has **Edit**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**. Select rows to use the same menu in the table header, labeled with the count, such as **Duplicate 3 rows**.

A banner above the list lets you try a new version of the table. Click **Try it** to switch, and **Switch back** to return. In the new table, the status and country filters are in the **Filters** menu.

## Create an enrollment pack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new pack">
    On the **Enrollments** screen, click **Add Enrollment**. The **New Enrollment** page opens with the status set to **Draft**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    In the **Enrollment Details** card, enter a **Title** and a **Description**. Both are required.

    **Member Type** sets the member type that buyers of this pack enroll into. If you leave it unset, the pack takes your company's default rep type when it's saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add images">
    Under **Media**, click **Upload Images** and choose up to 10 images at a time. JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP and SVG files up to 10MB are supported. The first image is the primary image. To change it, hover over another image and click its star icon. To remove an image, click its trash icon and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the product">
    In the **Pack Details** card, click **Select Product** and pick one product variant. The pack needs a product, a subscription, or both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the subscription">
    Click **Select Subscription**. The list shows only products that have subscription plans. After you pick a variant, choose a plan from **Select plan** in the **Frequency** column. A subscription can't be saved without a plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on agreements">
    In the **Agreements** card, turn on the switch for each legal page buyers should see. Active agreements are grouped as **Optional**, **Required** or **Footer**, based on how each agreement is set up. Drag an agreement to reorder it within its group. To add or change legal pages, click **Manage All Legal Pages**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the cost">
    In the **Enrollment Cost** card, enter an **Enrollment Fee (Optional)** in USD. The fee is shown in the currency of the country the buyer selects at checkout. The **Initial Costs** summary shows the product price, the fee, and the subscription price and frequency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set availability and template">
    In the **Countries** card, choose the countries where the pack is available. Leave it empty to offer the pack in every country you sell in. In the **Theme Template** card, choose the template the pack's page uses. If your theme has no enrollment template, choose **Create Template**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the pack">
    Click **Create Enrollment**. The button is available once the pack has a title, a description and a product or subscription. The pack's page opens and a confirmation message appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Metafields** card and the **SEO And Link Sharing** card are optional. The SEO card previews how the pack appears in a Google search result and in a text message, and lets you change its SEO image and text.

## Edit a pack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pack">
    In the list, click the pack's title, or choose **Edit** from its actions menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change any card on the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The button is available when you have unsaved changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The pack's page also has:

- **Preview**: opens the pack on your storefront in a new tab.
- **Translations**: edit the **Enrollment Title** and **Description** for each of your languages. On narrower screens, it's in the actions menu.
- **Analytics**: on a saved pack, shows **Page Views**, **Shares**, **Page Speed** and **Content Compliance**. Click a metric for details.
- The actions menu, with **Duplicate**, **Reset Cache** and **Delete**. **Reset Cache** refreshes the pack's cached storefront page.

## Publish or schedule a pack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pack">
    Open the pack from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a status">
    In the **Status** card, choose **Published** to make the pack live, or **Draft** to hide it. To publish later, choose **Scheduled**, pick a date and time, and click **Schedule Activation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**, or **Create Enrollment** for a new pack.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Scheduling isn't a privacy setting. Before its scheduled date, a pack is already reachable by direct link. Until then, it doesn't appear in storefront listings and search engines can't index it.
</Warning>

## Duplicate a pack

Choose **Duplicate** from a pack's actions menu in the list, or from the actions menu on the pack's page. The copy gets the original's title with **(Duplicate)** added. It keeps the product, subscription, agreements, images, countries, fee, status and SEO settings. From the pack's page, the copy opens when it's ready. From the list, the list refreshes and returns to its first page, and a confirmation message shows how many packs were copied.

<Tip>
  The copy has the same status as the original, so a copy of a published pack is published too. Change its status before you edit it if you don't want it live.
</Tip>

## Delete a pack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    In the list, choose **Delete** from the pack's actions menu. To delete several packs, select their rows and choose **Delete** from the table header's menu. You can also choose **Delete** from the actions menu on the pack's page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the confirmation dialog, click **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a pack can't be undone.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is Create Enrollment or Save grayed out?" id="save-disabled">
    The pack needs a **Title**, a **Description**, and a product or subscription. On a saved pack, **Save** is also grayed out until you change something. If you can't change any fields, your role doesn't have **Full access** for **Enrollments**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why won't my pack save with a subscription?" id="subscription-needs-plan">
    Choose a plan from **Select plan** next to the subscription product. A subscription product without a plan can't be saved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to create products first?" id="products-first">
    Yes. The product and subscription pickers list products you've already created. A product appears in the subscription picker only if it has at least one subscription plan. See [Products](/help/admin/products) and [Subscription Plans](/help/admin/settings/subscription-plans).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does All in the Countries column mean?" id="all-countries">
    The pack has no country limit, so it's available in every country you sell in. To limit it, choose countries in the pack's **Countries** card.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do agreements come from?" id="agreements-source">
    The **Agreements** card lists your company's legal pages. Whether an agreement is optional, required or shown in the footer comes from the agreement itself. To change that, or to add a new one, click **Manage All Legal Pages**. See [Agreements](/help/admin/settings/agreements).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
