The **Tools** screens hold what you share with your reps: trainings, news in announcements, and events with their dates and reminders.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, select **Tools**. It opens to show its screens. You see only the screens your role can view.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Trainings" icon="graduation-cap" href="/help/admin/trainings">
    Add training videos, images and PDFs with a title and text, and choose their countries and category.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Announcements" icon="bullhorn" href="/help/admin/announcements">
    Post news with a video, image, PDF or PowerPoint file, and send a push notification about it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Events" icon="calendar" href="/help/admin/events">
    Create events with their dates, time zone and countries, and an optional reminder before they start.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
