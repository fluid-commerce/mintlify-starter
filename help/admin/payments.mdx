The **Payments** screens show how your company's payments are going: how many are approved and why the rest are declined, each transaction, the subscription renewals that failed or are at risk, and where your payments setup stands.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, select **Payments**. It opens to show its screens. You see only the screens your role can view.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Performance" icon="chart-column" href="/help/admin/payments/performance">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Transactions" icon="receipt" href="/help/admin/company-payment-reports">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Subscription Billing" icon="calendar-days" href="/help/admin/subscriptions/billing">

    Follow your Fluid Payments setup and see which processors and countries are live.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
