Use the **Products** screen to manage your catalog. You create products and bundles here, set up their variants, prices and stock, and choose where each one appears.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Storefront**, select **Products**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Products**. To create, edit or delete products, it needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Products** is in the **Commerce** card. Open the card and set access from the **Products** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

**Add Product** is at the top of the page. It opens a menu with **New Product** and **New Bundle**.

- The **All**, **Active**, **Draft**, **Archived** and **Bundles** tabs filter the list. **Active** lists products that are published or scheduled.
- The search box finds products by name, description or URL slug. It doesn't search SKUs.
- The filter panel has **Status**, **Bundles Only**, **Availability** (**In Stock**), **Country Availability**, **Category** and **Collection**. Click **Apply Filters** to use them.

The list has these columns:

- **Product**: the product's main image.
- **Name**: the product title. You can sort by it.
- **Status**: **Published**, **Scheduled**, **Draft** or **Archived**.
- **Price**: the product's price.
- **SKU**: the SKU of the product's default variant.
- **Date Modified**: when the product last changed. You can sort by it.

Click a product to open it. Each row's actions menu has **Edit** and **Delete**. To delete several products, select their rows and use the table header's menu. Its delete action shows the count, such as **Delete 3 products**.

A banner above the list lets you try a new version of the table. Click **Try it** to switch, and **Switch back** to return.

## Create a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new product">
    Click **Add Product**, then **New Product**. The **Create Product** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the details">
    In the **Product Details** card, enter a **Product Title** (up to 255 characters). Add a **Short Description** and a **Description** if you want them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add media">
    Click **Select Media** and choose up to 10 files at a time. Supported formats are JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP, SVG, MP4 and MOV, up to 10MB each. The first item must be an image; a video can't be the featured media. Drag items to reorder them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the SKU and stock">
    In the **Inventory** card, enter the **SKU** and **Barcode (UPC)**. To track stock, check **Track Quantity**, then enter **Available** or **On Hand** for each warehouse. Changing one also changes the other. After you check **Track Quantity**, **Keep selling when out of stock** and the warehouse table appear; check **Keep selling when out of stock** if you want to allow backorders. **Limit Subscription Quantity** sets a **Subscription Max Quantity** and a **Max Quantity Per Person**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set prices">
    In the **Variant Pricing** card, click the arrow at the end of a variant's row to show its countries. Turn on each country where the product is sold and enter its prices. Use **Search Countries** to find a country.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose status and channels">
    In the **Status** card, a new product starts as **Draft**. In the **Publish Options** card, choose where it shows: **Storefront**, **Mobile App**, **Portal Shop** and **Share Tab**. All four are on for a new product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize it">
    Under **Tax Categories**, choose a **USA Tax Category** and an **International Tax Type**. Under **Product Organization**, choose **Collections**, a **Category** and **Tags**. You can type a new tag name to create it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the product">
    Click **Create Product**. It stays grayed out until the product has a title and the page has finished loading. The product's page opens when it's created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Inventory** card only appears while the product has a single variant. Once you add variants, set SKUs and stock on each variant's page. Only active warehouses are listed.

## Edit a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the product">
    In the list, click the product. The **Product Details** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change any card. To offer subscriptions, click **Manage Subscription Plans** in the **Subscription Plans** card, turn on the plans you want, and click **Done**. A plan's menu has **Set as default plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. It's grayed out until you change something. If you leave the page with unsaved changes, the admin asks you to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Preview** opens the product on your storefront once it has a URL. **Translations** opens a grid where you translate the **Product Title**, **Short Description**, **Description**, **Features** and images into your active languages.

To publish later, choose **Scheduled** in the **Status** card and pick the date and time it should go live.

## Add variants

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a variant type">
    In the **Variant Pricing** card, click **Add Variant Type** and choose **Color**, **Size**, **Material**, **Pattern** or **Create Manually**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the options">
    Fill in **Variant Type (e.g. Color, Size)** and the **Variant Options (e.g. Purple, Large)**, then click **Save**. Fluid creates a variant for each combination of options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the product">
    Click **Save** at the top of the page, or **Create Product** if the product is new.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a variant">
    Click a saved variant's row to open its **Variant Details** page. Clicking a variant you haven't saved yet only shows its countries.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The variant page has these cards:

- **Status**: **Active** (available for purchase) or **Inactive** (hidden from customers).
- **Options and Variants**: every variant of the product. Click one to switch to it.
- **Variant Details**: **Display Name** (optional; replaces the generated title for customers), **SKU**, **Barcode (UPC)** and the variant's **Media**.
- **Pricing**: one row per country, with **Enable**, **Price**, **Subscription Price**, **Wholesale Price**, **Wholesale Sub**, **Compare Price**, **QV**, **CV**, **COGS**, **Shipping** and your company's points. Use **Search countries** to find a row.
- **Inventory**: **Track Quantity**, **Keep selling when out of stock**, **Subscription Only**, **Limit Subscription Quantity** and **Limit Quantity Per Person**, plus the warehouse table.
- **Shipping & Measurements**: **Physical Product** or **Digital Product or Service**, **Country of Origin** and **Harmonized System (HS) Code**. Physical products also have **Weight** (lb, kg, g or oz) and **Height**, **Width** and **Length** (In, cm, mm or ft).
- **Variant metafields**.

Click **Save** to keep your changes.

## Duplicate, archive or delete a product

Open the product and click the actions menu at the top of the page. Roles with view-only access don't see this menu.

- **Duplicate** makes a copy and opens it.
- **Archive** hides the product from your sales channels. An archived product shows **Unarchive** instead, which makes it active again.
- **Delete** removes the product. Deleting can't be undone.

Each of these except **Duplicate** asks you to confirm. If the product is part of a bundle, the archive confirmation names the bundle. Saving a status change to **Draft** or **Archived** on such a product also warns you first. Click **Save anyway** to continue or **Go back** to keep editing.

## Create a bundle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new bundle">
    Click **Add Product**, then **New Bundle**. The **Create Bundle** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the details">
    Enter a **Bundle Title** and the other details, as for a product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add groups">
    In the **Bundle Groups** card, add a **Static** group for items that are always in the bundle, or a **Customizable** group for items the customer chooses. Add at least one item to each group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the bundle">
    Click **Create Bundle**. A bundle needs at least one group, and every group needs at least one item.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Bundles appear on the **Bundles** tab.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I find a product by its SKU?" id="search-by-sku">
    The search box matches product names and descriptions, not SKUs. Search by name, or use the filters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Create Product grayed out?" id="create-product-disabled">
    The product needs a **Product Title**. If you've entered one, wait for the page to finish loading.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see the Inventory card?" id="no-inventory-card">
    The product has more than one variant. Open each variant from the **Variant Pricing** card and set its stock there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I change a product's SEO?" id="product-seo">
    On the product's page, find the **SEO And Link Sharing** card and click **Edit**. You can set the **SEO Image**, **Title Tag** and **Meta Description**, use a custom **URL Slug**, or turn on **Block search engine crawlers**. Then click **Save** on the product page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I make a product available in another country?" id="country-availability">
    In the **Variant Pricing** card, click the arrow at the end of the variant's row, or open the variant and use its **Pricing** card. Turn on the country, enter its prices, and save.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
