A playlist groups media and pages into one storefront page you can share or embed, with products and enrollment packs tagged alongside. Use the **Playlists** screen to create, edit, schedule and delete them.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Storefront**, select **Playlists**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Playlists**. To create, edit or delete playlists, it needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Playlists** is in the **Content & Website** card. Open the card and set access from the **Playlists** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

**Create Playlist** is at the top of the page.

- The **All**, **Published**, **Scheduled** and **Draft** tabs filter the list by status.
- Use the search box to find a playlist.
- Click the **Title** column header to sort by title.
- Use the previous and next controls at the bottom to page through the list.

The list has these columns:

- **Preview**: the playlist's image, or an empty frame when it has none.
- **Title**: the playlist's title, or **Untitled**.
- **Status**: **Published**, **Draft**, or **Scheduled** with the date and time it goes live.
- **Countries**: flags for the countries the playlist is available in, with **+2** and so on when there are more. **All** means it isn't limited to any country.
- **Owner**: **Company** for your company's playlists, or the member's name for a playlist a member made.
- **Date Modified**: when the playlist last changed.

Click a playlist to open it. Each row's actions menu has **Edit** and **Delete**. Select rows to delete several at once from the table header's menu, which reads **Delete 2 rows** and so on.

A banner above the list lets you try a new version of the table. Click **Try it** to switch, or **Switch back** to return. The new table adds **Status** and **Country** filters. Your choice is saved in your browser.

## The playlist page

The same page opens when you create a playlist, click a playlist in the list, or choose **Edit**. A new playlist's page is titled **New Playlist**. A saved playlist's page is titled **Playlist Details**, with its status badge next to its name in the breadcrumb.

The main column holds:

- **Playlist**: a preview of the playlist's media and pages, one at a time, with arrows when there's more than one. Click the preview to view it full screen. It appears once the playlist is saved.
- **Playlist Details**: **Title** (required), **Description**, and **Media**, the image used when the playlist is shared.
- **Playlist Items**: the media and pages in the playlist, in order.
- **Tagged Products**: products and enrollment packs shown alongside the playlist.
- **Metafields**: the custom fields your company defines for playlists. Each field has its own **Save**. **Manage Definitions** opens the [Metadata Definitions](/help/admin/settings/metadata-definitions) settings.

The cards on the right:

- **Status**: **Published**, **Draft** or **Scheduled**. A new playlist starts as **Draft**.
- **SEO And Link Sharing**: a preview of how the playlist looks when its link is shared. Click **Edit** to change the SEO title, description and image.
- **Countries**: the countries the playlist is available in. Leave it empty to make it available in every country you sell in.
- **Theme Template**: the template from your theme that displays the playlist's page.
- Analytics: **Shares**, **Page Speed** and **Content Compliance** for a saved playlist, plus **Page Views** when there's data. Click a metric for details.

On a saved playlist, the top of the page also has an actions menu with **Delete**, and the **Embed**, **Translations** and **Preview** buttons.

## Tasks

### Create a playlist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new playlist">
    On the **Playlists** screen, click **Create Playlist**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the details">
    Enter a **Title**. Add a **Description** and choose **Media** if you want them. Fluid builds the playlist's URL from the title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add media and pages">
    In **Playlist Items**, click **Add Item**, then choose the media and pages to include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag products">
    In **Tagged Products**, click **Add Item**, then choose the products and enrollment packs to show with the playlist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set status, countries and template">
    Choose a **Status**, limit **Countries** if you need to, and choose a **Theme Template**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. If your active theme has no default playlist template, a dialog offers to create one. Click **Create template** to continue, or **Not now** to leave the playlist unsaved. When it's created, a confirmation message appears and the playlist's page opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Edit a playlist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the playlist">
    In the list, click the playlist, or choose **Edit** from its actions menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change the details, status, countries, template or SEO.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. A confirmation message appears and you stay on the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On a saved playlist, items you add or remove in **Playlist Items** and **Tagged Products** are saved right away. To reorder items, drag them in their table, then click **Save**.

To remove an item, open its row's menu and choose **Delete Item**. **Edit Item** opens the item's own page, such as the media item or product.

### Schedule a playlist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Scheduled">
    On the playlist's page, in the **Status** card, choose **Scheduled**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the date and time">
    Pick when the playlist should go live and click **Schedule Activation**. The card shows the scheduled date and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Scheduling isn't a privacy setting. Before the scheduled date, anyone with the playlist's direct link can still open it. The date controls when it appears in listings and can be indexed by search engines.
</Warning>

### Share, embed or translate a playlist

On a saved playlist's page:

- Click **Preview** to open the playlist on your storefront in a new tab.
- Click **Embed** to get embed code for your website or blog, then copy it from the **Embed Playlist** window.
- Click **Translations** to translate the **Playlist Title**, **Description** and **Playlist Image** into your company's active languages.

### Delete a playlist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    In the list, choose **Delete** from the playlist's actions menu. You can also open the playlist and choose **Delete** from the actions menu at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Delete Playlist** dialog, click **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a playlist can't be undone.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is Save grayed out?" id="save-grayed-out">
    **Save** stays grayed out until you change something. If you've made changes and it won't save, check that **Title** isn't empty.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I change a playlist?" id="cannot-edit-playlist">
    Your role can view playlists but doesn't have **Full access** for **Playlists**. The fields are grayed out and there's no **Save** button. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the list show playlists my company didn't make?" id="member-playlists">
    The list includes playlists that members made for themselves. The **Owner** column shows the member's name for those, and **Company** for your company's playlists.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where did the item count and cart value columns go?" id="removed-columns">
    The list no longer shows them. Open a playlist to see its items.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
