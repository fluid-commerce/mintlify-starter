Use the **Collections** screen to group products for display on your storefront. Each collection has its own title, description, image, product list and storefront page.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Storefront**, select **Collections**.

<Note>
  To open this screen, your role needs **View only** or **Full access** for **Collections**. To create, edit or delete collections, it needs **Full access**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Collections** is in the **Commerce** card. Open the card and set access from the **Collections** area's menu, not the card's.
</Note>

## What's on the screen

The **Add Collection** button is at the top of the page.

- The **All**, **Published**, **Scheduled**, **Draft** and **Archived** tabs filter the list by status.
- Use the search box to find a collection.

The list has these columns:

- **Preview**: the collection's image, or an empty frame if it has none.
- **Name**: the collection's title. Click it, or anywhere on the row, to open the collection.
- **Status**: **Published**, **Draft**, **Archived**, or **Scheduled** with the date and time it goes live.
- **Countries**: the countries the collection is limited to, shown as up to two flags plus a count of the rest, such as **+3**. **All** means the collection isn't limited and is available in every country you sell in.
- **Date Modified**: when the collection was last changed.

The newest collections are listed first. Click the **Name** or **Date Modified** header to sort by that column.

Each row's actions menu has **Edit** and **Delete**. To delete several collections, select their rows and choose **Delete** from the table header's menu.

<Tip>
  A banner above the list lets you try a new version of the table. Click **Try it** to switch, or **Switch back** to return. The new table adds **Status** and **Country** filters. Your choice is saved in your browser.
</Tip>

## Create a collection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new collection">
    On the **Collections** screen, click **Add Collection**. The **Create Collection** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    In the **Collection Details** card, enter a **Title**. Add a **Description** and, under **Media**, click **Select Image** to choose an image. Images can be JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP or SVG, up to 10MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add products">
    In the **Associated Products** card, click **Add Products**. In the picker, click **Add** next to each product you want, then click **Done**. Drag products by their handle to set the order they appear in on your storefront.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set status, countries and template">
    Use the cards on the right. See [Collection settings](#collection-settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the collection">
    Click **Create Collection**. A confirmation message appears and the collection's page opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Create Collection** stays grayed out until the collection has a **Title**. A new collection starts as **Published** and available in all countries.

## Edit a collection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the collection">
    In the list, click the collection, or choose **Edit** from its actions menu. The **Collection Details** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change the details, products or settings. To remove a product, click **Remove** next to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. A confirmation message appears. **Save** is grayed out when there's nothing to save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

At the top of a saved collection's page you also have:

- **Preview**: opens the collection's storefront page in a new tab. It's grayed out until the collection has a storefront URL.
- **Translations**: opens a panel where you translate the **Collection Title**, **Description** and **Collection Image** into your company's other languages. Click **Save** in the panel.
- **Actions**: a menu with **Reset Cache**, which starts clearing the stored copy of the collection's storefront page, and **Delete Collection**.

## Collection settings

The cards on the right of the collection's page:

- **Status**: choose one of these.
  - **Published**: live and visible to the public.
  - **Draft**: hidden from public view.
  - **Scheduled**: goes live at the date and time you choose. Pick them, then click **Schedule Activation**. If you close the picker without a date, the status goes back to what it was.
  - **Archived**: retired and hidden from public view.
- **SEO And Link Sharing**: click **Edit** to open the panel. See [Set SEO and link sharing](#set-seo-and-link-sharing).
- **Countries**: the countries the collection is available in. Leave it at **All Countries** to make it available everywhere you sell, or choose countries to limit it.
- **Theme Template**: the template from your active theme that displays the collection's page. If your theme has no collection templates, choose **Create Template**. You need an active theme to create one.
- **Analytics**: **Shares**, **Page Speed** and **Content Compliance** for the collection's page. Click a metric to see its details.

<Note>
  A scheduled collection can still be opened by direct link before its date. Scheduling controls when it appears in listings and can be indexed by search engines. It isn't a privacy setting.
</Note>

The **Metafields** card below **Associated Products** shows a field for each metafield definition your company has for collections. **Manage Definitions** opens the [Metadata Definitions](/help/admin/settings/metadata-definitions) settings.

## Set SEO and link sharing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    On the collection's page, in the **SEO And Link Sharing** card, click **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the fields">
    Enter a **Title Tag** and a **Meta Description**, and choose an **SEO Image**. Use **Preview** to see how the link looks as a **Google Search Result**, a **Text Message** or on **X (formerly Twitter)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the URL and visibility">
    The collection's URL slug is made from its title. To set your own, turn on **Use custom slug** and enter the **URL Slug**. Turn off **Search engine visibility** to keep the collection out of Google and other search engines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the panel and save">
    Click **Done**, then click **Save** (or **Create Collection** for a new collection).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Done** is grayed out until the **Title Tag** has at least 3 characters and the **Meta Description** at least 5.

## Delete a collection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    In the list, choose **Delete** from the collection's actions menu. On the collection's page, open **Actions** and choose **Delete Collection** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Delete collection?** dialog, click **Delete**. On the collection's page, the dialog is **Delete Collection** and so is its button. A confirmation message appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a collection can't be undone.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is Create Collection grayed out?" id="create-button-disabled">
    The collection needs a **Title**. Enter one in the **Collection Details** card.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I change anything on a collection's page?" id="read-only-collection">
    Your role can view collections but doesn't have **Full access** for **Collections**. The fields and settings are grayed out and there's no **Save** button. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I change the order of products in a collection?" id="reorder-products">
    In the **Associated Products** card, drag each product by its handle to where you want it, then click **Save**. Products appear on your storefront in this order.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does All mean in the Countries column?" id="all-countries">
    The collection isn't limited to any countries, so it's available in every country you sell in. To limit it, choose countries in the collection's **Countries** card and click **Save**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
