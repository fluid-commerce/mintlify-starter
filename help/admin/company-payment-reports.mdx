Use the **Transactions** screen to see how much your company processed and how many payments were approved, and to look up and export individual transactions. Its page header reads **Payment Reports**.

## Where to find it

In the admin sidebar, under **Payments**, select **Transactions**.

To open the screen, your role needs **Reports** set to **On**. On the [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles) screen, **Reports** is in the **Commerce** card. Open the card and turn it on from the **Reports** area's menu. The same permission opens [Home](/help/admin/home), [Traffic](/help/admin/traffic), [Performance](/help/admin/payments/performance) and [Subscription Billing](/help/admin/subscriptions/billing).

## What's on the screen

### Date range

Next to the page header, a menu sets the date range for the charts: **Last 24 hours** (the default), **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last month**, **Last year**, or **Custom range**. **Last month** is the previous calendar month and **Last year** is the previous calendar year. The other ranges run up to the present.

With **Custom range**, pick a **Start date** and an **End date**. The custom range starts as the last 7 days. The end date can't be after today, and the start date can't be after the end date. The menu then shows the dates you picked.

The date range applies only to the charts. The **Transaction History** table isn't limited by it.

### Charts

Two charts cover the date range. Each shows one figure for the whole range and its change from the previous period, in green when it rose and in red when it fell. The charts plot the range hour by hour for **Last 24 hours**, month by month for **Last year**, and day by day for the other ranges.

- **Transaction Amount**: the total of your successful captures, purchases and settlements over time. Click **# Volume** to see the number of transactions of every status and action instead, under the title **Transactions Volume**. Click **\$ Amount** to switch back.
- **Approval Rate**: the share of approved transactions out of all approved and declined ones in the range, with a bar chart of **Approved** and **Declined** transactions. To see one provider, choose it in the menu that starts at **All Providers**.

The [Payment performance](/help/admin/payments/performance#differs-from-transactions) screen counts only charge requests to the bank, so its approval rate can differ from the one here.

While the charts load, a placeholder shows in their place.

### Transaction History

Below the charts, **Transaction History** lists your company's transactions. The **Export CSV** button above the table opens the export (see [Export transactions](#export-transactions)).

**Tabs**: **Live** (the default) lists live transactions and **Test** lists test transactions. **Declined** and **Pending** list the transactions with that status.

**Columns**, in order: **Type** (**Live** or **Test**), **ID**, **Created**, **Action**, **Status**, **Amount**, **Card**, **Customer**, **Customer ID**, **Cart Token**, **Payment Account**, **Payment Method**, **Order Source**, **Cart ID**, **Transaction**, **Transaction ID**, **Currency**, **Billing Country**, **Processing Fee**, **Platform Fee** and **Failure Reason**.

- **Status**: point at a declined status to see the reason it was declined.
- **Amount**, **Processing Fee** and **Platform Fee** are shown in the transaction's currency.
- **Transaction** is the transaction's reference. It also appears under the title of the transaction's details.
- **Failure Reason** shows **-** for a successful transaction. For any other status, it shows the response message recorded for the transaction, or **N/A** if there's none.
- A cell shows **-** when the transaction has no value for it, for example a fee of zero.

You can sort by **Type**, **ID**, **Created**, **Action**, **Status**, **Amount**, **Customer**, **Customer ID**, **Payment Account**, **Order Source**, **Cart ID**, **Currency** or **Billing Country**.

**Filters**: **Order ID**, **Transaction ID**, **Payment Action**, **Status**, **Integration**, **Gateway**, **Currency** (a currency code such as `USD`), **Cart ID**, **Billing Country** and **Order Source**. Each list starts at its "all" option, for example **All Statuses** or **All Gateways**. The table shows how many filters you've applied. On the **Declined** and **Pending** tabs, **Status** starts at the tab's status.

The table has no search box. It shows 100 transactions a page by default. You can choose a page size from 5 to 100 and move to the next or previous page.

## Find a transaction

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a tab">
    Choose **Live**, **Test**, **Declined** or **Pending**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the table">
    Open the table's filters. Enter an **Order ID**, a **Transaction ID** or a **Cart ID**, or choose values such as **Status**, **Gateway** or **Billing Country**, and apply them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the transaction">
    Click the transaction's row to open its details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Transaction details

Clicking a row in **Transaction History** opens **Transaction Details**, with the transaction's reference under the title. It shows:

- **Overview**: **ID**, **Type**, **Status**, **Action**, **Amount**, **Currency**, **Billing Country**, **Processing Fee**, **Platform Fee** and **Created At**.
- **Customer & Company**: **Company**, **Customer** and **Customer ID**. If the customer has no name, **Customer** shows their ID.
- **Payment Details**: **Payment Account**, **Integration Class**, **Gateway**, **Transaction ID**, **Card**, **Order Source**, **Cart ID** and **Cart Token**.
- **Response**: the response **Status** and **Message**. This section appears only when the transaction has a response message.
- **Related Transactions**: other transactions linked to this one, each with its reference, action, date, amount and status.

The details are read-only. Nothing in the window changes the transaction. To close it, click the **X** or click outside the window.

## Export transactions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the export">
    In **Transaction History**, click **Export CSV**. The **Export Transactions** window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a status">
    In **Status**, keep **All Statuses** or choose **Unsent**, **Sent**, **Received**, **Success**, **Declined**, **Pending**, **Error** or **Expired**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the dates">
    **Timezone** starts at your browser's time zone, and the start and end dates cover whole days in the time zone you choose. **Start Date** starts at 30 days ago and **End Date** at yesterday. Change them as needed. The end date can't be before the start date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Click **Export**. The window closes, and when the file is ready, your browser downloads it as a CSV file and you see **File downloaded successfully**. To close the window without exporting, click **Cancel** instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The export covers your company's transactions created from the start date through the end date, both days included, with the status you chose. It doesn't follow the table's tab or filters, or the charts' date range. If no transactions match, you see an error message instead of a file.

<Tip>
  **End Date** starts at yesterday, so today's transactions aren't in the export unless you set **End Date** to today.
</Tip>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why doesn't the table change when I pick a date range?" id="table-date-range">
    The date range next to the page header applies only to the charts. To narrow **Transaction History**, use its tabs and filters. To get the transactions between two dates, export them with a **Start Date** and an **End Date**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why doesn't the approval rate match Payment performance?" id="approval-rate-differs">
    This screen counts every approved or declined transaction in the range, whatever the action, so voids, refunds and captures count. Pending, error and expired transactions don't. **Payment performance** counts only charge requests to the bank. See [Payment performance](/help/admin/payments/performance#differs-from-transactions).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I refund or void a transaction here?" id="refund-or-void">
    No. **Transaction History** and **Transaction Details** are read-only. Nothing on this screen changes a transaction.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Transactions in the sidebar?" id="transactions-missing">
    **Transactions** appears in the **Payments** group only if your role has **Reports** set to **On**, in the **Commerce** card. Ask an admin who manages roles to update your role. See [Roles](/help/admin/settings/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
