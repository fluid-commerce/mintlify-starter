Instead of sending random content and hoping something sticks, use playlists to give every rep a repeatable way to share the right content with the right person at the right time.

A playlist groups media and pages into one page you can share. Brand teams create company playlists in the admin's [Playlists](/help/admin/playlists) screen. In the mobile app, reps can share those playlists, copy them to **My Playlists**, and create their own.

## Share: make a strong first impression

Sharing good content cuts through the noise and starts real conversations, whether you use your company's content or your own.

Build each playlist from a mix of content:

- Photos, videos and documents
- An attention-grabbing opener that works in the first 3 seconds
- Product demonstrations and testimonials
- Before-and-after results
- Lifestyle content that inspires

Keep the quality high:

- **Images:** sharp and well lit.
- **Videos:** short, about 1 to 3 minutes.
- **Documents:** clean, easy-to-scan PDFs.

## Engage: turn interest into conversations

Engagement turns passive viewers into conversations. Fluid helps you spot the right moment:

- **Catch Up**, in the mobile app, suggests follow-ups, for example when a contact views a page you shared, starts a video but doesn't finish it, or abandons their cart. Your company chooses which suggestions are on in [Catchups](/help/admin/settings/catch-ups).
- **Messaging** lets you reply in the app while their interest is high.
- **Playlist analytics** in the admin show how often each playlist is shared and viewed.

Make the most of it:

- Respond within hours when someone engages.
- Follow up on what they viewed, not on what you want to sell.
- Notice which content gets the most interaction, and use more of it.

## Personalize: tailor playlists to each audience

Content that fits the person converts better than one-size-fits-all.

- Build focused playlists for specific needs, such as a product line or a common question.
- Make different playlists for customers and for potential team members.
- Use what a contact viewed to decide what to share next and when.

## Set up your playlist system

<Steps>
  <Step title="Organize your content">
    Review the content you have and sort it into categories, such as product showcases, opportunity stories, training resources and success stories. Then create a playlist for each theme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan how you'll share">
    When you share with a specific person, send a SmartLink to that contact, so what they view is tracked to them. Prepare a few short replies for common questions so you can respond quickly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor and refine">
    Watch what people view and which playlists lead to orders. Keep what works and replace what doesn't.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips

- Keep videos to 3 minutes or less.
- End with a clear call to action.
- Use your brand's voice consistently.
- Reply to engagement within 24 hours, and make every message personal.
- Review your results every week, and rotate in fresh content.
- Train your team on what's working.
- Start small, test, and double down on what works.

<Tip>
  Playlists are living tools. Keep them fresh, relevant and in line with your company's policies.
</Tip>
