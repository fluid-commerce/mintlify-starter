Turn your company's app into a place your reps open every day. Whether you're managing content, watching results or building community, this guide helps you create an app experience that keeps people coming back.

## Build your engagement foundation

Engagement starts with compelling content and a clear design. Your app needs regular care to stay useful.

### Keep your content fresh

Your content library drives engagement. Keep it fresh in three ways:

**1. Refresh regularly**

- Review your content every week.
- Remove outdated material promptly.
- Highlight new products.

**2. Organize it**

- Create clear categories.
- Build targeted playlists.
- Tag content so it's easy to find.
- Feature your most popular items.

**3. Share success stories**

Nothing drives engagement like real results from real people. Feature your reps' and customers' genuine stories, with their permission.

### Think mobile first

Your app should feel like a personal assistant in every rep's pocket, not a scaled-down website. Build the Home screen in the admin's [Home Tabs](/help/admin/mobile/home) from widgets that put the most-used tools up front:

| Feature | Purpose | Result |
| - | - | - |
| **Quick Links** widget | One-tap access to key screens | Less friction |
| [Announcements](/help/admin/announcements) with push notifications | Targeted updates | More engagement |
| Announcements for specific countries or ranks | Content that fits the reader | More relevance |

<Tip>
  Test your app on different devices and screen sizes. What looks right on your phone might need adjusting on others.
</Tip>

## Track and improve engagement

Your numbers show what's working.

### Daily signs to watch

Check these every morning:

- How many reps are active. The **Field** tab of the admin's [Home](/help/admin/home) dashboard shows reps active yesterday and this month.
- How much reps share
- When the app is busiest
- How much your team communicates
- How often customers interact with reps

These numbers are your app's pulse. They tell you whether your engagement strategy is working.

## Training and development

A good training program is built in layers, from a strong foundation up.

### Map the learning journey

| Stage | Focus | Outcome |
| - | - | - |
| Onboarding | Platform basics | Confident navigation |
| Core skills | Essential features | Daily tasks mastered |
| Advanced | Strategic tools | Business growth |
| Leadership | Mentoring others | Community building |

### Build your resource library

Make your training as dynamic as your platform. Include:

**Quick guides:**

- Feature walkthroughs
- Task checklists
- Tips and tricks
- Troubleshooting guides

**In-depth content.** The best training explains why something matters, not just what to do. Real success stories make training more engaging.

### Learn by doing

1. **Live demonstrations**
   - Show real-world uses.
   - Address common challenges.
   - Share insider tips.
2. **Practice sessions**
   - A safe place to experiment
   - Immediate feedback
   - Learning from peers
3. **Success workshops**
   - Share winning strategies.
   - Discuss real examples.
   - Build best practices.

<Tip>
  Record your live training sessions. They add to your resource library and help people who couldn't attend.
</Tip>

## Key engagement features

### MySite

Help reps turn their MySite into a sales tool:

- Guide their theme choices.
- Encourage regular updates.
- Showcase the right products.
- Track how well it captures leads.

### Contacts

Help reps build relationships at scale:

- Import and organize their contacts.
- Follow what contacts engage with.
- Set follow-up tasks.
- Keep nurturing relationships.

### Announcements

[Announcements](/help/admin/announcements) can go to everyone, or only to certain countries or ranks, and you can send a push notification about each one. For a team or one person, use [Messaging](/help/admin/messages) instead.

| Audience | Best for | How often |
| - | - | - |
| Everyone | Major updates | Monthly |
| A country or region | Local events | Weekly |
| A rank | Recognition and rank-specific news | As achieved |
| A team or one person | Coaching | As needed |

### Shopping experience

Make buying easy:

- Feature products strategically.
- Promote current offers.
- Keep checkout smooth.
- Follow up on abandoned carts. See [Cart abandonment](/help/playbook/optimizing-your-cart-abandonment-strategy).
- Watch what people buy.

## Keep the home screen working

The Home screen is mission control for engagement. Useful widgets in [Home Tabs](/help/admin/mobile/home) include:

- **Quick Links**, for one-tap access to key screens
- **Analytics**, showing each rep's visitors, views and shares
- **Calendar**, for upcoming events
- **Leaderboard**, ranking reps by shares, order volume or web traffic
- **Catchup** and **To-Do List**, for each rep's follow-ups

Refresh it regularly:

- Update your announcements.
- Feature upcoming events.
- Spotlight achievements.

## Communicate well

Great communication is the heartbeat of engagement. Build it in layers:

1. Essential communications that everyone needs
2. Regular engagement
3. Feature updates
4. Targeted announcements

### Choose the right channel

| Channel | Best for | Timing |
| - | - | - |
| Push notifications | Urgent updates | Immediately |
| In-app announcements | Feature and company news | While people use the app |
| Email | Detailed information | At planned times |
| Chat | Personal conversations | In real time |

### Keep your community informed

- Share platform updates promptly.
- Announce new features with clear benefits.
- Highlight results.
- Recognize achievements regularly.
- Give constructive feedback.

## Build a rhythm

Different tasks need different rhythms.

### Every day

**Morning:**

- Check recent activity.
- Handle urgent follow-ups.
- Review your key numbers.

**Midday:**

- Add fresh content.
- Reply to messages.
- Watch your results.

**End of day:**

- Plan tomorrow's content.
- Schedule announcements.
- Note your wins.

### Every week

| Day | Focus | Actions |
| - | - | - |
| Monday | Strategy | Review results, plan content |
| Tuesday | Content | Refresh featured items |
| Wednesday | Community | Share leaderboard highlights |
| Thursday | Growth | Plan upcoming events |
| Friday | Celebration | Share success stories |

### Every month

1. **Content**
   - Review your whole library.
   - Archive outdated content.
   - Plan next month's themes.
   - Review engagement.
2. **Performance review.** Look for the stories behind your numbers, not just the numbers.

### Every quarter

1. **Strategy review**
   - Look at long-term trends.
   - Find growth opportunities.
   - Plan major updates.
2. **App improvements**
   - Roll out new features.
   - Improve existing tools.
   - Refresh training materials.
3. **Community building**
   - Celebrate the quarter's wins.
   - Recognize top performers.
   - Launch new initiatives.

<Tip>
  Try spending 20 minutes each morning reviewing contact engagement and planning follow-ups.
</Tip>

## Run a sharing contest

Contests can drive app adoption and growth.

### Set it up

| Part | Details | Result |
| - | - | - |
| Goals | Clear, measurable goals | Focused effort |
| Timeframe | Weekly, monthly or quarterly | Sustained engagement |
| Tracking | Shares and engagement | Measurable results |
| Lead generation | New contacts | Business growth |
| Conversion | Sales from shares | Return on investment |

### Write clear rules

- Who can take part
- Minimum shares
- Which content counts
- How results are verified
- What the prizes are

### Keep it exciting

- Post leaderboard updates. The **Leaderboard** widget can rank reps by shares.
- Highlight successful sharing strategies.
- Celebrate milestones.
- Share progress regularly.
- Share examples of what works.

Consistent communication and recognition keep excitement high.

### Recognize and reward

1. **Right away**
   - Announce winners promptly.
   - Share their stories.
   - Deliver prizes quickly.
2. **Over time**
   - Recognize more than one category.
   - Write down what worked.
   - Turn it into a playbook for next time.

## Your path forward

Engagement works like a flywheel: better features lead to more use, more use brings more feedback, and feedback leads to better results.

### Your next steps

1. **Start small, think big**
   - Choose one area to improve first.
   - Measure where you are now.
   - Make improvements step by step.
   - Write down what works.
2. **Build momentum**

   | Week | Focus | Goal |
   | - | - | - |
   | 1 | Review | Find opportunities |
   | 2 | Plan | Create action items |
   | 3 | Act | Make the changes |
   | 4 | Measure | Check the results |

3. **Scale what works**
   - Share wins with your team.
   - Document best practices.
   - Train others on what works.
   - Repeat and refine.

<Tip>
  The most successful apps aren't built in a day. Focus on steady progress over perfection.
</Tip>
