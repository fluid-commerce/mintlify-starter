A cold lead is someone who showed interest once and then went quiet. This guide shows how to use your contacts in Fluid to reconnect with them through personal, useful follow-ups.

## Start with your cold contacts

Each contact has a status, such as **New** or **Cold**. In the mobile app, the status shows on the contact. In the admin, it's in the **Status** column of the [Contacts](/help/admin/contacts) list. Start with the contacts marked **Cold**.

## Know your contact before you reach out

In the mobile app, a contact's page has four tabs. Check them before you write:

- **Activity**: what the contact has done, such as pages they viewed, videos they watched and items they added to their cart.
- **Tasks**: follow-ups you've planned for this contact.
- **Notes**: what you talked about, their interests and their preferences.
- **Profile**: their contact details.

After each conversation, add a note with the key points and the products they asked about, and create a task for your next follow-up.

## Group contacts to plan your follow-ups

If your app has contact groups, use them to plan your outreach. The **Groups** tab is on the **Contacts** screen. Useful ways to group contacts:

| Location | Interest | Product focus | Priority |
| - | - | - | - |
| Region or market | How engaged they are | Product categories | High-value leads |
| Local area | Website activity | What they need | Recent conversations |
| Time zone | Content they viewed | Price range | Who replied before |

## Rewrite your follow-up messages

Skip the generic "just checking in". Write messages that show you remember the person and what they care about. For example:

> "Sarah, I noticed you were interested in our wellness collection. Here's a quick video showing how other busy parents use these products to keep their energy up..."

| Instead of | Try this | Why it works |
| - | - | - |
| "Just checking in!" | "I thought of you when I saw this new solution for \[a challenge they mentioned]" | Shows you remember and care about their needs |
| "Don't miss this amazing opportunity!" | "Based on your interest in \[something they did before], I wanted to share..." | Builds on your last conversation |
| "Limited time offer!" | "Since we talked about \[topic], I wanted to make sure you knew about..." | Gives context and shows you were listening |
| "Check out our latest product!" | "You mentioned struggling with \[problem]. Here's how our new \[product] helps..." | Connects your product to a need they stated |
| "Don't miss out!" | "Following up on your question about \[topic] with some helpful information..." | Gives them something useful |

## Share content that re-engages

Make every share feel like part of a conversation, not a presentation.

**Open with something that stops the scroll:**

- Before-and-after results
- Quick wins
- Real customer stories
- Behind-the-scenes moments

**Then build trust with content that helps:**

- Solves a specific problem
- Answers a common question
- Shows your expertise
- Helps them decide

## Watch for signs that a lead is warming up

Track how people respond:

- How often they view what you share
- How quickly they reply to your messages
- Whether they click your links
- Whether they fill in your forms

And watch for signals that they're ready for a next step:

- They visit your site more often.
- They spend more time with your content.
- They add items to their cart.
- They reply faster.

## Be patient and consistent

Warming up a cold lead takes time. Focus on:

1. **Consistent value.** Build trust with helpful, relevant content.
2. **Personal communication.** Make every message about them.
3. **Patience.** Let the relationship develop at its own pace.
4. **What the data tells you.** Let their engagement guide your next step.

<Tip>
  The best follow-ups feel like a helpful check-in from a trusted advisor, not a sales pitch from a stranger.
</Tip>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the best way to craft follow-up messages?" id="whats-the-best-way-to-craft-follow-up-messages">
    Replace "just checking in" with a message that mentions something specific: a challenge they told you about, a question they asked, or content they viewed. Each follow-up should give them something useful and move the relationship forward. See [Rewrite your follow-up messages](#rewrite-your-follow-up-messages) for examples.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I capture and nurture leads?" id="how-do-i-capture-and-nurture-leads">
    **Capture.** Ask for contact details where interested visitors already are:

    - **Pop-ups:** time them well and offer clear value. Show them when a visitor is about to leave or a few seconds after they arrive, and don't show more than one or two per visit.
    - **Forms:** keep them to four or five fields, and send an automatic reply.
    - **Landing pages:** give each one a single purpose, a strong call to action and fast loading on phones.

    Give people a reason to sign up, such as a free guide, a plan or a product comparison that fits what you sell. Test different offers to see which one works best.

    **Nurture.** Follow up in stages:

    | Stage | Timing | What to send |
    | - | - | - |
    | Welcome | Right away | A welcome video, a quick-start guide or a product overview |
    | Educate | Days 2 to 7 | How-to content, success stories and answers to common questions |
    | Engage | Week 2 | A personal check-in to learn what they need |
    | Convert | Weeks 3 to 4 | A special offer, a bundle or an invitation |

    Adjust the timing to how each person responds. Always make it easy to opt out, and respect people's privacy choices. To manage the contacts you capture, see [Contacts](/help/admin/contacts).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
