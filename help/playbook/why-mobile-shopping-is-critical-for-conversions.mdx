Many of your customers browse, share and buy on their phones. A mobile-first approach to design and shopping helps you turn more of those visits into orders.

## Why mobile matters

- **Shopping habits have shifted.** For many shoppers, the phone is now the main way they shop.
- **Expectations are high.** Your customers don't just compare your mobile experience with other direct selling companies. They compare it with the best shopping apps they use every day.
- **Complexity costs sales.** Shoppers and potential leaders give up on enrollment and checkout flows that take too many steps. Reduce the taps it takes to enroll and to buy.

## What Fluid gives you on mobile

### A faster checkout

- Fluid Checkout keeps the whole purchase on one page, and returning customers with a saved profile can pre-fill their details. See [Checkout](/concepts/checkout).
- Offer PayPal and other alternative payment methods, and turn on Apple Pay for your payment gateways. See [APM](/help/admin/settings/apm) and [Gateways](/help/admin/settings/gateways).
- Keep forms short to reduce abandonment.

### A personal shopping experience

- Build product playlists for different customer groups. See [Smart playlists](/help/playbook/transform-your-sales-with-smart-playlists).
- Keep customers informed about products with messages and notifications in the app.
- Give every rep a personal MySite that uses your company's theme. See [Mysite Theme](/help/admin/mysite-themes).
- Announce new products in the app with [Announcements](/help/admin/announcements).

### Follow-up on abandoned carts

- Reps get follow-up suggestions in **Catch Up**, such as a **Cart Abandoned** catchup when a shopper leaves items in their cart. Reps with a premium subscription in the app also see a suggested text message, drafted with AI, on some catchups. See [Catchups](/help/admin/settings/catch-ups).
- For recovery emails and more ideas, see [Cart abandonment](/help/playbook/optimizing-your-cart-abandonment-strategy).

## Optimize for phones

Marketing brings visitors to your store. A good mobile experience makes the most of every visit.

- **Speed:** every second a page takes to load costs you shoppers. Optimize image sizes before you upload them.
- **Structure:** create a clear content hierarchy with logical sections, and keep paragraphs short, 3 to 4 lines at most.
- **Calls to action:** place important buttons within easy reach of the thumb, and above the fold when you can. Use contrasting colors and action verbs.
- **Readability:** keep high contrast between text and background.
- **Forms:** ask only for what you need. Fewer fields on enrollment and checkout forms mean more completions.
