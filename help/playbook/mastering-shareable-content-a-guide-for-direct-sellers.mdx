The right content turns casual browsers into loyal customers. This guide covers what to give your team to share, how to keep it consistent and compliant, and how to tell what's working.

## Why shareable content matters

Shareable content is your digital storefront. Every piece your team shares shapes how customers see your brand. When you give reps carefully chosen content to share, your message stays consistent while their connections stay personal.

Consistency helps you:

- Build brand recognition and trust
- Stay compliant across channels
- Give reps professional resources they can use right away

## Content that converts

### Visual stories that sell

Visual content makes an instant connection. The most effective mix includes:

**Photos:**

- Before-and-after results
- Lifestyle images that inspire action
- Real people using real products
- Behind-the-scenes glimpses of your culture

**Videos,** about 1 to 3 minutes long:

- Quick tutorials that solve problems
- Unscripted testimonials
- Live product demonstrations
- Team celebrations that show your culture

### Resources that build credibility

Back up your visual content with:

- Well-designed product catalogs
- Step-by-step training guides
- Roadmaps for new team members
- Compliance documentation

## Hook viewers in the first 3 seconds

You have about three seconds to stop the scroll. Start with movement, open with something intriguing, and lead with your strongest result. Then keep the attention you've earned:

- Keep videos short and to the point.
- Add captions. Many people watch with the sound off.
- End with a clear call to action.

## Make sharing easy for your team

Reps share more when the content is easy to find and easy to send. In the mobile app, reps share from the Share screen, which your company builds in the admin's [Share Tabs](/help/admin/mobile/share). Give them:

- Content that's ready to share from their phones
- Up-to-date versions as soon as you change something
- Wording your company has approved

Then help them get good at sharing, with training sessions, best practices such as this Playbook, and feedback on what works.

## Measure what matters

Watch these signals:

- **Views** tell you what captures attention.
- **How often reps share** shows team adoption.
- **Engagement** shows content quality.
- **Orders** show what really works.

Use what you learn to:

1. Find your top-performing content.
2. Improve when and how often you share.
3. Refine your content mix.
4. Adjust your training.

## Play it safe: compliance guidelines

**Do:**

- Share real, verified experiences.
- Focus on lifestyle benefits.
- Stay within the claims your company has approved.
- Use clear, compliant language.

**Don't:**

- Make income claims or guarantees.
- Make medical claims.
- Use hard-sell tactics.
- Compare yourself to competitors.

## Use Fluid to share smarter

- **Playlists** put content in a deliberate order, to guide each prospect from first look to purchase. See [Smart playlists](/help/playbook/transform-your-sales-with-smart-playlists).
- **Catch Up** in the mobile app suggests follow-ups when contacts engage, for example when someone views a page a rep shared or starts a video without finishing it. Your company chooses which suggestions are on in [Catchups](/help/admin/settings/catch-ups).

## Your action plan

1. **Audit and plan.** Take stock of your content and find the gaps. Plan new material that fills them and supports your goals.
2. **Set your team up.** Organize what reps share in the app, and make sure everyone knows where to find it.
3. **Monitor and adapt.** Track your key signals, gather feedback, and keep refining your approach.

<Tip>
  The most effective content strategy balances professional polish with authentic connection. Keep it real, keep it compliant, and measure what works.
</Tip>
