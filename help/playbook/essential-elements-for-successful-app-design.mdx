The most successful apps help people reach their goals with little effort. This guide covers the elements that turn a good company app into one your team opens every day.

## A clear purpose

From the moment someone opens your app, they should understand what it does and why it matters to them. The first screens should answer three questions:

- What can I do here?
- How do I get started?
- Why should I come back?

When people see the value right away, engagement follows.

## Easy onboarding

Each first step should lead naturally to the next.

### Make getting started effortless

People expect:

- Quick sign-in
- As few required fields as possible
- A profile they can complete later
- Immediate access to the main features

The best onboarding feels like a conversation. Guide people through their first actions with help that appears right when they need it.

## Content that converts

Great content needs great presentation. Your app's visual content should be:

- Sharp and professional
- Consistently branded
- Easy to share
- Designed for phones

Organization matters as much as quality. Put content in clear categories, so people find what they need without searching.

## Communication: the heart of engagement

In direct selling, communication is how the business runs. Use each channel for what it does best:

1. **Push notifications** for timely updates
2. **In-app messaging** for team collaboration
3. **SMS** for critical alerts
4. **Email** for detailed communications

Push notifications should feel like a tap on the shoulder, not a bullhorn. See [Push notifications vs SMS](/help/playbook/push-notifications-vs-sms-mastering-multi-channel-communication).

## Growth tools that work

Your app should help your team grow the business, not just run it.

**For sales:**

- One-tap product sharing
- Up-to-date product availability
- An easy shopping experience
- A fast checkout

**For team building:**

- Training your team can take on the go
- Recognition that celebrates success
- Results that show reps where to focus
- Tools for managing a growing team

## Design without code

A successful app feels both capable and easy to use. With Fluid, your team builds the app without writing code:

- Build the app's Home, Share and Shop screens from widgets in the admin. See [Mobile App](/help/admin/mobile).
- Capture leads with forms. See [Forms](/help/admin/forms).
- Make every part of the app reflect your brand.

<Tip>
  Design with intention. Every element should serve your users and reflect your brand.
</Tip>

## Analytics that drive action

Data matters only when it leads to action. Your analytics should answer:

- Which features drive the most engagement?
- Where do people spend their time?
- What content leads to action?
- Which paths lead to a sale?

Track:

- How people use the app
- How well your content performs
- Sales and conversion
- Team activity

And watch the trends:

- How quickly people adopt the app
- Which features they use
- How many keep coming back
- How revenue changes

## Best practices

### Keep it fresh

Your app should feel alive, with:

- Regular content updates
- New features
- Performance improvements
- Changes your users ask for

### Support that scales

Build trust with:

- Clear help documentation
- More than one way to get help
- Quick answers to issues
- Proactive communication

<Tip>
  Reliable, responsive support turns frustrated users into loyal advocates.
</Tip>

## Looking ahead

The best apps evolve with their users. Create feedback loops:

- Gather user feedback continuously.
- Test new features carefully.
- Monitor your results.
- Improve based on real data.

Your app is never finished. It grows with your business.

<Tip>
  Success isn't about having every feature. It's about having the right features, done well. Focus on what matters most to your users.
</Tip>
