People respond to conversations, not pitches. This guide covers what to share, how to word your messages, and when to follow up.

## Share content that connects

Whether you share through Fluid or on social media, share things the other person will find useful or interesting:

- Product demonstrations
- Success stories
- Team achievements
- Training resources
- Business opportunities

### Mix your content types

Each type of content does a different job.

**Photos** make a quick connection. A single strong image can tell a story faster than a paragraph. Share:

- Before-and-after results
- Real people using real products in everyday situations
- Candid team celebrations
- Lifestyle images that inspire

**Videos** build trust, because people can see and hear you. Share:

- Short tutorials, about 1 to 3 minutes long
- Unscripted testimonials that sound like a friend's recommendation
- Behind-the-scenes clips
- Live product demonstrations

**Documents** carry detail and look professional. Share:

- Product catalogs
- Step-by-step training guides
- Roadmaps for new team members
- Event playbooks

## Write messages that start conversations

### Replace pitches with personal messages

| Avoid | Try instead |
| - | - |
| "Amazing opportunity!" | "Sarah, remember how you mentioned..." |
| "Life-changing products!" | "This addresses exactly what we discussed..." |
| "Check this out!" | "Based on your interest in natural wellness..." |
| "Don't miss out!" | "I thought of you when I saw this..." |

### What every message needs

1. **Personalization.** Use their name and something they told you.
   - "Michelle, since you're passionate about clean beauty..."
   - "John, following up on our conversation about growing your business..."
2. **Value first.** Lead with something that helps them.
   - "This quick video shows how to get the results we talked about..."
   - "Here's the solution we discussed for your skincare concerns..."
3. **A natural call to action.** Invite a reply without pressure.
   - "When you have a moment, I'd love your thoughts on this."
   - "Let me know if you'd like to hear more about what worked for me."

## Make videos people watch

Video works because it puts a face to your message. People see real experiences and real results, which builds trust.

**Before you record:**

- Outline your key points, but stay natural.
- Choose a clean, well-lit spot.
- Test your sound.
- Have your products or props ready.

**While you record:**

- Hook viewers in the first 3 seconds.
- Keep it to 1 to 3 minutes.
- Speak with energy, in your own words.
- Show, don't just tell.

**Before you share:**

- Add captions. Many people watch with the sound off.
- Include your brand elements.
- End with a clear call to action.
- Watch it on more than one device.

## Follow up when they engage

Fluid shows you when people engage with what you share. In the mobile app, **Catch Up** suggests follow-ups, for example when a contact views a page you shared, starts a video but doesn't finish it, or hasn't ordered two days after you sent them a SmartLink. Your company chooses which suggestions are on in [Catchups](/help/admin/settings/catch-ups).

To see what a specific person does with your content, share it as a SmartLink: choose the contact, then send the link by text or email, or copy it. Their activity is then tracked to that contact.

When a suggestion appears:

1. Check what they engaged with.
2. Respond with something relevant to it.
3. Add value, such as an answer or a related item.
4. Suggest a next step, without pressure.
5. Keep it conversational.

## Keep your content compliant

Compliance keeps you and your company safe while leaving room to be creative.

**Do:**

- Share real, verified experiences.
- Focus on relatable, everyday moments.
- Keep your messages truthful and clear.
- Follow your company's guidelines.

**Don't:**

- Make income claims or guarantees.
- Make medical or health claims.
- Pressure people or use hard-sell tactics.
- Compare yourself to competitors.

## Build an engagement routine

**Every week:**

- Post at consistent times.
- Share fresh content 3 to 5 times.
- Reply to comments within hours.
- Review what got the most engagement.

**In every post:**

- Ask questions that start a discussion.
- Share stories that invite replies.
- Try polls and other interactive content.
- Celebrate your community's successes.
