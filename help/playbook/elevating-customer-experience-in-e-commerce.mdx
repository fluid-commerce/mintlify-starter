Customers expect a smooth, personal experience at every step. This guide shows how to build one with Fluid, from support to content, and how to measure it.

## Build your foundation

Like a well-designed building, a great customer experience needs solid foundations, thoughtful design and regular maintenance.

### Make support easy to reach

Support is where customer relationships are won or lost. A well-run support setup turns problems into chances to build loyalty.

Add a chat button to your store in the admin, under **Settings** › [Floating Action Buttons](/help/admin/settings/floating-action-buttons). The button opens a short form, so:

- Write a welcoming form title and description.
- Set the button text and the message people see after they send.
- Match the button's colors to your brand.

Then answer quickly. When a web chat message goes unanswered, reps can get an **Unresolved WebChat** catchup in the mobile app, if your company turns it on in [Catchups](/help/admin/settings/catch-ups).

### Make a great first impression

Pop-ups and banners can guide visitors toward meaningful engagement without getting in their way. They should feel like a warm, well-timed handshake.

**Craft your message:**

- Write headlines that speak to what visitors need.
- Time them so they feel natural.
- Test and refine your approach.

**Use them for:**

- Time-sensitive promotions
- Category highlights
- Trust signals
- Personal recommendations

## Personalization that converts

One size fits nobody. Great customer experience comes from understanding each person and responding to what they need.

### Know your contacts

Every interaction is an opportunity. In the admin's [Contacts](/help/admin/contacts) screen, each contact has a status, such as **New** or **Cold**, and a rep. Use them to send the right message at the right time:

- **New:** fresh leads, ready for a first message
- **Cold:** contacts who need re-engaging. See [Warming up cold leads](/help/playbook/warming-up-cold-leads).

Assign each contact to the rep best placed to help, and keep the assignment up to date.

### Mobile first: meet customers where they are

Mobile is now the main channel for many customers. Your app should feel like a personal concierge, not a scaled-down website.

Set up your company's app in the admin's [Mobile App](/help/admin/mobile) screens. Make sure it has:

- Product catalogs with an easy checkout
- One-tap access to common tasks
- Events, training and team updates
- Offers and recommendations that fit each person

## Content that captivates

Content is the currency of digital engagement. Manage your images, videos and documents in the admin's [Media](/help/admin/media) screen.

### Look great on every screen

Use sharp images, and check how each one looks wherever it appears, on large screens and on phones.

### Build pages that convert

Build pages that guide visitors naturally toward action, in the admin's [Pages](/help/admin/pages) screen. High-converting pages have:

1. Compelling headlines
2. A clear value proposition
3. Well-placed calls to action
4. Social proof that builds trust
5. A layout that works on phones

### Extend your reach on social media

- Encourage reps to share their MySite and playlists.
- Share transformation stories.
- Celebrate customer successes.
- Build community through engagement.

## Measure what matters

The admin's [Home](/help/admin/home) dashboard shows revenue, orders by type, sales channels, subscriptions and live visitors. Measure the rest with the tools you already use. A useful rhythm:

**In real time:**

- Website traffic
- Completed checkouts
- Support response times

**Weekly:**

- Conversion rates
- Return visits
- Satisfaction scores

**Monthly:**

- Customer lifetime value
- Cost to acquire a customer
- Brand loyalty

**Quarterly:**

- Process reviews
- Business reviews
- Strategy alignment

## Your roadmap

Great customer experience comes from steady, step-by-step work.

**Foundation:**

- Set up your core systems.
- Configure the basic tools.
- Record your starting metrics.

**Growth:**

- Add engagement tools.
- Smooth out the paths customers take.
- Start personalizing.

**Excellence:**

- Refine based on data.
- Scale what works.
- Keep innovating.

## Best practices

### Put customers first

- Set up clear notifications.
- Follow up automatically where you can.
- Offer more than one way to get support.
- Track customer interactions.
- Monitor engagement.

### Keep your technology current

- Keep admin access up to date.
- Keep your payment gateways current.
- Watch how your systems perform.
- Review your security settings.

<Tip>
  Every improvement in customer experience affects your bottom line. Start with high-impact, low-effort changes, and build from there.
</Tip>

Track these measures of success:

- Customer satisfaction
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- How many carts become orders
- How many customers come back to buy again

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I set up an effective customer support system?" id="how-do-i-set-up-an-effective-customer-support-system">
    **Set up chat**

    In the admin, under **Settings** › [Floating Action Buttons](/help/admin/settings/floating-action-buttons), turn on the chat button and fill in its form: **Form Title**, **Form Description**, **Button Text** and **Success Text**. Keep the form short, set clear expectations for when you'll reply, and match the button to your brand colors.

    **Plan support levels**

    | Level | Who handles it | Best for | Response target |
    | - | - | - | - |
    | 1 | Self-service, such as FAQs or an AI assistant | Basic questions, product information, order status | Immediate |
    | 2 | Your support team | Complex issues, custom requests, technical help | Within minutes |
    | 3 | Specialists | Strategic support, key accounts, escalations | Within hours |

    **Cover after-hours requests**

    | Situation | Automatic reply | Follow-up |
    | - | - | - |
    | Evenings | "We'll reply by \[time]" | Priority the next day |
    | Weekends | A note about weekend coverage | Reviewed Monday morning |
    | Holidays | Your holiday schedule | Confirm your return date |
    | Technical issues | Status updates | Let people know when it's fixed |

    Always offer a clear path to a person, even in automated replies. See [Make support easy to reach](#make-support-easy-to-reach).
