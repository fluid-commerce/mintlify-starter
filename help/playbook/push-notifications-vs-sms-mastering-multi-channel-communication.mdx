Good communication keeps a direct selling team moving. Push notifications and SMS each have a job. This guide helps you choose the right channel for each message and write messages that get read.

## Push notifications

A push notification is a tap on the shoulder: easy to notice, and easy to act on. Used well, push turns passive app users into active participants.

### What push does well

- **Low cost at scale.** Push notifications don't carry per-message carrier fees the way SMS does.
- **Targeting.** Reach the right people. In Fluid, a push notification about an [announcement](/help/admin/announcements) goes to the countries you choose, and the announcement itself can be limited to certain ranks. A notification about an [event](/help/admin/events) goes to every active rep and admin, so save it for company-wide news.
- **Straight into the app.** A tap takes people right to what you want them to see.

Make your push notifications feel like a conversation, not a broadcast.

**Get the timing right.** Send when your audience is most likely to engage. Test windows such as early morning, lunchtime and early evening, in your team's time zones, and keep the ones that work.

**Write messages that get action.** Combine urgency with value. For example:

- _Sarah, you're just 2 sales away from Silver rank! Tap to see who's ready to buy._
- _Flash sale: your team's favorite products at 40% off, for the next 2 hours only._

## SMS

Push excels at engagement. SMS is the channel to use when a message absolutely has to arrive. Use it for:

| Critical updates | Time-sensitive information | Security and verification |
| - | - | - |
| System outages | Flash sales | Sign-in codes |
| Emergency announcements | Limited-time offers | Account alerts |
| Company-wide alerts | Event reminders | Password resets |

SMS costs money for every message, so use it carefully:

1. **Target carefully.** Send only to the people who need the message.
2. **Combine messages.** Group updates into a daily or weekly digest where you can.
3. **Time it well.** Track delivery and adjust your sending times.

## Use both together

Know when to use each channel.

**Push notifications** for everyday engagement and motivation:

- Achievement celebrations
- Performance updates
- Training reminders
- Progress toward personal goals
- Team recognition

**SMS** for business-critical messages:

- System alerts
- Security verification
- Flash sale announcements
- Event reminders
- Account updates

Using push for day-to-day updates and saving SMS for critical messages keeps costs down and keeps your SMS messages meaningful.

## Your action plan

1. **Review.** Look at how you communicate today, and find where to improve.
2. **Plan.** Decide which channel each kind of message uses.
3. **Measure and adjust.** Track the results, and keep refining.

<Tip>
  Start small, test thoroughly, and scale what works. The best communication strategy grows with your team.
</Tip>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How can I optimize message engagement?" id="how-can-i-optimize-message-engagement">
    **Choose the channel**

    | Message | Push notification | SMS | Notes |
    | - | - | - | - |
    | Achievement updates | Preferred | Avoid | Celebrate right away |
    | Sales milestones | Preferred | Avoid | Link to the details in the app |
    | Training reminders | Preferred | Avoid | Send while people are active |
    | Flash sales | Primary | Backup | Use both for important promotions |
    | System outages | Avoid | Required | SMS gets through |
    | Security alerts | Avoid | Required | For verification codes |
    | Event reminders | Primary | Optional | Push first, SMS on the day |
    | Password resets | Avoid | Required | For security |
    | Team recognition | Preferred | Avoid | Make it easy to share |

    **Write better push notifications**

    - Keep them under about 125 characters.
    - Include a clear call to action.
    - Use one or two emojis at most.
    - Personalize them.
    - Link to the right place in the app.

    Examples:

    - _Congrats Sarah! You've hit 80% of your monthly goal. Tap for your final-sprint plan._
    - _Next 3 hours: 40% off bestsellers. Your team's favorites are in stock._
    - _5 team members just ranked up! See their winning strategies._

    **Plan your SMS messages**

    Before you send, decide how urgent it is, choose the audience carefully, check the timing, and make sure it really needs to be a text. Then follow a simple structure:

    ```text
    [Your company] ALERT:
    Flash sale starts now. Top 10 products at 50% off.
    Shop by 11:59 PM ET: [link]
    Questions? Reply HELP
    ```

    **Time and pace your messages**

    | Message | Best time | How often |
    | - | - | - |
    | Achievements | The same day | Once per achievement |
    | Sales alerts | Late morning to early afternoon | Twice a week at most |
    | Updates | Morning or early afternoon | Three times a week at most |
    | Reminders | A day before | As needed |

    Track open and click rates by time and type of message, and test different formats. Avoid generic messages, too many messages, vague calls to action, poor timing and too many emojis. See [Use both together](#use-both-together).
