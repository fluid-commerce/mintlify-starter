Videos that catch attention start conversations. This guide covers how to hook viewers, what to film, and how to learn from what they watch.

## The 3-second challenge

People scroll fast, so you have only a few seconds to make an impact. Hook your audience right away:

- Start with movement.
- Open with an intriguing question.
- Show your product in action.
- Lead with your best result.

Think of your opening like a newspaper headline: it has to grab attention and promise value.

## Content that converts

Include three kinds of video.

### Before-and-after stories

Nothing sells like transformation. Show:

- Product results
- Real lifestyle changes
- Highlights from your business journey

Authenticity matters more than polish. Real stories resonate.

### Quick tips and tutorials

Become the go-to expert with:

- 30-second product demonstrations
- Simple how-to guides
- Daily tips that solve common problems

The best tutorials feel like advice from a friend, not a lecture.

### Behind the scenes

Build trust through transparency:

- Product unboxings
- Day-in-the-life clips
- Team celebrations and milestones

## Get the technical basics right

**Lighting:**

- Face natural light.
- Avoid harsh overhead lighting.
- Consider a simple ring light.

**Sound:**

- Record in a quiet space.
- Speak with energy and clarity.
- Always add captions. Many people watch with the sound off.

**Framing:**

- Keep the camera steady.
- Film training content in landscape.
- Film social content in portrait.

## A simple structure

Plan your video like a short movie:

1. **Hook, about 3 seconds:** grab attention with something unexpected.
2. **Problem, about 5 seconds:** name your viewer's pain point.
3. **Solution, about 10 seconds:** show how you can help.
4. **Proof, about 10 seconds:** show real results.
5. **Call to action, about 5 seconds:** tell viewers exactly what to do next.

Aim for 30 to 45 seconds in total. Shorter is often better.

## Share it with Fluid

1. Choose your best video.
2. Write a caption that sparks curiosity.
3. Include a clear call to action.
4. When you send it to a specific person, share it as a SmartLink, so you can see when they watch.
5. Share it on more than one channel.

## Boost engagement

### Test and learn

- Try different video lengths.
- Test different thumbnails.
- Experiment with posting times.
- Compare different types of content.

Let your results guide what you make next.

### Get the timing right

- Find out when your audience is most active.
- Post consistently in those windows.
- Test different days of the week.
- Consider your audience's time zone.

### Follow up fast

The moment someone watches your video is your best chance to connect. In the mobile app, **Catch Up** can suggest a follow-up when a contact starts one of your videos but doesn't finish it, if your company turns that suggestion on in [Catchups](/help/admin/settings/catch-ups). Then:

- Answer comments and questions while interest is high.
- Share related content.
- Start a real conversation.

<Tip>
  The faster you engage with viewers, the more likely they are to become loyal followers and customers.
</Tip>

## Mistakes to avoid

- Language that's too sales-focused
- Poor lighting or shaky footage
- Long, complicated explanations
- Generic content that isn't personal

## Track your results

Watch:

- How much of each video people watch
- How often people click through
- Which videos lead to sales
- When people engage

Your first video won't be perfect, and that's fine. Authenticity often beats perfection. Start creating, start sharing, and refine as you learn.
