The **Contacts** tab keeps the people you work with in one place. You can text, email or call a contact, sort contacts into groups, and keep notes and tasks for each person.

## Where to find it

In the app, tap the **Contacts** tab.

## What's on the screen

The top bar has:

- **+**, which opens **Add New**: **Create Contact** and **Create Group**.
- A three-dots menu with **Import Contacts** and **Export Contacts**, when your company offers them, and **Bulk Delete**.

Below it are the **Contacts** tab and, if your app shows it, the **Groups** tab.

### Contacts tab

- Search by name, email address or phone number.
- Filter by group with the chips. If you select several, you see only the contacts in all of them. **All** clears the filter.
- Contacts are listed alphabetically. Tap a letter on the right edge to jump to it.
- Each row shows the contact's name and up to two of their groups, plus a count such as **+2** for the rest.
- Swipe right on a contact for **Add to Group**, or left for a delete button.
- Pull down to refresh.

An empty list offers **Sync Phone Contacts**.

### Groups tab

Each group shows its avatar, its name and how many contacts it has. Tap a group to open it, or swipe left on it to delete it.

### A contact's page

Tap a contact to open their page. **+** offers **New Task** and **New Note**. The menu has **Edit Contact**, **Edit Group** and **Delete Contact**.

- **Call** opens your phone app. **Text** opens a **New Text Message** menu and **Mail** opens a **New Email** menu. Tap **Open in Messages** or **Open in Mail** there to continue in your phone's messaging or email app. If your company has turned these menus off, **Text** and **Mail** open the apps directly. A grayed-out button with a **+** means a phone number or email address is missing. Tap it to add one.
- If the contact is in any groups, **Groups** lists them. Tap it to change them.
- The tabs:
  - **Activity**, if your app shows it: the contact's activity, newest first. Tap an entry for details.
  - **Tasks**: the contact's tasks.
  - **Notes**: pinned notes first, then the rest by date.
  - **Profile**: phone numbers and email addresses, each with a copy button.

## Add and edit contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Tap **+**, then **Create Contact**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Fill in what you know:

    - **Upload your contact photo**.
    - **Profile Information**: **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Primary Email Address**, **Secondary Email Address**, **Primary Phone Number** and **Secondary Phone Number**. Enter phone numbers as digits only, with an optional `+` at the start.
    - **Address**: **Country**, **Address Line 1**, **Address Line 2**, **State**, **City** and **Zip Code**. Choose the country first, because the **State** list depends on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save**. A contact needs at least a name, an email address or a phone number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change a contact, open their page and choose **Edit Contact** from the menu.

## Notes

Notes belong to a contact.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a note">
    On the contact's page, tap **+**, then **New Note**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write it">
    Enter a **Note Title** and the note's text. Both are required. To attach a photo or file, tap the paperclip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save Note**. The note stays open, so tap the back arrow when you're done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On the **Notes** tab, tap a note to edit it. Swipe left on a note to **Pin**, **Unpin** or **Delete** it. Pinned notes stay at the top.

## Tasks

A task (to-do) can be linked to a contact or stand alone. A contact's tasks are on the **Tasks** tab of their page. All your tasks are on the **To-Do List** screen. If your **Home** tab has a **To-Do List** widget, tap it to open that screen. See [Getting around the app](/help/mobile/getting-around).

The **To-Do List** screen has **Open** and **Done** tabs, and **+** for a new task. Each task shows its title, its contact and its due date. Overdue dates are red.

### Add a task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start it">
    On the **To-Do List** screen, tap **+**. For a contact's task, tap **+** on their page, then **New Task**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Enter a **Task Title**. It's required. Optionally, tap the person button to link a contact, or the calendar button to pick a due date and time in the future. A task started from a contact's page is already linked to them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save Task**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Complete, edit or delete a task

- Tap the checkbox next to a task to mark it done. Tap it again to reopen it. The **Done** tab lists finished tasks.
- Swipe left on a task and tap **Edit** to change it, or **Delete** to remove it. **Delete** doesn't ask you to confirm.

## Groups

### Create a group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Tap **+**, then **Create Group**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Group Name** that none of your other groups uses. Optionally, pick an emoji on **Change Emoji** and a background color on **Change Color**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save**. It appears once you've made a change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Add contacts to a group

- Swipe right on a contact, tap **Add to Group**, then tap a group.
- On a contact's page, choose **Edit Group** from the menu. Tap a group to add the contact, or tap a checked group to take them out.
- On a group's page, choose **Add New Contacts** from the menu. Check the contacts you want, then tap **Add** with the count, such as **Add (3)**.

### Manage a group

Tap a group on the **Groups** tab to open its page, which lists its members.

- To create a contact in the group, tap **+**, then **Create Contact**.
- To take someone out of the group, swipe left on them, tap the delete button, then **Remove**. The contact stays in your list.
- The menu has **Edit group info**, for the name, emoji and color, and **Delete Group**.

## Import and export

### Sync your phone contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open phone import">
    From the menu, choose **Import Contacts**, then **Add Phone Contact(s)**, and tap **Sync Phone Contacts**. On an empty list, tap **Sync Phone Contacts** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the sync">
    In the panel that opens, tap **Sync Phone Contacts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow access">
    If the app asks to access your contacts, allow it. If you turned access off before, your phone's settings open so you can turn it on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The app adds phone contacts that have a name and an email address or phone number, and skips ones already in your list. A **Syncing contacts** banner shows while it works. If you've shared only some of your phone's contacts with the app, a message may offer **Choose Contacts** so you can share more. **Manage Contacts Access** opens your phone's settings for the app.

### Import a CSV file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open CSV import">
    From the menu, choose **Import Contacts**, then **Import .CSV**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the template">
    Tap **Download .CSV Template** and fill it in. On iOS, the share sheet opens so you can save the file. On Android, choose a folder to save it in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import the file">
    Tap **Import Contacts** and choose your file. Your contacts appear shortly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Keep the template's columns: `First Name`, `Last Name`, `Email`, `Phone` and `Status`. Each row needs an email address or a phone number, and rows with an invalid email address are skipped. For a second email address or phone number, add it to the same cell after a comma. List the contact's groups in the `Status` column.

### Export your contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open export">
    From the menu, choose **Export Contacts**, then tap **Export Contacts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and save">
    The message shows how many contacts you're exporting. Tap **Export**. On iOS, the share sheet opens so you can save or send the file. On Android, choose a folder to save it in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The file has `First Name`, `Last Name`, `Phone`, `Email` and `Status` columns. `Status` lists each contact's groups.

## Delete contacts

To delete one contact, swipe left on them, tap the delete button, then **Remove**. Or open their page, choose **Delete Contact** from the menu, then tap **Delete**.

To delete several at once:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start bulk delete">
    From the menu, choose **Bulk Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select contacts">
    Tap each contact you want to delete, or tap **Select All** to select all your contacts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete">
    Tap **Delete** with the count, such as **Delete (5)**, then tap **Delete** again to confirm. To leave without deleting, tap **Cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Before you confirm, check that the number of contacts in the message is what you expect.
</Warning>

## Tips

- Start every CSV import from **Download .CSV Template**, so your columns match what the app expects.
- To add a missing phone number or email address quickly, tap the grayed-out **Text**, **Mail** or **Call** button on the contact's page.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What can I see in a contact's activity?" id="what-information-can-i-see-about-my-contacts-activity">
    If your app shows the **Activity** tab on a contact's page, it lists their activity, newest first and grouped by day. Each entry has a title, a short description and the time. Tap an entry for details. Activity appears as you share with the contact and they engage. See [Sharing content](/help/mobile/sharing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I create and manage contact groups?" id="how-do-i-create-and-manage-contact-groups">
    Tap **+**, then **Create Group**, and name the group. To add contacts, swipe right on a contact, use **Edit Group** on a contact's page, or use **Add New Contacts** on the group's page. See [Groups](#groups).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I manage tasks for a contact?" id="how-do-i-manage-tasks-for-my-contacts">
    Open the contact's page and go to the **Tasks** tab. To add a task, tap **+**, then **New Task**. See [Tasks](#tasks).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I import or export contacts?" id="mobile-app-importing-and-exporting-contacts">
    From the menu on the **Contacts** tab, choose **Import Contacts** to sync your phone's contacts or import a CSV file, or **Export Contacts** to save your list as a CSV file. See [Import and export](#import-and-export).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get back contacts I deleted?" id="restore-deleted-phone-contacts">
    If they're still in your phone's contacts, run a force sync. From the menu, choose **Import Contacts**, then **Add Phone Contact(s)**, and tap **Sync Phone Contacts**. Tap **Force Contacts Sync**, then **Yes**. Your phone's contacts sync again, including ones you deleted from the app.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
