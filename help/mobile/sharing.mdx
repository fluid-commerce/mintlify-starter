Use the **Share** tab to find the media, pages, products and other content your company provides for you to share. You can send an item to a contact, post its link anywhere, and see who engaged with it.

## Where to find it

In the app, tap the **Share** tab.

## What's on the screen

The top bar shows the **Share** title and a plus icon that opens the **New Shareable** menu. See [Create a trackable link](#create-a-trackable-link).

Your company builds the rest of the Share tab, so it varies. If it has more than one tab, the tabs run across the top, with names your company chooses. Your Share tab may include tabs such as **Media**, **Pages**, **Products** and **Enrollments**. It may also include **Uploads** and **Playlists** tabs for your own content, which [Playlists and content](/help/mobile/playlists) covers.

### Sections

Some tabs are made of sections. Each section shows a row of items under a title.

- If a title has an info icon, tap it to read the section's description.
- Tap the arrow button next to a title to open the section's page, where you can switch between a list and a grid.
- Tap an item to open it. Press and hold an item to open its menu. See [Item menu](#item-menu).
- At the bottom of the tab, a round filter button opens **Filter + Sort**. Choose a **Country** and **Language**, then tap **Apply**. The Share tab then shows content for your choices.

### Item lists

Other tabs list one kind of item, such as media or products. Each item shows its **Views** and **Leads**, and products and enrollment packs show their price. Use the search box at the top to search the list. The filter button at the top opens **Filter + Sort**, where you can sort the list and filter it by **Country** and **Language**. Media lists also filter by **Tags**, **Category** and **Type**. Tap **Apply** to use your choices.

### Item menu

Press and hold an item in a section to open its menu:

- **Add to MySite**: show the item on your [MySite](/help/mobile/my-site).
- **Add to Playlist**: add it to one of your playlists.
- **Save for Later**: add it to your saved items.
- **Copy Share Link**: copy the item's share link.
- **Create Smart Link**: send the item to a contact. See [Send a smart link to a contact](#send-a-smart-link-to-a-contact).

Playlists don't have **Add to Playlist** or **Save for Later**, and items in your saved-items section don't have **Save for Later**. Tap outside the menu to close it.

### Saved items

If your Share tab has a section for your saved items, tap its arrow button to open your saved items. There you can switch between a list and a grid and drag items into a new order. Tap an item's minus button to remove it from your saved items, or its share icon to share it.

### Item pages

Each kind of item has its own page:

- **Media**: the picture or video at the top, with the title, a QR code, your share link and a **Share** button. Below are the description and **Visitor Detail**, which shows the item's **Views** and **Leads**.
- **Product**: the same top area, then two tabs. **Related Shareable** shows the price, the description and the product's marketing media, which you can filter by type and tap to open. If your company uses reward points, it also shows the points the product earns. **Analytics** shows **Visitor Detail**.
- **Page**: a preview of the page, under the banner **Preview mode. Share link for full use.**
- **Enrollment pack**: the pack's pictures or video, description and price, then the products it includes. Tap **Learn More** under a product to open it.

On media, product and page items, the three-dots button at the top opens the **Add To** menu. Its **MySite**, **Playlists** and **Save for Later** options work like the ones in the [item menu](#item-menu). On media, it also has **Copy to Uploads**, which copies the item to your uploads.

Pages and enrollment packs have a bar at the bottom with the item's **Views** and **Leads** and a **Share Link** button. Tap the views and leads to open a map of the item's views, with its **Leads** listed below.

## Tasks

### Share an item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the share drawer">
    On a media or product page, tap **Share** at the top. On a page or enrollment pack, tap **Share Link** at the bottom. The **Quick Share** drawer opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share your link">
    The top of the drawer shows your share link and a QR code for it.

    - Tap the link to copy it.
    - Tap **Preview** to open the link in preview mode.
    - Tap **Share** to send the link with another app on your phone.
    - Tap **Download** to download the item's picture or file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or send it to a contact">
    Under **Send To Your Contacts**, search by name or email, or tap a group to show only its contacts. Tap **Send** next to a contact. Then tap the email, message or three-dots icon to send them a link made for them by email, text message or another app. After 5 seconds, the icons turn back into **Send**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The email and message icons appear only when the contact has an email address or phone number. The drawer lists up to 100 contacts at a time, so search to find others.

### Send a smart link to a contact

A smart link is a link made for one contact. **Send** in the share drawer creates one. You can also start from an item in a section:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Create Smart Link">
    Press and hold the item, then tap **Create Smart Link**. Your contacts list opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a contact">
    Tap the contact. The **Method** menu opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to send it">
    Tap **Send via Text** or **Send via Email** to start a message to the contact with the link in it. Each appears only if the contact has a phone number or email address. Or tap **Copy Smart Link** to open your phone's share options, where you can copy the link or send it with another app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Track engagement in Recent Activity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Recent Activity">
    Tap your profile picture at the top of **Home**, then tap **Recent Activity**. The **Activity** screen opens, and your new activity is marked as read.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a tab">
    **All Activity** lists all your activity, and **Last 7 Days** lists the last seven days. Each entry shows the contact's name, or **Visitor** and a number when it isn't tied to a contact. It also shows the date, what happened and the item it's about.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an entry">
    Tap an entry to see its title, date and description.

    - Tap the contact's name to open their page in [Contacts](/help/mobile/contacts).
    - For a visitor, the row reads **Someone near** and their city and state. Tap it to see the visitor's details.
    - If the entry is about an item, tap the item's card to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See a visitor's details">
    The visitor page has two tabs. **Activity** lists that visitor's activity. **Profile** shows details such as their **Country**, **Campaign**, **Source** and **Term**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your Home tab has a **Recent Activity** section, tap it to open the **Activity** screen on **Last 7 Days**.

### Create a trackable link

A trackable link saves a web address, such as `https://shop.example.com/spring-sale`, as an item you can share.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Shareable menu">
    On the **Share** tab, tap the plus icon at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Create Trackable Link">
    Tap **Create Trackable Link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the address">
    Paste the web address into the field. The app fetches the page and shows a preview card with its title, description and picture. If it can't, **Cannot fetch the url. Try again.** appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish it">
    Tap **Publish**, which turns on once the preview card appears. The **Uploads** screen then opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **New Shareable** menu also has **Create Playlist**, and **Create Media** if your company lets you add your own media. See [Playlists and content](/help/mobile/playlists).

## Tips

- Before you send something, tap **Preview** in the share drawer to see your link in preview mode.
- Use **Save for Later** for items you share often, so they're together in your saved items.
- While a file is uploading, the plus icon at the top of the Share tab doesn't respond. Wait for the upload to finish.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I share a product with a customer?" id="how-do-i-share-products-with-customers">
    Open the product from your **Share** tab and tap **Share**. Under **Send To Your Contacts**, tap **Send** next to the customer and choose how to send it. Later, check **Recent Activity**, or the **Activity** tab on the customer's page in [Contacts](/help/mobile/contacts).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How can I track engagement with my shared content?" id="how-can-i-track-engagement-with-my-shared-content">
    - **Recent Activity**, on your Dashboard. See [Track engagement in Recent Activity](#track-engagement-in-recent-activity).
    - Each item's **Views** and **Leads**, on its card in a list and on its page. For a product, they're on the **Analytics** tab.
    - The **Activity** tab on a contact's page in [Contacts](/help/mobile/contacts).
    - The **Recent Activity** section on your Home tab, if your company added one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between a smart link and a general share link?" id="contact-smart-link-vs-general-share-link">
    - **General share link**: the link at the top of the share drawer. Its QR code, its **Share** button and **Copy Share Link** use the same link. It's the same no matter who you share it with, and it isn't tied to a contact. Use it for posts many people see, such as on social media.
    - **Smart link**: a link made for one contact. You create one when you tap **Send** next to a contact in the share drawer, or with **Create Smart Link**. Use it when you share with one person.

    In **Recent Activity**, an entry tied to a contact shows the contact's name. Other entries show a visitor, such as **Visitor 1234**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I share content from the app?" id="mobile-app-sharing-content-from-mobile-app">
    Open an item from the **Share** tab and tap **Share** or **Share Link**. Then copy or share your link, or send it to a contact. See [Share an item](#share-an-item) and [Send a smart link to a contact](#send-a-smart-link-to-a-contact).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
