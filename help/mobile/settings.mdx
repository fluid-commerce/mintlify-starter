The **Dashboard** holds your own settings: your profile, language, notifications, orders and subscriptions, and account details. This page also covers updating the app, clearing its cache and signing in.

## Where to find it

In the app, tap your profile picture at the top of **Home**.

## What's on the screen

From top to bottom, the **Dashboard** shows:

- **Notifications**: the bell icon at the top. It opens your notification preferences. See [Choose your notifications](#choose-your-notifications).
- **Organization**: the company you're using. It appears only if you belong to more than one company. See [Switch organizations](#switch-organizations).
- Your business card, with your profile photo, name and email. If you have a MySite, the card also has a QR code that opens it.
- Shortcut cards for **MySite** (if you have one), **My Uploads** and **My Playlists**. See [MySite](/help/mobile/my-site) and [Playlists and content](/help/mobile/playlists).
- **Profile**: your photo, name, contact details, username, bio and social media accounts.
- **Recent Activity**: see [Sharing content](/help/mobile/sharing).
- **Orders** and **Subscriptions**, if your company shows them: the orders and subscriptions you placed yourself.
- **Language**: the app's language. The row shows the language you're using.
- **Get Help**: contact support.
- **Account**: your shipping addresses and payment methods.
- **General Information**: the privacy policy, terms of use, app details and **Clear Cache**.
- **Log Out**: signs you out of the app.

## Edit your profile

Your profile has these sections:

- **Profile Photo**: add or change your photo. It uploads when you save.
- **Profile Information**: **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Primary Email Address** and **Mobile Number**. You can't change your email address here.
- **Username**: the last part of your MySite link, so changing it changes the link. See [MySite](/help/mobile/my-site).
- **About You**: your **Bio**.
- **Social Media**: your LinkedIn, Facebook, X, Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest, TikTok, WhatsApp and WeChat accounts.

If your company manages your name or username, you can't edit that field, and a note such as **Your admin manages the name on this account.** appears.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    On the Dashboard, tap **Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Change the fields you need. **First Name** and **Last Name** can't be empty, unless your company manages them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save**. An **Updated successfully** message appears and you return to the Dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A new username needs at least 2 characters, using only lowercase letters, numbers, hyphens and underscores. A hyphen can't come first or last, or next to another hyphen. Capital letters are saved as lowercase.

**Delete Account** is at the bottom of your profile. Tap it, then tap **Delete** to confirm, and the app signs you out. To leave the app without deleting your account, tap **Log Out** on the Dashboard instead.

## Orders and subscriptions

**Orders** and **Subscriptions** list the orders and subscriptions you placed yourself.

### Orders

Each order shows its number, date and **Total**. It also shows **Total CV/QV**, unless your company hides volume. If you don't have any orders, the list says **No Orders**.

Tap an order to open its **Order Detail** page:

- The order number, date, number of items, and the items.
- **Payment**: **Subtotal**, any points you used, **Discount**, **Tax**, **Shipping** and **Total**. Unless your company hides volume, the order's CV and QV appear below.
- **Payment & Shipping**: **Shipping Address**, **Shipping Method**, **Tracking Information** and **Payment Method**. Each shipment shows its tracking number, with a **Track package** link to the carrier's tracking page when one is available. Digital orders show **No shipping required**, and free orders show **No payment required**.

Tap **Manage Order** at the bottom to open the order's management page inside the app.

### Subscriptions

Each subscription shows its status, ID, next billing date, how often it's delivered, and its price. A cancelled subscription shows the date it was cancelled instead. A past-due subscription shows **Next Retry At**, and a trial shows **Trial Ends**.

Tap a subscription to open its **Subscription Detail** page:

- **Your Upcoming Order**: **Next Order Date** and **Next Bill Amount**.
- **Subscription Details**: **Order Frequency** and **Next Payment**.
- **Subscription ID**, the product, and the **Payment** totals.
- **Payment & Shipping**: **Shipping Address** and **Payment Method**.

To change a subscription:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the subscription">
    On the Dashboard, tap **Subscriptions**, then tap the subscription.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Manage Subscription">
    The subscription's management page opens inside the app. Make your changes there. For example, you can pause, cancel or reactivate the subscription.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back">
    Tap **Back**. The subscription's page and your subscriptions list refresh to show your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change the language

The **Language** list shows the languages your company offers that the app supports.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Language">
    On the Dashboard, tap **Language**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a language">
    Tap a language. The list closes and the app switches to that language.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Get help

Tap **Get Help** to contact support. If your company has a support email address, a new email to that address opens in your mail app. Otherwise, a support request form opens in your web browser. For other ways to get help, see [Getting help](/help/getting-help).

## Manage addresses and payment methods

Tap **Account** to manage your shipping addresses and payment methods. The page opens inside the app. Tap **Back** when you're done.

## General Information

Tap **General Information** to see:

- **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Use**: these open in your web browser.
- **Clear Cache**: see [Clear the cache](#clear-the-cache).
- **App Info**: your device, app version and update details. Tap **Copy** to copy them. Opening **App Info** also checks for an app update.

## Switch organizations

If you belong to more than one company, **Organization** near the top of the Dashboard shows the company you're using.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Organization">
    On the Dashboard, tap **Organization**. A list of your companies opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a company">
    Tap the company you want. The app switches to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose your notifications

Tap the bell at the top of the Dashboard to open **Notifications**. Your changes save as soon as you make them.

- **Do Not Disturb** silences notifications until tomorrow morning. Turn on **All** to silence push and email notifications, or turn on only **Push** or **Email**.
- Under **Presets**, tap **Default**, **Essential**, **Balanced** or **Engaged** to update all your preferences at once.
- Below the presets, your notifications are grouped by category. A category's switch turns all of its notifications on or off. Each notification has two switches: the first is for push notifications, the second for email.

## Troubleshooting

### Update the app

When a new version of the app is ready, an **Update Available** banner appears at the top of **Home**. Tap the banner to update. Depending on the update, the app restarts with the new version, or your app store opens so you can install it.

If an update is required, the banner covers the **Home** screen. Tap it to update.

To check for an update yourself, tap **General Information** on the Dashboard, then tap **App Info**. If an update is ready, the **Update Available** banner appears on that screen.

### Clear the cache

Clearing the cache makes the app load your data again, which can help when a screen shows out-of-date information. You stay signed in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open General Information">
    On the Dashboard, tap **General Information**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Clear Cache">
    A **Cache cleared** message appears with your app details, such as its version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the message">
    Tap **Ok**, or tap **Copy** to copy the details for support.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Trouble signing in

The sign-in screen shows your company's logo, or the app's name, and a **Sign in with email** button. Tapping it opens the sign-in page inside the app.

- The sign-in screens follow your phone's language when the app supports it, not the language you chose on the Dashboard.
- If the sign-in page doesn't load, tap **Back** at the top and try again.
- If a message such as **There was an error signing in** appears, the app takes you back to the first screen. Tap **Sign in with email** to try again.
- If you still can't sign in, see [Getting help](/help/getting-help).

## Tips

- If support asks for your app details, open **General Information**, tap **App Info**, then tap **Copy** and paste the details into your message.
- Pull down on **Notifications**, your orders or subscriptions, or an order's or subscription's page to refresh it.
