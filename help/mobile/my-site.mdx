MySite is your personal website, where customers and prospects can see the products, media and links you pick. Use the **MySite** screen to share it, change how it looks, and choose what it shows.

## Where to find it

In the app, tap your profile picture at the top of **Home**, then tap **MySite**.

The **MySite** card shows only if you have a MySite.

## What's on the screen

From top to bottom, the **MySite** screen shows:

- The info button at the top right. It opens **Introducing MySite**, a short overview of what MySite is for.
- **Visitor Details**: two counts for your site, **Views** and **Leads**.
- **Your Share Link**: your MySite link. Tap it to open **Quick Share**. See [Share your MySite link](#share-your-mysite-link).
- A preview of your site, with **Current Theme** and your theme's name. Tap the preview or **Preview** to open your site in a browser window. The edit button next to **Preview** opens **Theme**.
- **MySite Content**: the products and media on your site.
- **Buttons**: the links on your site.

## Tasks

### Share your MySite link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Quick Share">
    On the **MySite** screen, tap your link under **Your Share Link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link">
    Choose one:

    - Tap the link at the top of **Quick Share** to copy it. **Copied to clipboard** appears.
    - Tap **Share** to send it with your phone's share options.
    - Tap **Preview** to see your site first.
    - Under **Send To Your Contacts**, search by name or email to find a contact and send them the link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Quick Share** also shows a QR code for your link. For more on the share options and sending to contacts, see [Sharing content](/help/mobile/sharing).

### Add products and media to your MySite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    Open the product, media or page you want on your site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Add To">
    Tap the three-dot button at the top of the screen. The **Add To** menu opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose MySite">
    Tap **MySite**. **This has been added to MySite** appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Cards that have a plus button open the same **Add To** menu. If the item is already on your MySite, **You have already added this card to MySite** appears instead.

### Manage your MySite content

On the **MySite** screen, tap **MySite Content**. It shows what you've added to your site as cards. If you haven't added anything yet, **MySite Favorites** appears with an example card.

On each card:

- Tap the card to open the item.
- Tap the share button to open **Quick Share** for that item.
- Tap the minus button to remove the item from your MySite. It's removed right away, and **This has been deleted from MySite** appears.

Above the list, two buttons show the cards in a single column or in a grid.

To change the order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Touch and hold a card">
    When you have more than one item, **Drag to Re-arrange content** appears above the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag it to its new place">
    Let go to save the new order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Change your theme

The **Theme** screen shows a preview of your site with **Current Theme** and a **Preview** button. Below it, **Explore Themes** lists the themes you can choose, by name. A check mark marks your current theme.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Theme">
    On the **MySite** screen, tap the edit button next to **Preview**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a theme">
    Under **Explore Themes**, tap a theme. A **Preview** sheet opens and shows your site in that theme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply it">
    Tap **Apply**. The sheet closes. To keep your current theme, tap **Cancel** instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Apply** doesn't appear when you preview the theme you already use. If the change fails, **There was an error updating your theme. Please try again.** appears.

### Add a button

Buttons are named links on your MySite. On the **Buttons** screen, each button shows its name, its link and its number of **Clicks**. If you have no buttons yet, **No buttons** appears with a **New Button** button.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Buttons">
    On the **MySite** screen, tap **Buttons**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new button">
    Tap the plus button at the top right. If you have no buttons yet, you can tap **New Button** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and add the link">
    The **Button** form opens. Enter the **Button Name** and the **Link**. Both are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save**. You return to **Buttons**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Edit, reorder or delete a button

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the button">
    On the **Buttons** screen, tap the button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change or delete it">
    Change the **Button Name** or the **Link**, then tap **Save**. To delete the button, tap **Delete Button**. It's deleted right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To reorder your buttons, touch and hold one, drag it to its new place, and let go.

## Tips

- Pull down on **MySite Content**, **Buttons** or **Theme** to refresh it.
- Your **Home** tab may include a MySite widget. Tap it to open your MySite.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my MySite link?" id="how-to-customize-my-mysite-url">
    Yes, if your company allows it. The last part of your MySite link is your username. To change it:

    1. Tap your profile picture at the top of **Home**, then tap **Profile**.
    2. Under **Username**, enter the new username.
    3. Tap **Save**.

    Changing your username changes your MySite link, and links you shared with your old username stop working.

    A username needs at least 2 characters. It can use lowercase letters, numbers, hyphens and underscores. A hyphen can't come first or last, or next to another hyphen. Capital letters are saved as lowercase.

    If the field shows **Your admin manages the username on this account.**, you can't change it in the app. For the other profile fields, see [Settings and troubleshooting](/help/mobile/settings).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I add products and media to my MySite?" id="how-do-i-add-products-and-media-to-mysite">
    Open the product or media, tap the three-dot button at the top, and tap **MySite** in the **Add To** menu. Cards that have a plus button open the same menu. See [Add products and media to your MySite](#add-products-and-media-to-your-mysite).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I edit my MySite?" id="how-do-i-edit-my-mysite-page">
    You make every change from the **MySite** screen:

    - To change how your site looks, tap the edit button next to **Preview**. See [Change your theme](#change-your-theme).
    - To choose the products and media it shows, tap **MySite Content**. See [Manage your MySite content](#manage-your-mysite-content).
    - To add or change its links, tap **Buttons**. See [Add a button](#add-a-button).

    To change your MySite link, see [Can I change my MySite link?](#how-to-customize-my-mysite-url).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I preview my MySite?" id="how-do-i-preview-my-mysite">
    On the **MySite** screen, tap **Preview** on the preview of your site. Your site opens in a browser window. **Quick Share** has a **Preview** button too.

    To see a theme before you apply it, tap the edit button next to **Preview**, then tap the theme.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I create custom buttons on my MySite?" id="creating-custom-buttons-on-my-mysite">
    On the **MySite** screen, tap **Buttons**, then tap the plus button at the top right. Enter a **Button Name** and a **Link**, then tap **Save**. To edit or delete a button later, tap it. See [Add a button](#add-a-button).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see MySite on the Dashboard?" id="no-mysite-card">
    The **MySite** card shows only if you have a MySite. If you think you should have one, tap **Get Help** on the Dashboard to contact support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
