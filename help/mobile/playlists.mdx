Playlists group items, such as media, products and pages, so you can share them together with one link. This page also covers **My Uploads**, where you find the media you've uploaded, and how to create new media.

## Where to find it

In the app, tap your profile picture at the top of **Home**, then tap **My Playlists**.

Your **Share** tab may also have a tab with the same list, such as **Playlists**.

## What's on the screen

**My Playlists** opens a screen titled **Playlists**. The plus icon at the top right creates a playlist.

Each playlist appears as a card that shows pictures of its items and its name.

- Your own playlists have two icons on the card. The minus icon deletes the playlist. The plus icon opens the **Add To** drawer, where you can tap **MySite** to add the playlist to your MySite.
- Playlists from your company have no icons. You can open, share and copy them, but you can't change them.

Pull down to refresh the list. If you have no playlists yet, you see **No playlists** and a **Create Playlist** button.

### A playlist's page

Tap a playlist to open its page. The top has **Back**, a three-dot icon and the playlist's name. Below that:

- Two buttons switch the items between a list and a grid.
- Each item appears as a card. Tap a card to open the item's page, or tap its share icon to share the item.
- On your own playlists, each card also has a drag handle and a minus icon. With two or more items, the hint **Drag to Re-arrange content** appears.
- On a company playlist, each card has a plus icon instead. It opens the **Add To** drawer for that item.
- An empty playlist shows **No Items Added**.
- The bar at the bottom shows the playlist's views and leads, and the **Share Link** button.

The three-dot icon opens:

- On your own playlist, the **Add To** drawer with **MySite**, **Edit** and **Delete**.
- On a company playlist, **Copy to my Playlists**.

### My Uploads

To see the media you've uploaded, tap your profile picture at the top of **Home**, then tap **My Uploads**. The screen is titled **Uploads**. Your **Share** tab may also have a tab with the same list, such as **Uploads**.

- Each upload appears as a card with its views and leads at the top. Tap a card to open the upload's page.
- The share icon on a card shares the upload. The plus icon opens the **Add To** drawer, where you can add the upload to your MySite or a playlist, or save it for later.
- Pull down to refresh. The list also refreshes when an upload finishes.
- With no uploads, you see **No uploads**. You may also see an **Upload** button, which opens **New Media**.

To copy a media item into your uploads, open the item's page, tap the three-dot icon, then tap **Copy to Uploads**.

### An upload's page

An upload's page shows the upload, with **Share Link** at the bottom. On an upload you made, the three-dot icon at the top right opens the **Add To** drawer with **MySite**, **Playlists**, **Save for Later**, **Edit** and **Delete**.

## Tasks

### Create a playlist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new playlist">
    On the **Playlists** screen, tap the plus icon at the top right. If you have no playlists, you can tap **Create Playlist** instead.

    You can also start on the **Share** tab: tap the plus icon at the top, then tap **Create Playlist** in the **New Shareable** drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    In the **New Playlist** drawer, enter a name in **Title**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Tap **Create Playlist**. The new playlist appears in your list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Add items to a playlist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add To drawer">
    On the item's page, tap the three-dot icon at the top. Where an item's card shows a plus icon, you can tap that instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Playlists">
    The **Playlists** drawer lists your own playlists. A check mark shows the ones that already have this item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a playlist">
    Tap a playlist. A message confirms that the item was added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add the item to a new playlist, tap **Create Playlist** in the **Playlists** drawer, enter a name and tap **Create Playlist**. Then tap the new playlist in the list.

To take the item out of a playlist from this drawer, tap a playlist that has a check mark.

### Share a playlist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the playlist">
    Open one of your playlists or a company playlist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Share Link">
    Tap **Share Link** at the bottom of the screen. See [Sharing content](/help/mobile/sharing) for the share options.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To show one of your playlists on your MySite, tap the three-dot icon on its page, then tap **MySite**. See [MySite](/help/mobile/my-site).

### Arrange and remove items

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your playlist">
    Open one of your own playlists. You can't rearrange a company playlist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder items">
    Touch and hold an item, then drag it to a new place. The app saves the new order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove items">
    Tap the minus icon on an item's card. The item is removed right away, without asking you to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Copy a company playlist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the company playlist">
    On the **Playlists** screen, tap a playlist from your company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy it">
    Tap the three-dot icon at the top right, then tap **Copy to my Playlists**. A message confirms the copy, and it's added to your playlists.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Rename a playlist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Edit">
    On your playlist's page, tap the three-dot icon, then tap **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the new name">
    In the **Edit Playlist** drawer, enter the new name in **Title**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Delete a playlist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    On your playlist's page, tap the three-dot icon, then tap **Delete**. Or, on the **Playlists** screen, tap the minus icon on the playlist's card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Delete Playlist** message, tap **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a playlist can't be undone.
</Warning>

### Create media

The plus icon at the top of the **Share** tab opens the **New Shareable** drawer, with **Create Trackable Link** and **Create Playlist**. If your company lets reps create media, it also has **Create Media**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open New Media">
    On the **Share** tab, tap the plus icon at the top, then tap **Create Media**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your file">
    Under **Media**, tap **Upload Media** to choose an image or video, or **Upload Document** to choose a PDF. To choose a different file, tap the trash icon on the preview. For a video, you can also tap the plus icon under **Select Cover Image** to choose a cover image.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a title and description">
    Enter a **Title**, which is required. A **Description** is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a button, if you want one">
    Turn on **Enable Action Buttons**, then enter **Button Text** and **Button Link**. The link must be a web address, such as `example.com/page`. If you leave off `https://`, the app adds it.

    To add a form that collects contact information from new leads, also turn on **Lead Capture**. Then only **Button Text** is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save**. A message asks you to confirm that the post makes no income or product claims that aren't allowed, and says that uploaded media is monitored for compliance. Tap **Agree**. When the upload finishes, the **Uploads** screen opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If something required is missing, a message tells you what to add.

### Edit or delete an upload

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the upload">
    On the **Uploads** screen, tap an upload you made.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Edit or Delete">
    Tap the three-dot icon at the top right, then tap **Edit** or **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    **Edit** opens the **Update Media** form, which has the same fields as **New Media**. Make your changes, tap **Save**, then tap **Agree**. If you tap **Back** instead, the app asks whether to discard your changes. Tap **Agree** to leave without saving.

    **Delete** asks you to confirm. Tap **Delete**. Deleting an upload can't be undone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips

- Company playlists can't be changed. To get a version you can change, copy it to your playlists.
- To see how a playlist is doing, check the views and leads at the bottom of its page.
- The plus icon at the top of the **Share** tab is unavailable while an upload is in progress.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I save content to view later?" id="how-do-i-save-content-to-view-later">
    Open the item's page and tap the three-dot icon at the top. Where the item's card shows a plus icon, you can tap that instead. In the **Add To** drawer, tap **Save for Later**. A message confirms that it's saved.

    If your company has added a list of saved items to your app, your saved items appear there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I change a playlist?" id="cant-change-company-playlist">
    It's a playlist from your company. Company playlists have no icons on their cards, and their three-dot icon offers only **Copy to my Playlists**. Copy the playlist to get a version you can change.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see Create Media?" id="no-create-media">
    Your company chooses whether reps can create media in the app. If it hasn't turned this on, the **New Shareable** drawer shows only **Create Trackable Link** and **Create Playlist**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
